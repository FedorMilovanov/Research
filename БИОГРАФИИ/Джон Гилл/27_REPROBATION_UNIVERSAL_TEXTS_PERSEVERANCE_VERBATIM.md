## 1. GAP-4: Reprobation / Отвержение (Book II ch.3, ccel doctrinal.iii.iii)

### 1.1 Терминология (вербатим)
> «I make use of the word **›rejection‹** in this article, partly because it is a scriptural phrase and ascribed to God… partly because… **the other word reprobation, through wrong and frightful ideas being affixed to it, carries in it with many a sound harsh and disagreeable**.»

→ Гилл осознанно избегает «reprobation» из-за неверных ассоциаций. Опора: Иер 6:30 («reprobate silver shall men call them, because the Lord hath rejected them»).

### 1.2 Двучастная структура (вербатим)
> «1a. A **non-election, or preterition** of them, a passing over them… when others were chosen.»
> «1b. The **appointment of them to wrath and damnation**; in this they were viewed as sinful, fallen creatures.»

→ Та же структура, что в Cause Part I (напр. Section LVI, 2 Пет 3:9): preterition (оставление) + predamnation (назначение к осуждению). Мф 25:41 («everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels»).

### 1.3 Гилл — супралапсарий (новое, важно)
> «they were viewed as not yet created and fallen, but **as lying in the pure mass of creatureship or creability**; God saw in his power what creatures of this kind he could produce into being… and of those he could and would create, he determined to choose some and leave others.»

→ «Pure mass» (а не «corrupt mass» post-lapsus) — это **супралапсарианская** позиция (декрет об избрании/отвержении логически до декрета о грехопадении). Это уточняет досье 02 и подтверждает оценку Curt Daniel (хотя Daniel это использовал против Гилла).

### 1.4 Психологическое наблюдение Гилла (вербатим, «золотая» цитата)
> «we can hear and read of the non-election and rejection of **angels**… with very little emotion of mind… but if anything of this kind is hinted at, with respect to any of the apostate sons of Adam, presently there is an outcry against it… **It can be only this, that the latter comes nearer home… it is partiality to ourselves, our nature, and race.**»

→ Гилл-полемист: возражения против reprobation продиктованы не логикой, а самопристрастием.

## 2. GAP-3: «Texts seeming to Favour Universal Redemption» (Book VI ch.4, ccel doctrinal.vii.iv)

### 2.1 Структура главы (вербатим)
> «these may be divided into **three** classes: 1. Such in which the words ‹all›, and ‹every› one, are used, when the death of Christ… are spoken of. 2. Those in which the words ‹world›, and the ‹whole world›, occur… 3. Those that seem to intimate, as if Christ died for some that may be destroyed and perish.»

→ Это **систематический аналог** Cause of God and Truth Part I (досье 21/23): та же классификация «универсальных» текстов, но в систематическом труде, а не полемическом. Подтверждает единство метода Гилла.

### 2.2 Лк 2:10-11 (ангел: «радость всем людям») — вербатим
> «Christ is **not here said to be the Saviour of all men; but to be born for the sake of some**, that he might be the Saviour of them… ‹great joy to all people›… not to all the people of the world, many of whom never heard of it… but to all the people of God and Christ.»

### 2.3 Ин 1:7 («все через него могли уверовать») — duty-faith нюанс (важно для гипер-баланса)
> «all men are bound to believe that Christ came to save them, and that he died for them; and if he did not die for them, then they are bound to believe a lie… But… **men may be bound to believe, and yet not to the saving of their souls; or that Christ died for them.**»
> «The Indians who have never heard of Christ, **are not bound to believe in him; nor will they be condemned for their unbelief; but for their sins against the light of nature**.»

→ **Это против обвинения в гипер-кальвинизме**: Гилл признаёт долг веры (duty) — но различает историческую веру (в факт откровения) и спасающую (во Христа умершего за меня). Осуждение язычников — не за неверие во Христа (они не слышали), а за грех против света природы (Рим 2:12). Это тонкое, но решающее различие, которое Rathel/Toon упускают.

### 2.4 Ин 12:32 («привлеку всех к Себе») — вербатим
> «this is not true of all and every individual person; for there were multitudes then, as now, **who will have no will to come to Christ**, and are never wrought upon by the grace of God.»

## 3. GAP-5: Претерпение святых (Book VI ch.15, ccel doctrinal.vii.xv)

### 3.1 Определение (вербатим)
> «those who are truly regenerated, effectually called, and really converted, and internally sanctified by the Spirit and grace of God, **shall persevere in grace to the end, and shall be everlastingly saved; or shall never finally and totally fall, so as to perish everlastingly**.»

→ Подтверждает Declaration of Faith арт. IX (досье 02 §1) и Cause of God and Truth (Section L, Евр 6:4-6). Теперь верифицировано из систематики.

### 3.2 Библейские доказательства (вербатим, выборка)
- **Иов 17:9**: «The righteous also shall hold on his way… though he may slip, and slide, and stumble, and even fall; yet as the traveler… gets up again… so the believer rises again in the strength of Christ.»
- **Пс 94:14**: «The Lord will not cast off his people… they are engraven on the palms of his hands… set as a seal [on his heart].»
- **Пс 125:1-2**: «they cannot be removed; not from the love of God… nor from the covenant of grace… nor out of the hands of Christ, out of whose hands none can pluck.»

→ Ср. Declaration of Faith арт. IX: «all those who are chosen by the Father, redeemed by the Son, and sanctified by the Spirit, shall certainly and finally persevere» (досье 02 §1) — тождество формулировки с систематикой.
