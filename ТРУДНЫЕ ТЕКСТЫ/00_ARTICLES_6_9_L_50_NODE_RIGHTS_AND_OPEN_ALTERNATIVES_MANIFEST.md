## Дополнительные контрольные узлы, не включённые в счёт 50

### Codex Sinaiticus

- official project copyright page;
- XML/transcription licensing page (`CC BY-NC-SA 3.0` for the stated project content);
- folio 320 locus for 1 Pet. 2:20–4:1;
- separate check required for each selected image asset and the site's actual NonCommercial status.

### Site implementation

- no IAA/BAV image should enter `/public`, build artefacts, OG images, cache or repository history before permission;
- external thumbnails must not be hotlinked unless terms expressly allow it;
- IIIF availability is a technical delivery method, not a publication licence;
- screenshotting a viewer does not create new rights;
- alt text may describe a manuscript without reproducing its image;
- exact permissions and credit lines must be stored next to the asset metadata.
