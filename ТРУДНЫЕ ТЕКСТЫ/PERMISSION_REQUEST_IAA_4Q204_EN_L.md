1. whether this use can be licensed;
2. whether a fee applies;
3. the required credit line;
4. the maximum permitted resolution;
5. the permitted licence term and territories;
6. whether any crop or annotation is allowed;
7. whether you require a preview of the page before publication;
8. any other technical or legal conditions.
