## 4. Lexical `ἄγγελος` safeguard

The lexeme can mean a human messenger. Human use is real elsewhere in the NT.

But lexeme-range does not override local discourse.

Within 1 Corinthians the explicit comparators are celestial:

- angels vs humans (4:9);
- believers judge angels (6:3);
- human vs angelic tongues (13:1).

Therefore:

```text
ANGELOS_HUMAN_MESSENGER_LEXICALLY_POSSIBLE = A
V10_HUMAN_MESSENGER_CONTEXTUALLY_LEADING = false
```
