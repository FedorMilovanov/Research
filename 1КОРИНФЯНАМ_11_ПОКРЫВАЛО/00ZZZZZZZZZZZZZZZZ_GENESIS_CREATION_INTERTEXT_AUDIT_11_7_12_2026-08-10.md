# 1 Коринфянам 11:7–12 — Genesis / creation intertext audit

**Дата:** 2026-08-10  
**Статус:** `PRIMARY-INTERTEXT / CREATION-REASONING / RESEARCH-ONLY / PUBLICATION-HOLD`

## 0. Purpose

This audit isolates the **creation-text layer** of 1 Cor 11:7–12 before social reconstruction is allowed to control it.

Questions:

1. What does Genesis 1:26–28 actually say about image, male and female?
2. Which parts of 1 Cor 11:7–12 echo Genesis 2 directly?
3. Does v7 deny that woman is image of God?
4. What exactly is asymmetrical in vv8–9?
5. What exactly changes in vv11–12?
6. Is mutuality a cancellation, a qualification, or a restatement of creation?
7. Does proposed 1 Esdras 4 intertext add explanatory value without becoming necessary to the argument?

Method:

```text
PRIMARY_GENESIS_TEXT
    -> DIRECT_VERBAL/THEMATIC_ECHO
    -> PAULINE_TRANSFORMATION
    -> ONLY THEN theological/historical reconstruction
```

Do not infer a Genesis claim from later theological systems and then read it back into Paul.

# 1. Genesis 1:26–28 LXX — image belongs to the created human who is male and female

Greek LXX Genesis 1:26–27:

```text
ποιήσωμεν ἄνθρωπον κατ’ εἰκόνα ἡμετέραν ...
καὶ ἐποίησεν ὁ θεὸς τὸν ἄνθρωπον,
κατ’ εἰκόνα θεοῦ ἐποίησεν αὐτόν,
ἄρσεν καὶ θῆλυ ἐποίησεν αὐτούς.
```

Genesis 1:28 then blesses **them** and gives **them** the creation mandate.

## 1.1. Minimum textual facts

```text
HUMAN_CREATED_IN_GODS_IMAGE = A_GENESIS_TEXT
HUMAN_CREATION_IS_ARTICULATED_AS_MALE_AND_FEMALE = A_GENESIS_TEXT
BOTH_RECEIVE_DIVINE_BLESSING/MANDATE = A_GENESIS_TEXT
```

The grammar moves from singular `τὸν ἄνθρωπον / αὐτόν` to plural `ἄρσεν καὶ θῆλυ ... αὐτούς`.

This does not answer every later philosophical question about whether “image” is individual, corporate, relational, functional, Christological, etc.

But it creates a major burden for the categorical claim:

```text
GENESIS_1_TEACHES_WOMAN_IS_NOT_GODS_IMAGE = false
```

The creation of the human in God’s image is immediately specified as male and female.

---

# 2. 1 Cor 11:7 — what Paul actually says and does not say

Paul says approximately:

```text
MAN = image + glory of God
WOMAN = glory of man
```

Crucial observation:

```text
PAUL_SAYS_WOMAN_IS_NOT_IMAGE_OF_GOD = false
```

He omits `εἰκών` from the second clause; he does not write a negation.

Therefore the inference:

```text
woman = glory of man
therefore woman ≠ image of God
```

is **not direct syntax**. It is an argument from the asymmetry/ellipsis of v7 and must be tested against Genesis 1:27.

## 2.1. Conservative-safe formulation

The strongest fail-closed statement is:

> In the local rhetorical argument Paul predicates “image and glory of God” of the man and “glory of man” of the woman; this creates a real asymmetry in the argument, but does not explicitly deny the woman’s participation in the Genesis 1 imago Dei.

Grade:

```text
V7_LOCAL_ASYMMETRY = A_TEXT
V7_DENIES_FEMALE_IMAGO_DEI = C_LOW / NOT_EXPLICIT
GEN1_MALE_FEMALE_IMAGE_CONTROL = A
```

This protects the creation-order argument without forcing Paul to contradict the wording of Genesis 1.

---

# 3. How Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 can both be active

Paul appears to combine two creation perspectives:

## Genesis 1

```text
human -> image of God
male + female -> created / blessed / commissioned
```

## Genesis 2

```text
man formed first
woman formed from man
woman made as corresponding helper for man
woman brought to man
man recognizes woman as bone/flesh from him
marital one-flesh union follows
```

Thus:

```text
GEN1 = SHARED HUMAN IMAGE/CREATION MANDATE
GEN2 = SEX-DIFFERENTIATED ORIGIN/RELATION NARRATIVE
```

Paul can reason from the second without denying the first.

This is more textually economical than forcing Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 into mutually exclusive anthropologies.

# 4. Genesis 2:18–24 LXX — primary controls

## 4.1. Genesis 2:18

```text
οὐ καλὸν εἶναι τὸν ἄνθρωπον μόνον·
ποιήσωμεν αὐτῷ βοηθὸν κατ’ αὐτόν
```

The human alone is “not good”; God will make a helper `κατ’ αὐτόν` — a helper corresponding to/appropriate to him.

Genesis 2:20 similarly says no `βοηθὸς ὅμοιος αὐτῷ` was found.

Textual facts:

```text
MAN_ALONE_NOT_GOOD = A
WOMAN_CREATED_AS_CORRESPONDING_HELPER = A
```

The noun/helper concept itself is not a lexical synonym for inferiority. Paul’s **use** of the purpose relation in v9 must be assessed in its own context.

## 4.2. Genesis 2:21–22

God takes a side/rib from Adam and builds it into a woman.

```text
WOMAN_DERIVED_FROM_MAN_IN_NARRATIVE = A
```

## 4.3. Genesis 2:23

Adam says:

```text
ὀστοῦν ἐκ τῶν ὀστέων μου
...
γυνή,
ὅτι ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρὸς αὐτῆς ἐλήφθη αὕτη
```

This phrase is particularly important for Paul’s `ἐκ` language.

## 4.4. Genesis 2:24

The man leaves father/mother and is joined to **his wife**, becoming one flesh.

This confirms that the Genesis narrative naturally supports both:

- primordial man/woman anthropology;
- marriage/husband-wife application.

Therefore Genesis 2 itself helps explain why `ἀνήρ/γυνή` can glide between sex-class and marital relation in 1 Cor 11 without requiring one English gloss for every occurrence.

# 5. 1 Cor 11:8 — woman `ἐξ ἀνδρός`

Paul:

```text
οὐ γάρ ἐστιν ἀνὴρ ἐκ γυναικός,
ἀλλὰ γυνὴ ἐξ ἀνδρός
```

This is a strong Genesis 2 origin echo.

Genesis 2:23 supplies:

```text
ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρός
```

## Grade

```text
V8_GENESIS_2_ORIGIN_ALLUSION = A/B_HIGH_INTERTEXT
V8_WOMAN_FROM_MAN = A_PAULINE_TEXT
```

The wording strongly constrains interpretation.

## Important lexical safeguard

Paul expresses **origin** here explicitly with `ἐκ`.

Therefore:

```text
V8_PROVES_ORIGIN_CONCEPT = A
V8_PROVES_KEPHALE_LEXICALLY_MEANS_SOURCE_IN_V3 = false
```

Origin may contribute to v3 contextually; it does not become a dictionary definition of `κεφαλή`.

---

# 6. 1 Cor 11:9 — woman “because of” man

Paul:

```text
καὶ γὰρ οὐκ ἐκτίσθη ἀνὴρ διὰ τὴν γυναῖκα,
ἀλλὰ γυνὴ διὰ τὸν ἄνδρα
```

Genesis 2 does not use this exact Greek sentence.

The conceptual basis is Genesis 2:18–22:

- the man’s aloneness is not good;
- God determines to make a corresponding helper **for him**;
- the woman is created and brought to him.

Thus:

```text
V9_GENESIS_2_PURPOSE_RELATION = B_HIGH_INTERTEXTUAL_INFERENCE
```

## What v9 establishes

Within Paul’s argument there is a real creational asymmetry of purpose/orientation:

```text
WOMAN_CREATED_DIA_THE_MAN = A_PAULINE_TEXT
```

Any interpretation that makes vv7–9 say nothing substantive about created sex differentiation is too weak.

## What v9 does not automatically establish

It does not by itself lexicalize:

```text
permanent civil inferiority
ontological lesser worth
lack of female divine image
all possible forms of male authority
```

Those are additional theological conclusions requiring separate argument.

---

# 7. vv7–9 as hierarchy/order argument

The sequence is not random:

```text
v7 glory/image predicates
  -> γάρ
v8 origin asymmetry
  -> καὶ γάρ
v9 purpose asymmetry
```

The repeated explanatory particles make vv8–9 reasons for the v7 asymmetry.

Therefore:

```text
V7_9_CREATION_REASONING_IS_REAL = A_DISCOURSE
V7_9_SEX_DIFFERENTIATION/ORDER_FORCE = B_HIGH
```

The project’s conservative weighting is justified here: Paul is not merely citing a local fashion code.

But “order” must remain a contextual/theological descriptor rather than pretending `τάξις` or “hierarchy” is an overt noun in the verses.

---

# 8. v10 — creation reasoning reaches female agency, not passive erasure

The next sentence begins:

```text
διὰ τοῦτο
```

= “because of this / for this reason.”

Thus v10 is inferentially connected to vv7–9.

But the result is:

```text
ἡ γυνὴ ... ἐξουσίαν ἔχειν ἐπὶ τῆς κεφαλῆς
```

The lexical audit independently established that the woman is the grammatical subject/bearer of `ἐξουσία`.

Therefore the creation argument cannot be represented accurately as:

```text
vv7–9 = male order
therefore v10 = woman has no agency
```

The text itself moves from creation asymmetry to an obligation in which **the woman has/bears authority/right**.

Exact referent remains B/C, but female syntactic agency is A.

This is one of the central internal tensions that the whole-model synthesis must preserve rather than flatten.

---

# 9. v11 `πλὴν` — real qualification, not automatic reversal

Paul:

```text
πλὴν οὔτε γυνὴ χωρὶς ἀνδρὸς
οὔτε ἀνὴρ χωρὶς γυναικὸς
ἐν κυρίῳ
```

`πλὴν` is a discourse qualifier/transition.

Thus:

```text
V11_QUALIFIES_PRIOR_CREATION_ARGUMENT = A_DISCOURSE
```

Two errors are blocked:

### Error A

> vv11–12 mean nothing important; vv7–9 are the entire theology.

False: Paul explicitly adds a qualifying mutuality statement.

### Error B

> `πλὴν` proves Paul repudiates vv7–10 as somebody else’s quotation.

False: a qualification can occur inside one speaker’s argument.

Therefore:

```text
V11_COUNTERBALANCE = A/B_HIGH
V11_CANCELS_V7_10 = false
V11_PROVES_SPEAKER_CHANGE = false
```

---

# 10. v11 — “neither woman without man nor man without woman in the Lord”

The syntax is reciprocal:

```text
οὔτε ... χωρὶς ...
οὔτε ... χωρὶς ...
```

This is not merely “woman needs man.” Paul adds the inverse.

```text
V11_MUTUAL_INTERDEPENDENCE = A_TEXT
```

The phrase `ἐν κυρίῳ` locates the mutuality in Christian/Lord-related reality, not merely generic reproduction.

Exactly how `ἐν κυρίῳ` relates creation to new creation/ecclesial life is a theological question, but the qualifying mutuality is overt.

---

# 11. v12a — Paul deliberately returns to Genesis wording

Paul:

```text
ὥσπερ γὰρ ἡ γυνὴ ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρός
```

Genesis 2:23:

```text
ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρός
```

This is an especially strong verbal echo.

```text
V12A_GENESIS_2_23_ECHO = A/B_HIGH
```

Paul does not abandon the origin asymmetry of v8. He restates it inside the mutuality section.

This is decisive against readings where vv11–12 simply erase vv8–9.

---

# 12. v12b — `ὁ ἀνὴρ διὰ τῆς γυναικός`

Paul then adds:

```text
οὕτως καὶ ὁ ἀνὴρ διὰ τῆς γυναικός
```

The obvious human-life referent is male birth **through woman**, although the wording is broad enough to function as a general dependence statement.

Key distinction:

```text
WOMAN_FROM_MAN = ἐκ
MAN_THROUGH_WOMAN = διά
```

Paul does **not** make the relations lexically identical.

Thus:

```text
V12_PRESERVES_DIFFERENCE_OF_RELATION = A_SYNTAX
V12_ESTABLISHES_RECIPROCAL_DEPENDENCE = A_DISCOURSE
```

Gundry-Volf/Newberry and others rightly note that `ἐκ` vs `διά` preserves distinction even while the argument balances male/female dependence.

---

# 13. v12c — `τὰ δὲ πάντα ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ`

Paul closes:

```text
τὰ δὲ πάντα ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ
```

> but all things are from God.

This creates a final origin hierarchy that relativizes any attempt to absolutize the human origin chain.

```text
ULTIMATE_SOURCE_OF_ALL = GOD = A_TEXT
```

Therefore the creation logic is:

```text
woman from man
man through woman
all from God
```

not:

```text
woman from man
therefore man is ultimate source / self-sufficient head of woman
```

This does **not** destroy male/female differentiation. It places it beneath divine ultimacy.

---

# 14. Conservative synthesis of vv7–12

The strongest conservative reading must preserve **all four** data blocks:

```text
A. GENESIS-BASED SEX DIFFERENTIATION = real
B. ORIGIN/PURPOSE ASYMMETRY vv8–9 = real
C. WOMAN'S ACTIVE EXOUSIA v10 = real
D. MUTUAL INTERDEPENDENCE + ALL FROM GOD vv11–12 = real
```

A model that suppresses B becomes too egalitarianizing for the actual sequence.

A model that suppresses C/D becomes too unilateral for the actual sequence.

Thus the current calibrated description is:

```text
CREATIONAL_ORDER/ASYMMETRY = B_HIGH
MUTUAL_INTERDEPENDENCE = A
DIVINE_ULTIMACY_OVER_HUMAN_ORIGIN_CHAIN = A
```

This is stronger than either slogan:

- “Paul simply teaches hierarchy”; or
- “Paul retracts hierarchy and teaches indistinguishable equality.”

# 15. Does v7 deny female image of God? — dedicated adversarial test

## Model A — woman not image of God in creation

### Support

- v7 predicates `εἰκὼν καὶ δόξα θεοῦ` only of man;
- woman gets only `δόξα ἀνδρός`.

### Costs

1. no explicit negation;
2. Genesis 1:27 places male and female immediately inside the image-creation statement;
3. Paul can foreground one aspect of Genesis 2 without providing an exhaustive anthropology;
4. v11–12 strongly resist female ontological marginalization.

### Grade

```text
FEMALE_NOT_IMAGE_OF_GOD_MODEL = C_LOW
```

## Model B — both sexes bear divine image, but v7 assigns differentiated glory relation

### Support

- directly respects Genesis 1:27;
- respects v7’s actual asymmetry without turning omission into negation;
- allows Paul to read Genesis 1 through Genesis 2 for local argument.

### Grade

```text
BOTH_SEXES_PARTICIPATE_IN_GENESIS_IMAGO = A/B_HIGH_PRIMARY_CONTROL
V7_DIFFERENTIATED_GLORY_RELATION = A_TEXT
```

This is the preferred fail-closed synthesis.

# 16. `δόξα` — what Genesis alone does not explain

Genesis 1 gives `εἰκών`; Genesis 2 gives origin/purpose relations.

Genesis does **not** simply state:

```text
woman = glory of man
```

Therefore v7’s `δόξα` formulation may involve:

- Pauline theological/rhetorical development;
- honor/shame cultural semantics;
- another Jewish intertext;
- relational manifestation/reflection.

This is where 1 Esdras becomes relevant as a **possible** conversation partner.

---

# 17. 1 Esdras 4:13–17 — proposed “woman as glory of man” intertext

Greek 1 Esdras 4:17 says of women:

```text
αὗται ποιοῦσιν τὰς στολὰς τῶν ἀνθρώπων,
καὶ αὗται ποιοῦσιν δόξαν τοῖς ἀνθρώποις,
καὶ οὐ δύνανται οἱ ἄνθρωποι εἶναι χωρὶς τῶν γυναικῶν
```

Minimum sense:

- women make men’s garments;
- women bring/make glory for men;
- men cannot exist/be without women.

This is strikingly relevant to 1 Cor 11:7b and 11:11.

## 17.1. Availability

The relevant Greek 1 Esdras tradition predates Paul sufficiently to be a possible intertextual resource; scholarly work notes Josephus’ use of 1 Esdras as a chronological control.

## 17.2. Verbal/thematic correspondences

```text
1ESD 4:17: women -> glory to men
1COR 11:7: woman -> glory of man

1ESD 4:17: men cannot be without women
1COR 11:11: man not without woman
```

1 Esdras 4 also emphasizes that men are **born from women** (4:15–16), which resonates with Paul’s v12b “man through woman.”

## 17.3. Why this is not A

Paul never cites 1 Esdras by name or formula.

The parallels may arise from shared Jewish/anthropological language, Genesis reception, or coincidental thematic convergence.

Therefore:

```text
1ESDRAS_4_INTERTEXT = B_C_SERIOUS_PROPOSED_ECHO
1ESDRAS_REQUIRED_FOR_CORE_EXEGESIS = false
```

Newberry 2018/2019 provides the most developed modern allusion argument and explicitly treats Genesis 1, Genesis 2 and 1 Esdras 4 as interacting conversation partners.

---

# 18. 1 Esdras does not replace Genesis

A methodological danger is to let an attractive intertext become the engine of the passage.

Genesis echoes are stronger because:

- `image of God` is direct Genesis language;
- `woman from man` closely reproduces Gen 2:23;
- woman-for-man fits Gen 2:18–22;
- the creation logic is explicitly signaled by Paul.

1 Esdras is useful primarily for:

```text
GLORY_OF_MAN
MAN_NOT_WITHOUT_WOMAN
MAN_BORN/THROUGH_WOMAN
possibly EXOUSIA thematic field
```

Thus evidence hierarchy:

```text
GENESIS_1_2 = A/B_HIGH_PRIMARY_INTERTEXT
1_ESDRAS_4 = B_C_PROPOSED_SECONDARY_INTERTEXT
```

# 19. Does v12 “reverse” Genesis 2?

No.

Paul explicitly **repeats** Genesis 2:23’s `ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρός` in v12a.

Then he adds the reciprocal fact `man through woman`.

Therefore the best discourse description is:

```text
PRESERVE + COUNTERBALANCE + RELOCATE_UNDER_GOD
```

not:

```text
RETRACT
```

This supports the existing project grade:

```text
MUTUAL_INTERDEPENDENCE_11_11_12 = A
```

while maintaining:

```text
CREATION_ORDER/ORIGIN_ASYMMETRY = B_HIGH
```

# 20. Does creation reasoning make head covering universally identical across all cultures?

Not by itself.

Genesis can ground:

- sex differentiation;
- relational order;
- theological rationale.

It does not name:

- a palla;
- toga;
- exact veil dimensions;
- one universal hairstyle;
- one modern garment form.

Thus:

```text
CREATION_GROUNDING = STRONG_ARGUMENT_AGAINST_PURE_ARBITRARY_FASHION
CREATION_GROUNDING != IDENTICAL_MATERIAL_FORM_IN_ALL_CULTURES_BY_ITSELF
```

The normative sign/application question still requires:

- vv4–6 covering semantics;
- vv13–15 nature/hair analogy;
- v16 church practice;
- hermeneutical analysis.

# 21. Creation-order model stress test

## Hard hierarchical model

```text
man = image of God
woman ≠ image
woman derived/for man
vv11–12 merely soften tone
```

### Problems

- female-image denial exceeds v7 wording and strains Genesis 1:27;
- mutuality becomes rhetorically underweighted;
- “all from God” loses force.

Grade:

```text
HARD_UNILATERAL_CREATION_HIERARCHY = C
```

## No-order egalitarian model

```text
vv7–9 have no enduring creation-order force
vv11–12 undo them
```

### Problems

- repeated `γάρ` chain makes vv8–9 real reasons;
- v12a repeats, rather than repudiates, woman-from-man;
- sex-differentiated origin remains active.

Grade:

```text
V11_12_CANCEL_CREATION_ASYMMETRY = C_LOW
```

## Calibrated conservative mutual-order model

```text
real creation asymmetry/order
+ shared imago Dei under Genesis 1
+ woman’s active authority in v10
+ reciprocal dependence in the Lord
+ God as ultimate source of all
```

Grade:

```text
CALIBRATED_CREATION_MUTUAL_ORDER_MODEL = B_HIGH_LEADING
```

Not A because “order/hierarchy” is a theological synthesis of the relations rather than an overt noun in the text, and exact `κεφαλή` force remains B/C-contested.

# 22. Translation/research-data implications

Future Russian analytical layer should preserve distinctions:

## v7

Do **not** translate or annotate as:

> «женщина не образ Божий»

unless clearly labeled as a disputed inference.

Use:

> «муж есть образ и слава Бога, а женщина — слава мужа»

with an expandable note:

```text
Genesis 1:27 remains a control on whether the omitted `image` is a negation.
```

## v8

Preserve `из / от` origin:

```text
γυνὴ ἐξ ἀνδρός
```

## v9

Preserve purpose relation:

```text
woman because of / for the man
```

without silently expanding to an entire authority doctrine.

## v11–12

Make the reciprocal structure visually explicit:

```text
woman not without man
man not without woman
IN THE LORD

woman FROM man
man THROUGH woman
ALL FROM GOD
```

This is ideal source material for the future flip/matrix Product, but no UI is implemented here.
