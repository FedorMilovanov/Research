# 1. Why M2 leads

M2 wins not because it chooses a political midpoint, but because it requires the fewest textual deletions.

It preserves:

```text
GENESIS_1_SHARED_IMAGE_CONTROL
GENESIS_2_ORIGIN/PURPOSE_ASYMMETRY
V7_9_REAL_ARGUMENT
V10_REAL_FEMALE_AGENCY
V11_12_REAL_MUTUALITY
GOD_AS_FINAL_SOURCE
```

The apparent “tension” is therefore data to be modeled, not noise to be eliminated.

Current:

```text
CALIBRATED_CREATION_MUTUAL_ORDER_MODEL = B_HIGH_LEADING
```

---

# 2. Why M1 is too hard

A conservative reading does not need to claim more than Paul/Genesis say.

The hard form incurs avoidable burdens when it says:

- woman is not divine image;
- mutuality is merely sentimental;
- v10 authority is entirely someone else’s authority;
- male origin from woman is theologically irrelevant.

Those claims exceed the minimum textual data.

```text
HARD_UNILATERAL_HIERARCHY = C
FEMALE_NOT_IMAGO_DEI = C_LOW
```

A less extreme headship/order reading remains fully compatible with M2.

---

# 3. Why M3 is too weak on vv7–9

M3 correctly feels the force of vv11–12 but pays by under-reading:

- `γάρ`;
- `ἐξ ἀνδρός`;
- `διὰ τὸν ἄνδρα`;
- v12a’s deliberate repetition of the Genesis origin relation.

Paul counterbalances the origin story; he does not delete it.

```text
V11_12_CANCEL_CREATION_ASYMMETRY = C_LOW
```

---

# 4. Why M4 remains edge

The long quotation model explains the ideological contrast by assigning different voices.

But it requires:

1. an unmarked quotation beginning around v3/v7;
2. `γάρ` chains inside the quotation;
3. `διὰ τοῦτο` v10 inside the quotation or an awkward speaker transition;
4. `πλὴν` v11 as a speaker-switch marker despite its ordinary qualifying force;
5. no manuscript or overt discourse signal marking the large quote.

Therefore:

```text
LARGE_QUOTATION_REFUTATION = D_C_LOW
```

The real contrast between vv7–10 and vv11–12 does not itself prove two speakers.

---

# 5. 1 Esdras pressure test

The 1 Esdras proposal does not require a fifth whole model. It can modify M2/M3/M4 differently.

Most plausible contribution:

```text
woman -> glory to men
men cannot be without women
men born from women
```

Those phrases can help explain why Paul’s rhetoric moves from “glory of man” to mutual dependence.

But because Genesis alone already anchors the main creation logic:

```text
1_ESDRAS_4 = OPTIONAL_B_C_INTERTEXT
```

A model does not fail if it rejects the 1 Esdras echo.
