# 1. Virgil, Aeneid 3.403–409 — garment over the head during sacrifice

Virgil instructs Aeneas to cover his hair with a purple mantle during sacrificial vows so that a hostile face will not disturb the sacred omen; the ritual custom is then to be retained.

Open text:
- https://aeneid.co/text/book-3/403-419

