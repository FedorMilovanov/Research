# 3. Loren T. Stuckenbruck — cosmic-order model, not simply “good angels” vs “Watchers”

## 3.1 Direct 2001 peer-reviewed article

Durham institutional repository verifies:

> Loren T. Stuckenbruck, “Why Should Women Cover Their Heads Because of the Angels? (1 Corinthians 11:10),” *Stone-Campbell Journal* 4.2 (2001): 205–234. Peer reviewed.

- https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/output/1559081

Official journal page:

- https://www.stone-campbelljournal.com/the_journal/research/volume-4-issue-2/40203/

The official abstract is especially important. It states that:

- Paul negotiates between theological ideals in vv11–12 and assumptions about the sexuality of men, women **and angels**;
- Paul’s instructions derive from what he considers proper to **the ordering of the cosmos**;
- consequently, it is less important for this interpretation to decide whether the angels in v10 are “good” or “bad”.

Thus:

```text
STUCKENBRUCK_2001_COSMIC_ORDER_MODEL = DIRECT_AUTHOR_ABSTRACT
STUCKENBRUCK_GOOD_VS_BAD_IDENTITY_NOT_DECISIVE_FOR_MODEL = DIRECT_AUTHOR_ABSTRACT
STUCKENBRUCK_ANGEL_SEXUALITY_ASSUMPTIONS = DIRECT_AUTHOR_ABSTRACT
```

## 3.2 Important correction to simplistic attribution

Do not reduce Stuckenbruck to:

```text
STUCKENBRUCK = WATCHERS_ONLY
```

or:

```text
STUCKENBRUCK = HOLY_ANGELS_ONLY
```

His direct abstract explicitly makes the model more structural:

```text
COSMIC_ORDER / GENDERED-SEXUAL PROPRIETY
```

can explain the angel clause without making the precise moral classification of angels the only interpretive hinge.

This strongly supports the project’s existing separation:

```text

!=
EXACT_ANGELIC_FUNCTION
```

and helps explain why early reception can diverge on good/bad while retaining an angelic rationale.
