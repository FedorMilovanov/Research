## 10. Website/article sources to avoid as primary authority

These may illustrate modern reception or provide leads, but should not be treated as primary scholarly evidence:

- blog posts at BibleHub commentaries (useful as index, not source of verified quotes);
- CBE articles unless specifically cited as egalitarian argument;
- Head Covering Movement articles;
- sermons and parachurch websites;
- Reddit/forum threads;
- AI-generated or unattributed summaries.

They can be used in a reception-history file with explicit label `RECEPTION/EXAMPLE`, not as `B1` scholarship.
