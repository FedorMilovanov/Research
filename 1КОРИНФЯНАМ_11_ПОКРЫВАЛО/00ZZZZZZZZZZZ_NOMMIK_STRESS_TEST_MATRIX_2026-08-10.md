# 1. Where Nõmmik genuinely improves the field

The model should not be dismissed because it is reconstructive. It improves the question in at least three ways.

## 1.1. It takes the male half of the passage seriously

Many popular discussions begin with “why did Corinthian women remove veils?” and explain v4 secondarily.

Nõmmik begins with the historically odd male act:

```text
MAN + PRAYER/PROPHECY + MATERIAL_OVER_HEAD
```

Roman ritual culture gives that combination a concrete analogue.

## 1.2. It seeks one dispute rather than two unrelated dress problems

The uniformity hypothesis potentially explains why male and female instructions are inverse forms within a single pericope.

That is explanatory economy **if** the hidden uniformity proposal can be independently constrained.

## 1.3. It treats Corinthians as reasoning interlocutors

This fits the broader letter, where Paul repeatedly responds to Corinthian slogans, questions, practices and conclusions rather than addressing passive recipients.

But the existence of active interlocutors does not identify their exact missing argument.

# 2. Where the calibrated conservative synthesis remains stronger

The current project synthesis does **not** claim to know one exact social trigger.

It is intentionally layered:

```text
A/B text-level:
  material covering
  shame/honor
  creation/order
  woman’s agency in v10
  mutuality vv11–12
  hair/nature analogy
  translocal church appeal

A/B historical:
  Roman male covered ritual background

B/C reconstruction:
  exact Corinthian trigger
```

This has lower explanatory ambition but also lower hidden-event burden.

Nõmmik can improve the **third layer** without needing to replace the first two.

# 4. Comparison to major alternatives

## Model A — calibrated conservative textile/order

Advantages:

- direct fit to v3 and vv7–12;
- few hidden historical events;
- accommodates Roman background without being dependent on one trigger;
- accommodates female prophecy and real agency.

Cost:

- leaves exact Corinthian trigger partly open;
- may feel less historically specific.

Current status: **leading text-level synthesis**.

## Model B — Nõmmik ritual uniformity

Advantages:

- strongest integrated explanation of male covered prayer among current whole-models;
- one conflict can explain inverse male/female instructions;
- historically Roman and ritual-specific.

Cost:

- requires hidden uniformity proposal;
- detailed v10/angels fit presently unread;
- divine-knowledge conflict may be theory-laden.

Current status: **C serious exact-trigger model**.

## Model C — Finney elite-status male attire

Advantages:

- honor/shame and status are explicit/rich in context;
- explains male behavior in Roman social hierarchy.

Cost:

- female side requires a related but distinct social-honor explanation;
- less unified than Nõmmik.

Current: **B/C historical reconstruction**.

## Model D — rebellious/unveiling women

Advantages:

- simple reading of female commands;
- large reception history.

Costs:

- underexplains male v4;
- often relies on overgeneralized prostitution/adultery folklore;
- does not automatically explain whole passage.

Current: not accepted as sole whole-model.

## Model E — hair-only

Advantages:

- vv14–15 hair is explicit;
- can reduce need for external garment reconstruction.

Costs:

- material-covering lexical evidence is substantial;
- Roman male ritual data becomes underused;
- v4–6 distinctions remain harder.

Current: **C serious alternative**, not leading.

# 5. Critical path to promote or reject Nõmmik

The full-text audit should not spend equal time everywhere.

Priority order:

1. **v10 authority + angels** — if model cannot integrate these, whole-model ambition drops sharply.
2. **evidence for Corinthian ritual-uniformity proposal** — distinguish textual inference from cognitive plausibility.
3. **v4 exact lexical link to capite velato** — material is not enough; exact rite matters.
4. **divine-knowledge efficacy evidence** — find ancient parallels genuinely analogous to Christian prophecy.
5. **vv7–9 creation** — check whether creation logic is generated by the dispute or merely compatible.
6. **v16** — determine whether church-wide custom is explanatory support or conclusion-import.
