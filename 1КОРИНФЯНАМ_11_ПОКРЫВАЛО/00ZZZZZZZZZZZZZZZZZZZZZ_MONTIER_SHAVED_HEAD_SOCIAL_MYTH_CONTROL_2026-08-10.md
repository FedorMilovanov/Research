# 2. Direct research question

Montier explicitly begins with the widespread modern assertion that a woman in the ancient world with a shaved head was a prostitute and perhaps specifically a temple prostitute.

That makes this source unusually well targeted to a recurring 1 Cor 11 folklore claim.

The source is not a major NT commentary and does not control Pauline syntax. Its value is narrower:

```text
SOURCE_ROLE = HISTORICAL_MYTH_CHECK
NOT = PRIMARY_EXEGETICAL_AUTHORITY
```

# 3. Negative finding: temple-prostitute marker is not supported

The UNT abstract states that the evidence does not support the claim that shaved heads were a marker of prostitutes/temple prostitutes.

Montier reports that surviving Greek erotic art depicts prostitutes with:

- long hair;
- short hair;
- head coverings;
- uncovered heads.

He does not find artistic evidence establishing shaved-headed prostitutes as the relevant norm.

Therefore:

```text
SHAVED_HEAD_AS_TEMPLE_PROSTITUTE_MARKER = REJECTED_UNIVERSAL
SHAVED_HEAD_AS_PROSTITUTE_MARKER = REJECTED_UNIVERSAL
```

This is stronger than merely saying “we should be cautious”; it is a direct historical challenge to the popular explanatory trope.

---

# 4. Positive evidence: female hair cutting/shaving had multiple contexts

Montier surveys literary examples from several cultural settings, including material associated with:

- Lucian / *The Syrian Goddess*;
- Tacitus / *Germania*;
- Plutarch;
- Talmudic traditions;
- *On Fortune II* traditionally associated with Dio Chrysostom.

The existence of multiple contexts itself blocks a one-symbol equation.

```text
FEMALE_SHAVING_SEMANTICS = CONTEXT_DEPENDENT
ONE_UNIVERSAL_SOCIAL_MEANING = UNSUPPORTED
```

# 5. Montier’s adultery hypothesis

Montier concludes that punishment for adultery is, in his judgment, the best explanation for the shame attached to a shaved female head in relation to Paul’s comparison.

This must be separated from his much stronger negative finding against the prostitute myth.

## Evidence-status distinction

```text
NEGATIVE_CLAIM:
  shaved = prostitute/temple prostitute as universal marker
  => strongly rejected by Montier's survey

POSITIVE_RECONSTRUCTION:

```
