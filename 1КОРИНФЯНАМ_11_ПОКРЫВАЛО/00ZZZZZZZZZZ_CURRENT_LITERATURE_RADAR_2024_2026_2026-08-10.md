# 2. Aldar Nõmmik — major current whole-model

> Aldar Nõmmik, *Robes, Romans, and Rituals in First Corinthians: Paul and the Conflict over Head-Coverings*. Dissertations Theologicae Holmienses 9; current Wipf & Stock edition 2025.

Routes:
- https://wipfandstock.com/9798385259823/robes-romans-and-rituals-in-first-corinthians/
- https://ehs.se/forskning/dth/

Institutional listing advertises a DiVA full-text route; do not ask the user for the book before exhausting it.

Publisher-level model:

```text
ROMAN_CAPITE_VELATO
+ RITUAL_COGNITION_DIVINE_KNOWLEDGE
+ CORINTHIAN_PRESSURE_FOR_UNIFORMITY
-> MALE_UNCOVERING_GENERALIZED_BY_SOME_CORINTHIANS
-> PAUL_REJECTS_SEX_NEUTRAL_RITUAL_UNIFORMITY
```

Strengths:

- takes real Roman ritual evidence seriously;
- explains both male and female sides;
- links prayer/prophecy with ritual head-state;
- is dissertation-length rather than paragraph speculation.

Costs:

- exact uniformity demand is reconstructed;
- divine-knowledge efficacy as conflict-frame requires proof;
- cognitive-science plausibility is not historical proof;
- must still explain `κεφαλή`, creation, v10, angels, `φύσις`, v16.
