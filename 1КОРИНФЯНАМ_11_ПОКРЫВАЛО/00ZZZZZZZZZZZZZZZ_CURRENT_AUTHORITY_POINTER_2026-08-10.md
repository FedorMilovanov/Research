## Translation correction

Greek has:

```text
τοιαύτην συνήθειαν
```

= “such a custom/practice.”

It does **not** overtly contain `ἄλλην` = “other.”

Therefore:

```text
“we have no other practice” = CONTEXTUAL_PARAPHRASE
“we have no such practice” = CLOSER_FORMAL_RENDERING
```

The former can still be the better interpretive translation if the referent is the contrary practice, but Research must expose the supplied word.
