# 1. Irenaeus, *Adversus haereses* I.8.2 — Valentinian reading, not Irenaeus’ own exegesis

Primary/early-text routes:

- New Advent, *Against Heresies* I.8: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103108.htm
- CCEL/ANF Book I: https://ccel.org/ccel/irenaeus/against_heresies_i/anf01.ix.ii.ix.html

Irenaeus introduces the section as examples of what **the Valentinians accommodate from Scripture to their opinions**. They use 1 Cor 11:10 to symbolize the Saviour’s coming with angelic attendants to Achamoth/Sophia.

Thus:

```text
VALENTINIAN_1COR11_10_ACHAMOTH_READING = DIRECTLY_ATTESTED_BY_IRENAEUS
IRENAEUS_PERSONALLY_ENDORSES_ACHAMOTH_READING = FALSE
IRENAEUS_ROLE = HERESIOLOGICAL_REPORTER/CRITIC
```

Reported model:

```text
WOMAN = ACHAMOTH/SOPHIA
ANGELS = SAVIOUR'S ATTENDANTS
VEILING = ACHAMOTH'S MODESTY/REVERENCE
```

It is neither Tertullian’s Watchers model nor Clement’s righteous-human observer reading.

# 2. Clement of Alexandria, lost *Hypotyposeis* — righteous/virtuous human observers

## 2.1 Fragment content

The fragment is transmitted in the Oecumenius/catena tradition and attributed to Clement.

A traditional fragment route preserves the substance:

```text
ἀγγέλους φησὶ τοὺς δικαίους καὶ ἐναρέτους
```

and explains that the woman should be covered so as not to scandalize them toward sexual sin, while real heavenly angels can see her even if she is covered.

Safe model:

```text
ANGELS = RIGHTEOUS_AND_VIRTUOUS_HUMANS
VEILING_FUNCTION = AVOID_SEXUAL_STUMBLING_OF_THOSE_HUMANS
HEAVENLY_ANGELS = CAN_SEE_HER_EVEN_VEILED
```

This is a very early **human-observer** interpretation.

# 3. PG118 also preserves the Photius-parallel transmission
