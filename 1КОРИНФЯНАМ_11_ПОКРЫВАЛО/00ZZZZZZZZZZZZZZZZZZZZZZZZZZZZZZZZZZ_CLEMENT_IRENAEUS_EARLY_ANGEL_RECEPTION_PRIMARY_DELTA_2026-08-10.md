# 1 Коринфянам 11:10 — Clement / Irenaeus early angel-reception primary delta

**Дата:** 2026-08-10  
**Статус:** `EARLIEST-RECEPTION / PRIMARY-FRAGMENT / SOURCE-OWNERSHIP / RESEARCH-ONLY / PUBLICATION-HOLD`

## 0. Purpose

Этот слой проверяет две очень ранние интерпретации 1 Cor 11:10, которые легко смешать:

1. валентинианскую аллегорию, сохранённую Иринеем;
2. отдельный фрагмент Климента Александрийского из *Hypotyposeis*.

Главный source-hygiene вопрос:

```text
WHO_HOLDS_THE_VIEW?
```

Ириней может **сообщать** чужую интерпретацию, а поздний катенический источник может **сохранять** утраченную интерпретацию Климента. Ни один случай нельзя описывать как простой современный авторский комментарий.

# 1. Irenaeus, *Adversus haereses* I.8.2 — Valentinian reading, not Irenaeus’ own exegesis

## 1.1 Direct context

Primary/early-text routes:

- New Advent, *Against Heresies* I.8: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103108.htm
- CCEL/ANF, Book I: https://ccel.org/ccel/irenaeus/against_heresies_i/anf01.ix.ii.ix.html
- Wikisource/ANF Book I: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Ante-Nicene_Fathers/Volume_I/IRENAEUS/Against_Heresies:_Book_I

Irenaeus explicitly introduces the section as examples of what **the Valentinians attempt to accommodate from Scripture to their opinions**.

Within that report, he says that they use 1 Cor 11:10 to symbolize the coming of the Saviour with his angelic attendants to Achamoth/Sophia. Achamoth’s veiling is linked to modesty before the Saviour and his attendants.

The surrounding Valentinian myth is independently described earlier in Irenaeus I.4: the Saviour is sent to Achamoth with angels; she initially veils herself out of reverence/modesty; later she gazes on the angels and produces spiritual offspring after their image.

Therefore:

```text
VALENTINIAN_1COR11_10_ACHAMOTH_READING = DIRECTLY_ATTESTED_BY_IRENAEUS
IRENAEUS_PERSONALLY_ENDORSES_ACHAMOTH_READING = FALSE
IRENAEUS_ROLE = HERESIOLOGICAL_REPORTER/CRITIC
```

This corrects the shorthand:

```text
“IRANAEUS' INTERPRETATION = ACHAMOTH”
```

which is source-ownership inaccurate.

## 1.2 What the Valentinian reading actually does

In this reported allegory:

```text
WOMAN = ACHAMOTH/SOPHIA
ANGELS = SAVIOUR'S ATTENDANTS
VEILING = ACHAMOTH'S MODESTY/REVERENCE AT HIS COMING
```

This is neither:

- Tertullian’s Watchers/Gen 6 danger;
- Clement’s righteous-men reading;
- Ambrosiaster’s bishops reading;
- the modern heavenly-worship-witness model.

It is a distinct **Valentinian mythological reception**.

Safe status:

```text
VALENTINIAN_ACHAMOTH_READING = A_RECEPTION_FACT
VALENTINIAN_READING_AS_PAULS_LIKELY_INTENT = D_RECEPTION_ONLY
```

## 1.3 Text-form caution: “veil” in the Irenaean transmission

The ANF/CCEL translation of Irenaeus’ quotation renders 1 Cor 11:10 as:

> a woman ought to have a veil upon her head because of the angels.

ANF’s editorial note comments that Irenaeus here “reads” `κάλυμμα` rather than `ἐξουσία`.

That is historically interesting, but the current project must not turn an old translation/editorial inference into an A-grade reconstruction of Irenaeus’ Greek exemplar without a dedicated textual-transmission audit.

Why:

- *Adversus haereses* has a complex transmission history;
- the immediate form available in common editions/translations does not by itself prove what stood in the Greek manuscript of 1 Corinthians available to Irenaeus;
- an early gloss/paraphrase is possible and is already recognized as secondary in the project’s textual controls.

Therefore:

```text
IRENAEUS_TRANSMITTED_QUOTATION_HAS_VEIL_FORM = HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED_TRANSLATION/TRADITION
IRENAEUS_GREEK_1COR11_10_EXEMPLAR_KALYMMA = TEXTUAL_LOCATOR_HOLD
KALYMMA_ORIGINAL_IN_1COR11_10 = REJECTED_BY_CURRENT_TEXTUAL_BASE
```

Do not use Irenaeus to overturn `ἐξουσία` as the current Greek text.

# 2. Clement of Alexandria, lost *Hypotyposeis* — righteous/virtuous men

## 2.1 Surviving fragment

The interpretation survives in a later catena/Oecumenius transmission attributed to Clement.

Direct Greek fragment route:

- Documenta Catholica Omnia, *Clemens Alexandrinus, Fragmenta*: https://www.documentacatholicaomnia.eu/1004/1002/0150-0215%2C_Clemens_Alexandrinus%2C_Fragmenta%2C_MGR.html

The preserved Greek begins:

```text
Διὰ τοὺς ἀγγέλους.
ἀγγέλους φησὶ τοὺς δικαίους καὶ ἐναρέτους.
κατακαλυπτέσθω οὖν, ἵνα μὴ εἰς πορνείαν αὐτοὺς σκανδαλίσῃ·
οἱ γὰρ ὄντως καὶ ἐν οὐρανοῖς ἄγγελοι καὶ κατακεκαλυμμένην αὐτὴν βλέπουσιν.
```

The ancient interpretation is therefore approximately:

```text
ANGELS = RIGHTEOUS_AND_VIRTUOUS_MEN
VEILING_FUNCTION = AVOID_SEXUAL_STUMBLING/TEMPTATION_OF_THOSE_MEN
REAL_HEAVENLY_ANGELS = CAN_SEE_HER_EVEN_VEILED
```

This is a very early **human-observer** interpretation.

## 2.2 Important distinction from Murphy-O’Connor

Clement’s human referents are not automatically the same as Murphy-O’Connor’s modern proposal of `angels` as human messengers from other churches.

```text
CLEMENT_HUMAN_OBSERVERS = RIGHTEOUS/VIRTUOUS_MEN_IN_SEXUAL-TEMPTATION_FRAME
MURPHY_OCONNOR_HUMAN_MESSENGERS = MESSENGERS_FROM_OTHER_CHURCHES_MODEL
CLEMENT != MURPHY_OCONNOR
```

Thus the modern project should split:

```text
HUMAN_ANGEL_RECEPTION_FAMILY
  -> RIGHTEOUS/VIRTUOUS_MEN (Clement fragment)
  -> CLERGY/BISHOPS (Ambrosiaster; priest-reading reported by Severian)
  -> HUMAN_MESSENGERS_FROM_OTHER_CHURCHES (Murphy-O’Connor modern proposal)
```

The lexical fact that `ἄγγελος` can mean messenger does not make these models historically or contextually identical.

## 2.3 Transmission/ownership caution

The original *Hypotyposeis* are lost. The fragment is transmitted through Oecumenius/catena tradition and explicitly attributed to Clement in later fragment collections.

Therefore:

```text
CLEMENT_FRAGMENT_TEXT = DIRECT_SURVIVING_CATENA_FRAGMENT
CLEMENT_ORIGINAL_AUTOGRAPH = LOST
CLEMENT_ATTRIBUTION = STRONG_TRADITIONAL_FRAGMENT_ATTRIBUTION
```

This is stronger than a modern secondary paraphrase, but still not identical to possessing Clement’s original book.

## 2.4 Book-number discrepancy

Modern/older fragment presentations are inconsistent about the internal book number:

- ANF-style headings print `Oecumenius from Book III. On 1 Corinthians 11:10`;
- another synchronized fragment edition says `Thus Clement in the fourth book of the Hypotyposeis`.

The Greek fragment itself as exposed by Documenta Catholica Omnia gives the Oecumenius locator but does not resolve this discrepancy in the displayed lines.

Therefore:

```text
CLEMENT_HYPOTYPOSEIS_EXACT_BOOK_NUMBER = BIBLIOGRAPHIC_HOLD
DO_NOT_CITE_BOOK_III_OR_IV_AS_UNQUALIFIED_CERTAINTY
```

Safe citation wording:

> Clement of Alexandria, fragment of the *Hypotyposeis* preserved via Oecumenius on 1 Cor 11:10.

# 3. Chronological significance

The early interpretation map now becomes more diverse at a very early date:

```text
VALENTINIAN_READING (reported by Irenaeus) = ACHAMOTH + SAVIOUR'S ANGELIC ATTENDANTS
CLEMENT_FRAGMENT = RIGHTEOUS/VIRTUOUS HUMAN MEN
TERTULLIAN = FALLEN ANGELS / GENESIS 6
```

This is methodologically important:

> already in the second/early-third-century reception horizon, 1 Cor 11:10 did not have one universally remembered explanatory gloss for `because of the angels`.

But this does not mean all options are equally likely as Pauline intent.

```text
EARLY_DIVERSITY_OF_RECEPTION = A_RECEPTION_FACT
EARLY_DIVERSITY = NO_SINGLE_EARLY_EXPLANATION_MEMORY
EARLY_DIVERSITY != ALL_MODELS_EQUAL_EXEGETICAL_PROBABILITY
```

This supports the current project distinction:

```text
HEAVENLY_HOLY_ANGELS_REFERENT = B_HIGH_LEADING
EXACT_ANGELIC_FUNCTION = B_C
```

The identity may be more stable than the exact function; ancient reception especially demonstrates uncertainty about function/referent mapping.
