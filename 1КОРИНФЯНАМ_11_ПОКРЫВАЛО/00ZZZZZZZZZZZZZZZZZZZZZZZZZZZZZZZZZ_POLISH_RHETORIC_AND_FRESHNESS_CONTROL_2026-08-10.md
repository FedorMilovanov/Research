The journal page records publication online 2019-07-03 in volume 13.1 (2020). KUL repository exposes the named PDF object and identifies the publisher as UMK.

## 1.2 Direct abstract-level rhetorical structure

The official abstract gives an unusually explicit whole-pericope model:

```text
v2 = introduction
v3 = Pauline thesis
vv4–6 = cultural argument
vv7–12 = Christological/theological argument
vv13–15 = natural-law argument
intro + ending = ecclesial frame
```

Kowalski’s model therefore treats the passage as a **continuous Pauline argument**, not a large hidden quotation/refutation.

The abstract says the thesis of v3 concerns priority and respect for male/female differences, modeled on the God/Christ relation; the three argument blocks support maintaining sex difference for social roles, witness to divine revelation, full human existence and the order of nature; the ecclesial frame ties this to the good/unity of the church.
