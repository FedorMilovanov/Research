### Шкала

- `A` — direct/high: текст, синтаксис, прямой исторический факт.
- `B` — probable: лучшее объяснение, но есть серьёзная альтернатива или reconstruction step.
- `C` — possible: реальная конкурирующая модель.
- `D` — speculative/history-of-interpretation.
- `HOLD` — нужен прямой locator/object/full-section.

### Adversarial rule

Для каждого стиха задаются пять вопросов:

1. Что текст говорит независимо от нашей богословской позиции?
2. Какое консервативное чтение лучше всего объясняет данные?
3. Какая **сильнейшая**, а не карикатурная, альтернатива?
4. Какие редкие версии реально публиковались?
5. Что могло бы изменить текущий grade?

# 11:2 — παράδοσις и рамка похвалы

**Ключ:** `τὰς παραδόσεις κατέχετε` — коринфяне удерживают/сохраняют переданные Павлом традиции.

### A — direct

- Павел начинает этот блок похвалой за память о нём и удержание переданного.
- `παράδοσις` здесь — переданное apostolic teaching/practice, не автоматически «человеческое предание» в негативном смысле.
- v.2 создаёт рамку, которую v.16 возвращает к более широкой церковной практике.

### B — leading conservative reading

Следующие инструкции являются корректировкой внутри уже существующей apostolic/church order, а не случайным личным советом Павла без ecclesial weight.

### Strongest alternative

Похвала может быть риторически общей: они сохраняют traditions **в целом**, но конкретная практика головы является exception/problem. Это вполне совместимо с текстом и не ослабляет v.2.

### Edge reading

Иногда v.2 используют как доказательство, что head-covering не может быть apostolic tradition, иначе похвала была бы невозможна. Это слишком сильный inference: похвала «за traditions» не утверждает безошибочного соблюдения каждой детали.

### Verdict

`A`: apostolic-tradition frame.  
`B`: head-practice рассматривается как вопрос ecclesial order.  
Не использовать v.2 отдельно как доказательство eternal identical garment form.

# 11:3 — три отношения и `κεφαλή`

**Ключ:** `παντὸς ἀνδρὸς ἡ κεφαλὴ ὁ Χριστός ... κεφαλὴ γυναικὸς ὁ ἀνήρ ... κεφαλὴ τοῦ Χριστοῦ ὁ θεός`.

### A — direct

- Три пары представлены одной и той же метафорой `κεφαλή`.
- Последовательность не является простой chronology-of-origin: Christ/man/woman/God расположены риторически.
- `ἀνήρ` / `γυνή` могут означать man/woman или husband/wife по контексту; полный scope нельзя решить одной словарной статьёй.

### B — leading conservative reading

`κεφαλή` здесь лучше всего читается как **headship / authority / relational precedence**, при том что source/origin dimension в последующем creation argument vv.8–12 реальна и должна учитываться.

Почему B, не A:

- Fitzmyer защищает authority/headship reading;
- Grudem corpus поддерживает значение authority/prominence;
- но Cervin и другие показали, что debate о source/origin филологически не исчезает.

### C — strongest alternative

`κεφαλή = source/origin`, особенно с учётом vv.8,12 (`woman from man`, затем `man through woman`, all from God). Сильная форма альтернативы должна объяснить, почему одна и та же metaphoric chain включает God as head of Christ и Christ as head of man.

### Lower alternatives

- `prominence/preeminence` без прямого authority sense;
- `source/person/head` mixed senses по разным членам chain;
- marital-only chain (Christ-husband-wife) — возможна, но `παντὸς ἀνδρός` и дальнейший v12 требуют осторожности.

### Failure modes

Не писать:

- `κεφαλή lexically = ruler and nothing else`;
- `κεφαλή lexically = source and never authority`;
- v.3 сам по себе доказывает весь modern office structure;
- God→Christ relation автоматически идентичен husband→wife relation во всех аспектах.

### Verdict

`KEPHALE_HEADSHIP_AUTHORITY = B_LEADING`  
`KEPHALE_SOURCE_ONLY = C_VIABLE`

**Что могло бы изменить grade:** новый decisive corpus-level lexical evidence или closed technical commentary argument, который решает именно contextual mapping всех трёх пар, а не только dictionary possibility.

Key debate source: Joseph Fitzmyer, “Another Look at Kephale in 1 Corinthians 11.3”, NTS 35 (1989).

# 11:4 — мужчина, молитва/пророчество и `κατὰ κεφαλῆς ἔχων`

### A — direct

- Мужчина `praying or prophesying` с `κατὰ κεφαλῆς ἔχων` бесчестит свою `κεφαλή`.
- Здесь намеренно работает wordplay между anatomical head и relational `head` из v.3.

### B-high — leading material-covering reading

Фраза лучше всего относится к **материальному покрытию/одежде, ниспадающей на голову**, а не просто к длине волос.

Preston Massey специально исследует `κατακαλύπτω` и `κατὰ κεφαλῆς ἔχων` и заключает в пользу textile covering: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/abs/meaning-of-and-in-1-corinthians-11216/3B94F4462B2AC39852FE16FBECD46E38

### B/C — Roman ritual reconstruction

Oster/Gill делают сильной гипотезу, что мужская проблема может быть связана с Roman `capite velato` worship/status practice. Сам такой Roman ritual background — historical **A**; то, что **именно эту практику отражает v.4**, остаётся `B_C`, а конкретный Corinthian trigger — reconstruction-layer.

Gill open full-text route: https://www.tyndalebulletin.org/article/30525-the-importance-of-roman-portraiture-for-head-coverings-in-1-corinthians-11-2-16

### C — strongest alternative

Hair/hairstyle reading: `having something down from the head` как длинные/распущенные волосы. Эта модель существует серьёзно, особенно в Isaksson/Payne/Murphy-O'Connor line, но должна преодолеть lexical case Massey и contrast с explicit hair vocabulary vv.14–15.

### D/C-low edge

- male long hair = specific homosexual role (Murphy-O'Connor/MacGregor);
- Judaizing male prayer covering;
- ecstatic/cultic hairstyle.

Исторически знать; не превращать в lexical meaning.

### Verdict

`MATERIAL_COVERING_v4 = B_HIGH`  
`ROMAN_CAPITE_VELATO_BACKGROUND = A`  
`ROMAN_CAPITE_VELATO_AS_EXACT_v4 = B_C`  
`EXACT_CORINTH_TRIGGER = RECONSTRUCTION_LAYER`  
`HAIR_ONLY_v4 = C`

# 11:5 — женщина молится и пророчествует

### A — direct

`πᾶσα ... γυνὴ προσευχομένη ἢ προφητεύουσα` — Павел формулирует норму для **женщины, которая молится или пророчествует**.

Это одно из самых твёрдых наблюдений всего отрывка.

Нельзя утверждать, что v.5 запрещает сам факт female prayer/prophecy: форма аргумента регулирует **как** она это делает.

### B — assembly setting

Leading contextual reading: речь о собранной общине/public worship. В пользу:

- переход 11–14 к worship life;
- prophecy по 1 Cor 14 имеет congregational edification function;
- Watson прямо связывает shared prayer/prophecy с dialogue God↔congregation.

Open case-study summary: https://scholars.wlu.ca/consensus/vol7/iss1/2/

### B — practical scope after cross-status stress

The current project scope is **all women / sex-class practical scope = B-leading contextual**, not because `γυνή` can never mean wife, but because the whole vv7–12 discourse traverses woman/man more naturally than a rigid wife/husband gloss, while direct current controls include unmarried/celibate women and cross-status participation.

Peters 2021 directly includes ascetic, unmarried and married persons within her reading of the 1 Cor 11 creation/body-of-Christ vision; Wang 2022 directly reads women rather than only wives. Massey/Callon preserve a serious wife/free(d)-wife reconstruction but require additional marital/status bridges.

```text
ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL
FREE_D_MARRIED_WIVES_PRIMARY_SCOPE = C_SERIOUS_CURRENT_ALTERNATIVE
```

### C — strongest conservative alternative on setting

Некоторые консервативные авторы предлагают private/non-assembly setting, чтобы гармонизировать v.5 с 14:34–35 / 1 Tim 2. Holmyard специально защищал эту возможность. Она не невозможна, но требует объяснить literary movement 11–14 и normal communal role of prophecy.

### A/B boundary with office

`female prayer/prophecy = A textual fact`  
`female pastor/elder/ordination settled by v5 = NO`.

Prophecy ≠ автоматически elder/pastor office. Отдельный canonical/ecclesiological dossier обязателен.

### Covering clause

`ἀκατακαλύπτῳ τῇ κεφαλῇ` — uncovered head; material covering остаётся B-high leading, hair-only C.

### Verdict

`WOMEN_PRAY_PROPHESY = A`  
`ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL`  
`PUBLIC_ASSEMBLY = B_LEADING`  
`OFFICE_CONCLUSION = OUT_OF_SCOPE_FROM_v5_ALONE`

# 11:5b–6 — shaved/shorn comparison

### A — direct

Павел сравнивает uncovered woman с `ἐξυρημένῃ` / shorn-shaved condition и использует shame logic:

- если не покрывается → пусть и стрижётся;
- если стрижка/бритьё shameful → пусть покрывается.

### B-high — leading inference

Hair/shaving serves as **analogy/reductio supporting a distinct covering practice**, а не как простой синоним самого покрытия. Это усиливает two-things reading: covering ≠ hair.

### C — strongest alternative

Hair-only advocates читают vv.5–6 как разные hairstyle states: loose/unbound vs cut/shaved. Это real alternative, но linguistic burden выше из-за `κατακαλύπτεται` и subsequent explicit `κόμη` vocabulary.

### Historical caution

Нельзя превращать shame comparison в универсальную формулу:

- shaved woman = prostitute;
- shorn woman = convicted adulteress;
- every respectable Corinthian woman always veiled.

Callon 2024 показывает более сложный slave/free-body-control background; Gill показывает сложность visual evidence.

Callon: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/harvard-theological-review/article/authority-over-whose-head-did-paul-instruct-wives-or-all-women-to-cover-their-heads-1-corinthians-11216/5D602D820F9CA0E6C55906BDF68466ED

### Verdict

`COVERING_DISTINCT_FROM_HAIR = B_HIGH`  
`PROSTITUTION_SHAVING_UNIVERSAL = REJECT_AS_OVERCLAIM`

# 11:7 — image and glory

### A — direct

- man is called `εἰκὼν καὶ δόξα θεοῦ`;
- woman is called `δόξα ἀνδρός`;
- текст **не говорит**, что woman is not image of God.

Genesis 1:27 and broader Pauline theology prevent turning omission into denial of female imago Dei.

### B — leading conservative reading

Paul focuses not on unequal human worth but on **ordered relational symbolism in worship**, then grounds `woman = glory of man` via Genesis 2 in vv.8–9.

### Strongest alternative

Watson/Newberry note semantic slippage possibility: `glory of God` and `glory of man` may not function identically; woman's glory relation may include attraction/relational honor rather than simple authority hierarchy.

Newberry: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/abs/pauls-allusive-reasoning-in-1-corinthians-11712/EDE6D54A62D2265EA2C22291B6F2BA39

### Failure mode

`woman = glory of man` ≠ ontological inferiority, lesser humanity or absence of God's image.

### Verdict

`RELATIONAL_ORDER_SYMBOLISM = B`  
`ONTOLOGICAL_INFERIORITY = X / NOT_SUPPORTED`

# 11:8 — woman from man

### A — direct

`οὐ ... ἀνὴρ ἐκ γυναικὸς ἀλλὰ γυνὴ ἐξ ἀνδρός` alludes to Genesis 2 formation of woman from man.

### B — leading conservative reading

The verse contributes a **creation-order/origin rationale** to the worship distinction. Это сильнее purely local fashion argument: Paul does not reason only from Corinthian etiquette.

### C — strongest scope alternative

Creation argument can be weighted toward the **marital dyad** (husband/wife), especially if `γυνή/ἀνήρ` are read matrimonially. Massey 2013 and Callon 2024 make this a serious model.

However, this is no longer co-leading for practical scope. The same lexical pair reaches v12, where a rigid “wife from husband / husband through wife” reading becomes contextually awkward and even wife-focused models must broaden or switch the referent. Peters' cross-status/unmarried controls independently pressure exclusion of non-wives.

```text
ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL
MARITAL_DYAD_PRIMARY_SCOPE = C_SERIOUS_CURRENT_ALTERNATIVE
```

### Counterweight already anticipated

v.12 will reverse the origin direction in lived generation: man is now `through woman`; therefore v.8 cannot be isolated into a one-direction ontology or used by itself to force every `γυνή/ἀνήρ` token into wife/husband.

### Verdict

`GENESIS2_ORIGIN_ARGUMENT = A`  
`ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL`  
`MARITAL_PRIMARY_SCOPE = C_SERIOUS`  
`EVERY_WOMAN_FOR_EVERY_MAN = NOT_SUPPORTED`

# 11:9 — woman created `διὰ` man

### A — direct

Paul says man was not created `because of/for` woman, but woman `διὰ τὸν ἄνδρα`.

### B — leading conservative reading

Genesis 2's helper/relational purpose contributes to ordered male-female relation and is one reason the passage cannot be reduced to arbitrary fashion.

### Strongest alternative

The verse may be narrowly **marital** and functional, not a universal statement that every woman exists for every man. Genesis 2 itself concerns the first pair and marriage formation. This remains a serious local observation even though the passage-wide practical scope is now B-leading all-women.

### Quotation/refutation edge

Recent rhetorical proposals make vv.4–9 a Corinthian quotation which Paul later corrects. This could radically reduce direct Pauline ownership of vv.7–9, but evidence is presently D/C-low: no manuscript marker and discourse case remains reconstructive.

### Verdict

`CREATION_PURPOSE_ARGUMENT = A textual`  
`ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL`  
`MARITAL_LOCAL_FORCE = C_SERIOUS`  
`EVERY_WOMAN_FOR_EVERY_MAN = NOT_SUPPORTED`  
`LARGE_QUOTATION_v4_9 = D/C_LOW`

# 11:10 — `διὰ τοῦτο`, `ἐξουσία`, `angels`

Это центр самого большого adversarial pressure в перикопе.

## 10a. `διὰ τοῦτο`

### A

`διὰ τοῦτο` demands logical connection with preceding vv.7–9; any interpretation of v.10 must explain why it follows creation/glory reasoning.

## 10b. `ἐξουσίαν ἔχειν ἐπὶ τῆς κεφαλῆς`

### A — syntax/lexical core

- woman is grammatical subject of `ὀφείλει ... ἔχειν`;
- `ἐξουσία` normally denotes authority/right/power, active capacity;
- Greek text does **not** contain a noun meaning `symbol/sign` before authority.

Hooker's classic discussion records the difficulty of reading `ἐξουσία` as authority merely suffered from another: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/abs/authority-on-her-head-an-examination-of-i-cor-xi-10/947E8A98C64ACEA00D2BD815F0F8BDE5

### B-high — semantic direction

The active construction gives a strong semantic pull toward the woman as **bearer/possessor of authority/right/control**. That direction is not merely an egalitarian alternative; it is part of the current lexical baseline.

```text
EXOUSIA_ACTIVE_WOMAN_BEARER_PULL = B_HIGH
```

### B/C — exact referent / contextual synthesis

A material covering can still function as an **authority-related sign**, but that is contextual interpretation, not lexical substitution `ἐξουσία = veil/sign of subjection`.

Likewise, the active direction does not itself decide whether the exact referent is:

- control/right regarding her head/head presentation;
- authority/right to pray or prophesy;
- authorized ministry;
- a status/authority sign embodied in covering;
- another discourse-specific relation.

Conservative formulations must preserve woman's grammatical agency.

Newberry interprets v.10 as affirming woman's authority while holding together patriarchal vv.7–9 and mutuality vv.11–12.

### Lower alternatives

- authority = control of hairstyle as the exact referent;
- veil as purely passive metonym of another person's authority;
- textual gloss `κάλυμμα` reflected in weak later evidence, not original control.

## 10c. `διὰ τοὺς ἀγγέλους`

### A minimum

Paul invokes `the angels`; exact identity/function is unstated.

### B-high leading referent

The best current referent family is **heavenly/holy angels**. Same-letter angel language, especially 1 Cor 4:9, plus Qumran/heavenly-assembly controls make a celestial referent strongly preferable to human messengers or clergy.

Fitzmyer's Qumran evidence strengthens this existing interpretation while explicitly recognizing historical alternatives: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/feature-of-qumran-angelology-and-the-angels-of-i-cor-xi-10/59CE5686A3600CB7F51184CD960286F1

A second independent direct control now comes from D. François Tolmie, “Angels as arguments? The rhetorical function of references to angels in the Main Letters of Paul,” *HTS Teologiese Studies / Theological Studies* 67.1 (2011), Art. #825, DOI `10.4102/hts.v67i1.825`. The full OA body is available from HTS and SciELO:

- https://hts.org.za/index.php/hts/article/view/825/1642
- https://www.scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S0259-94222011000100042&script=sci_arttext

Tolmie treats v.10 as an angel reference used rhetorically to motivate women's head-related conduct and places it in a good/heavenly-angel, worship-presence field, but explicitly warns that the exact historical problem and precise role of the angels remain obscure. This is important because it independently supports both halves of the present calibration: **heavenly/worship presence is the best family, while exact function remains open**.

```text
TOLMIE_2011_DIRECT_OA_FULL_BODY = CLOSED_DIRECT
TOLMIE_V10_ANGELS = RHETORICAL_MOTIVATION_FOR_HEAD_CONDUCT
TOLMIE_EXACT_ROLE = EXPLICITLY_UNDETERMINED_FROM_V10
```

Within the heavenly family, cosmic witness/presence is leading, while the **exact** liturgical/purity/decorum function remains open.

```text
HEAVENLY_HOLY_ANGELS_REFERENT = B_HIGH_LEADING
ANGELS_AS_COSMIC_WITNESSES_PRESENT_ASSEMBLY = B_LEADING
EXACT_ANGELIC_FUNCTION = B_C
```

### C serious

Watchers/fallen angels (Tertullian/Gen 6 reception). Ancient background and reception are real, but Paul never identifies v10's angels as fallen.

### Lower alternatives

```text
GUARDIAN_ANGELS = C_LOW
HUMAN_MESSENGERS = D_C_LOW
BISHOPS_CLERGY = D_C_LOW_RECEPTION
HOOKER_MISDIRECTED_WORSHIP_FUNCTION = D_C_LOW_TO_C_PUBLISHED_ALTERNATIVE
```

Other low-evidence proposals include prophets, betrothal messengers and a modern intertextual proposal that women exercise authority because believers will judge angels.

### Verdict

`EXOUSIA_WOMAN_SUBJECT = A_SYNTAX`  
`EXOUSIA_ACTIVE_WOMAN_BEARER_PULL = B_HIGH`  
`EXOUSIA_EXACT_REFERENT = B_C`  
`HEAVENLY_HOLY_ANGELS_REFERENT = B_HIGH_LEADING`  
`ANGELS_AS_COSMIC_WITNESSES_PRESENT_ASSEMBLY = B_LEADING`  
`EXACT_ANGELIC_FUNCTION = B_C`  
`WATCHERS = C_SERIOUS_ALTERNATIVE`  
`GUARDIAN_ANGELS = C_LOW`  
`HUMAN_MESSENGERS / BISHOPS_CLERGY = D_C_LOW`

# 11:11 — `πλὴν ... ἐν κυρίῳ`

### A — direct

`πλὴν` introduces a qualification/counterbalance: neither woman without man nor man without woman **in the Lord**.

### B — leading conservative reading

Paul does not erase vv.7–9; he **prevents creation order from becoming unilateral self-sufficiency or superiority**. Ordered distinction and mutual dependence coexist.

### Strongest egalitarian reading

vv.11–12 may substantially relativize/hollow out hierarchy, redirecting the whole passage toward equality/interdependence. Watson argues hierarchy-vs-equality is itself a poor binary; Newberry says Paul redirects attention from relative status to interdependence.

### Conservative adversarial answer

The text says interdependence, not that all role/order distinctions in vv.3,7–9 vanish. Conversely, conservative exegesis fails if it treats vv.11–12 as decorative afterthought.

### Verdict

`MUTUAL_INTERDEPENDENCE_IN_LORD = A`  
`ERASES_ALL_ORDER = C`  
`ORDER_WITHOUT_MUTUALITY = X / TEXTUALLY_UNBALANCED`

# 11:12 — woman from man; man through woman; all from God

### A — direct

Three-direction structure:

1. woman `ἐκ τοῦ ἀνδρός`;
2. man `διὰ τῆς γυναικός`;
3. `τὰ δὲ πάντα ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ`.

Paul explicitly counterbalances first-creation origin with ordinary human generation and finally divine source.

### B — leading conservative reading

Creation order remains real, but no male may turn it into autonomous superiority: subsequent men come through women, and **everything is from God**.

### Scope control

v12 is the strongest local control against forcing one rigid husband/wife gloss through the entire passage. A wife-focused practical model can survive by broadening this token to representative woman/man, but that additional move is exactly why **all-women / sex-class practical scope now carries fewer forced exceptions**.

```text
ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL
RIGID_WIFE_HUSBAND_GLOSS_V12 = CONTEXTUALLY_AWKWARD
```

### Strongest alternative

Source/origin advocates use this verse as major support that `κεφαλή` v.3 may be source. This is contextually meaningful but does not prove source-only sense in v.3.

### Intertextual alternative

Newberry proposes echoes of 1 Esdras 4 alongside Genesis, strengthening a reading in which Paul deliberately balances patriarchal creation hierarchy with female power/interdependence and divine source.

### Verdict

`INTERDEPENDENCE_AND_GOD_SOURCE = A`  
`ALL_WOMEN_PRACTICAL_SCOPE = B_LEADING_CONTEXTUAL`  
`KEPHALE_SOURCE_ONLY_PROVEN_BY_v12 = NO`

# 11:13 — appeal to Corinthians' judgment

### A — direct

Paul asks them to judge among themselves whether it is `πρέπον` for a woman to pray to God uncovered.

### B — leading reading

This is an appeal to perceived propriety that **supports**, rather than replaces, the earlier theological arguments. It shows the practice was socially intelligible to the audience.

### Strongest alternative

If local propriety is the operative premise, modern application may translate the underlying sex-distinction/order principle into a different cultural sign rather than reproduce the same garment.

### Conservative counterpoint

The argument is not **only** culture: vv.3,7–12 invoke Christology/creation/angels. Therefore v.13 cannot collapse the whole pericope into mere fashion.

### Verdict

`LOCAL_PROPRIETY_APPEAL = A`  
`PURELY_CULTURAL_WITH_NO_THEOLOGICAL_ANCHOR = C/WEAK`

# 11:14 — `ἡ φύσις αὐτὴ` and male long hair

### A — direct

Paul appeals to `nature itself` and states that if a man `κομᾷ` (wears long hair / has long tresses), it is `ἀτιμία` for him.

### B-high leading model

Current primary-social corpus best explains `φύσις` here as **sexed naturalized propriety**: perceived embodied/created sexual difference is expressed through socially legible grooming and evaluated through honor/shame. This is neither raw follicular biology nor arbitrary local custom.

```text
PHYSIS_SEXED_NATURALIZED_PROPRIETY = B_HIGH_LEADING
EXACT_BIOLOGY_CULTURE_MIX = B_C
```

### Strongest alternative

`φύσις` can be weighted more heavily toward socially habituated convention/internalized propriety. The lexical/corpus range keeps the exact biology/culture ratio open.

### Edge ancient-physiology reading

Troy Martin connects hair physiology to ancient reproductive theory; Goodacre directly challenged the central testicle/peribolaion linkage. Retain as D/C-low history of modern debate, not interpretive key.

### Sexual-role edge

Murphy-O'Connor/MacGregor connect male long hair to homosexual gender coding. Published, historically interesting, but far too specific to be treated as lexical entailment of `κομᾷ`.

### Verdict

`V14_15_EVALUATIVE_SEX_CODE = A`  
`PHYSIS_SEXED_NATURALIZED_PROPRIETY = B_HIGH_LEADING`  
`EXACT_BIOLOGY_CULTURE_MIX = B_C`  
`PHYSIS_ONLY_BIOLOGY = REJECTED`  
`PHYSIS_ONLY_ARBITRARY_CUSTOM = REJECTED`

# 11:15 — female long hair, glory, `ἀντὶ περιβολαίου`

### A — direct

- long hair (`κόμη`) is called woman's `δόξα`;
- hair is given `ἀντὶ περιβολαίου`.

### Central adversarial question

Does v.15 prove that hair itself is the only covering intended in vv.4–13?

### B-high — leading answer: no

The explicit switch to hair vocabulary at vv.14–15 is better read as **analogy from natural hair-covering to the disputed worship covering**, not redefinition of every earlier covering verb.

Massey's lexical case remains important here.

### C — strongest hair-only alternative

`περιβόλαιον` and `ἀντί` can be read so that hair **is** the covering Paul has meant all along. This gains rhetorical simplicity but must explain why vv.5–6 use covering/uncovering language alongside cutting/shaving as distinct operations.

### D/C-low physiology

`περιβόλαιον = testicle/reproductive organ` proposal is not controlling; Goodacre's critique blocks use as decisive interpretation. Martin 2013 remains a direct-body published defense of a specialized contextual/euphemistic reconstruction, not evidence that “testicle” is the normal lexical sense.

### Translation caution

`ἀντί` can express corresponding/in-place-of relation; do not load a full theology into English `instead of` without contextual argument.

### Verdict

`HAIR_AS_ANALOGICAL_NATURAL_COVERING = B_HIGH`  
`HAIR_ONLY_FOR_WHOLE_PERICOPE = C_SERIOUS_ALTERNATIVE`  
`PERIBOLAION_TESTICLE_THEORY = D_C_LOW`

# 11:16 — contentiousness, `τοιαύτην συνήθειαν`, churches of God

### A — direct

- Paul anticipates a `φιλόνεικος` / contentious person;
- appeals to `we` and `the churches of God`;
- invokes `τοιαύτην συνήθειαν` — “such a custom/practice”.

### Main ambiguity

What is the referent of `such custom`?

### B — leading conservative reading

The best contextual reading is that Paul rejects the **contrary/uncovered or contentious practice under dispute** and appeals to broader church conformity. Classic Greek commentaries commonly take the church practice as decisive against the Corinthian innovation.

### Serious alternatives

1. `no such custom` = no custom of being contentious;
2. `no other practice` = the practice Paul has just described is the common church norm;
3. `no such practice` = churches do not have the disputed uncovered/sex-confusing practice.

The difference matters for translation, but all three major continuity readings make v.16 a **closure against continued Corinthian resistance**, not a casual cancellation of vv.2–15. The exact antecedent remains a real discourse dispute.

Peer-reviewed convention study exists: E. A. Judge, “The Appeal to convention in Paul” (Macquarie metadata): https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/the-appeal-to-convention-in-paul/

A passage-specific peer-reviewed rhetorical control is Joop F. M. Smit, “‘We Have no Such Custom’. A Rhetorical Analysis of 1 Corinthians 11,2–16,” *Ephemerides Theologicae Lovanienses* 97.1 (2021): 135–148. Peeters and KU Leuven independently expose the exact article identity and abstract. Smit analyzes the passage through `genus`, `inventio`, `dispositio`, and `elocutio`; the abstract's explicit result is that Paul urges the Corinthian women to wear a veil during prayer. Thus Smit is a direct publisher-level control against the **cancellation reading** of v.16.

### Fee Revised 2014 — normative force is not identical to formal command

Fee’s revised TOC directly places vv13–16 at pp.580–586. The exact pages are still not directly acquired, but a page-specific secondary quotation chain pins his v16 discussion to pp.585–586. It reports two points that must be held together:

- Fee treats the conclusion as church custom/practice and says the multi-part argument does not amount to a formal command **as such**;
- he nevertheless takes “such practice” to refer to what the contentious opponents are advocating, not to a cancellation of everything just argued.

### Costa counter-control

Costa's direct full body remains an important adversarial control: inside her broader quotation/refutation model, `τοιαύτην συνήθειαν` functions as an encapsulating anaphora that denies the Corinthian obligation woman-cover/man-uncover. This is direct linguistic pressure on the leading reading, but it depends on her more expensive unmarked speaker-shift model, whose overall textual fit remains D/C-low.

### Weak reading to reject

But rejecting that cancellation reading does **not** license the opposite overclaim that `συνήθεια` itself lexically means a formal, timeless command about an identical material form.

### Application question

v.16 strengthens ecclesial/common-practice weight but still does not by itself determine whether modern obedience requires identical ancient material form or faithful translation of the underlying symbol/order.

# Cross-verse adversarial synthesis

## 1. Что наиболее твёрдо (`A`)

1. Перикопа регулирует male/female conduct in prayer/prophecy.
2. v.5 прямо представляет женщину praying/prophesying.
3. vv.8–9 используют Genesis creation/origin/purpose reasoning.
4. woman is grammatical subject of `ἐξουσίαν ἔχειν` v.10.
5. `angels` invoked but not identified.
6. vv.11–12 прямо affirm male-female interdependence in Lord and all-from-God source.
7. vv.14–15 explicitly discuss hair; earlier verses use covering language.
8. v.16 appeals beyond Corinth to broader church practice.

## 2. Leading synthesis (`B/B-high`)

- material/textile covering is the best reading of vv.4–6,13;
- practical scope is **all women / sex-class B-leading**, while free(d)-married-wives remains a serious C reconstruction;
- `κεφαλή` carries headship/authority/predominance force in v.3, though origin dimension remains relevant;
- creation order is theological, therefore issue is not mere arbitrary fashion;
- v.10 preserves woman's active authority/right-bearing semantic direction while leaving the exact referent B_C;
- heavenly/holy angels are the leading referent family; cosmic witness/presence is leading while exact angelic function remains B_C;
- vv.11–12 prevent hierarchy from becoming unilateral superiority and serve as a scope control against rigid wife/husband glossing;
- `φύσις` is best modeled as sexed naturalized propriety, with exact biology/culture weighting open;
- hair functions as analogical natural covering while hair-only remains a serious alternative;
- v16 has B_HIGH **ecclesial/exhortative** normative force, while the exact `τοιαύτην συνήθειαν` referent remains B_C and an identical timeless garment form is unproved.

## 3. Strongest alternatives that must remain visible (`C`)

- source/origin-only `κεφαλή`;
- hair/hairstyle-only covering;
- free(d)-married-wives as primary practical scope;
- private/non-assembly prayer/prophecy;
- specific own-head/control/ministry construal of the exact v10 `ἐξουσία` referent;
- Watchers;
- culturally translated rather than identical-material modern application.

## 4. Edge map (`D/C-low`)

- homosexual/lesbian hairstyle decoding;
- specific Dionysiac/ecstatic loose hair trigger;
- `angels` = bishops, prophets, betrothal messengers, visiting church messengers;
- large quotation/refutation of vv.4–9;
- interpolation of major portion 11:3b–15;
- ancient physiology/testicle `περιβόλαιον` as master key.

These remain in Research because people actually proposed them, not because the project gives them equal weight.
