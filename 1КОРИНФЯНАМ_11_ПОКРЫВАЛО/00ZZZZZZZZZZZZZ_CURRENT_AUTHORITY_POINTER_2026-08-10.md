## Main lexical delta

### Covering vs hair

```text
KATAKALYPTO_COVER_SEMANTICS = A
AKATAKALYPTOS_UNCOVERED = A
KOMAO_LONG_HAIR = A
KOME_HAIR = A
TWO_DISTINCT_LEXICAL_FIELDS = A
V4_MATERIAL_COVERING = B_HIGH
HAIR_ONLY = C_SERIOUS_ALTERNATIVE_WITH_HIGHER_LEXICAL_BURDEN
```

`κατὰ κεφαλῆς` itself is spatial (“down/over/from the head”); the omitted object in v4 is contextually supplied. Do not claim an overt Greek noun “veil” occurs there.

### `κεφαλή`

```text

```

Judges 11:11 LXX and Isaiah 7:8–9 block the categorical claim that leadership/prominence is impossible in relevant Judeo-Greek.

### `ἐξουσία` v10

```text
EXOUSIA_WOMAN_SUBJECT = A_SYNTAX
EXOUSIA_RIGHT/AUTHORITY/CONTROL_CLASS = A_B_HIGH
```

Paul’s same-letter parallels (1 Cor 7:37; 9:4) make the subject the possessor of right/authority. “Sign of someone else’s authority over her” is an interpretive paraphrase because neither “sign” nor an external possessor is overt.
