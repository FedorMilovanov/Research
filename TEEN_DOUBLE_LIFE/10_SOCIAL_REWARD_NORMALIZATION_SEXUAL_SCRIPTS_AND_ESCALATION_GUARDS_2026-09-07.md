Это популярная explanatory story, но adolescent longitudinal evidence гораздо сложнее и heterogeneous.

Лучше строить статью на механизмах, которые действительно подтверждены.

## 2. Подростковый возраст — период повышенной чувствительности к социальному сигналу

2024 review по neural sensitivity to peers суммирует evidence, что adolescence — период heightened sensitivity to peer influence и social information.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38823235/

2026 review in Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews likewise emphasizes heightened attunement to social stress **and** support, with sensitivity potentially increasing susceptibility to both harmful and beneficial environments.

Source:

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763426000771

### Critical guard

Adolescent reward/social sensitivity is **not pathology by itself**.

It supports:

- friendship;
- learning;
- prosocial bonding;
- belonging;
- exploration;
- responsiveness to positive mentors.

The same plasticity can, however, make peer norms unusually consequential.

Thus future Christian framing should avoid:

> «подростковый мозг defective».

Better:

> **Подросток особенно настроен на вопрос “что думают обо мне мои люди?” — и поэтому чрезвычайно важно, кто именно становится “моими людьми”.**

## 3. “Лайк” — реальная форма social endorsement

Sherman et al. experimental fMRI study simulated Instagram.

Adolescents were more likely to like images when they appeared to have many peer likes, including images depicting risky behavior.

High-like images also elicited stronger activation in neural regions associated with reward processing, social cognition, imitation and attention.

Sources:

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27247125/
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5387999/

### Why it matters

Online culture externalizes peer approval into a visible metric.

Traditional peer approval:

- smile;
- laughter;
- inclusion;
- praise.

Digital peer approval:

- like;
- reaction;
- repost;
- follower count;
- comments;
- group-chat reinforcement.

This allows approval to become:

- countable;
- repeatable;
- visible after the moment;
- available at home and at night.

That is a genuine environmental change even though human desire for approval is ancient.

The future article should avoid technological determinism.

Biblical source of sin remains the heart/desire:

- Mark 7:20–23;

- Galatians 5:16–24.

But Scripture also recognizes social shaping:

- Proverbs 13:20;
- 1 Corinthians 15:33;

- Proverbs 1:10–19.

Therefore digital platforms can be described as **infrastructure of reinforcement**, not source of original sin.

Working formula:

> **The platform does not invent the desire to be approved; it can measure, multiply and continuously feed that desire.**

## 5. Sexual communication with friends can move perceived norms

Longitudinal Project STARS, N=1,116 adolescents 11.5–17.9 years:

- more frequent sexual communication with friends predicted larger increases in sexual behavior experience;
- it also predicted stronger perceptions that peers behave sexually, approve of sex and pressure toward sex;
- when all three norms were modeled together, **injunctive norms (perceived peer approval)** explained part of the association.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31751153/

### Key future article line

> **Подросток может сначала просто обсуждать то, чего сам ещё не делает. Но постоянный разговор меняет его представление о том, что “мы одобряем”, и это само становится частью поведения.**

Do not imply inevitability.

## 6. “Все так делают” can be more powerful than direct pressure

The broader peer-norm literature in existing module `05` shows descriptive/injunctive norms often have stronger associations with sexual behavior than explicit direct pressure.

This matters for hidden chat culture.

A teen may never hear:

> «ты обязан заняться сексом».

Instead:

- everyone jokes sexually;
- explicit stories are rewarded;
- virginity becomes comic/inferior status;
- sexual boldness becomes adulthood;
- modest speech becomes childish;
- pornography references become shared cultural knowledge.

Then conformity may occur without explicit coercion.

This is norm internalization/social learning, not necessarily direct pressure.

## 7. Music: not all sexual lyrics are empirically equivalent

National longitudinal survey of 1,461 adolescents found exposure to **degrading sexual lyrics** associated with later sexual initiation/progression after adjustment for multiple covariates, whereas nondegrading sexual content was not associated in the same way.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16882784/

Another longitudinal study (N=548) found sexual content in music associated with earlier initiation of intercourse, while aggressive content predicted increased aggression/decreased prosocial behavior over time.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25828552/

A 2022 longitudinal study found exposure to sexual music lyrics predicted later sexting among boys but not girls in that sample.

Source:

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S074756322200382X

### Evidence discipline

Never write:

> «матерная/сексуальная песня программирует ребёнка».

Better:

> **Repeated media content can contribute to scripts and perceived norms, with effects varying by content, child, context and outcome.**

## 8. Sexual scripts: why repeated language matters

Sexual-script theory is useful because it does not require a crude stimulus-response model.

Media/peer narratives can teach:

- what counts as desirable;
- what men/women supposedly want;
- how sexual status is gained;
- what is embarrassing;
- how quickly relationships “normally” become sexual;
- whether objectification is funny/normal;
- whether restraint signifies maturity or weakness.

The degrading-lyrics longitudinal literature is consistent with this mechanism.

For a Christian future article, sexual-script language can sit alongside the biblical concept of learning a “way” or pattern of life without equating the constructs.

## 9. Pornography and adolescent behavior: serious, but evidence must be stated honestly

2023 systematic review of adolescent pornography exposure and sexual behavior:

- 19 eligible studies;
- 5 of 8 studies examining early sexual initiation found significant associations, including one longitudinal study;
- evidence for several other outcomes was conflicting/insufficient;
- measurement heterogeneity and cross-sectional designs limited causal inference.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36853749/

2025 rapid systematic review focused **only on longitudinal studies** and included 44 studies.

Themes included:

- sexual behavior;
- risk behavior;
- permissiveness;
- aggression/victimization/rape myths;
- psychological wellbeing;
- religiosity;
- desensitization;
- age of exposure;
- pornography type;
- problematic pornography use.

Overall results were heterogeneous.

Source:

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12223668/

### Critical publication rule

We can say:

> adolescent pornography exposure is associated longitudinally with multiple attitudes/behaviors in parts of the literature, and problematic use exists for some adolescents.

We cannot say:

> pornography produces one inevitable escalation trajectory in every adolescent.

## 10. Desensitization is a research topic, not a license for overclaiming

The 2025 rapid longitudinal review explicitly includes desensitization among investigated topics, but describes the overall literature as heterogeneous.

Therefore future article may discuss a **possible pattern**:

`initial shock → repeated exposure → reduced shock / normalization → broader tolerance`

but must not claim this sequence is universally demonstrated for all adolescent pornography use.

It is better supported when discussing **social/moral normalization** than a precise neurochemical tolerance mechanism.

## 11. What “обычная жизнь становится пресной” can mean without dopamine mythology

This phrase may refer to several distinct processes:

### A. Attention displacement

High-engagement digital activity occupies time that otherwise could go to:

- family conversation;
- reading;
- outdoor activity;
- church/community;
- sleep;
- boredom-based creativity.

### B. Social contrast

Ordinary family life may provide:

- fewer rapid novelty events;
- no quantified approval;
- less sexual excitement;
- slower conversational rhythm.

The issue is not proven “dopamine depletion”; it is a changed comparison environment.

### C. FOMO / constant social field

Longitudinal PSMU review identifies FOMO and peer factors among recurring risks.

Source:

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40894-025-00264-4

### D. Moral reclassification

What once felt vulgar may, through repeated group use, become:

- ordinary;
- humorous;
- identity-marking;
- socially safe.

This is especially relevant to profanity/sexual joking.

### E. Preoccupation

Sexually explicit material literature includes sexual preoccupancy among studied longitudinal correlates.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28433892/

Thus future article can use «пресность» as a phenomenological description, but should explain the mechanisms rather than attach an invented biomarker.

## 12. “Нравится всё больше” may mean different things

Possible meanings:

1. more frequent consumption;
2. stronger social belonging around the behavior;
3. greater perceived normality;
4. less disgust/shock;
5. stronger preoccupation;
6. broader content range;
7. stronger sexual arousal;
8. more active participation instead of passive viewing;
9. more willingness to disclose sexual identity/content to peers;
10. greater cost of leaving the peer group.

These are not interchangeable.

Future article should not collapse them into one word “addiction”.

---

## 13. Problematic pornography use exists, but prevalence/diagnosis need caution

Problematic pornography use (PPU) usually refers to poorly controlled use that continues/escalates despite impairment/distress.

Adolescent literature recognizes PPU, but measurement and causal models are still developing.

Source:

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12223668/

Clinical treatment literature across ages is limited and often low-quality.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37880509/

Therefore:

- do not label every adolescent viewer “addicted”;
- do not deny compulsive/problematic patterns;
- assess frequency, control, impairment, escalation, distress and function separately.

## 14. Moral normalization can happen without clinical addiction

This is essential for the theological article.

A teenager does **not** need to meet any addiction/PPU threshold for repeated sexualized content to matter morally.

Possible change:

`I know this is filthy → everyone jokes about it → it stops feeling strange → I join the joke → I defend it → I resent anyone who calls it wrong`.

Psychological categories here may include:

- norm perception;
- social reinforcement;
- moral disengagement;
- habituation/desensitization;
- identity signaling.

Biblical categories may include:

- deceitfulness of sin (Hebrews 3:13);
- darkened understanding / hardness (Ephesians 4:17–19);
- calling evil good or suppressing truth (broader biblical moral categories).

Do not claim empirical studies prove the biblical category.

## 15. The adolescent can be simultaneously agent and target of influence

A false binary:

- «the environment made him do it»;
- «the environment means nothing because the heart is sinful».

Better model:

`heart/desires ↔ chosen peers ↔ peer reinforcement ↔ media exposure ↔ perceived norms ↔ actions ↔ secrecy ↔ further environment selection`.

The adolescent:

- chooses;
- is influenced;
- influences others;
- can select a more permissive group;
- can then be socialized by that group.

That is compatible with both human responsibility and serious attention to formation.

## 16. Why “крутость” deserves its own section in the future article

For some adolescents, sexualized/profane behavior is not primarily private pleasure.

It is **status performance**.

Examples:

- knowing explicit references;
- having sexual stories;
- mocking chastity;
- using profanity without embarrassment;
- sending provocative content;
- appearing fearless before taboos;
- demonstrating detachment/cynicism.

The social reward literature makes this plausible and measurable.

Future research gate:

- masculinity/status and sexual boasting;
- femininity/status and sexualized self-presentation;
- peer ridicule of restraint;
- reputational economy inside group chats.

## 17. Positive peer sensitivity is also a major opportunity

The same adolescent social sensitivity that makes corrupt peers powerful can make good peers powerful.

2026 neuroscience review stresses susceptibility to **supportive as well as adverse** social contexts.

Source:

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763426000771

This matters greatly for church strategy.

The answer is not only:

> remove bad peers.

It is also:

> build a social world where courage, chastity, confession, seriousness, kindness and loyalty are socially embodied by admired peers and adults.

This is a pastoral inference but fits differential-susceptibility and peer-socialization evidence.

## 21. Research conclusion

The strongest evidence-based version of the user's intuition is:

> **Digital culture can make sexualized and vulgar norms more visible, more repetitive, more socially rewarded and more continuously present. During a developmental period of high sensitivity to peer evaluation, that can change what feels normal, admirable or embarrassing.**

The weakest and least defensible version would be:

> «Internet chemically burns the adolescent brain until only extreme sin produces dopamine.»

Future article should choose the first.

It is scientifically stronger and theologically deeper, because Scripture never needed a simplistic neurochemical theory to explain the escalating deceitfulness of sin, the formative power of companions or the human desire for approval.
