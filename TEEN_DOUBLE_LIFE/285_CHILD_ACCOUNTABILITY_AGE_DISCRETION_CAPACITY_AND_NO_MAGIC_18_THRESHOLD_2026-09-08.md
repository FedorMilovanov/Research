# Child Accountability — Age, Discretion, Capacity, and No Magic “18” Threshold

**Status:** RESEARCH / PASTORAL-EXEGETICAL CONTROL / NOT PUBLICATION
**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Depends on:** `34`, `70`, `150`, `159–160`, `273–284`

## 1. Problem

Modern civil categories can silently become theology:

> child under 18 = not really responsible / adult at 18 = suddenly fully responsible.

Scripture does not establish such a switch.

## 2. Distinguish kinds of maturity

### Moral creaturehood

Exists before legal adulthood. Children can sin, obey, lie, honor, rebel, learn, repent and believe according to capacity.

### Cognitive/developmental capacity

Grows. It affects:

- complexity of instruction;
- foreseeability;
- degree of knowledge;
- proportional consequences;
- credibility/clarity of profession;
- prudential freedoms.

It does **not** mean God has no claim before an arbitrary civil birthday.

### Civil status

Law assigns rights/duties at jurisdiction-specific ages. This changes real parental/legal authority and must be respected.

It does not define when God first becomes Creator/Judge.

### Household dependency

A 19-year-old dependent at home and a 19-year-old fully independent are both adults civilly but may stand in different household arrangements.

### Ecclesial status

Separate again:

- Baptist nonmember child;
- Baptist baptized member minor;
- Presbyterian baptized noncommuning member;
- communicant/professing member;
- adult noncommuning baptized member in some paedobaptist polities.

## 3. Biblical anchors

### Ephesians 6 / Colossians 3

Children are directly commanded to obey/honor. No salvation-or-age prerequisite appears before the command.

### Proverbs

Parental address assumes growing capacity across youth; wisdom instruction seeks to form moral judgment rather than wait for adult independence.

### Luke 12:47–48 principle

Knowledge affects aggravated responsibility. This supports **graded culpability by received light/capacity** without creating a zero-responsibility childhood.

### 2 Timothy 3:14–15

Scripture known from childhood demonstrates meaningful religious formation before adult status.

## 4. Paedobaptist `age/years of discretion`

PCA language is useful even for Baptist comparison:

- it does not identify one universal age;
- it speaks of ability to understand the gospel;
- at that stage covenant obligations are pressed personally and profession is sought.

### Transferable insight

Responsibility/pastoral address should be **capacity-sensitive rather than age-magical**.

### Nontransferable premise

A Baptist need not accept pre-profession covenant membership to use a capacity-sensitive model.

## 5. Baptist profession/baptism and age

A Baptist church should avoid two opposite rules:

### Fixed low-age presumption

> “At X years old, a Christian child can now be baptized if he gives correct answers.”

### Fixed high-age moratorium

> “No one under 18 can be credibly converted/baptized.”

Neither follows from Scripture as a universal rule.

Use credible profession calibrated to child/youth capacity while taking time/fruit seriously enough to avoid manufactured certainty.

## 6. Responsibility grading

A useful model:

`OBJECTIVE WRONGNESS` can be stable while `SUBJECTIVE CULPABILITY / PASTORAL RESPONSE` is graded by:

- understanding;
- intention;
- repetition;
- concealment/planning;
- prior warning;
- coercion/victimization;
- developmental ability;
- mental capacity;
- degree of received gospel light.

This is superior to both:

- flat adult-level treatment of every child failure;
- moral-exemption treatment of all childhood conduct.

## 7. “Full responsibility” wording guard

The user’s intuition that paedobaptist children have “full responsibility” should be refined before publication.

Avoid:

> “A baptized three-year-old has exactly the same culpability/judicial responsibility as a thirty-year-old communicant member.”

Better:

> **The child already stands under real divine, parental and — in paedobaptist polity — ecclesial claims; the form and measure of accountable response develop with understanding and status.**

## 8. Application to serious adolescent sin

By adolescence, especially after years of explicit teaching, a youth may have:

- high factual understanding;
- clear awareness of wrong;
- long concealment/planning;
- rejected admonition;
- strong peer influence.

In such a case:

> “He is only a child / not converted”

can drastically understate genuine responsibility.

But still distinguish:

- moral culpability;
- parental consequences;
- criminal/civil categories;
- formal church censure;
- eternal-state judgment.

## 9. Final synthesis

> **Библия не ждёт восемнадцатилетия, чтобы обратиться к ребёнку как к нравственному существу. Но библейская ответственность также не означает, что трёхлетнего, пятнадцатилетнего и взрослого нужно судить одинаково по способности, знанию и юрисдикции. Правильная модель — реальная ответственность с детства, возрастающая ясность и вес полученного света, пропорциональное родительское управление и точное различение гражданского, семейного и церковного статуса.**
