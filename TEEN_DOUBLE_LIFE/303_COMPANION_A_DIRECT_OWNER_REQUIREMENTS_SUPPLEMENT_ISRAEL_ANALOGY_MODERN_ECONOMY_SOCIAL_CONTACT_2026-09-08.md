## 0. Why this supplement is necessary

`DEPARTURE → CONTACT → WAITING → REPENTANCE CREDIBILITY → RETURN → RELATIONAL RECEPTION / RE-ENTRUSTMENT`.

The following owner questions must remain visible **inside Companion A**, even when detailed mechanics are deferred elsewhere:

1. Does Scripture support a period of repeated warning/contact rather than immediate emotional abandonment after a secret departure?
2. Can parents later stop shielding a competent adult from consequences without trying to manufacture a `far country` or `rock bottom`?
3. What may and may not be learned analogically from God's dealings with rebellious Israel?
4. How does Luke 15 change when the modern adult child may already have wages, banking, rent, transport and digital social support rather than an inheritance-funded departure in the narrative world of the parable?
5. How do stories, public posts, messaging and blocking change contact without creating a right to surveillance, fake accounts or covert pursuit?
6. What if the adult child returns very quickly — before the parents think he has `suffered enough`?

These are direct owner-intent questions. They must not vanish merely because the final article is shorter than the Research corpus.

### Evidence class

## What survives the analogy

Across texts such as 2 Kings 17, 2 Chronicles 36, Hosea 2, Hosea 11 and the Judges cycle, Scripture can support this broad moral pattern:

`patience / kindness`
`→ repeated warning`
`→ stubborn refusal`
`→ real consequence / discipline / handing over to the chosen path`
`→ in some texts, humbling and restoration by sovereign grace`.

Safe Product synthesis:

> **Святая любовь не исчерпывается одним предупреждением — и святая любовь не обязана бесконечно отменять последствия.**

This directly answers the owner's concern that `real trust in God = say it once, immediately detach, never ask again` is too crude.

## Strongest anti-overreach guard

Hosea 11 itself gives the controlling warning:

> `I am God, and not man.`

Therefore:

`PARENT ≠ YAHWEH`.

`ADULT CHILD ≠ COVENANT ISRAEL`.

`PARENTAL HOME ≠ THE COVENANT LAND`.

`PARENTAL CONSEQUENCE ≠ DIVINE COVENANT CURSE`.

Parents do not possess:

- omniscience;
- final judgment;
- sovereign providence;
- covenant-national judicial authority;
- certainty about the child's final spiritual state;
- the power to determine which suffering would regenerate him.

## Mandatory negative guards

Do **not** write:

- `God exiled Israel, therefore expel the 18-year-old after N warnings`;
- `Romans 1 authorizes the father to give his son over in the same judicial sense`;
- `1 Cor. 5 / 1 Tim. 1:20 gives a parent private “hand him over to Satan” authority`;
- `2 Chron. 36 “no remedy” proves this living child is beyond repentance`;
- `let him sin until he becomes disgusted enough to convert`.

## Allowed pastoral inference

A sudden secret departure may legitimately be followed for a season by:

- safety clarification;
- direct conversation;
- attempts to meet;
- repeated but proportionate warning;
- prayer;
- an invitation to truth and return.

If repeated speech becomes only quarrel, the mode may change without changing the truth:

`MODE CHANGE, NOT TRUTH CHANGE`.

Later parental love may mean:

- fewer repetitive arguments;
- refusal to fund or cover the rebellion;
- ordinary non-enabling good;
- a clear standing moral testimony;
- an open relational door where contact remains safe;
- prayer and waiting under God's providence.

### Locked

`ALLOW CONSEQUENCES ≠ ENGINEER MISERY`.

The parent's task is not to construct Babylon, Assyria, famine or wilderness for the child.

# 2. Luke 15 — preserve historical/narrative distance from the modern economy

Primary controls:

- Luke 15:11–32;
- `59_LUKE15...`;
- `90_ADULT_PRODIGAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFICATION...`;
- `99...` only as source packet;
- `216_RETURN_HOME...`.

### Evidence class

Narrative facts: `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE`.

Modern-economic comparison: `HISTORICAL / CONTEXTUAL OBSERVATION + PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`.

## Narrative fact

The younger son asks for his share of the property, leaves for a far country, dissipates his substance, then reaches severe want amid famine.

This is not a timeless economic algorithm for every adult departure.

## Modern difference the Product article must acknowledge briefly

A contemporary 18- or 20-year-old may leave with:

- employment or remote work;
- salary paid to his own account;
- rented accommodation;
- public benefits or student support;
- transport;
- friends/romantic partner providing housing;
- continuous digital community;
- access to credit or consumer finance;
- the ability to remain socially connected to family while physically absent.

Therefore:

> **Физический уход сегодня не обязательно быстро создаёт тот же материальный кризис, который описан в притче.**

The parent cannot assume:

> `If I simply stop contact, hunger will reproduce Luke 15 and repentance will follow.`

That is precisely why Companion A must remain providential, not mechanical.

### Locked

`LUKE 15 CONSEQUENCE ≠ MODERN PARENTAL TECHNIQUE`.

`MATERIAL COLLAPSE ≠ GUARANTEED REPENTANCE`.

Detailed cash/debt/rent/house mechanics still belong to Companion B.

Companion A only needs enough modern-economic realism to prevent misuse of the parable.

# 3. Social media and messaging — contact is no longer identical to physical proximity

Primary controls:

- `60_ADULT_CHILD_SECRET_DEPARTURE...`;
- `71_POST_DEPARTURE_CONTACT_CADENCE...`;
- `62`, `78` continuity requirements;
- `231` evidence ladder.

### Evidence class

`PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`; acute safety/legal cases may become `CIVIL_OR_SAFEGUARDING_DEPENDENT`.

## Modern fact pattern

An adult child can live elsewhere while parents still see:

- public stories;
- public posts;
- profile changes;
- ordinary messages;
- read receipts;
- location that the adult voluntarily shares;
- mutual family/group chats.

This means `he left` does not necessarily mean `all relationship/contact ceased`.

If the adult willingly answers and does not reject contact, there is no biblical rule requiring the parents to create artificial silence merely because physical residence changed.

They may:

- write;
- call;
- meet;
- invite;
- listen;
- warn;
- pray together where willing;
- discuss return/repentance when the child raises it or remains receptive.

## Public information guard

Looking at what the adult **publicly posts** is not automatically equivalent to unlawful surveillance.

But the moral question remains:

> Am I looking because there is a concrete duty/safety question — or because I cannot tolerate not knowing everything?

Repeated compulsive checking can become a parental bondage to knowledge/control even where the information is technically public.

### Locked

`PUBLICLY VISIBLE ≠ PARENTAL RIGHT TO OMNISCIENCE`.

`PRAYER ≠ SURVEILLANCE`.

# 4. Blocking changes the moral situation

A competent adult may block parents or clearly request no ordinary contact.

### Evidence class

General relational response: `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`.

Threat/self-harm/coercion/abuse/emergency exceptions: current `CIVIL_OR_SAFEGUARDING_DEPENDENT`.

## Ordinary case

If the adult is known to be safe enough for ordinary purposes and clearly rejects contact, parents should not treat their natural relation as authorization to defeat the boundary through:

- fake accounts;
- repeated new numbers;
- covert geolocation;
- friends used as spies;
- physical following;
- password/account intrusion;
- impersonation.

### Locked

`NATURAL RELATION ≠ LICENSE FOR COVERT PURSUIT`.

This does not mean the parents must cease:

- prayer;
- truthful standing position;
- ordinary openness to renewed contact;
- appropriate communication through a legitimate intermediary when genuinely necessary and safe;
- emergency action where concrete danger changes the jurisdictional question.

## Emergency guard

A credible concern about:

- imminent self-harm;
- abduction/coercion;
- violence;
- exploitation;
- severe incapacity;
- other acute danger

is not simply an ordinary `respect my block` relationship question. It may require competent emergency/safeguarding/civil action depending on place and facts.

Do not create universal legal instructions in Companion A.

# 6. Quick return — no minimum “far-country duration”

The owner explicitly asked what happens if the adult leaves and almost immediately wants to return.

### Evidence class

`DIRECT_SCRIPTURE + PASTORAL APPLICATION` from Luke 15, with repentance-credibility controls from `212/216`.

## Locked

There is no biblical minimum duration of exile required before mercy may be shown.

Do not say:

> `You have not suffered enough yet. Go back out for six months so the lesson becomes real.`

If the child quickly returns and shows credible repentance, the parent's task is not to manufacture extra suffering.

At the same time:

`RETURN HOME ≠ AUTOMATIC REPENTANCE`.

A rapid return may be driven by:

- fear;
- money;
- relational collapse;
- genuine conviction;
- mixed motives.

Listen, assess truth and fruit, and distinguish:

`RECEIVE THE PERSON`
from
`RESTORE EVERY PREVIOUS PRIVILEGE`.

Detailed co-residence rules remain Companion B.

# 7. Proverbs 1–9 — continuing fatherly warning without a permanent-minor theory

The owner requested a stronger direct-biblical foundation for continuing parental speech.

### Evidence class

`DIRECT_SCRIPTURE / WISDOM` for fatherly instruction; modern adult-contact application = `CANONICAL / PRUDENTIAL`.

Proverbs repeatedly depicts a father continuing to address a son with:

- warning;
- instruction;
- appeals to wisdom;
- sexual warnings;
- descriptions of consequences.

This supports the moral legitimacy of sustained fatherly counsel.

### Guard

Proverbs does **not** specify:

- modern civil majority;
- contact cadence after estrangement;
- passwords/location rights over an adult;
- a permanent child-level jurisdiction.

Use Proverbs to defend **continuing wise speech**, not indefinite minor-child government.
