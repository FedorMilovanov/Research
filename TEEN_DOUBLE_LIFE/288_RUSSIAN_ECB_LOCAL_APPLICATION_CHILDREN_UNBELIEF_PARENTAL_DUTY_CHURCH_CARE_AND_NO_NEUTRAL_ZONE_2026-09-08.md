# Russian ECB Local Application
## Children, Unbelief, Parental Duty, Church Care, and No Neutral Zone

**Status:** RESEARCH / LOCAL CONFESSIONAL APPLICATION / NOT PUBLICATION
**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Depends on:** `273–287`

> Purpose: prevent the final Russian article from criticizing a caricature of Russian Baptist doctrine. Distinguish official regenerate-church / believer-baptism commitments from weak local pastoral habits that may exist despite those commitments.

## 1. Confessional center

Official Russian ECB materials preserve a Baptist ecclesiological pattern:

- local church is normatively a community of regenerated believers;
- baptism follows personal faith/repentance/new birth rather than parental status;
- parents carry direct responsibility for the spiritual upbringing of children;
- church supports the family in Christian formation rather than replacing it.

Therefore the proposition

> “Because the child is not yet regenerate/baptized, parents should not require Christian moral/religious order”

is not a necessary implication of Russian ECB ecclesiology.

## 2. Practical drift worth naming carefully

A congregation can nevertheless drift into a functional model:

`CHILD NOT YET BAPTIZED/MEMBER -> MAINLY FAMILY MATTER -> YOUTH PROGRAM PROVIDES ENVIRONMENT -> DIRECT ACCOUNTABILITY WAITS FOR PROFESSION`.

Possible results:

- moral expectations softened under “he is still unbelieving”;
- church-raised youth treated as an ordinary external unbeliever despite years of gospel light;
- direct pastoral admonition begins only after baptism candidacy;
- youth/social/choir belonging communicates inclusion without corresponding accountability;
- parents feel isolated when a child openly rejects faith;
- civil age 18 is allowed to function as an unexamined release from prior family/church responsibility.

These are empirical/pastoral possibilities, not claims about every Russian ECB congregation. Public use would require evidence if generalized.

## 3. Strong Russian Baptist alternative

A locally recognizable high-accountability model can remain fully Baptist:

### Before credible profession

- child is not called a church member merely by birth;
- no baptism is administered to manufacture belonging;
- parents maintain Christian household order;
- child receives Scripture, catechesis, prayer, public worship exposure and direct gospel call;
- known serious sin is named rather than excused by unbelief;
- pastors know the child and support/correct the parents as needed;
- choir/youth/trusted roles are stewarded separately from public worship access;
- no false testimony is forced.

### After credible profession/baptism/admission

- formal church-accountability layer is added;
- minor status does not cancel membership accountability;
- parental and ecclesial stewardships coexist.

## 4. Russian wording problem — “верующий / неверующий ребёнок”

In Baptist speech the binary can silently carry too much:

`верующий = Christian obligations apply`

`неверующий = what can you expect?`

The series should explicitly break that inference.

Better categories:

- `невозрождённый / не исповедующий веру` — soteriological/professional status;
- `ребёнок под родительской властью` — household status;
- `выросший под Евангелием` — gospel-light history;
- `член / не член` — ecclesial status;
- `несовершеннолетний / взрослый` — civil/developmental status.

A single label must not replace all five axes.

## 5. Russian pastoral sentence

> **“Он неверующий” отвечает на вопрос о его нынешнем исповедании; эта фраза не отвечает автоматически на вопросы о Божьем законе, родительской власти, полученном им свете, ответственности церкви за наставление или допустимых церковных ролях.**

## 6. Where Presbyterian comparison can help Russian Baptists

The comparison should not be framed:

> “Presbyterians care about children; Baptists do not.”

Instead:

> “Presbyterian polity formalizes child-church responsibility through covenant membership. Baptist polity rejects that membership before personal profession. The useful challenge is whether Baptist churches have built an equally intentional pastoral structure for children they deliberately do not yet call members.”

That is fair, sharp and locally applicable.

## 7. What not to import into Russian ECB language without explanation

Avoid unexplained technical expressions:

- `covenant breaker`;
- `non-communing member`;
- `federal holiness`;
- `baptismal membership`.

If used, explain as the other tradition’s categories rather than project doctrine.

## 8. Integration

Primary public homes:

- Part II: parents cannot use sovereign regeneration to abdicate present duty;
- Part III: church must distinguish formal membership from sustained pastoral responsibility;
- optional theological companion: compare covenant-child systems explicitly.

## 9. Final local thesis

> **Реформированному баптисту не нужно становиться педобаптистом, чтобы признать проблему: если ребёнок годами растёт под проповедью, знает Писание, участвует в церковной жизни и получает личные увещевания, церковь не должна вспоминать о его духовной ответственности впервые в день заявления на крещение. Баптистская граница членства может оставаться узкой; пастырская ответственность не должна становиться узкой вместе с ней.**
