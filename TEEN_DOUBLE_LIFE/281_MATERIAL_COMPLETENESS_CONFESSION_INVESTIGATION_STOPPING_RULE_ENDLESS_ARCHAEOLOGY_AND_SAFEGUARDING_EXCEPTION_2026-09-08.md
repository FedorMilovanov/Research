## 0. Executive verdict

The project needs a category between two false extremes.

### Error A — premature closure

> `He admitted the screenshot we already found. Stop asking questions; confession happened.`

This can leave intact:

- another account;
- another person at risk;
- hidden images;
- continuing contact;
- theft/debt;
- a planned meeting;
- a materially false timeline;
- another person who was lied about or harmed.

### Error B — unlimited archaeology

> `Because you lied before, repentance requires us to recover every message, every deleted image, every fantasy, every exact date, every sexual detail and every private thought until we feel no uncertainty.`

That standard is neither attainable nor a sound universal biblical rule.

### Locked center

> **MATERIAL COMPLETENESS ≠ EXHAUSTIVE OMNISCIENCE.**

> **ENOUGH TRUTH TO END THE LIE ≠ EVERY POSSIBLE DETAIL OF THE PAST.**

> **REPEATED DECEPTION JUSTIFIES STRONGER FACT-FINDING; IT DOES NOT MAKE PARENTS OMNISCIENT INVESTIGATORS.**

> **AN INVESTIGATION SHOULD HAVE A STEWARDSHIP QUESTION AND A STOPPING CONDITION.**

> **SAFEGUARDING / CRIMINAL / EXPLOITATION CASES CAN REQUIRE PROFESSIONAL FACT-FINDING BEYOND ORDINARY FAMILY INQUIRY.**

# 1. Scripture requires truth; it does not promise creaturely exhaustive knowledge

## Proverbs 28:13

The contrast is between:

- covering sin;
- confessing and forsaking.

Gill is useful because he identifies covering not only with silence but with:

- denial;
- justification;
- extenuation;
- excuse;
- blame-shifting.

He also explicitly says confession to people is according to the **nature of the offense**, rather than indiscriminate public self-exposure.

Source:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/proverbs/28.htm
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/proverbs/28-13.htm

### Project inference

The biblical target is the **covering mechanism**.

If a child says enough technically true words to preserve a materially false picture, the covering remains.

But the text does not institute:

> `parents must attain an exhaustive historical database before mercy or ordinary life may resume`.

Evidence class: `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE` for confession/forsaking; `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION` for the stopping rule.

---

# 2. Ephesians 4:25 — repentance from lying becomes a truthful relation, not one perfect archive

Paul commands believers to put away falsehood and speak truth with their neighbor.

Calvin treats the exhortation as flowing from new-person righteousness and opposes deceit to sincere truthful dealing.

Source:
- https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom41/calcom41.iv.v.vii.html

### Strong transfer

Where deception was systemic, repentance should include correcting **materially false representations** that still govern relationships or decisions.

Examples:

- `I told you there was one account; there were three.`
- `I said contact ended in May; it continued into August.`
- `I blamed X for initiating it; that was false.`
- `I said no one else received the image; Y did.`

This is different from requiring perfect recollection of every timestamp.

### Locked

> **TRUTHFULNESS REQUIRES CORRECTING MATERIAL FALSEHOOD; IT DOES NOT REQUIRE PERFECT MEMORY.**

# 3. Define “material” before asking for “complete”

`Material truth` in this project is a prudential category:

> **a fact whose presence or absence could reasonably change moral assessment, safety action, restitution, church/civil jurisdiction, household boundaries, credibility evaluation, or protection of another person.**

It is not a legal definition imported wholesale from one jurisdiction.

Potentially material facts include:

- another person was harmed or endangered;
- another minor/adult was involved;
- coercion, grooming, blackmail or threat existed;
- sexual images were made, stored, requested, redistributed or threatened;
- contact is continuing;
- a planned physical meeting exists;
- another device/account/channel exists;
- money/property was taken or misused;
- public statements materially misrepresented events;
- evidence was deliberately destroyed to obstruct legitimate safety/accountability;
- a younger sibling or vulnerable person has exposure/access risk;
- church role/member discipline/safeguarding is materially implicated.

Often non-material or needlessly intrusive details include:

- exact pornographic visual description;
- every sexual thought;
- every bodily detail;
- exact wording of every message after the governing category is established;
- every minute/date where chronology does not change action;
- repeated retelling solely to reduce parental anxiety.

### P0

`MATERIAL ≠ INTERESTING`.

`MATERIAL ≠ EMOTIONALLY SATISFYING TO KNOW`.

`MATERIAL ≠ EVERYTHING THAT CAN BE RECOVERED FROM A DEVICE`.

# 4. A useful four-question materiality test

Before asking for another layer of detail, a parent/helper can ask:

1. **Safety:** could this answer change who needs protection now?
2. **Repair:** could this answer change who was wronged or what must be corrected/restituted?
3. **Stewardship:** could this answer change a concrete house/device/contact/access boundary?
4. **Jurisdiction:** could this answer change whether church, safeguarding, civil or professional involvement is needed?

If the honest answer to all four is `no`, the burden for intrusive questioning rises substantially.

This is not inspired law. It is a `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION` designed to keep fact-finding connected to duty.

# 5. “Tell us everything” is too vague

After repeated deception, parents often say:

> `Tell us everything now.`

The child may genuinely not know what `everything` means.

Does it mean:

- every category of conduct?
- every person involved?
- every account?
- every date?
- every thought?
- every detail of sexual content?
- every lie ever told?

### Better moral boundary

The family can define:

> `We need the material facts that would change safety, repair, household decisions or whether someone else must know. We do not need graphic sexual narration or perfect recollection of every minor detail.`

This makes later evaluation of `you said that was all` more just.

### Mirror guard

If parents never defined the requested scope, they must be cautious about treating every later remembered minor fact as proof that the child deliberately lied about completeness.

Use `274`.

# 6. The five states of “more came out later”

Do not collapse them.

## A. Material fact knowingly withheld after a clear completeness question

Example:

Parent clearly asks whether another account/person exists. Child knows yes, says no. Evidence later proves yes.

This is strong evidence of renewed deception.

## B. Material fact volunteered later without new external pressure

This may be evidence that truth is beginning to outrun investigation, even though the earlier account was incomplete.

Credibility still needs context.

## C. Non-material detail remembered later

Example:

child originally said `around March`, later remembers it was late February.

Do not automatically call this another lie if the date did not materially change the account and memory uncertainty was real.

## D. Earlier honest uncertainty becomes later clearer memory

`I don't remember` can be truthful.

Later recollection does not retroactively prove the earlier answer false.

## E. New allegation generated mainly by repeated pressure

Parents must not treat their own suspicion as evidence.

`LOW CREDIBILITY ≠ PROVED GUILT OF EVERY SUSPICION`.

# 7. An ordinary-family investigation should have an explicit purpose

Bad governing purpose:

> `We will keep digging until we finally feel certain there can be no more.`

Because finite parents cannot reach that state.

Better purposes:

- determine whether immediate danger exists;
- identify all active channels/accounts relevant to ongoing risk;
- identify people who may have been harmed;
- determine what lie must be corrected;
- establish enough chronology to make current boundaries rational;
- determine whether competent external safeguarding/civil processes must take over.

### Locked

> **UNCERTAINTY CAN REMAIN AFTER RESPONSIBLE FACT-FINDING.**

Christian stewardship does not require pretending uncertainty disappeared.

---

# 8. A stopping rule for ordinary non-forensic family inquiry

A parent may reasonably move from active investigation to observation/rebuilding when, provisionally:

1. immediate safety questions are answered enough to act;
2. known active channels/contacts have been addressed;
3. identifiable harmed persons and repair duties are known enough to begin repair;
4. no concrete new evidence currently indicates another material layer;
5. current household/device/access rules no longer depend on discovering more detail;
6. any safeguarding/civil/church referral that is required has been transferred to competent process;
7. remaining uncertainty is being managed through proportionate boundaries and time rather than endless questioning.

### Important

This is **not**:

> `case permanently closed; no future evidence may be considered`.

It means:

> `ordinary investigation pauses because further intrusion presently has no defined stewardship yield`.

### P0

`STOPPING ACTIVE SEARCH ≠ DECLARING FULL TRUST`.

Parents can stop excavating while still keeping reduced access because established deception itself justifies prudential caution.

---

# 9. New concrete evidence can reopen a material question

Stopping is not amnesia.

Examples that may justify reopening:

- a newly discovered account;
- contradictory reliable third-party report;
- evidence of continuing contact;
- new safeguarding allegation;
- unexplained money/property issue linked to the prior pattern;
- new device/log evidence materially inconsistent with the settled account.

### Guard

Reopening should follow **new evidence or a newly relevant duty**, not merely recurrent anxiety:

> `I woke up afraid there must be more, therefore interrogation restarts.`

Parental fear can be understandable without becoming evidence.

---

# 10. Search intensity should track risk, not parental injury alone

Repeated lying wounds parents deeply. `279` owns that truth.

But hurt itself does not answer how intrusive fact-finding should be.

Two families can be equally hurt while stewardship needs differ.

### Case 1

Repeated lying about private pornography with no evidence of harmed third parties.

May justify substantial device/contact restrictions and credibility caution.

It does not automatically require reconstruction of every viewed image.

### Case 2

Possible coercion, sextortion, image distribution, adult-minor contact or abuse.

Now precise information may affect immediate safety and external jurisdiction.

The inquiry standard changes.

### Locked

`PAIN MAY EXPLAIN THE URGE TO KNOW EVERYTHING; STEWARDSHIP MUST GOVERN WHAT IS ACTUALLY ASKED.`

# 11. Safeguarding / abuse allegations are not ordinary family archaeology

When abuse, exploitation, grooming, serious violence or criminal conduct may be present, parents/pastors should not simply apply the ordinary household stopping rule and conduct a home forensic investigation.

APSAC's 2023 forensic-interviewing guidelines describe child forensic interviewing as a specialized investigative practice and emphasize reliable information, professional competence and the best interests of the child.

APSAC overview material characterizes good practice as:

- neutral;
- supportive;
- objective;
- non-leading;
- non-suggestive;
- non-blaming;
- non-shaming.

Sources:
- https://apsac.org/practice-guidelines/
- https://apsac.org/overview-in-forensic-interviewing-of-children/

### Project use

The empirical/professional material supports a narrow safety guard:

> **when the case becomes forensic/safeguarding, ordinary parents/pastors should not treat repeated improvised interrogation as the gold standard for discovering truth.**

It does not tell the church what repentance means.

Evidence class: `SAFEGUARDING/PROFESSIONAL SECONDARY`.

# 12. Do not repeatedly expose parents to sexual images merely to “verify everything”

APSAC's practice-guideline catalogue includes a 2025 guideline specifically opposing showing child sexual abuse material and sexted images to parents, guardians and other adults.

Source:
- https://apsac.org/practice-guidelines/

### P0

Where illegal/exploitative sexual imagery may exist:

- do not redistribute it;
- do not make family viewing part of confession;
- preserve/handle evidence according to competent legal/safeguarding procedure;
- exact operational rules depend on jurisdiction.

This reinforces existing `165`, `24`, `266`:

`ACCOUNTABILITY ≠ CIRCULATION OF SEXUAL DETAIL OR IMAGES`.

# 13. Evidence-bounded confession and investigation stopping belong together

`273` correctly warns:

> if the child's story repeatedly expands only to the exact boundary of what parents can prove, the confession remains evidence-bounded.

But that principle must not mutate into:

> `Parents must keep producing new evidence forever so that the child can prove he can outrun it.`

The positive alternative is not endless discovery.

It is a changed relation to truth:

- voluntary correction of material falsehood;
- disclosure of material truth that could still be hidden;
- asking for help before the next fall;
- accepting that some trust/access will rebuild over time rather than through one exhaustive interrogation.

### Locked pair

`TRUTH GETTING AHEAD OF INVESTIGATION = POSITIVE EVIDENCE`.

`INVESTIGATION NEED NOT RUN FOREVER TO TEST WHETHER TRUTH CAN GET AHEAD OF IT`.

---

# 14. “We may never know everything” is not surrender to lies

After a long hidden life, parents may never know:

- exact number of incidents;
- every deleted conversation;
- every thought;
- every date;
- every peer reaction.

That can be painful.

Christian response can still be truthful:

> `We know enough to call established sin what it is. We know enough to set present boundaries. We do not know every historical detail. Because prior deception was extensive, credibility remains damaged. We will not invent facts to fill the gaps, and we will watch what truthfulness produces over time.`

### P0

`UNKNOWN ≠ INNOCENT` where other wrongdoing is established.

And:

`UNKNOWN ≠ GUILTY OF THE SPECIFIC UNKNOWN`.

---

# 15. Parent anxiety must not become the child's impossible epistemic burden

A parent can feel:

> `If I do not know everything, I am unsafe.`

The feeling can be understandable after betrayal.

But the child cannot give omniscience.

Even a sincere child cannot prove a universal negative:

> `There is absolutely no forgotten or unknown detail anywhere in the past.`

### Product guard

Do not make restored peace depend on an impossible epistemic achievement.

Parents may need to live with:

- bounded uncertainty;
- stronger controls for a season;
- outside counsel;
- time-based observation;
- prayer;
- willingness to act on actual new evidence.

### Locked

> **BOUNDARIES CAN CARRY UNCERTAINTY THAT INTERROGATION CANNOT REMOVE.**

# 16. Scrupulosity / false-guilt guard

For a scrupulous adolescent, an undefined command:

> `Confess absolutely everything you might ever have done or thought`

can create an endless self-search loop.

The project must distinguish:

- material moral truth;
- deliberate concealment;
- ordinary memory uncertainty;
- intrusive thoughts/temptation;
- compulsive reassurance/confession seeking.

Use specialized scrupulosity companion routing from `188`.

### P0

`ANTI-CONCEALMENT ≠ COMPULSIVE TOTAL DISCLOSURE`.

# 17. Product-ready parent decision sequence

This is a prudential sequence, not a biblical checklist.

### 1. Ask what duty the missing fact would change

Safety? Repair? Household boundary? Jurisdiction?

### 2. Define the requested scope

Do not use `everything` when you mean specific material categories.

### 3. Ask minimally sufficient questions

Prefer morally/safeguarding relevant facts over explicit detail.

### 4. Distinguish denial from uncertainty

`No` / `I don't remember` / `I am not sure` are different claims.

### 5. Record established material contradictions

Do not globalize them into `therefore every statement is false`.

### 6. Transfer forensic/safeguarding issues

Do not become amateur investigators where competent processes are needed.

### 7. Decide a stopping condition

Once further searching has no defined stewardship yield, pause active excavation.

### 8. Manage residual uncertainty by boundaries and time

Not by pretending trust is restored.

### 9. Reopen only on material new evidence/duty

Not merely because anxiety returned.

# 18. Product overclaim kill-switch

Reject or rewrite:

- `True repentance means remembering and reporting every detail.`
- `If one detail appears later, the whole confession was definitely false.`
- `Parents must search until no uncertainty remains.`
- `Because the child lied before, any suspected hidden act may be treated as fact.`
- `Stopping device investigation means trusting the child again.`
- `Privacy means parents cannot investigate serious deception.`
- `Parental authority means there is no limit to the information parents may demand.`
- `Safeguarding interviews should be performed repeatedly by every concerned adult.`
- `Sexual images should be shown to parents so they know exactly what happened.`
- `If parents still feel anxious, the child has not disclosed enough.`

Preserve:

> **Ask enough to end material concealment and fulfill real stewardship. Do not turn the impossibility of omniscience into a permanent interrogation regime.**

# 20. Final locked synthesis

> **После долгой лжи родителям действительно может понадобиться гораздо больше проверки, чем после единичного добровольного признания. Но цель проверки — не достичь невозможного всеведения. Нужна материально достаточная правда: кто был вовлечён, продолжается ли контакт, кому причинён вред, есть ли опасность, какие ложные версии надо исправить и какие границы реально зависят от этих фактов. Когда эти обязанности можно выполнять, а новых конкретных оснований для следующего слоя нет, активное расследование может остановиться, даже если доверие ещё далеко не восстановлено. Оставшуюся неопределённость несут границы, время и наблюдаемая правдивость — не бесконечный допрос.**
