The new targeted search adds several useful controls.

## A. `Братский вестник` 1969 — parental honour as marriage discernment evidence

A 1969 exposition of Ephesians 6 includes a pastoral anecdote:

- a young believing man was considering marriage with a believing woman;
- after visiting her home and hearing how she spoke to her mother, he concluded she could not be his wife;
- the article treats dishonouring father/mother as a serious moral defect relevant to adult/marital life.

### What this supports

Allowed:

> **Filial honour remained morally relevant to mate discernment in Russian ECB pastoral teaching.**

It supports the project's anti-autonomy thesis:

> adult romantic choice does not erase the moral significance of how a person treats parents.

### What it does not support

It does not establish:

- paternal veto;
- required formal parental permission;
- father selecting the spouse;
- universal adult-daughter jurisdiction.

## B. `Братский вестник` 1947 — personal marital consent / anti-forced-marriage control

A historical marriage discussion in the 1947 issue explicitly reasons that a marriage should not be approved **without the voluntary desire of both persons entering marriage**.

This is important direct Russian evangelical/ECB evidence for:

`PERSONAL_MARITAL_CONSENT = REQUIRED`.

It fits the project's existing anti-forced-marriage guard and the 1689/Gill/Stennett personal-consent line.

### P0

A future conservative family-government article must not recover parental authority by erasing the woman's/man’s own real marital consent.

## C. 1947 autobiographical forced/pressured marriage anecdote — do not turn narrative into doctrine

Another `Братский вестник` 1947 autobiographical passage reports a man who, at age 18, was pressed by his mother to marry because of household/economic needs, despite not wanting marriage at that time.

This is a historical life narrative.

It is **not** a denominational normative rule and must not be used to claim:

> `Russian Baptists taught parents may force adult children to marry.`

Indeed the separate marriage-theology material above gives a voluntary-consent guard.
