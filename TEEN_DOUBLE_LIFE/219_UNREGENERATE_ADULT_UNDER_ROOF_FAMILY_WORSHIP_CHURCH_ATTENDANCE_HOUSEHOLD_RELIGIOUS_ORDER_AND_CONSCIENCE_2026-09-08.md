# Unregenerate / Hypocritical Adult Under Parents' Roof — Family Worship, Church Attendance, Household Religious Order, and Conscience

**Status:** RESEARCH-ONLY / SCRIPTURE-FIRST / CONFESSIONAL + PRACTICAL-THEOLOGY AUDIT / NOT PUBLICATION APPROVAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Project:** `TEEN_DOUBLE_LIFE` adult-child / family-government companion  
**Question:** What may Christian parents rightly require when an unregenerate, professedly unbelieving, or exposed hypocritical older teenager / legally adult son or daughter still lives in the parental household? May parents require family worship, church attendance, Scripture hearing, prayer/singing, and Christian household order without coercing a false profession or pretending regeneration can be produced by family authority?

---

## 1. Executive verdict

The biblical/confessional evidence refuses both common shortcuts.

### Shortcut A — permissive autonomy

> `He is unregenerate / eighteen / does not believe, therefore religious duties no longer apply and the Christian household must become religiously neutral around him.`

This is incompatible with the project's 1689 framework.

The 1689 Confession explicitly teaches:

- God has lordship over all and is to be feared, loved, praised, called upon, trusted and served;
- prayer is part of natural worship and is **required of all men**;
- God is to be worshipped **in private families daily**;
- public assemblies are not carelessly or wilfully to be neglected when God calls thereto;
- works performed by the unregenerate cannot please God without faith/right manner/right end, **yet neglect of commanded duties is more sinful and displeasing to God**.

Therefore:

> **UNREGENERATE ≠ RELIGIOUSLY DUTYLESS.**

### Shortcut B — coerced profession

> `Because this is a Christian home, the parent may require the unregenerate adult to speak as though converted, confess assurance he does not possess, take church privileges, or perform an outward Christian identity so the household looks orderly.`

This is also incompatible with the same theology.

The acceptable worship of God requires truth, faith, reverence and the Spirit's grace. Human authority cannot regenerate, create saving faith, or make hypocrisy acceptable to God.

Therefore:

> **HOUSEHOLD WORSHIP ORDER ≠ POWER TO MANUFACTURE SPIRITUAL WORSHIP.**

### Controlling synthesis

A Christian parent may ordinarily govern a resident household as a Christian household and require reasonable presence, order, listening, non-disruption, and participation appropriate to the person's actual status. But the parent must not command a false profession, sacramental presumption, fabricated testimony, or statements of inward faith the person does not truthfully possess.

For a competent legal adult, the precise scope of requirement is **status-sensitive**. Residence under the parental roof creates real household obligations, but does not restore every jurisdiction of minority.

## 2. 1689 LBCF 22.1 — the foundation is God's lordship, not parental preference

Primary:

- 1689 LBCF ch. 22: https://www.the1689confession.com/1689/chapter-22

Paragraph 1 begins with the light of nature and God's universal lordship. God is to be feared, loved, praised, called upon, trusted and served with the whole person.

This matters because family worship must not be framed merely as:

> `Dad likes religious routines, therefore everybody must humour Dad.`

The deeper claim is:

> **God is God of the believing father, the unbelieving child, the visiting relative, and the whole household.**

Parental authority is secondary and ministerial. The parent does not create the child's duty to God; the parent governs within an already-existing divine claim.

### Guard

Because God's lordship is universal, the father must not confuse **his own preferences** with God's revealed worship.

`GOD'S CLAIM ≠ EVERY RELIGIOUS HOUSE RULE THE PARENT CAN INVENT`.

The regulative principle of 22.1 itself limits human invention.

## 3. 1689 LBCF 22.3 — prayer is required of all men

Primary:

- 1689 LBCF 22.3: https://www.the1689confession.com/1689/chapter-22

The confession says prayer with thanksgiving, as part of natural worship, is **required of all men**.

This is a decisive control against:

> `An unregenerate child should not pray because prayer is only for believers.`

The confession then distinguishes the **duty** from the **acceptable/spiritual performance** of that duty: acceptable prayer is in Christ's name, with the Spirit's help, according to God's will, with understanding, reverence, humility, fervency, faith, love and perseverance.

### John Gill confirms the same distinction

Primary:

- John Gill, *A Body of Practical Divinity*, prayer: https://ccel.org/ccel/gill/practical/practical.iv.v.html

Gill explicitly says prayer is a natural duty and binding on all men. Men should pray even though only spiritual persons can pray in a spiritual manner. Elsewhere he notes Simon Magus, known as unregenerate, being directed to pray (Acts 8:22).

### Locked distinction

`DUTY TO PRAY`

is not identical to

`ABILITY TO PRAY ACCEPTABLY BY SAVING FAITH`.

Therefore parents must not teach:

> `Until you are converted, God has no claim on your prayer.`

But neither should they assure an unregenerate child:

> `Your outward prayer proves everything between you and God is fine.`

## 4. 1689 LBCF 16.7 — the crucial unregenerate-duty paragraph

Primary:

- 1689 LBCF ch. 16.7: https://www.the1689confession.com/1689/chapter-16

The confession says works of unregenerate people may be things God commands and useful to themselves/others, yet because they do not proceed from a heart purified by faith, are not done according to the Word in the right manner, and do not have God's glory as their right end, they cannot please God or merit grace.

Then comes the critical final clause:

> **neglect of such duties is more sinful and displeasing to God.**

### Project implication

This paragraph destroys a false binary:

`either regenerated spiritual obedience`

or

`no obligation at all`.

The unregenerate person's inability to render saving/spiritual obedience does not dissolve God's moral claim.

### Pastoral application

A parent can truthfully say:

> `Your unbelief does not make God irrelevant. You need a new heart; outward religion cannot save you. But refusing God's Word, prayer and worship is not morally neutral simply because you presently do not believe.`

This is stronger and more honest than either forced religiosity or moral neutrality.

## 5. 1689 LBCF 22.6 — daily family worship is itself confessional Baptist doctrine

Primary:

- 1689 LBCF ch. 22.6: https://www.the1689confession.com/1689/chapter-22

The confession says God is to be worshipped:

- everywhere in spirit and truth;
- **in private families daily**;
- secretly by each person;
- more solemnly in public assemblies, which should not be carelessly or wilfully neglected when God calls thereto.

Therefore family worship is not merely a Presbyterian borrowing in this project.

The exact Westminster Directory procedures are Presbyterian/Scottish ecclesiastical documents, but the underlying Baptist confessional obligation of daily private-family worship is explicit in 1689.

### Strong allowed claim

> **A Christian head of household is not required to suspend family worship because one resident son or daughter rejects the faith.**

Indeed allowing the most rebellious member to determine whether the whole household may pray/read Scripture would invert household government.

## 6. Gill — heads of households have private teaching/worship duties

Primary sources:

- Gill, private/relative worship: https://ccel.org/ccel/gill/practical/practical.v.i.html
- Gill, private teaching / ministry: https://ccel.org/ccel/gill/practical/practical.iv.iii.html
- Gill, duties of parents/children: https://ccel.org/ccel/gill/practical/practical.v.ii.html

Gill treats:

- Scripture reading in the family for instruction;
- family prayer;
- family singing;
- parental teaching;
- masters/heads commanding households in the way of the Lord;
- bringing children up in the nurture/admonition of the Lord

as real duties.

Gill's household theology is not religious neutrality until regeneration occurs.

### Important status guard from Gill himself

Gill simultaneously says parental power is **less when children are grown**, while filial reverence and duty continue.

Therefore his family theology must not be flattened into:

> `Every adult living in a parent's house is under exactly the same coercive parental jurisdiction as a seven-year-old.`

The project must keep age/status and household order together.

## 7. Westminster Directory for Family Worship — strong historical comparator, not Baptist confession

Historical Reformed comparator:

- *Directory for Family Worship* (1647), e.g. https://www.fpchurch.org.uk/about-us/important-documents/the-directory-for-family-worship/
- transcription: https://westminster1647.com/en/dafamilia

The Directory makes the strong claim that the head of the family should ensure that **none of the family withdraw from any part of family worship**.

It also treats family worship as:

- prayer/praise;
- Scripture reading;
- catechising/instruction;
- edifying conference;
- admonition/reproof by those with family authority.

### Evidence classification

`WESTMINSTER_FAMILY_WORSHIP_HOUSEHOLD_PRESENCE = VERIFIED_HISTORICAL_REFORMED_COMPARATOR`

It is **not** an article of the 1689 Confession and must not be cited as though London Particular Baptists formally adopted this Scottish disciplinary directory.

### Legitimate use

It demonstrates that the classic Reformed household tradition understood family worship as a governed household act, not as a collection of individually optional devotions performed only by already regenerate household members.

## 8. Presence, listening, speaking, professing, sacraments — do not collapse them

This is the main practical distinction.

### Level A — HOUSEHOLD PRESENCE / ORDER

Examples:

- come to the room/table when the family reads Scripture and prays;
- do not mock, swear, play explicit media or deliberately disrupt worship;
- listen respectfully;
- permit the household to maintain its daily Christian order.

For a resident/dependent older teenager this is strongly within ordinary household government.

For a competent legal adult resident, it can still be a legitimate house expectation, but status/proportionality and actual household relation matter. It is not automatically equivalent to minor-child jurisdiction.

### Level B — OUTWARD PARTICIPATION IN A TRUE DUTY

Examples:

- read a Scripture passage aloud;
- join the family physically in hearing;
- bow respectfully during prayer;
- sing a psalm/hymn whose words the person can truthfully utter as objective praise/petition;
- pray honestly for mercy, repentance, wisdom, forgiveness, conversion.

The 1689/Gill distinction means unregenerate status does not itself make all such duties forbidden.

### Level C — PERSONAL SPIRITUAL CLAIM

Examples:

- `I know I am born again`;
- `Christ is mine in saving assurance`;
- `I have repented when I know I am defending the sin`;
- testimony of conversion;
- baptismal profession;
- pretending reconciliation with God.

Parents must **not require lying**.

A Christian household must not train hypocrisy by demanding testimony the child says is false.

### Level D — CHURCH COVENANT / SACRAMENTAL PRIVILEGE

Examples:

- baptism;
- church membership;
- Lord's Supper;
- membership vows;
- ministry role/platform.

These cannot be treated as ordinary house chores.

`FAMILY AUTHORITY ≠ CHURCH KEYS`.

The user project's existing guard remains:

`PUBLIC WORD HEARING ≠ MEMBERSHIP/TABLE ≠ CHOIR/PLATFORM ≠ LEADERSHIP/TRUSTED ACCESS`.

## 9. Singing is especially nuanced

The user's concern about children singing to God while consciously living a double life is valid and must not be reduced to either:

> `just keep singing; it does not matter how you live`

or

> `if unconverted, never open your mouth in any song to God`.

1689 ch. 22 treats singing as worship to be done with understanding, faith, reverence and godly fear. Scripture also condemns religious speech/song used as a cloak for rebellion (e.g. Psalm 50/49:16–21 in Russian numbering; Amos 5 context).

### Therefore distinguish

**Duty:** God remains worthy of praise; unregenerate status does not create a right to despise worship.

**Hypocrisy:** singing while deliberately using religious performance to project a false spiritual identity can compound rather than neutralize sin.

**Choir/platform:** a child has no automatic entitlement to represent the church visibly merely because worship itself is a duty.

### Product-safe pastoral line

> `Не используй пение как маску. Бог достоин поклонения и тогда, когда ты от Него бежишь; поэтому проблема не решается тем, что ты просто перестанешь петь. Но не воображай, будто воскресная песня делает безопасной тайную жизнь, которую ты сознательно защищаешь.`

This preserves both sides.

## 10. Church attendance — hearing the Word versus pretending church status

1689 ch. 22.6 says public assemblies should not be carelessly or wilfully neglected when God calls thereto.

1 Cor. 14:23–25 itself anticipates unbelievers entering Christian assembly and hearing truth.

Therefore:

> **UNBELIEVER AT PUBLIC WORD = BIBLICALLY INTELLIGIBLE.**

The church need not tell the unbelieving son:

> `Stay away until you become a Christian.`

But neither should attendance be interpreted as:

> `You attend, therefore your defended sexual/lying life is no longer a matter requiring admonition.`

### Parents of a minor/dependent teenager

It is ordinarily reasonable within Christian parental government to establish Lord's Day/public-worship attendance as part of the household's religious order, absent safety/medical/other exceptional circumstances.

### Competent legal adult resident

A resident adult remains subject to real household arrangements and filial obligations, but the precise coercive reach is smaller/status-sensitive. The project should not invent a universal biblical rule such as:

> `Every competent 25-year-old lodger-child must attend every church event or is biblically required to be evicted.`

Nor should it say:

> `Because he is eighteen, parents may have no religious expectation whatsoever while subsidizing his life inside their Christian household.`

The legitimate question is cumulative:

- what is God's direct duty?
- what is the household's established order?
- how dependent/resident is the adult?
- is the requirement proportionate?
- is the parent asking presence/hearing or a false profession?
- does refusal form part of a larger settled rejection of the household's lawful order?
- is separate residence already appropriate for broader stewardship reasons?

## 11. Family worship cannot be used as a loyalty test to the parent

A father can sin even while commanding a true duty.

Forbidden distortions include:

- `If you do not pray exactly my wording, you rebel against me`;
- forcing public confession of sexual details before siblings;
- using prayer time to ambush or humiliate one child every evening;
- demanding a conversion testimony to protect family reputation;
- turning every Scripture reading into prosecution of the exposed child;
- requiring physical affection, performative tears or emotional display as proof of repentance;
- treating disagreement over an application as rejection of God;
- using family worship to bypass church/civil authority where abuse/crime exists.

Use `215` and concurrent `240_CULTURAL_FAMILY_MYTHS...` as controls.

### Key formula

> **A true command can be administered sinfully.**

Parental abuse of family worship does not make worship false; the child's rebellion does not make the parent's administration automatically righteous.

## 12. Unregenerate adult at home: a practical decision matrix

### Case 1 — minor/dependent child, unbelieving but generally orderly

Ordinary approach:

- family worship remains normal;
- public Word remains normal;
- teach and admonish;
- do not force false profession;
- do not grant sacramental/ministry privileges merely to preserve appearances;
- speak honestly about regeneration and faith.

### Case 2 — older teenager / newly adult, exposed double life, still resident and dependent

Ordinary approach:

- do not suspend family worship because it makes him/her uncomfortable;
- direct Scripture can address the real sin without turning every worship period into interrogation;
- require respectful household order;
- distinguish presence/hearing from claims of conversion;
- visible ministry/choir/trusted youth privileges should be separately reviewed;
- repeated refusal of all household order is evidence relevant to the broader co-residence question.

### Case 3 — competent adult resident, says plainly `I do not believe`

Possible legitimate model:

> `This remains a Christian home. We read Scripture and pray together. We are not asking you to lie and say you are converted. We do ask you to respect and ordinarily be present in the household's common worship/order while you choose to live here.`

But this is a **prudential household application**, not a verbatim biblical civil statute.

If the adult has long-standing independent status and pays/holds tenancy rights, civil law and the actual housing arrangement can significantly affect what may lawfully be enforced.

### Case 4 — competent adult resident openly mocks/disrupts worship and rejects basic house order

This is no longer merely a question of inward unbelief.

It becomes a household-government question:

`belief state + conduct + respect + shared-space order + effect on siblings + subsidy + adult capacity`.

Separate residence may become legitimate, but not because parents can expel unbelief from the heart. The issue is whether co-residence can continue under a truthful and orderly household government.

### Case 5 — adult child will attend family worship only if parents pretend everything is fine

Do not buy attendance through silence.

Existing P0 applies:

> `retention of attendance is not a higher good than truthful admonition`.

A quieter mode of correction may be wise (`211`), but the household need not create a false moral truce.

## 13. What parents must never say

### Unsafe

> `You are unbelieving, so God expects nothing from you.`

Contradicts 1689 22.3 / 16.7.

### Unsafe

> `If you pray/read/sing outwardly, that means God accepts you.`

Contradicts faith/acceptable-worship controls.

### Unsafe

> `You must tell everyone you are saved because this is a Christian home.`

Commands hypocrisy/falsehood.

### Unsafe

> `My parental authority gives me the church keys.`

Confuses family/church jurisdiction.

### Unsafe

> `You turned eighteen, so whether God's Word is heard in this household is now entirely your private autonomy.`

Confuses civil adulthood with religious/moral sovereignty.

### Unsafe

> `Living here means I own your conscience and may require every inward spiritual claim.`

Confuses household government with divine lordship of conscience.

## 14. The deeper theological distinction: external means and sovereign regeneration

The project must retain its controlling anthropology:

`TOTAL DEPRAVITY + SOVEREIGN REGENERATION + ORDAINED MEANS + FULL HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY`.

That means all four are true together:

1. A parent cannot regenerate a child.
2. An unregenerate child remains morally responsible before God.
3. Word, prayer, worship, admonition and church hearing are real appointed means/contexts of truth.
4. External participation without faith is not saving obedience.

Therefore the Christian parent neither abandons the means nor worships the means.

### Locked formulation

> **Family worship cannot give a new heart. That is precisely why parents must not turn family worship into theatre. But sovereign regeneration is not an argument for removing Scripture, prayer, worship and truthful admonition from the house while waiting passively for God to act.**

## 15. Implication for the broader co-residence question

Refusal of family worship or church hearing is not mechanically equivalent to sexual violence, theft, drugs, corruption of siblings, or household danger.

Therefore the project must not invent:

`miss family worship three times → eviction`.

But persistent refusal can become one item in a cumulative pattern:

- rejects God's moral law;
- rejects basic household religious order;
- rejects truthful conversation;
- mocks/disrupts the faith of others;
- uses the home as a base for defended gross sin;
- refuses reasonable adult responsibility;
- damages younger siblings;
- demands financial/material support while denying all legitimate household government.

At that point the question is not:

> `Can parents punish unbelief out of him?`

It is:

> **Can this competent adult continue sharing this household without requiring the household itself to abandon truthful Christian government?**

Use `172`, `202`, `210–217` for the wider decision.

## 16. Church / family jurisdiction guard

Parents may govern the home.

Parents do **not** unilaterally determine:

- church membership;
- church discipline verdicts;
- sacramental eligibility;
- ordination/leadership qualification;
- civil crimes/safeguarding adjudication.

The church likewise must not casually erase legitimate parental household government by telling a resident adult child:

> `Because you are eighteen, your parents have no meaningful claim on how you live in their home.`

Nor may church leaders turn parental claims into divine law merely because the father is respected.

Each institution must stay within its own jurisdiction under Scripture.

## 18. Product-safe synthesis

> **Неверие ребёнка не превращает Бога в необязательного Бога. Писание и 1689 исповедание не говорят: “пока человек не возрождён, молитва, Слово и поклонение его не касаются”. Напротив, Бог требует поклонения от всех, хотя только вера и действие Духа делают поклонение духовно угодным Ему. Поэтому христианский дом не обязан становиться религиозно нейтральным ради живущего в нём неверующего взрослого ребёнка. Но отец не имеет власти произвести новое сердце и не должен требовать от сына или дочери лгать о вере, изображать обращение или получать церковные привилегии ради семейной картинки. Семья может требовать порядка и присутствия перед Словом; Христос один может дать истинное поклоняющееся сердце.**
