# Debate map — feminism, evangelical egalitarianism, complementarianism, autonomy, and false targets

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Mode:** research-only / worldview and hermeneutics map / not publication approval  
**Purpose:** sharpen the project's critique of modern autonomy and feminism without attacking claims Scripture itself does not condemn.

---

## 0. Why this module exists

The user correctly identifies a cultural pressure toward independence and feminist/egalitarian assumptions. But the future article will lose force if it uses `feminism` as a catch-all label for every modern feature of female adulthood.

A stronger apologetic distinguishes:

- **economic competence** from moral autonomy;
- **legal personhood** from denial of family authority;
- **protection from forced marriage** from rejection of parental involvement;
- **female giftedness** from female church eldership;
- **mutual love** from egalitarian erasure of husband headship;
- **abuse resistance** from treating all hierarchy as abuse.

The target should be the actual theological/anthropological claim, not a stereotype.

---

# 1. Mainstream complementarian baseline — Danvers / CBMW

The Danvers Statement affirms:

- man and woman equal in image-bearing/personhood;
- real created-order role distinctions;
- Adamic headship before the Fall;
- husband's loving/humble headship and wife's intelligent/willing support/submission;
- male-only governing/teaching roles in church as understood by complementarians;
- all earthly authority remains under Christ;
- no domestic/religious/civil submission requires following a human authority into sin.

Primary source:
https://cbmw.org/about/the-danvers-statement/

### Key implication for this project

Mainstream complementarianism is already **anti-egalitarian** in marriage and church without necessarily teaching the strong claim:

> every adult unmarried daughter must have a private human male governor over all major choices until marriage.

CBMW's own “mere complementarian” formulations concentrate role asymmetry most explicitly in **marriage and church**.

Source:
https://cbmw.org/2019/11/20/mere-complementarianism/

Therefore:

`COMPLEMENTARIAN` ≠ automatically `FATHER-GUARDIANSHIP-UNTIL-MARRIAGE`.

# 2. Evangelical egalitarian baseline — CBE

Christians for Biblical Equality explicitly affirms equal creation/redemption and interprets the Christian family in terms of mutual submission/responsibility.

Its official statement treats husband and wife as jointly responsible and reads “head” in a non-governing / self-giving framework rather than as unilateral male authority.

Sources:
- https://www.cbeinternational.org/resource/men-women-and-biblical-equality/
- https://www.cbeinternational.org/resource/what-biblical-equality/
- https://www.cbeinternational.org/resource/biblical-submission-within-marriage/

### Actual point of dispute

The fundamental disagreement is not:

> “Are women intelligent?”

or

> “May women work?”

It is substantially hermeneutical/ecclesiological:

- does `kephalē/head` in Ephesians 5 include governing authority?
- is wife submission asymmetrical or simply one expression of mutual Christian submission?
- are governing/teaching church offices sex-restricted by creation order or open by gifting/calling?

Future Product criticism should address those claims honestly.

# 3. Secular/liberal autonomy is broader than evangelical egalitarianism

A person can be evangelical egalitarian while remaining strongly pro-family, anti-pornography and sexually conservative.

A person can also be formally complementarian while practically living by secular autonomy:

> “My parents, church and inherited community may advise me, but my authentic self is the final source of meaning and obligation.”

Therefore the project's deeper target is not only a label but an anthropology:

> **Who authors the obligations of the self?**

Biblically:

- God creates the person;
- God assigns natural and covenantal relations;
- the individual is a real moral agent but not a self-created sovereign.

Modern expressive individualism often moves in the opposite direction:

- inner desire defines identity;
- inherited authority must justify itself to the autonomous self;
- dependence is interpreted as immaturity;
- emancipation from unchosen obligations becomes a sign of adulthood.

See existing `189` for genealogy controls.

# 4. What the project may legitimately criticize as feminist/egalitarian pressure

Depending on source and definition, legitimate targets include:

## 4.1. Denial of husband headship

If the article's confessional stance is complementarian, it may directly reject readings that erase the asymmetrical husband/wife relation in Ephesians 5 / Colossians 3 / 1 Peter 3.

### Guard

Critique the interpretation, not the intelligence or sincerity of all egalitarian Christians.

## 4.2. Suspicion of hierarchy as intrinsically oppressive

Danvers itself recognizes two distortions:

- domination/passivity in men;
- usurpation/servility dynamics in response.

A Christian can condemn domination while still affirming ordered authority.

The project should explicitly reject:

> `HIERARCHY => OPPRESSION BY DEFINITION`.

Scripture contains legitimate asymmetrical relations while condemning tyranny.

## 4.3. Adult autonomy as relational emancipation

The project can critique cultural assumptions such as:

> “At eighteen my parents' moral claim becomes no more important than any acquaintance's opinion.”

Historic Reformed evidence in `193` strongly contradicts that flattening, especially around marriage.

## 4.4. Private-couple model of courtship/marriage

The Reformers' anti-clandestine-marriage tradition shows that making marriage solely a private decision of two romantic individuals is historically foreign to much Protestant thought.

## 4.5. Equality interpreted as sameness of role

The project can distinguish:

`equal image-bearing/value`

from

`identical office/authority/role`.

# 5. False targets — things that should NOT be attacked as feminist merely because modern women do them

## 5.1. Education

Scripture does not teach female ignorance as virtue.

A learned/competent woman is not thereby rejecting biblical authority.

## 5.2. Productive work

Proverbs 31, Lydia, widows' household responsibilities, and historical household economies all block the claim:

> `woman working/productively managing property => feminist rebellion`.

## 5.3. Property competence

Daughters of Zelophehad inherit land by divine judgment. Achsah seeks material provision. Proverbs 31 acquires a field.

Property competence does not equal moral autonomy from God/family.

## 5.4. Refusal of forced marriage

Classic Protestants who strongly required parental consent also denied parents the right to compel an unwilling child into marriage.

Therefore resistance to forced marriage is not an egalitarian concession; it is present inside strong historic patriarchy/Reformed family order.

## 5.5. Protection from abuse

Danvers itself says no earthly submission mandates following human authority into sin and explicitly laments family abuse.

Calling actual abuse “abuse” is not feminism.

## 5.6. Adult female agency before Christ

1 Corinthians 7, 1 Timothy 5, Acts narratives and existing `188` show that unmarried/widowed women are direct servants of Christ with moral agency.

This does not establish egalitarian church office or eliminate father/husband relations where they actually exist.

# 6. Strong father-led household model — where it goes beyond Danvers

Some conservative Christian models add claims such as:

- adult unmarried daughter remains under father in a robust governance relation until marriage;
- father substantially controls courtship access;
- daughter ordinarily remains in paternal household;
- potential suitors approach and negotiate through father;
- large adult decisions should remain father-guided.

These claims may be argued from:

- fifth-commandment continuity;
- Numbers 30;
- Genesis 24;
- 1 Corinthians 7:36–38 guardian reading;
- historic parental-consent practice;
- household theology.

### P0 classification

This is a **stronger inference/application model**, not identical to the minimum Danvers complementarian position.

Future Product must not say:

> “Anyone who rejects lifelong father-guardianship over unmarried adult daughters is therefore egalitarian/feminist.”

That is historically and theologically too broad.

# 7. Mainstream complementarian parental-blessing model — Piper as a contrast case

John Piper strongly urges adult couples to seek, work and wait for parental blessing, treat parental objections seriously, pray, involve others and avoid defiance.

But he explicitly calls parental approval **important, not absolute** for competent adults forming a new household.

Sources:
- https://www.desiringgod.org/interviews/is-it-important-to-have-your-parents-blessing-in-marriage
- https://www.desiringgod.org/interviews/must-i-wait-for-my-parents-to-approve-of-my-future-spouse

### Research value

This illustrates the difference between:

- modern mainstream complementarianism;
- stronger historic Westminster/Second Helvetic consent expectations;
- strong father-guardianship courtship systems.

The project should **compare** these rather than conceal the difference.

# 8. Why historic Reformed material may be more demanding than modern complementarian practice

`193` establishes:

- Luther: no secret engagement without parental consent + parents may neither compel nor unjustly hinder;
- Second Helvetic: marriage with parental consent;
- Westminster Directory: even adults with living parents at first marriage should follow the consent procedure; parents must not force or deny without just cause.

Therefore a future conservative Baptist article can fairly argue:

> **Modern evangelical courtship norms have often moved toward greater individualization even within complementarian circles.**

This is stronger and more precise than saying merely:

> “Feminism destroyed everything.”

---

# 9. Anti-feminist critique must also critique male autonomy

If autonomy is the problem, it cannot be criticized only when daughters exercise it.

A son who says:

> “I am eighteen; my parents' wisdom, church, family obligations and future wife's welfare are nobody's business but mine”

is operating from the same autonomous anthropology.

Likewise a husband who says:

> “I am head, therefore nobody can question me”

has made **his own authority autonomous from God**.

### P0

> **Biblical anti-autonomy applies to fathers, husbands, sons and daughters.**

This prevents the series from turning theology of order into a gendered excuse for male self-will.

# 11. Proposed reader-facing thesis

> **Проблема не в том, что взрослая дочь умеет работать, распоряжаться деньгами или мыслить самостоятельно. Проблема начинается тогда, когда способность отвечать за себя превращается в убеждение: “я никому ничем не обязана, пока сама не выбрала эту обязанность”. Писание знает взрослую ответственность, но не знает суверенного человека без данных Богом отношений.**

And complement:

> **Библейский ответ на феминистское стирание порядка — не сделать женщину вечным ребёнком. Это значит вернуть каждому отношению его настоящий вес: отец остаётся отцом, муж становится главой именно жены, церковь имеет свою власть, государство — свою, а над всеми стоит Христос.**
