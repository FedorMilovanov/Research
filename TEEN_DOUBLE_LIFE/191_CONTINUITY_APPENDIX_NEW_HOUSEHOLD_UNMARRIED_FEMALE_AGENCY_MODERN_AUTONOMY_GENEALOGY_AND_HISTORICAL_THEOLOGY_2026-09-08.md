## Locked finding 1 — marriage creates a new primary household relation

Gen. 2:24 is repeated by Jesus and Paul and therefore functions as creation-order marriage theology.

Calvin explicitly says:

- filial duty remains;
- marriage does not erase fifth-commandment obligations;
- nevertheless spouse relation has stronger comparative priority.

Chrysostom likewise treats leave/cleave as evidence of the remarkable strength of marital union.

Therefore avoid both:

`marriage => parents no longer matter`

and

`marriage => parental government remains unchanged`.

Prefer:

> **Marriage reorders relational priority while lifelong filial honour remains.**

## Locked finding 2 — fatherhood is not husband-headship-in-waiting

The father/daughter relation and husband/wife relation are both real but distinct.

Marriage does not simply rename the same male authority.

Do not write ownership-transfer language.

A father prepares daughter for faithfulness to God and, if she marries, for a new covenantal household in which her **own husband** has the marital headship Scripture assigns him.

## Locked finding 3 — unmarried/widowed women can have direct Godward calling and substantial agency

1 Cor. 7:34 describes unmarried woman/virgin as directly concerned with the Lord's things and holiness.

1 Cor. 7:39 gives widow freedom to marry whom she wishes `only in the Lord`.

1 Tim. 5 distinguishes family-supported widows, widows indeed, and younger widows called toward marriage/household government.

Anna / Lydia / Prov. 31 prevent a model equating biblical womanhood with economic/intellectual incapacity.

Therefore:

> **Husband headship applies where husband exists; direct female accountability to Christ does not wait for a private male governor.**

This is compatible with complementarian marriage/church convictions and continuing parent/family obligations.

## Locked finding 4 — generic “male covering” must not replace biblical relations

`Male covering` is popular theological vocabulary, not a controlling biblical phrase.

Use actual categories:

- child/parent;
- wife/her own husband;
- church member/elders;
- citizen/magistrate;
- believer/Christ.

If stronger father-guardianship is argued, prove the exact household inference rather than importing generic male jurisdiction.

## Locked finding 5 — modern autonomy has a multi-causal genealogy

Modern `adult = self-directing independent individual` culture arises through interaction of:

- philosophical/liberal individualism;
- contract/property law;
- industrialization and wage labour;
- urbanization/mobility;
- mass education;
- consumer culture;
- sexual revolution/contraception;
- feminist legal/political movements;
- egalitarian theological movements;
- expressive individualism;
- digital privacy/network life.

Do not claim feminism alone invented adult independence.

## Locked finding 6 — feminism itself is not one theory of atomism

Some feminist philosophy explicitly criticizes atomistic/self-sufficient autonomy and argues for `relational autonomy`.

Therefore criticism must identify which claim is being opposed:

- asymmetrical authority is intrinsically oppressive;
- economic dependence is inherently degrading;
- marriage roles are nonnormative;
- private sexual choice is morally sovereign;
- self-definition overrides inherited duties.

Do not attack a generic label where a precise proposition can be examined.

## Locked finding 7 — capacity is not autonomy

Economic/legal maturity is real and can be good.

Do not equate:

- working;
- owning property;
- budgeting;
- living separately;
- education;
- exercising gifts

with rebellion by definition.

The biblical question is whether those capacities are exercised under God with truth, family honour, church/civil duties and, where applicable, marital order.

> **Christian maturity is neither permanent childhood nor sovereign self-authorship.**

## Locked finding 8 — dependence is not holiness by itself

A reaction against autonomy can idolize dependence.

Scripture also requires:

- work;
- provision;
- responsibility;
- wisdom;
- formation of new household in marriage;
- care for kin.

A competent adult who parasitically refuses responsibility is not more biblical merely because he/she remains financially dependent on parents.

## Locked finding 9 — historic Christianity is broadly hierarchical but jurisdiction-sensitive

Chrysostom, Calvin, Heidelberg, Westminster and Puritan household writers strongly contradict modern flat nonhierarchical assumptions.

They preserve:

- real authority/subjection;
- strong parental honour/obedience;
- differentiated husband/wife order;
- marriage as new relation;
- God as the ceiling of every authority;
- duties/sins of superiors themselves.

Exact details vary on:

- adult daughter jurisdiction;
- parental marriage veto;
- courtship procedure;
- civil enforcement.

Therefore use historical consensus for the broad ordered-family thesis, not as a shortcut for disputed details.

## Locked cultural formulation

Prefer:

> **Modern Western adulthood often treats emancipation from inherited authority as maturity. Scripture instead presents maturity as increasing capacity to bear responsibility faithfully under God within the relations He actually gives.**

And:

> **The answer to feminist/individualist flattening is not paternal absolutism, but ordered authority under God with real jurisdiction, real limits and real accountability.**
