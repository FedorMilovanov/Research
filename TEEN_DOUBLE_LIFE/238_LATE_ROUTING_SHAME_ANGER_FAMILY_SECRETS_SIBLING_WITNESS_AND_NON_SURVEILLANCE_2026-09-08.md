## 2. Mandatory P0s from `266` — truth without humiliation

Preserve:

- `PRIVATE SIN ≠ AUTOMATIC PUBLIC CONFESSION`;
- `CONFESSION ≠ DISCLOSURE ≠ RESTITUTION ≠ APOLOGY ≠ CHURCH DISCIPLINE ≠ SAFEGUARDING REPORT ≠ PUBLIC STATEMENT`;
- `SHAME IS NOT A SACRAMENT OF REPENTANCE`;
- `PRIVACY ≠ COVER-UP`; `PUBLICITY ≠ HOLINESS`;
- `FAMILY AUTHORITY ≠ CHURCH KEYS`;
- `ROLE REMOVAL ≠ ENTITLEMENT OF EVERYONE TO ALL DETAILS`;
- `COMPELLED WORDS ≠ PROOF OF REPENTANCE`;
- `ACCOUNTABILITY ≠ CIRCULATION OF SEXUAL DETAILS`;
- `VOLUNTARY TRUTH ≠ PROMISE OF TOTAL SECRECY`;
- `VOLUNTARY TRUTH ≠ AUTOMATIC MAXIMUM PUBLICITY`;
- `CONFIDENTIALITY ≠ SAFEKEEPING OF ABUSE`;
- restitution may require truth to the harmed person without requiring an audience;
- public falsehood/public harm may require proportionate public correction, not explicit sexual detail;
- `PAST SIN ≠ PERPETUAL LICENSE FOR GOSSIP`.

### Product compression

> **Не путайте свет с прожектором унижения. Грех должен быть назван, но круг людей, которым необходимо знать правду, определяется реальным вредом, ответственностью, защитой и восстановлением — не желанием сделать подростку как можно стыднее.**

This is editorial synthesis, not Scripture quotation.

## 3. Mandatory P0s from `267` — authority without intimidation

Preserve:

- `AUTHORITY ≠ INTIMIDATION`;
- `REVERENCE ≠ PANIC`;
- `SERIOUSNESS ≠ RAGE`;
- `CONSEQUENCE ≠ THREATENING`;
- anger may be morally intelligible; anger discharge is not a discipline method;
- `CHILD FEAR ≠ PROOF OF PARENTAL FAITHFULNESS`;
- `PARENTAL CALM ≠ MORAL INDIFFERENCE`;
- Eph. 6 / Col. 3 must retain both real child obedience and real parental non-provocation;
- `PARENTAL AUTHORITY MAY RESTRAIN OPPORTUNITY; PARENTAL INTIMIDATION CANNOT MANUFACTURE THE FEAR OF GOD`;
- truthful warnings, possible consequences, bluffs and intimidatory threats are different categories;
- raised voice in urgent danger is not automatically abuse; loudness is not automatically righteousness either;
- parental repentance after sinful anger does not erase the child's real sin or every just consequence.

### Core anti-causality guard

Do not write:

> `strict parents create liars`.

Allowed:

> a parent can make truth relationally more costly through unpredictable rage/intimidation while the child remains morally responsible for lying.

## 4. Mandatory P0s from `268` — privacy without concealment

Preserve:

- `PRIVACY ≠ SECRECY`;
- `CONFIDENTIALITY ≠ CONCEALMENT OF EVIL`;
- `LOYALTY ≠ FALSE WITNESS`;
- `FAMILY HONOR ≠ IMAGE MANAGEMENT`;
- `NEED-TO-KNOW ≠ EVERYONE-GETS-TO-KNOW`;
- `DISCLOSURE ≠ PUBLICATION`;
- `SAFE DISCLOSURE ≠ GOSSIP`;
- harmless surprise/privacy ≠ dangerous secrecy;
- active concealment of moral/safety-relevant facts ≠ ordinary privacy;
- `LIGHT ≠ PUBLIC SPECTACLE`; `PRIVACY ≠ DARKNESS`; `DECEPTION ≠ PRIVACY`;
- family loyalty never requires covering abuse, coercion, violence or other real danger;
- `Tell me everything` is not a safe universal rule after sexual/digital discovery; parents need enough truth to protect/govern/repair, not every explicit detail;
- disclosure recipient should track legitimate need/jurisdiction;
- exact civil reporting duties remain jurisdiction-dependent.

### Product compression

> **У семьи должна быть частная жизнь, но не культ тайны. Ребёнок должен знать разницу между “это не для всех” и “это нельзя рассказывать ответственному взрослому, потому что нам будет стыдно”.**

## 5. Mandatory P0s from `269` — siblings are witnesses, not spies

Preserve:

- `WITNESS ≠ SPY`;
- `REPORTING DANGER ≠ TATTLING`;
- `TATTLING ≠ EVERY REPORT OF WRONGDOING`;
- sibling knowledge does not authorize parents to recruit sibling investigators;
- loyalty to sibling ≠ covering sin/danger;
- truth-telling ≠ taking pleasure in sibling trouble;
- a child may report what he saw; he should not be made responsible to catch what he did not see;
- danger/safeguarding need not wait for private sibling confrontation;
- `PARENTS MAY RECEIVE COLLATERAL INFORMATION; THEY SHOULD NOT TURN A CHILD INTO A HUMAN TRACKING APP`;
- older-sibling corrupting influence is a real protection issue but does not justify making younger siblings monitors;
- one child's deception ≠ automatic guilt of all siblings;
- adult safeguarding/governance responsibility must not be parentified onto another child.

### Product compression

> **Не делайте братьев и сестёр системой сигнализации друг на друга. Учите их сообщать об опасности и серьёзном обмане, но не выращивайте семейную культуру конкурентного доносительства и скрытого наблюдения.**

## 6. Cross-module synthesis

`266–269` jointly prevent a serious overreaction error after hidden sin is exposed.

Bad anti-secrecy system:

`discovery → maximum exposure → parental eruption → forced confession → total information demand → sibling informants → family surveillance`.

This can look morally severe while confusing several jurisdictions and duties.

The project instead requires:

`truth → proportionate disclosure → real consequences → need-to-know protection → bounded privacy → adult stewardship → restoration path`.

### Locked synthesis

> **DOUBLE LIFE MUST NOT BE ANSWERED BY A HOUSEHOLD SYSTEM OF HUMILIATION, INTIMIDATION, TOTALIZED SECRECY-RULES OR CHILD-TO-CHILD SURVEILLANCE.**

This does not soften moral standards. It makes the response more truthful, ordered and accountable to Scripture.
