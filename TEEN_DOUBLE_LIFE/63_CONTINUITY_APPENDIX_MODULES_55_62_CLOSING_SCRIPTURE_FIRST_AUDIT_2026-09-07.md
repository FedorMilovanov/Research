## 1. Latest user requirement that must not be lost

The user emphasized that **children are not exempt from holiness requirements in church simply because they are children**.

Specific concern:

- openly rebellious/profane/sexually corrupt children can sometimes remain in children’s choir, youth roles or close church settings as if their conduct were irrelevant;
- adults may excuse this by saying “he is only a child,” “he is not regenerate,” or “at least he keeps hearing the Word”;
- this can teach children that one may consciously live in rebellion and still retain every visible church privilege without direct admonition;
- leaders must teach God’s holiness, admonish known sin, protect other children and distinguish gospel access from ministry/trusted access;
- however, Scripture does not give an explicit children’s-choir admission statute, so church policies must be labeled **pastoral/ecclesial applications of biblical principles**, not direct inspired qualification lists.

Required balance:

> **Do not close the door to the Word; do not open every door to influence.**

> **A child has no entitlement to a microphone merely because he has an invitation to hear the gospel.**

> **Church ministry is not a prize for perfect children, but neither should it become camouflage for known rebellion.**

## 4. Module 57 — deep exegesis pass

`57_EXEGESIS_DEEP_PASS_HEBREWS3_ROMANS1_6_8_MATTHEW5_7_2COR7_1JOHN1_2026-09-07.md`

### Hebrews 3

Historical context: wilderness warning to covenant community. Key chain:

`unbelief → deceit of sin → hardening` with daily mutual exhortation as a commanded communal means.

Application:

> retained religious vocabulary does not prove that truth still governs life.

Do not label every hidden-sin teen a Hebrews-3 apostate.

### Romans 1

Key correction: Romans 1:32 shows that **knowledge of God’s judgment can coexist with continued practice and social approval of evil**. This is stronger for the project than assuming every hardened youth has intellectually reclassified the behavior as good.

Guard: never declare of a particular teen, “God has certainly judicially given him over.”

### Romans 6

Yielding one’s members and slavery/dominion are moral categories. Repeated presentation to sin is not morally neutral habit formation.

### Romans 8

For the believer, mortification is **by the Spirit**. Do not reduce Christian warfare to willpower/filtering/self-control. External means can assist, but salvation/sanctification are not technology management.

### Matthew 5

Sexual lust is heart-level; eye/hand imagery demands radicality about stumbling occasions. No literal self-harm, abuse or universal identical technology rule.

### Matthew 7

Narrow/broad way is kingdom/eschatological discipleship language, not a generic psychology of delayed gratification.

### 2 Corinthians 7

Godly sorrow produces repentance and observable earnest response. Tears/panic alone do not establish repentance.

### 1 John 1

Walking in light does not mean sinless perfection. The contrast is profession while walking in darkness versus bringing sin into light/confession/cleansing.

Major integrated line:

> **The biblical opposite of hidden darkness is not a flawless biography. It is a life in which sin is brought into the light rather than protected as a second kingdom.**

## 8. Module 61 — struggling sinner vs settled impenitence

`61_STRUGGLING_SINNER_SETTLED_IMPENITENCE_REPENTANCE_FRUIT_AND_NO_HEART_READING_2026-09-07.md`

Primary controls:

- 1 John 1;
- 2 Cor. 7;
- 1689 ch. 15;
- 1689 ch. 17;
- Watson.

### Stronger present signs of repentance/warfare

- voluntary truth beyond what was discovered;
- plain naming of sin;
- acceptance of God’s moral verdict;
- willingness to remove occasions;
- acceptance of proportionate consequences;
- disclosure of relapse instead of only proven facts;
- concern for people harmed/corrupted;
- stopping recruitment/distribution;
- changing corrupt company;
- seeking counsel/accountability;
- continuing warfare after immediate panic fades.

### Stronger present signs of settled impenitence

- demands acceptance of the sin;
- planned concealment/new secret routes;
- lying whenever evidence permits;
- mocking holiness;
- recruiting others;
- church role as camouflage/access;
- refusing all reasonable accountability;
- weaponizing `grace`, `I am unregenerate`, election, or `Christians sin too` to silence correction;
- approving the same evil in others.

This is NOT an infallible regeneration test.

Critical formula:

`recurring struggle + truth + hatred + help-seeking + renewed warfare`

≠

`planned concealment + defense + social recruitment + refusal of correction + settled entitlement`.

1689 ch. 17 explicitly prevents the false claim that one grievous/repeated fall automatically proves false conversion.

## 10. Children’s choir / church-youth policy position is now stable

The project must preserve this exact distinction:

### Public preaching / ordinary hearing

Default openness, except concrete disruption/safety restrictions.

### Congregational singing / teaching access

Not automatically a ministry credential. Unconverted children can hear, learn and be commanded to worship God; outward duty does not justify rebellion.

### Formal choir / platform / representative ministry

Higher conduct threshold is legitimate. Scripture does not provide a verbatim choir statute, but biblical principles strongly support temporary removal where there is known, serious, persistent or corrupting rebellion.

### Leadership / vulnerable-child access

Higher still; safeguarding may permanently restrict particular access even after genuine repentance.

Do NOT require adult perfection from children. Do NOT grant moral exemption because of childhood.
