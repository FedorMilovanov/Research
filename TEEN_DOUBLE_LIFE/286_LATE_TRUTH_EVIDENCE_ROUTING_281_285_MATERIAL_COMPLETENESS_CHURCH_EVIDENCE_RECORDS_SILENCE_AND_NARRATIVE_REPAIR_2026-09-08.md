# 4. Apparent tension: truth must outrun investigation vs investigation must stop

No contradiction.

`273` says voluntary truth that could still have remained hidden is strong positive evidence.

`281` says parents need not keep searching forever merely to manufacture opportunities for the child to outrun the next discovery.

### Reconciled rule

> **A truthful child can get ahead of a finite, purpose-bounded investigation; the investigation does not need to become infinite to test that truthfulness.**

After active searching stops, the child can still demonstrate truthfulness by:

- correcting a material omission voluntarily;
- reporting a relapse;
- identifying a new risk before discovery;
- asking for help before acting;
- maintaining consistency over time.

# 5. Apparent tension: full repair vs no maximum publicity

No contradiction.

`285` says a materially false story may require correction to people actually misled.

`165`, `266`, `283` say sexual detail and evidence should not be circulated without real need.

### Reconciled rule

> **CORRECT THE PROPOSITION THAT MISLED; DO NOT REPRODUCE THE PRIVATE MATERIAL THAT PROVED IT.**

Example:

Allowed corrective scope:

> `I told you my parents fabricated the issue. That was false.`

Not required merely for reputational repair:

- show screenshots;
- read explicit messages;
- describe sexual acts;
- name unrelated minors.

---

# 6. Apparent tension: church needs evidence vs confidentiality

No contradiction.

`282` allows church judgment based on sufficiently established evidence.

`283` says evidence access should be role-bound and minimized.

### Reconciled rule

> **ENOUGH EVIDENCE FOR A CHURCH DECISION ≠ EVERY LEADER POSSESSES EVERY ARTIFACT.**

Different roles may need different information:

- safeguarding lead;
- elders;
- youth workers;
- congregation.

Need-to-act does not imply equal need-to-know.

---

# 7. Apparent tension: silence is a problem vs silence is not proof

No contradiction.

For a minor in a real stewardship domain:

- refusal to answer can itself be disobedient/noncooperative;
- re-entrustment may reasonably pause.

But:

- the disputed act remains disputed unless independently established.

### Locked language

> `We know you are refusing a material question.`

may be true.

> `Therefore we know the suspected act happened.`

may still be false.

# 8. Apparent tension: correct false accusation vs preserve true grievance

No contradiction.

`285` requires precision.
