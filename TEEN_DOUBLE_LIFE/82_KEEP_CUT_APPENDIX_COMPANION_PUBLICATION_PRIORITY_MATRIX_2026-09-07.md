# 0. Labels

- **KEEP-A** — essential to central argument; must appear in every viable architecture.
- **KEEP-B** — important, but may be compressed depending on format.
- **SIDEBAR** — useful but interrupts narrative if fully developed.
- **APPENDIX** — practical/policy detail best outside main flow.
- **COMPANION** — substantial enough to deserve separate article.
- **CUT-MAIN** — retain in Research, but do not use in normal main prose unless a specific claim requires it.

# 14. Modern-statistics budget recommendation

Avoid creating an epidemiology report.

### Flagship / Part I

Use perhaps **4–6 modern numeric anchors total**:

- one teen online/smartphone figure;
- one continuous-online-contact figure;
- one pornography exposure/pathway fact;
- one sexting or sextortion fact;
- one multi-country abuse/safety fact if victimization section retained.

Every statistic must have:

- geography;
- age/sample;
- date;
- what exactly was measured;
- no prevalence/report confusion.

# 17. Editorial verdict

The corpus contains more useful material than the publication should display.

A strong final article should feel as though its argument was inevitable—not as though the reader has been shown every folder that made it possible.
