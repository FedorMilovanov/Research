# Authority vocabulary — honour, obedience, counsel, consent, permission/veto, house rules, and husband headship

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Mode:** research-only / conceptual-control layer / not publication approval  
**Purpose:** prevent the family-government companion from using one word (`подчинение`, `власть`, `послушание`) for several materially different biblical relations.

---

## 0. Why terminology matters

Many apparent disagreements in this subject are partly category collapses.

A person may affirm:

- lifelong honour of parents;
- serious parental counsel;
- strong family involvement in marriage;

while denying:

- unchanged minor-like command authority at age 30;
- universal paternal veto over every adult decision;
- husband-like headship of an unmarried daughter.

Conversely, someone may affirm adult responsibility while wrongly treating parental counsel as morally weightless.

Therefore every Product claim must identify **which authority concept is meant**.

---

# 1. HONOUR (`почитание`)

### Core texts

- Exod. 20:12 / Deut. 5:16;
- Eph. 6:2–3;
- Prov. 23:22;
- Mark 7:9–13 / 1 Tim. 5:4,8 in the broader duty-of-care trajectory.

### Nature

Honour includes real reverence, gratitude, care and refusal to treat parents as morally insignificant.

### Duration

Historic Reformed/Baptist sources treat honour as lifelong.

### P0

`HONOUR ≠ DO WHATEVER PARENTS SAY IN EVERY LIFE STAGE`.

But equally:

`ADULTHOOD ≠ PARENTS BECOME ORDINARY PEERS`.

---

# 2. OBEDIENCE (`послушание / исполнение повеления`)

### Core texts

- Eph. 6:1;
- Col. 3:20;
- Prov. 1:8; 6:20; 23:22.

### Historic range

Gill/Calvin can read the command strongly and broadly; Baxter explicitly retains forms/degrees of obedience after maturity while also saying age/marriage changes the government.

Modern conservative Reformed sources often restrict direct child-obedience more closely to the dependent/household stage while retaining lifelong honour.

### Control

Product must **attribute** the stronger adult-obedience reading rather than announce that no serious Reformed alternative exists.

### P0

When using `obey`, answer:

- obey whom?
- in what relation?
- in what domain?
- at what life stage?
- under whose higher law?

---

# 3. COUNSEL (`совет / наставление`)

Parental counsel can retain special moral weight even where command jurisdiction has narrowed.

Examples:

- spouse selection;
- financial danger;
- character of friends;
- vocation;
- church concerns;
- risky relocation.

### Key distinction

A mature adult may sometimes lawfully decide differently while still sinning in **how** he handles parental counsel:

- contempt;
- concealment;
- mockery;
- refusal even to listen;
- recruiting outsiders to neutralize parents before hearing them.

Thus:

> **Not every parental counsel is a command, but filial honour can still govern how counsel is received.**

---

# 4. CONSENT (`согласие`)

Consent means affirmative concurrence of another party whose concurrence is morally/institutionally relevant.

In marriage discussions two different consents must not be collapsed:

1. **consent of the marrying parties**;
2. **parental consent/blessing**.

1689 ch. 25 explicitly requires competent consent of those marrying.

Historic Reformed documents can also require/expect parental consent, including adults in first marriage (Westminster Directory).

### P0

> **Parental consent cannot substitute for the bride's/groom's own consent.**

And:

> **The couple's own consent does not automatically make parental consent morally irrelevant.**

---

# 5. PERMISSION / VETO (`разрешение / запрет`)

This is stronger than advice or blessing.

Examples of texts historically used:

- Numbers 30 paternal vow disallowance;
- Exodus 22 father refusing marriage in its Mosaic civil case;
- traditional 1 Cor. 7:36–38 father/daughter reading;
- historic Protestant marriage procedure.

### Requirement

Any Product statement of adult paternal **veto** must identify:

- exact text/tradition;
- domain (marriage? vow? residence?);
- life/household status;
- covenant/civil transfer;
- whether refusal itself requires just cause.

### P0

Do not silently turn:

`PARENTAL COUNSEL`

into

`PARENTAL VETO`.

Do not silently turn a real veto in one domain into total jurisdiction over all domains.

---

# 6. HOUSEHOLD ORDER (`порядок дома`)

This category arises from actual co-residence and stewardship.

A competent adult may possess significant personal responsibility while still being bound by lawful conditions of sharing another household.

Examples:

- no sexual partners staying overnight;
- no drugs/violence/theft;
- no corrupting younger siblings;
- reasonable financial/work contribution;
- security/visitor rules;
- truthful disclosure of matters directly affecting the household.

### Distinction

> **A house rule need not imply that the parent possesses total personal jurisdiction over the adult outside the house.**

Example:

Parents can say:

> "You may not bring your sexual partner into our home."

That proposition is categorically different from:

> "Because you sleep here, I therefore possess husband-like authority over every decision you make at work."

---

# 7. FINANCIAL STEWARDSHIP / CONDITIONAL SUPPORT

Money creates another domain often confused with authority.

Parents are not required to finance every lawful preference of an adult child.

They may condition discretionary support on reasonable stewardship goals.

But:

`I WILL NOT PAY FOR X`

is not always identical to

`GOD FORBIDS YOU TO DO X`.

### P0

Never spiritualize a financial choice into a divine command without warrant.

Likewise adult children must not demand parental money while claiming parents have no legitimate interest in how subsidized resources are used.

---

# 8. HUSBAND HEADSHIP (`главенство мужа`)

### Core texts

- Eph. 5:22–33;
- 1 Cor. 11:3 in its own context;
- Col. 3:18–19;
- 1 Pet. 3:1–7.

This is a **marital relation**.

The wife is repeatedly spoken of in relation to **her own husband**.

### P0

`FATHERHOOD ≠ HUSBAND HEADSHIP`.

A father can have real authority over a daughter without being her quasi-husband.

A fiancé does not receive husband headship by emotional intensity before marriage.

A pastor does not become the private husband-like head of every unmarried woman.

---

# 9. CHURCH AUTHORITY (`церковная власть`)

### Core texts

- Heb. 13:17;
- 1 Thess. 5:12–13;
- 1 Pet. 5:1–5;
- Matt. 18 / 1 Cor. 5 within church-discipline scope.

A believing adult child may owe church officers real ecclesial submission in a domain where parents have no equivalent church jurisdiction.

### P0

Pastors must not:

- erase parents where parents have a live lawful role;
- become replacement fathers to gain loyalty;
- claim pastoral authority over every private adult decision.

Parents must not:

- treat church membership as an extension of their private household command;
- demand pastors enforce merely personal parental preferences as church law.

---

# 10. CIVIL AUTHORITY / CIVIL ADULTHOOD

Civil law determines real things:

- legal majority;
- contracts;
- tenancy;
- marriage formalities;
- safeguarding;
- property;
- compulsory support rules.

### P0

> **Civil 18 is not a sacrament, but civil law is not imaginary.**

Christian family application must respect lawful civil jurisdiction unless obedience to God requires otherwise.

Do not tell readers to evict, confine, monitor, seize property, intercept communications or prevent movement without jurisdiction-specific legal verification.

---

# 11. NATURAL RELATION (`естественное отношение`)

A son remains a son and a daughter remains a daughter even when:

- living separately;
- disciplined by church;
- refusing parental counsel;
- married;
- estranged.

Owen/Gill/Edwards controls already establish that even church excommunication does not dissolve natural family duties.

### P0

`NATURAL RELATION`

survives many changes in

`TRUST / ACCESS / RESIDENCE / MONEY / FELLOWSHIP / ROLE`.

---

# 12. TRUST / ACCESS / PRIVILEGE

These are not authority categories but must be separated from them.

A parent may forgive yet not restore:

- car access;
- password/private-device autonomy while dependent;
- unsupervised contact with younger siblings;
- money;
- house keys;
- overnight guest privileges.

A church may welcome hearing of the Word while withholding:

- choir/platform;
- youth leadership;
- private access to vulnerable minors;
- Table/membership privileges where discipline applies.

### P0

`FORGIVENESS ≠ RESTORATION OF EVERY PRIVILEGE`.

---

# 13. Status table

| Category | Minor at home | 18-year-old dependent at home | long-independent unmarried adult | married adult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fifth-commandment honour | strong/direct | strong/direct | strong/direct | strong/direct |
| Close parental government | high | still substantial but changing | substantially reduced / disputed in exact degree | reordered by new household |
| Household rules | direct | direct while resident | only when visiting/residing | own household governs its home |
| Parental counsel | weighty | weighty | weighty | weighty but subordinate to marital duties where conflict |
| Marriage consent | legally/civilly dependent + tradition-specific | historically strong in many traditions | historically still strong in Westminster first marriage; 1689 exact rule not established | already married |
| Husband headship | no | no | no | yes, wife to own husband |
| Church authority | according to membership | according to membership | according to membership | according to membership |

### Guard

This is a **conceptual map**, not universal legal advice.

---

# 14. Product writing rule

Every time future prose says one of these words:

- `власть`;
- `послушание`;
- `подчинение`;
- `согласие`;
- `разрешение`;
- `границы`;
- `главенство`;

ask:

> **Which exact category is meant?**

If the sentence could be read in two categories, rewrite it.

---

# 15. Final synthesis

> **Библейская семья — не демократия одинаковых ролей, но и не одна бесформенная вертикаль власти. Бог устанавливает разные отношения: родительские, брачные, церковные, гражданские. Каждое имеет свой предмет и предел.**

And:

> **Главная ошибка современной автономии — сделать любое унаследованное отношение необязательным. Главная ошибка семейного абсолютизма — сделать одно реальное отношение универсальной властью над всей жизнью человека.**
