## 2. Locked historical findings

### Ancient Israel

- household life was strongly corporate/kinship based (`bet ha-av`), not modern atomistic individualism;
- marriage often involved family negotiation, bride-price and household transition;
- father had real legal standing in some Torah marriage/vow cases;
- Genesis 24 nevertheless preserves Rebekah's own expressed answer;
- marriage creates a new household relation.

### Numbers 30

Do **not** claim a false consensus.

- Gill/Jewish age tradition: paternal vow-annulment power is tied to minority / not-yet-of-age status;
- Poole and some older Protestant interpreters: broader continuing jurisdiction over an unmarried daughter in father's family/house.

Therefore exact adult-unmarried-daughter jurisdiction remains an interpretation question, not an uncontested proof text.

### Later Jewish trajectory

Mishnah/Talmud Kiddushin distinguishes paternal authority to betroth a minor daughter from an adult daughter, who is responsible for her own betrothal unless she makes father her agent.

Use historically; do not make rabbinic law Christian authority.

## 3. Locked comparative tradition findings

### Gouge/Baxter/Gill

They support a far stronger family/parental role than contemporary individualist dating culture.

Preserve:
- parental counsel/consent is morally serious;
- secret/casual bypass is disordered;
- parents have real formative/governing duties;
- children should not treat family as irrelevant;
- forced marriage is not justified;
- grown children are not simply identical to small children forever;
- adult maturity changes degree/form of parental government;
- filial honour remains.

### Modern conservative complementarian/biblical counseling

Common controlled pattern:

`strong obedience while under child/dependent authority`  
→ `adult responsibility`  
→ `lifelong honour`  
→ `significant parental counsel/involvement`  
→ `new primary household at marriage`.

### Strict father-led courtship model

Treat fairly:
- real strengths in accountability, family solidarity and anti-secret-romance protection;
- real risks when prudence becomes divine law or adulthood/consent is erased.

### Individualist dating model

Critique where it treats parents as irrelevant advisers and allows a pre-covenant romantic bond to become functionally superior to family before wisdom/consent can be heard.

## 5. P0 — third parties must not casually undermine lawful parents

This is now explicit user intent.

Where parents are substantially faithful to God and are confronting real sin, a third party should not become the child’s alternative moral household merely by being more agreeable.

Examples of wrong bypass:
- private romantic alliance against parents;
- secret housing/resources designed mainly to avoid righteous consequences;
- teaching the child to hide lawful family-relevant facts;
- telling child parents are “legalists/abusers” merely because they call fornication, pornography, lying or profanity sin;
- giving unrestricted youth/church access after parents warned of known corrupting conduct, without investigation;
- hearing one side and treating it as adjudicated fact.

### Locked line

> **Do not build a Christian-looking escape hatch from righteous parental correction.**

## 6. P0 — “work through parents where possible” is not absolute parental ownership

When a child/young adult is still:
- minor or substantially dependent;
- recently departed;
- in active contact with parents;
- not long-established in a separate adult household;

outsiders should ordinarily respect the existing family relation and seek transparent parental engagement where appropriate.

Especially:
- courtship/romantic approaches;
- travel;
- housing;
- finances;
- private mentoring;
- youth/church access;
- plans that directly affect household rules.

For a competent adult, parents do not own every decision. The point is **respect, transparency and mediation**, not perpetual parental veto.

## 7. P0 — if parents are right, support repentance/reconciliation rather than partisan comfort

If evidence shows:
- child is protecting fornication/pornography/deception/intoxication/theft/corruption;
- parental response is lawful/proportionate;
- no credible abuse/safety issue,

then friends/church members should normally:
- refuse false justification;
- encourage repentance;
- encourage truthful reconciliation;
- encourage return to lawful order where appropriate;
- avoid replacing every lost consequence with a new benefit.

### Guard

Physical return home is prudential for an adult. `Repentance/reconciliation` is not identical to `move back into the same bedroom`.

## 8. P0 — if parents are sinning, support the child where obedience to God requires it

## 11. P0 — romantic/suitor bypass deserves special warning

Before marriage there is not yet a spouse covenant that gives the romantic interest household primacy.

Therefore a suitor should be especially cautious about:
- encouraging secret alienation from faithful parents;
- becoming the primary confidential attachment while simultaneously discrediting parents;
- using “she is an adult” to erase every family obligation;
- creating faits accomplis before parents can give counsel.

### Balanced conclusion

Parents can be wrong; adult consent is real; forced marriage is wrong. But **transparent family engagement is normally a virtue, not an embarrassment**, where parents are substantially faithful and contact remains viable.
