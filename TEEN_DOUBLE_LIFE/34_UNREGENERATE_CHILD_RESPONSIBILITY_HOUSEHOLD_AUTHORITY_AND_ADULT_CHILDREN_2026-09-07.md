# Невозрождённый ребёнок, ответственность, домашняя власть и взрослые дети

**Статус:** RESEARCH / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Дата:** 2026-09-07

---

## 1. Ошибка: «Он же невозрождённый — что с него взять?»

Это смешивает две разные категории:

1. **способность произвести духовное добро, угодное Богу, из нового сердца**;
2. **объективную обязанность повиноваться Божьему моральному закону**.

Reformed anthropology affirms fallen inability without converting inability into innocence.

1689 LBCF 19.5 states the moral law forever binds **all**, justified persons and others, because of God’s authority as Creator.

Source:
https://www.creeds.net/baptists/1689/original/1689bc19.html

### Therefore
An unregenerate child is still obligated to:

- honor/obey rightful parental authority;
- tell truth;
- reject sexual immorality;
- not steal;
- not abuse others;
- not corrupt peers;
- repent and believe the gospel.

“Not regenerated” is not a moral exemption card.

---

## 2. Another error: treating external obedience as regeneration

The opposite mistake is equally serious:

> «He follows rules, therefore he is converted.»

External restraint can come from:

- fear;
- temperament;
- love of parents;
- social conformity;
- prudence;
- desire for reputation;
- genuine common grace;
- saving grace.

Only God knows infallibly; church/parents assess profession and fruit, not hidden decree.

---

## 3. Practical model for an unregenerate minor

Parents should neither:

- surrender household moral order because child is unbelieving;
- nor pretend child is regenerate because compliance is easier.

Possible stance:

> «Ты не обязан притворяться христианином. Но в нашем доме ты не свободен лгать, приносить pornography, exploit others, use drugs, abuse siblings or turn the home into a base for behavior that destroys you/others. We will keep teaching you the truth and call you to repentance without forcing a false profession.»

This is pastoral synthesis, not a universal script.

---

## 4. Why parental restraint of an unregenerate child is still legitimate

Parental government serves more than regeneration.

It can:

- protect child from harm;
- protect siblings;
- form habits/self-control;
- limit opportunity for grave sin;
- preserve household order;
- teach consequences;
- expose false beliefs through instruction;
- keep means of grace present.

External restraint does not save, but “does not save” ≠ “has no moral/providential value.”

---

## 5. What changes after 18?

There is no biblical magic switch at a civil birthday where moral responsibility appears/disappears.

But **household authority changes** as offspring become adults.

Important distinctions:

### Minor/dependent child
Parents have strong custodial authority and protective duties. Abandonment/homelessness must not be used as punishment.

### Adult child living in parents’ home
Parents may set lawful household conditions for residence. Examples can include:

- no violence;
- no drugs in home;
- no sexual activity/overnight partners in the home;
- no pornography displayed/shared in household spaces;
- no criminal/exploitative behavior;
- respect for other residents;
- contribution/work expectations appropriate to circumstances.

Persistent rejection of reasonable household conditions may eventually make separate residence a legitimate prudential consequence.

### Adult child living independently
Parents no longer exercise the same household government. They still owe love/truth and may set relational/financial boundaries; church discipline may apply if person is a member/professing believer.

### Legal guard
Housing/eviction/family-support laws vary by jurisdiction. Future public article must not give jurisdiction-specific legal advice without verification.

## 6. “Throw him out for sexual sin” is too blunt

Questions before any adult-household removal:

1. Is person minor or adult?
2. Is conduct private sin, household disruption, exploitation, violence, or criminal behavior?
3. Is person repentant/struggling or defiant and recruiting others?
4. Are siblings at risk?
5. Has clear rule been communicated?
6. Is consequence proportionate?
7. Is there a safer alternative/residence?
8. Is removal punitive rage or boundary protection?
9. What does local law require?

### Strong distinction
A repentant adult struggling with pornography is not the same household case as an adult bringing sexual partners into home against explicit rules, distributing illegal content, abusing siblings, or persistently using the household to facilitate grave conduct.

## 7. Deuteronomy 21 and the rebellious son

Deut 21:18–21 demonstrates the seriousness of entrenched rebellion in Old Covenant Israel, but its judicial penalty is not a parental model for today.

Important features:

- repeated discipline has already occurred;
- both father and mother bring case;
- city elders/public judicial authority adjudicate;
- parents do not execute sentence privately.

1689 19.4: Israel’s judicial laws expired with that polity; general equity remains morally useful.

### General-equity inference
The passage refutes sentimental moral neutrality:
> persistent hardened rebellion against rightful authority is serious.

It does **not** authorize:
- stoning;
- violence;
- private quasi-magistrate punishment;
- abandoning minors.

---

## 8. What parents should not do with profession of faith

Do not force:
> «Say you’re saved / pray this prayer / tell church you repented or you cannot live here.»

That can reward false profession.

Better separate:

- household obedience required because parents have authority;
- Christian profession must be honest;
- conversion belongs to God’s grace;
- child remains commanded to repent/believe.

---

## 9. Why “let him live however he wants until salvation” is pastorally destructive

It teaches several false lessons:

- moral law applies only after conversion;
- sin before conversion is less serious;
- parents have no ordinary authority over unbelieving children;
- God’s commands are church-club rules rather than Creator’s claims;
- external consequences are spiritually pointless.

This conflicts with Reformed doctrine of moral law binding all.

---

## 10. Why “control everything until conversion” is also destructive

It can:

- produce false profession;
- confuse compliance with grace;
- encourage sophisticated concealment;
- deny age-appropriate autonomy;
- create a household where confession seems impossible.

The goal is not to manufacture Christian appearance. It is to govern faithfully, teach truthfully, protect wisely and refuse to call darkness light.

## 11. Future article formula

> Невозрождённый ребёнок не обязан играть христианина, но он не перестаёт быть Божьим творением под Божьим законом и ребёнком под законной родительской властью. Родители не могут дать ему новое сердце — и именно поэтому они не должны подменять новое сердце ни безразличием, ни спектаклем внешней религиозности.
