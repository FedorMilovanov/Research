## 0. Executive verdict

A hidden-life pattern can create a second injury beyond the original conduct:

> **other people may now believe a false moral story because the child supplied it.**

Examples:

- `My parents randomly took my phone; I did nothing.`
- `Dad is abusive because he will not let me see X` while omitting the proven deception/safety issue.
- `She initiated everything; I was only responding.`
- `I stopped months ago` told to a pastor/mentor while secret conduct continued.
- `My parents forbid church/friends because they hate me` when the actual restriction concerned one unsafe person or activity.
- a public testimony falsely representing freedom from a sin while the hidden pattern remained active.

### Locked center

> **CONFESSING TO PARENTS ≠ AUTOMATICALLY REPAIRING FALSEHOOD TOLD TO THIRD PARTIES.**

> **FALSE NARRATIVE REPAIR ≠ PUBLIC SEXUAL CONFESSION.**

> **CORRECTION SHOULD REACH THE PEOPLE MATERIALLY MISLED — NOT AN AUDIENCE CREATED FOR HUMILIATION.**

> **CORRECT THE FALSE PART WITHOUT ERASING TRUE PARENTAL WRONG.**

> **REPAIRING A LIE ≠ PERFORMING FAMILY IMAGE MANAGEMENT.**

> **A TRUE ABUSE/SAFETY REPORT MUST NEVER BE RETRACTED MERELY TO RESTORE FAMILY REPUTATION.**

# 1. Ephesians 4:25 — putting away falsehood requires truthful dealing with the neighbor deceived

Paul's command is not merely:

> `Stop telling new lies internally.`

It is:

- put away falsehood;
- speak truth with one's neighbor;
- because believers are members one of another.

Gill treats lying broadly as falsehood in words and dealings and emphasizes truth toward one's neighbor.

Source:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/ephesians/4.htm

### Project implication

If a materially false representation is still governing another person's judgment, merely telling the parents the truth may leave the external lie functionally alive.

### Example

Teen told a youth leader:

> `My parents fabricated the whole pornography issue.`

Later admits to parents that the issue was real.

If the youth leader continues believing the false accusation, there is an unresolved truth/repair question.

### Locked

> **REPENTANCE FROM LYING NORMALLY MOVES TOWARD THE PEOPLE WHO WERE ACTUALLY DECEIVED WHEN THE DECEPTION STILL MATTERS.**

Evidence class: `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE → CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS`.

# 2. Exodus 20:16 — false witness can damage another person's good name

The ninth commandment is not limited to courtroom perjury in Christian moral theology; it governs false witness against the neighbor.

Gill explicitly expands the moral scope to false accusations, slander, backbiting and reports injurious to another's good name.

Source:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/exodus/20.htm

### High-value application

A child can commit real false witness against parents while protecting his own secret life.

Parents can commit the same wrong against a child.

### Locked pair

`CHILD FALSE ACCUSATION ≠ PARENTAL GUILT`.

`PARENTAL FALSE ACCUSATION ≠ CHILD INNOCENCE OF OTHER ESTABLISHED SIN`.

Each false witness must be corrected on its own facts.

# 3. Proverbs 18:17 — a persuasive first narrative may recruit a coalition before facts are known

The first account can appear right until another side is heard.

Existing `179` owns the third-party duty:

- do not choose parent or child as a moral party before facts;
- mediate before coalition where safe;
- do not let sympathy become false witness.

### New repair question

What if the coalition was already formed because one party knowingly supplied a misleading story?

Then repentance may require more than privately conceding:

> `I exaggerated.`

It may require helping the people who were misled stop acting on the false narrative.

### Locked

`FALSE COALITION BUILT BY FALSE STORY → REPAIR MAY REQUIRE NARRATIVE CORRECTION`.

This is a `CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS / PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`, not a universal formula.

# 4. Distinguish four kinds of distorted narrative

## A. Direct factual lie

> `Dad hit me.`

when he did not.

If materially circulated, direct correction is ordinarily required.

## B. Material omission that creates a false overall impression

> `They took my phone because they are controlling.`

while knowingly omitting repeated secret accounts/sexting that caused the action.

Not every omission is lying.

But a strategically curated account can become deception when it is intended to create a materially false moral impression.

## C. Exaggeration / loaded category

> `They never let me speak to anyone.`

when restriction concerned one specific dangerous contact.

Correction should target the exaggeration.

## D. Mixed true and false complaint

> Parent did speak harshly/sinfully, **and** child falsely adds that the parent fabricated the underlying misconduct.

Repentance must not collapse the case into:

> `Everything I said about my parents was false.`

if some parental wrong was real.

### P0

> **MIXED CASES REQUIRE MIXED REPAIR: RETRACT THE FALSEHOOD; PRESERVE THE TRUE FACT.**

# 5. Correction should track the real audience of the lie

Use `266` circle-of-harm / circle-of-repair heuristic.

### Lie told to one friend

Ordinarily correct that friend, not the church.

### Lie told to pastor/elder that changed pastoral judgment

The pastor/elder may need correction.

### Lie told to siblings that damaged a parent or another sibling

The siblings who received the lie may need correction.

### False public post

A public correction may be appropriate because the falsehood was public.

### Public Christian testimony materially false

The audience may need enough correction to remove the false impression.

### Locked

`CORRECTIVE SCOPE SHOULD NOT BE NARROWER THAN THE MATERIAL FALSEHOOD REMAINS ACTIVE`.

And:

`CORRECTIVE SCOPE SHOULD NOT AUTOMATICALLY BE BROADER THAN THE PEOPLE ACTUALLY MISLED`.

Evidence class: `PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`.

# 6. Public correction need not contain the sexual details

Suppose a teenager publicly said:

> `I was falsely accused by my parents and never had this problem.`

A correction may say:

> `I publicly denied conduct that was real and blamed my parents unfairly. That denial was false.`

It need not contain:

- explicit sexual acts;
- exact images;
- graphic chat details;
- names of uninvolved minors;
- every account.

### Locked

`PUBLIC FALSEHOOD MAY REQUIRE PUBLIC CORRECTION`.

`PUBLIC CORRECTION ≠ PUBLIC SEXUAL INVENTORY`.

Use `165`, `167`, `266`.

# 7. Correcting a false narrative is restitution-like repair, not payment for forgiveness

The project already uses Zacchaeus as a guarded restitution pattern:

- repentance changes the person's relation to actual wrongs;
- repair can be concrete;
- repair does not purchase divine pardon.

Where a lie damaged another person's reputation or relationship, narrative correction can function as one form of repair.

### P0

`RETRACTION/CORRECTION = FRUIT/DUTY WHERE NEEDED`.

Not:

`RETRACTION EARNS FORGIVENESS`.

Not:

`PUBLIC HUMILIATION PROVES REPENTANCE`.

# 8. Parents must not demand a reputation-restoration performance

A parent wounded by false allegations may want the child to:

- post a public praise statement;
- tell the whole church `my parents were right in everything`;
- apologize to every relative whether or not they heard the lie;
- publish private sexual details so people understand why the parent acted strongly.

That can exceed truth/repair and become image management.

### Locked

> **THE PARENT IS OWED CORRECTION OF THE FALSEHOOD, NOT A MANUFACTURED PUBLIC TESTIMONY OF PARENTAL PERFECTION.**

`GOOD NAME ≠ FALSE NAME` applies to parents too.

Use `253`.

# 9. Parent sin must remain named where real

Suppose the true pattern is:

- child hid pornography and lied;
- father discovered it;
- father then screamed degrading sexual insults and publicly humiliated the child;
- child told a friend `Dad humiliated me` — true;
- child also said `Dad invented the pornography allegation` — false.

Correct repair is **not**:

> `Tell the friend Dad did nothing wrong.`

It is something like:

> `I told you the underlying allegation was invented. That was false. My father did discover real conduct. I still believe the way he humiliated me was wrong.`

### P0

`CHILD REPENTANCE ≠ PARENTAL EXONERATION`.

`PARENTAL REPENTANCE ≠ CHILD EXONERATION`.

Truth can indict both parties in different respects.

# 10. Never force retraction of a true abuse/safeguarding disclosure

This is a critical mirror guard.

Parents/churches may call a disclosure:

> `false narrative`

because it threatens family reputation.

Where there is a credible abuse/coercion/safeguarding claim:

- do not pressure the child to retract it to prove repentance;
- do not treat family reconciliation as proof the report was false;
- do not require confrontation with alleged abuser as a precondition for being heard;
- use competent safeguarding process.

### Locked

`TRUE DISCLOSURE ≠ SLANDER BECAUSE IT IS PAINFUL`.

`ALLEGATION ≠ PROVED FACT`.

`PRESSURED RETRACTION ≠ PROOF THE ORIGINAL REPORT WAS FALSE`.

Exact legal/safeguarding process is jurisdiction-dependent.

# 11. What if the child told different versions to different people?

This is common in a double-life system.

Example:

- parents receive version A;
- pastor receives version B;
- girlfriend/boyfriend receives version C;
- peers receive version D.

### Repentance task

Do not demand one massive joint confession meeting by default.

Instead identify:

- which material falsehood each person received;
- which relationship/decision remains affected;
- what correction each person actually needs.

### Locked

> **NARRATIVE CONSISTENCY SHOULD BE RESTORED BY TRUTH, NOT BY MAXIMUM AUDIENCE SIZE.**

This keeps correction proportionate.

# 12. Third parties who propagated the false story acquire their own truth duty

Suppose a friend, relative or church adult hears the child's allegation and repeats it as fact to others without adequate basis.

Later the allegation is materially corrected.

The third party cannot simply say:

> `Well, I only repeated what I was told.`

Depending on what was said/done, they may need to:

- stop repeating it;
- correct people they themselves misled;
- apologize for overclaiming certainty;
- repair damaged relationships where possible.

### P0

`ORIGINAL LIAR'S REPENTANCE ≠ AUTOMATIC REPAIR OF SECONDARY GOSSIP`.

`SECONDARY GOSSIPER'S DUTY ≠ ORIGINAL OFFENDER'S DUTY`.

Each owns his/her own speech.

Evidence class: `CANONICAL SYNTHESIS` from ninth-commandment/Eph 4 truth duties.

# 13. Pastor/counselor records may need correction without public disclosure

Suppose a pastor's counseling judgment was built on a false account.

The church may need an accurate correction so that it stops:

- accusing parents wrongly;
- giving counsel based on false facts;
- allowing unsafe access;
- treating a person as victim/offender incorrectly.

That does not automatically require a congregational announcement.

### Locked

`CORRECT THE DECISION-MAKER WHO WAS MATERIALLY MISLED`.

`DO NOT CREATE A PUBLIC AUDIENCE MERELY BECAUSE A PRIVATE COUNSELING RECORD WAS WRONG`.

Use `282` jurisdiction/evidence guard.

# 14. Romantic coalition and secret-courtship cases

A teenager/young adult may tell a romantic partner:

> `My parents oppose us only because they are legalistic/control freaks.`

while hiding:

- deception;
- sexual boundary violations;
- unsafe conduct;
- prior agreements;
- material parent concerns.

If the partner's loyalty/action is built on that false narrative, repentance may require correcting it.

### Guard

This does not mean the parents' judgment is automatically correct.

The partner should receive **the material truth**, not a command to adopt the parents' conclusion.

### Locked

`CORRECT FACTS ≠ DEMAND THIRD PARTY TAKE PARENTS' SIDE`.

Use `179`, courtship/family-authority controls.

# 15. Sibling coalitions need careful repair

A child may recruit a sibling by saying:

- parents are lying;
- another sibling `snitched` maliciously;
- rules are arbitrary;
- evidence was fabricated.

If false, the recruited sibling may now distrust parents or retaliate against the witness sibling.

Repair may need:

- correcting the false factual claim;
- correcting blame toward the witness sibling;
- stopping retaliation;
- preserving confidential details not needed by siblings.

### P0

`WHOLE-FAMILY TRUTH ≠ WHOLE-FAMILY SEXUAL DETAIL`.

Use `269`, `266`.

# 16. “I was venting” does not erase factual responsibility

Emotional venting can include hyperbole.

Not every frustrated sentence requires a formal retraction campaign.

But:

> `I was just venting`

cannot excuse a material false accusation that predictably caused others to act against the person accused.

### Materiality test

Ask:

- Was a factual claim made?
- Was it known to be false/materially misleading?
- Did another person's judgment/action change because of it?
- Is the false impression still active?

If yes, repair burden rises.

Evidence class: `PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`.

---

# 17. Correcting the record after restoration should not become perpetual self-branding

Once the materially false story has been corrected to the relevant people, parents/church should not require the child to repeat indefinitely:

> `I was a liar; my parents were right.`

at every new relationship or church setting.

Past relevant history may need disclosure in specific trust/safeguarding/office contexts.

But:

`CORRECTED PAST LIE ≠ PERMANENT PUBLIC IDENTITY`.

Use `130`, `266`, `283`.

---

# 18. A practical repair sequence

Research-only prudential sequence:

1. identify the material false statement/impression;
2. identify who actually received and still relies on it;
3. separate false statement from any true grievance;
4. correct the false part directly and plainly;
5. avoid graphic detail beyond what correction requires;
6. make additional restitution where reputation/property/relationship was harmed;
7. stop recruiting/using the old coalition;
8. allow third parties to reassess independently;
9. do not demand praise of parents or instant relational closeness;
10. close the correction circle when the material lie is no longer active.

### Locked

`REPAIR THE LIE; DO NOT CREATE A NEW SPECTACLE`.

# 20. Product overclaim kill-switch

Reject or rewrite:

- `If a child lied to parents, he must confess publicly to church.`
- `Repentance requires telling everyone everything.`
- `If parents were falsely accused, the child must publicly praise them.`
- `Any criticism of parents after the child's sin is slander.`
- `A mixed complaint must be wholly retracted if one part was false.`
- `An abuse report that harms family reputation is dishonoring parents.`
- `Once the child admits lying, everyone else who repeated it is automatically cleared.`
- `Public correction must include explicit details so people understand.`
- `A romantic partner must simply accept parents' interpretation after correction.`
- `A corrected lie remains the child's permanent public identity.`

Preserve:

> **When deception recruited other people into a false story, repentance may require going back to those people and correcting the material falsehood. But correction serves truth and repair, not humiliation or image management: retract what was false, preserve what was true, and tell no wider circle than the actual harm requires.**

# 22. Final locked synthesis

> **Двойная жизнь иногда создаёт не одну ложь, а сеть ложных версий: родителям говорится одно, пастору другое, друзьям третье. Тогда настоящее исправление может требовать не только признаться дома, но и вернуть правду туда, где ложь реально изменила чужое суждение. При этом исправляется именно ложь, а не переписывается вся история в пользу родителей: если родитель тоже согрешил, это остаётся правдой; если был реальный safeguarding-сигнал, его нельзя отозвать ради семейной репутации. Круг исправления должен соответствовать кругу реального обмана — и не превращаться в публичную сексуальную исповедь.**
