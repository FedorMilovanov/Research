# Когда человек меняется: борьба, покаяние, восстановление и дополнительная помощь

**Статус:** RESEARCH / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Дата:** 2026-09-07

---

## 1. Нет одной кнопки «перебороть»

Будущая статья не должна обещать:

- «просто сильнее захотеть»;
- «просто поставить фильтр»;
- «просто исповедаться один раз»;
- «просто молиться больше»;
- «просто сходить к психологу».

Изменение обычно требует сочетания уровней:

`truth / repentance`  
`+ reduced opportunity`  
`+ replacement habits`  
`+ relational disclosure`  
`+ accountability/support`  
`+ meaningful positive life`  
`+ clinical help when pattern is compulsive/severe`.

For Christian theology, none of these techniques substitutes regeneration/sanctifying grace.

## 2. Why environment change is not “weak self-control”

Modern self-control literature increasingly emphasizes strategic environment navigation rather than heroic late-stage inhibition.

Sources:
https://compass.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/spc3.12952
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01231/full

### Application
If pornography access repeatedly follows:

`alone + night + phone + bedroom + specific app/browser`,

then changing the environment can be rational self-government:

- phone outside bedroom;
- no private browser;
- remove app/account;
- shared-device use;
- avoid known trigger context;
- contact accountability person before acting.

This fits Matthew 5 / Romans 13 “no provision” logic better than glorifying maximum temptation exposure.

## 3. Why replacement matters

If old behavior serves functions such as:

- boredom escape;
- loneliness relief;
- stress regulation;
- status;
- sexual fantasy;
- emotional anesthesia;

then mere subtraction leaves those functions unmet.

Replacement does not mean finding an equally stimulating “clean dopamine hit.”

It means rebuilding ordinary goods:

- sleep;
- exercise;
- work/study competence;
- service;
- embodied friendships;
- family connection;
- church belonging;
- prayer/Scripture;
- creative skill;
- future-oriented goals.

## 4. What empirical treatment evidence says about PPU

2025 comprehensive meta-analysis:
- 20 studies;
- 2,021 participants;
- psychotherapy groups improved more than controls on PPU, use frequency/duration and sexual compulsivity;
- most interventions CBT/ACT-related;
- evidence is promising but field remains limited.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40126561/

2023 systematic review found only 28 treatment studies / about 500 participants and generally low/very-low evidence quality.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37880509/

2026 CBT scoping review found common components:
- emotion regulation;
- cognitive restructuring;
- craving management;
- relapse prevention;
- no included study reported complete abstinence;
- self-guided interventions had substantial dropout.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42044011/

### Guard
Most treatment evidence is adult/clinical, not adolescent Christian-family intervention evidence.

## 5. When professional help should move higher on the list

Possible triggers for qualified mental-health/medical assessment:

- persistent impaired control despite repeated serious attempts;
- major functional impairment;
- depression/anxiety/self-harm;
- trauma/abuse history;
- compulsive sexual behavior involving risk to others;
- escalating illegal/exploitative content;
- severe sleep/academic collapse;
- suicidal thinking after sextortion/exposure;
- inability to distinguish voluntary behavior from coercion/abuse.

### Critical distinction
Pastoral care and clinical care are not competitors.

A therapist cannot pronounce forgiveness/regeneration; a pastor/parent should not pretend to diagnose/treat a severe psychiatric or trauma condition without competence.

## 6. Being caught is not repentance

Possible reactions after discovery:

- panic;
- shame;
- fear of losing phone;
- fear of reputation loss;
- sadness at hurting parents;
- genuine hatred of sin;
- strategic compliance;
- partial truth;
- blame-shifting.

2 Cor 7:8–11 is central theological control: godly grief produces repentance with observable earnest moral movement; worldly grief can remain self-centered.

### Future discernment grid
Do not demand perfection, but watch trajectory:

- more truth or new concealment?
- accepts responsibility or only blames surveillance?
- voluntarily removes access or negotiates for loopholes?
- seeks help before next crisis?
- repairs harm?
- stops defending sin morally?
- accepts proportionate consequences?
- grows in openness over time?

No single item infallibly proves regeneration.

---

## 7. Relapse does not automatically mean repentance was fake

A person can genuinely repent and still battle recurring temptation/habit.

But repeated relapse plus:

- planned concealment;
- doctrinal defense;
- refusal of help;
- manipulation;
- creation of new secret channels;

is a more serious pattern than relapse accompanied by immediate voluntary confession and renewed concrete measures.

### Distinction
`struggle` ≠ `settled peace with sin`.

---

## 8. Why voluntary costly action matters

Evidence of seriousness can include willingness to surrender something valued:

- smartphone privacy;
- app/account;
- friendship/context;
- money/access;
- leadership role;
- social status;
- dating relationship;

not as penance earning forgiveness but as practical mortification/fruit.

Matthew 5’s radical amputation imagery makes “costly change” a legitimate category.

---

## 9. Accountability should mature from external to internalized

Early stage:
- parents/mentor impose structure.

Middle stage:
- adolescent cooperates and reports.

Mature stage:
- person initiates disclosure;
- identifies triggers;
- chooses boundaries without being forced;
- asks for help before fall.

This transition is important because perpetual external policing cannot substitute self-government or sanctification.

---

## 10. Recovery is identity reconstruction, but be careful

2026 qualitative PPU recovery study describes accountability, honesty, group “mirror effect,” hope and identity transformation.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42356013/

Useful pastoral idea:
> recovery is not only “days without porn”; it is learning to become a person who no longer needs secrecy to maintain his life.

Guard: small qualitative sample; not universal treatment model.

---

## 11. What parents/church should measure instead of “perfect behavior”

Better questions:

- Is truth increasing?
- Is self-deception decreasing?
- Is access being voluntarily surrendered where necessary?
- Is person seeking godly company?
- Is there growing ability to endure boredom/distress without escape?
- Is restitution/protection of others happening?
- Is doctrine being bent to defend behavior, or behavior being brought under doctrine?
- Is there sustained fruit over time?

> Самоконтроль часто начинается не у открытого сайта, а на несколько часов раньше — когда человек решает, будет ли он ночью один с тем устройством, которое уже много раз становилось его дверью ко греху.

> Покаяние не покупает доверие одной слезой. Но оно начинает строить его правдой, которую человек говорит даже тогда, когда ему выгоднее было бы солгать.

All are pastoral/theological rhetoric anchored in evidence, not measurement claims.
