# Childhood Religious Performance, False Assurance, Temporary Faith, and Premature Certification

**Status:** SCRIPTURE-FIRST / 1689 / SPURGEON-PURITAN PASTORAL CONTROL / RESEARCH ONLY / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Depends on:** `12`, `23`, `43`, `53`, `61`, `108`, `116`, `150–160`.

---

# 0. The pastoral danger

A church-raised child may accumulate many visible religious markers:

- early prayer;
- Bible knowledge;
- catechism answers;
- emotional response at camp/sermon;
- request for baptism;
- choir participation;
- testimony language;
- outward obedience;
- regular small-group attendance.

Parents/leaders may then convert those markers into a durable certificate:

> “He is definitely converted; we settled that years ago.”

Later, even when the child lives in serious defended sin, adults may preserve the old certificate because questioning it feels frightening or disloyal.

The opposite error is equally dangerous:

> “Any later serious sin proves the childhood profession was certainly false.”

### Governing thesis

> **Christian assurance must not be built on one childhood event detached from present faith, love, conscience, and walk; yet serious present sin does not authorize infallible reconstruction of the person's entire past.**

---

# 1. 1689 14.3 — temporary faith can resemble real faith without sharing its saving nature

Primary:

- https://www.the1689confession.com/chapter-14
- https://the1689.org/chapters/14

1689 14.3 explicitly distinguishes saving faith, even when weak, from the faith/common grace of **temporary believers**.

### Project consequence

A person may have:

- real religious impressions;
- doctrinal belief at some level;
- temporary zeal;
- moral reform;
- affection for church,

without possessing saving faith.

### Important

This protects against:

> “But he seemed so sincere at 11, therefore his current life cannot seriously call the profession into question.”

Sincerity of emotion at one moment is not infallible evidence of regeneration.

---

# 2. 1689 18.1 — unregenerate people can deceive themselves with false hope

Primary:

- 1689 ch. 18.

The confession explicitly says temporary believers and other unregenerate people may **vainly deceive themselves with false hopes and carnal presumptions** of being in God's favor/state of salvation.

It then contrasts this with true believers who:

- believe in Christ;
- love him sincerely;
- endeavor to walk in good conscience.

### Strong project use

> **False assurance is a recognized theological category, not merely parental pessimism.**

### Guard

Do not turn “endeavoring to walk” into sinless perfection.

Chs. 13 and 17 explicitly preserve remaining sin, warfare and grievous falls.

---

# 3. 1689 14.2 — saving faith is Christward, not merely informational

Saving faith includes believing God's Word, but its principal acts relate directly to Christ:

- accepting;
- receiving;
- resting on him alone for justification, sanctification and eternal life.

### Consequence

The child who knows:

- Calvinism;
- gospel vocabulary;
- Bible facts;
- doctrinal answers,

is not therefore proven regenerate.

### Strong line

> **Knowing the map of salvation is not the same thing as resting on Christ.**

---

# 4. Matthew 7:21–23 — religious activity can coexist with false assurance

The passage's immediate examples are extraordinary religious claims/works, not children's choir.

But the principle is severe:

> external Christian activity and even confident eschatological profession do not by themselves guarantee that Christ knows the professor savingly.

### Guard

Do not use Matt. 7 to terrify every tender child who lacks emotional certainty.

The point is not:

> “Never have assurance.”

The point is:

> **do not build assurance on religious performance while disregarding Christ's lordship.**

---

# 5. 1 John — assurance includes present-pattern evidence

1689 18 cites 1 John repeatedly.

1 John connects assurance with present realities such as:

- confession of Christ;
- love;
- obedience;
- walking in light;
- not making peace with sin.

### Important guard

1 John does not teach sinless perfection:

- 1 John 1:8–10 acknowledges ongoing sin/confession;
- believers need Christ the Advocate (2:1–2).

### Project distinction

> **Assurance is not maintained by pretending current rebellion is irrelevant; nor is it destroyed by every fall where confession/warfare remain.**

---

# 6. Spurgeon — do not sin against the child by manufacturing conversion certainty

Primary Spurgeon control:

- https://www.spurgeon.org/sermons/do-not-sin-against-the-child

Spurgeon's child-conversion preaching holds together:

- children really can be converted;
- their faith/repentance should be real, not a lower species;
- adult expectations should not demand adult maturity;
- fear, tears or religious excitement should not be hastily certified as conversion.

### Project use

Churches can sin against children in two directions:

## Disbelief

> “Children cannot really believe yet.”

## Premature certification

> “The child cried/prayed/asked for baptism, therefore never question his state again.”

Both are pastoral failures.

---

# 7. The “sinner's prayer” / childhood moment must not become a magic amulet

The project should avoid attacking prayer itself.

A child may genuinely call on Christ in prayer.

The danger is retrospective use:

> “You prayed at age 8, so no present evidence can ever challenge our conclusion.”

That is not biblical assurance.

### Better formulation

> **A past profession matters; present fruit matters too. The answer is not to erase the past or ignore the present.**

---

# 8. Choir and ministry can unintentionally certify a child

A child repeatedly placed in:

- choir;
- testimony slots;
- Scripture-reading platform;
- youth leadership-like functions,

may infer:

> “The church regards me as spiritually fine.”

Parents may infer the same.

### Guard

Formal choir participation is not church membership and need not require proven regeneration.

But where known serious rebellion exists, continued representative placement may communicate a false reassurance the church never intended.

### Strong line

> **A platform can preach a message even when no one says a word about the child's spiritual state.**

---

# 9. Baptism must not be used as parental anxiety medication

Because parents fear losing the child, they may rush toward baptism as a stabilizing marker:

> “Once baptized, maybe this will become real.”

1689 29.2 reverses the logic:

- baptism follows actual profession of repentance/faith/obedience;
- it is not a device for manufacturing the profession.

### Rule

> **Do not use an ordinance to create the reality the ordinance is meant to confess.**

---

# 10. Nor should churches delay baptism until impossible certainty

The opposite error:

> “We cannot see regeneration infallibly, therefore no teenager can ever be baptized.”

Baptist ecclesiology operates by **credible profession**, not omniscience.

The church makes a fallible judgment.

### Guard

Do not require:

- adult-level theological vocabulary;
- decades of fruit;
- freedom from adolescent immaturity.

Require a credible child/teen-appropriate profession consistent with the church's biblical standards.

---

# 11. What later rebellion should do to old assurance

If a youth now shows:

- settled serious sin;
- repeated deception;
- contempt for correction;
- repudiation of Christ's authority;
- corrupting others,

parents/church should not simply repeat:

> “But remember your profession at 12.”

They should say:

> “Your present life gives us serious reason not to reassure you as though all is well. We do not know God's final decree; we call you now to repentance and faith.”

### This is not Arminian loss-of-salvation doctrine

It is visible-profession discernment.

1689 perseverance remains intact:

- the truly regenerate persevere;
- visible assurance/profession can be clouded or contradicted;
- temporary believers exist.

---

# 12. What later repentance should NOT do

If the youth later turns credibly, do not say:

> “Therefore his childhood profession was certainly genuine after all.”

Maybe.

Maybe not.

God may have:

- preserved a true believer through grievous fall;
- genuinely converted a previous temporary/false professor later.

Humans may not be able to reconstruct the exact moment.

### Strong line

> **The pastoral priority is not always to solve the archaeology of conversion, but to deal truthfully with the person's present relation to Christ.**

---

# 13. Parent self-examination

Parents should ask:

- Did we reward Christian language more than honesty?
- Did our child learn that doubts were dangerous to admit?
- Did we equate church activity with regeneration?
- Did we use an old profession to avoid confronting present sin?
- Did family reputation make us need the child to “be saved” visibly?
- Did we pressure baptism?
- Did we ignore contradictory fruit because it frightened us?

### Guard

Self-examination is not automatic self-condemnation.

A child's false profession does not prove parental malpractice.

---

# 14. Church self-examination

Churches should ask:

- Do we pressure children toward testimonies before they understand them?
- Do choir/youth roles create implicit spiritual certification?
- Do we know how to say “we are not currently able to affirm this profession” without declaring reprobation?
- Do we distinguish tender struggling believers from settled impenitence?
- Are children allowed to ask honest questions?
- Does baptism preparation test both knowledge and professed repentance/faith rather than performance skill?

# 15. Article-ready formulations

> **Ребёнок может выучить язык спасения раньше, чем спасение станет его собственной реальностью.**

> **Старая молитва не должна становиться амулетом против всех нынешних предупреждений Писания.**

> **Церковь не должна требовать от ребёнка взрослой зрелости, но она также не должна производить взрослую уверенность из детской религиозной эмоции.**

> **Прошлое исповедание важно; настоящее отношение к Христу тоже важно.**

> **Иногда самый честный пастырский ответ — не “ты точно спасён” и не “ты точно никогда не был спасён”, а: “твоя нынешняя жизнь не позволяет нам успокаивать тебя; покайся и держись Христа”.**

---

# 16. Final locked synthesis

> **ДВР может научиться быть религиозно убедительным задолго до того, как его сердце станет новым. Поэтому родители и церковь не должны строить пожизненную уверенность на одном детском эпизоде, крещении, хоре или старом свидетельстве в отрыве от нынешней веры и жизни. Но и тяжёлое падение не даёт нам права переписать всю историю с безошибочной уверенностью. Библейская задача — не охранять старый сертификат и не выносить тайный приговор, а правдиво оценивать настоящее исповедание и звать ко Христу.**
