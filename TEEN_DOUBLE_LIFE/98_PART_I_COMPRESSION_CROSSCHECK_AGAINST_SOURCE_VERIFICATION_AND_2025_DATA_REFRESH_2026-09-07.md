# 6. DATA REFRESH — replace Pew 2024 with Pew 2025 where currentness matters

## Latest source

Pew Research Center, *Teens, Social Media and AI Chatbots 2025*:
- https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/

Survey:
- 1,458 U.S. teens ages 13–17;
- conducted Sept. 25–Oct. 9, 2025;
- probability-based KnowledgePanel recruitment through parents;
- weighted to U.S. teens 13–17 living with parents.

### Current verified facts

- 97% report using the internet daily;
- 40% report being online almost constantly;
- 36% report using at least one of five measured major social platforms almost constantly;
- roughly one in five report TikTok and YouTube use almost constantly;
- 64% report using AI chatbots, including about 3 in 10 daily.

### Comparison

Pew 2024 had:
- 96% daily internet use;
- 46% almost constantly online.

2025 therefore shows:
- daily use remains nearly universal;
- `almost constant` internet use dipped from 46% to 40%;
- still substantially above 24% in 2014–15.

### Publication recommendation

Use 2025 as primary current anchor.

Safe wording:

> **In Pew’s 2025 U.S. survey of 13–17-year-olds, 97% said they used the internet daily and 40% said they were online almost constantly.**

Do not retain:

> `nearly half are online almost constantly`

as the freshest 2025 wording unless explicitly saying `in the 2024 survey`.

### AI chatbot note

Do **not** introduce AI-chatbot statistics merely because they are new unless the article actually discusses AI-mediated companionship/sexualization. Freshness alone is not relevance.
