## 2. Household worship — locked controls from `219`

Preserve together:

- `UNREGENERATE ≠ RELIGIOUSLY DUTYLESS`;
- 1689 22.3: prayer is required of all men;
- 1689 22.6: God is worshipped in private families daily and public assemblies are not carelessly/wilfully neglected;
- 1689 16.7: unregenerate commanded works cannot please God without faith/right manner/right end, yet neglect is more sinful;
- Gill: prayer is a natural duty binding all men; family Scripture/prayer/singing/instruction are real duties;
- `HOUSEHOLD WORSHIP ORDER ≠ POWER TO MANUFACTURE SPIRITUAL WORSHIP`;
- `PRESENCE/HEARING ≠ PROFESSION ≠ SACRAMENTAL PRIVILEGE`;
- `FAMILY AUTHORITY ≠ CHURCH KEYS`;
- `CHRISTIAN HOUSEHOLD ≠ RELIGIOUSLY NEUTRAL HOTEL`;
- `HOUSEHOLD ORDER ≠ OWNERSHIP OF ADULT CONSCIENCE`;
- an unregenerate resident may be required to respect/be present for household worship in a status-sensitive way, but must not be commanded to lie about conversion/assurance;
- family worship must not be used as nightly ambush, humiliation, forced sexual disclosure or loyalty test to the parent;
- persistent rejection/disruption of Christian household worship may contribute to a cumulative co-residence judgment but is not itself a universal automatic eviction trigger.

### Critical public-writing distinction

Do not say:

> `An unbeliever should not pray/sing/hear until regenerated.`

Do not say:

> `Because prayer/worship is a duty, parents may require a false conversion testimony or church privilege.`

Locked synthesis:

> **External duty and appointed means remain real; acceptable spiritual worship requires what only God gives. Parents govern the household and use means; they do not regenerate.**

## 3. Church mediation — locked controls from `220`

Preserve together:

- `MEDIATE BEFORE COALITION`;
- `FIRST STORY HEARD ≠ FINAL FACT PATTERN`;
- Prov. 18:17 is a controlling wisdom text for disputed narratives;
- Matt. 18 gives real private/witness/church escalation in its proper scope; it is not a secrecy statute for crime/abuse;
- `MATTHEW 18 ≠ REPLACEMENT FOR CIVIL/SAFEGUARDING JURISDICTION`;
- 1689 ch. 26 gives real church discipline authority over church members/professing insiders;
- `CHURCH DISCIPLINE JURISDICTION TRACKS CHURCH RELATION, NOT BIOLOGICAL RELATION`;
- `NON-MEMBER ≠ OUTSIDE GOD'S MORAL LAW; NON-MEMBER ≠ UNDER MEMBER DISCIPLINE`;
- a pastor may counsel/admonish/mediate with an unbelieving adult child but may not pretend to excommunicate a non-member;
- if parent and child are members of the same church, both may be corrected/discipled;
- if in different churches, pastor-to-pastor cooperation can prevent two one-sided moral universes;
- `CONFIDENTIALITY ≠ ECCLESIASTICAL LICENSE FOR UNTESTED ACCUSATIONS`;
- pastoral help must not become a replacement household that enables defended sin;
- pastoral support must also resist actual parental abuse/forced marriage/violence/demands to sin;
- parental status gives no immunity from church correction;
- reconciliation may occur without immediate return to co-residence.

## 4. Combined jurisdiction matrix

Never collapse:

`GOD'S MORAL CLAIM`

`PARENTAL / HOUSEHOLD GOVERNMENT`

`ADULT PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY`

`CHURCH MEMBERSHIP GOVERNMENT`

`CIVIL / SAFEGUARDING JURISDICTION`.

A single event can touch several of these, but one authority does not automatically absorb the others.

Examples:

- family worship presence = primarily household + moral duty;
- profession/baptism/Table = church + personal profession;
- assault/abuse = moral + civil/safeguarding, not merely family/private church process;
- adult housing terms = household/property + civil law + moral stewardship;
- church discipline = church relation, not merely kinship.
