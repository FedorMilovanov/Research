# Adversarial claim-level evidence ladder — family government, adult child, daughter, marriage, household

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Mode:** RESEARCH-ONLY / adversarial synthesis / not publication approval  
**Series:** `TEEN_DOUBLE_LIFE` adult/family-government companions  
**Purpose:** prevent a strong conservative position from becoming stronger in wording than the biblical/confessional/historical evidence can actually carry.

---

## 0. Governing rule

The corpus now contains a very large amount of mutually reinforcing material. Volume itself is not authority.

Every future Product claim in the adult/family-government lane must be classified before publication.

### Evidence classes used in this audit

1. `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE` — an explicit biblical command, relation, prohibition, or clearly stated moral duty in its own context.
2. `CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS` — a conclusion requiring several biblical texts/relations to be held together; stronger than mere prudence, but not identical to one explicit sentence.
3. `CONFESSIONAL_EXPLICIT` — directly stated by 1689 LBCF or another confession when that confession is being described historically.
4. `CLASSICAL_EXEGESIS_STRONG` — substantial Calvin/Gill/Poole/Gouge/Baxter/Owen/etc. support for an interpretation/application; still subordinate to Scripture.
5. `HISTORICAL_PRACTICE_STRONG` — well-attested Reformed/Particular Baptist practice, not automatically a direct NT statute.
6. `CUMULATIVE_INFERENCE` — serious case from multiple texts/history/status relations, but not an undisputed explicit New Testament rule.
7. `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION` — a wisdom/stewardship judgment that may be very good but is not itself universally commanded in the exact form.
8. `CIVIL_OR_SAFEGUARDING_DEPENDENT` — application depends materially on current law, danger, capacity, reporting, tenancy, guardianship or safeguarding facts.
9. `HOLD` — evidence not yet sufficient for publication as fact/doctrine.

### Absolute drafting rule

> **Never promote a lower evidence class into a higher one merely because the conclusion fits the project's theological instincts.**

And the reverse:

> **Do not demote a direct biblical duty into optional advice merely because modern culture dislikes it.**

# 1. Adult children remain under the fifth commandment

### Verdict

`DIRECT_SCRIPTURE + CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS`.

The command to honour father and mother is not presented as a disposable childhood ethic. Mark 7 treats adult failure toward parents as a fifth-commandment violation. 1 Tim. 5:4 requires children/descendants capable of doing so to repay/care for family.

### Safe Product claim

> Adulthood does not abolish the duty to honour parents, gratitude, truthfulness, appropriate care and filial regard.

### Prohibited overclaim

> Therefore every command a parent gives a competent forty-year-old child carries the same obedience jurisdiction as it did when the child was twelve.

The first proposition does not entail the second.

Use `182`, `194`, `205`, `207`.

# 2. Adulthood changes parental government

### Verdict

`CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS + CLASSICAL_EXEGESIS_STRONG`.

Gill explicitly says parental power is less when children are grown while filial reverence/obligations remain. Marriage creates a new one-flesh household. Scripture also depicts competent adults bearing direct moral, vocational and household responsibility.

### Safe claim

> Biblical adulthood is a transition in jurisdiction and responsibility, not emancipation from every inherited obligation.

### Prohibited claims

- `18 = no parental claim whatsoever`;
- `unmarried = permanent minor`;
- `still son/daughter = unchanged childhood obedience in every domain`.

Civil age is legally meaningful but is not itself a biblical sacrament.

Use `194`, `202`, `205`, `207`.

# 3. A competent adult living in the parents' home is neither a hotel guest nor a minor child

### Verdict

`CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS + PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`, with some applications `CIVIL_OR_SAFEGUARDING_DEPENDENT`.

Parents remain stewards of their household; Scripture takes household order seriously. But residence does not recreate childhood jurisdiction over every adult decision or inner conviction.

### Safe claim

> Parents may establish lawful conditions for what occurs in their home and what they subsidize, especially where sexual conduct, substances, violence, theft, guests, security, younger siblings, household contribution and truthful Christian order are implicated.

### Prohibited claim

> Because you sleep under my roof, I possess God-given authority over every lawful adult decision you make outside the house.

Use `195`, `202`, `219`, `222`.

# 4. An unregenerate child/adult is not religiously dutyless

### Verdict

`CONFESSIONAL_EXPLICIT + CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS`.

1689 16.7 rejects the inference that duties become needless because an unregenerate person cannot perform them in saving faith. 1689 22 treats prayer/worship as real human duty and family/public worship as real obligations.

### Safe claim

> Unbelief does not create a morally neutral zone in which God may be ignored without guilt.

### Equal guard

`DUTY ≠ ABILITY TO MANUFACTURE FAITH`.

Parents cannot force regeneration, sincere profession, baptism, the Table or genuine spiritual worship.

Use `108`, `160`, `219`.

# 7. Scripture contains real daughter-specific paternal authority texts

### Verdict

`DIRECT_SCRIPTURE` inside their original Mosaic/narrative jurisdictions; current universal New Covenant extension = `CUMULATIVE_INFERENCE`.

# 8. Parental involvement/consent in marriage has strong Protestant and Baptist historical support

### Verdict

- Westminster/Second Helvetic/Reformed practice: `HISTORICAL_PRACTICE_STRONG` and in their own documents `CONFESSIONAL_EXPLICIT`;
- Gill: `CLASSICAL_EXEGESIS_STRONG + HISTORICAL_PRACTICE_STRONG` Particular Baptist evidence;
- Stennett: secondary/located supporting Baptist witness pending primary-level strengthening;
- 1689 itself: marital consent of the parties + marriage in the Lord = `CONFESSIONAL_EXPLICIT`; universal parental-permission procedure = not stated.

### Safe claim

> Strong parental consent/involvement in marriage is demonstrably present in historic Reformed theology and in mature eighteenth-century Particular Baptist practical theology; it is not merely a modern patriarchal invention or solely Presbyterian borrowing.

### Prohibited claims

- `1689 explicitly requires Westminster's exact adult-first-marriage parental-consent procedure`;
- `Keach/General Assembly definitely taught universal adult-daughter paternal veto`.

Those exact rules remain HOLDs.

Use `193`, `199–201`, `203`, `218`.

# 9. Parental consent is not forced marriage or indefinite arbitrary veto

### Verdict

`CLASSICAL_EXEGESIS_STRONG + HISTORICAL_PRACTICE_STRONG + CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS`.

Historic sources that demand serious parental involvement also restrict parental tyranny. Westminster forbids unreasonable refusal; Calvin/Poole/Baxter supply strong anti-tyranny limits; Gill requires the actual parties' voluntary consent.

### Safe claim

### Prohibited claim

Use `193`, `203`, `218`.

# 21. Product drafting protocol

Before writing any strong family-government sentence, ask:

1. **What exact relation?** minor child / resident adult / independent adult / unmarried daughter / engaged / married / church member / nonmember.
2. **What exact domain?** moral law / household conduct / money / marriage / vocation / church privilege / civil safety.
3. **What evidence class?** direct text, confession, historic interpretation, cumulative inference, prudence, law, HOLD.
4. **What authority is acting?** parent / husband / church / civil magistrate / employer / landlord / no formal authority.
5. **What changes if the authority is sinful or unreasonable?**
6. **What changes if the child is minor, dependent, newly departed or long-independent?**
7. **Does the wording preserve adult consent and forbid coercion into sin?**
8. **Are we transferring an OT/covenantal case without proving the transfer?**
9. **Are we confusing love with approval, money, co-residence, trust or role?**
10. **Are we calling a prudential conclusion 'the Bible plainly commands' when it does not?**

### Publication discipline

> **Strong theology does not require inflated certainty.**

The series should be unmistakably conservative where Scripture is unmistakable, historically informed where tradition is strong, and openly qualified where the application is cumulative or prudential.
