# Household Baptisms — Lydia, Jailer, Stephanas
## Evidence Limits and Household-Government Value

**Status:** RESEARCH / TARGETED EXEGESIS / NOT PUBLICATION
**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Depends on:** `273–281`

## 1. Why household texts are often overstated

Household baptism narratives are cumulative evidence for Christianity spreading through household structures. They are not direct demographic records of every household member.

Two opposite errors:

- `HOUSEHOLD -> THERE MUST HAVE BEEN INFANTS -> INFANTS WERE BAPTIZED`;
- `HOUSEHOLD -> TEXT MENTIONS HEARING/BELIEVING SOMEWHERE -> THERE COULD NOT HAVE BEEN ANY INFANTS`.

Both can exceed the narrative.

## 2. Lydia — Acts 16:14–15, 40

### Direct

- Lord opens Lydia’s heart to attend to Paul.
- Lydia and her household are baptized.
- Later Paul/Silas see the `brothers` at her house.

### Unknown

- marital status;
- whether she had biological children;
- whether any household member was an infant;
- exact identity of every baptized household member.

### Historical controls

Gill argues the household consisted of converted servants/attendants and notes no infant is established.

Calvin focuses less on demographic proof and more on the householder’s duty: Lydia consecrates/orders her family toward God; godly rulers of households should seek Christ’s rule in the home.

### Project verdict

`INFANT-BAPTISM PROOF = WEAK/UNPROVEN`.

`CHRISTIAN HOUSEHOLD GOVERNMENT PRINCIPLE = HIGH VALUE`.

## 3. Philippian jailer — Acts 16:31–34

### Direct

- salvation promise is announced to jailer and household in relation to faith;
- Word of the Lord is spoken to him and **all in his house**;
- he and all his are baptized;
- the household rejoices in connection with belief.

### Baptist strength

There is positive narrative linkage among hearing, faith and baptism across the household. This is more favorable to credobaptism than an argument from mere silence.

### Remaining guard

Do not claim Luke supplies an age census. The text’s theological focus is household reception of the gospel.

## 4. Stephanas — 1 Cor 1:16; 16:15–18

### Direct

- Paul baptized Stephanas’ household.
- household later described as firstfruits of Achaia and devoted to serving saints.

### Baptist strength

The later description positively associates the household with active Christian service, making it poor evidence for a baptism class defined independently of personal faith.

### Guard

It still does not say every person had exactly the same age/role at the baptismal moment.

## 5. What household texts do prove strongly for this series

### Christianity addresses households, not isolated consumers

The gospel’s entrance into a home creates consequences for:

- instruction;
- worship;
- hospitality;
- authority;
- moral order;
- relationships;
- children/dependents;
- the home’s relation to the church.

This is important against modern hyper-individualism:

> “Faith is Dad’s private choice; the children are religiously neutral until they independently opt in.”

The New Testament does not present conversion of a householder as having no implications for household life.

### Yet household implication ≠ household regeneration

No parent/master can regenerate another household member.

Therefore:

`HOUSEHOLD ORDER UNDER CHRIST` and `PERSONAL NEW BIRTH` must be held together.

## 6. Calvin transfer insight

Calvin’s Lydia application — household governed so as to give Christ place and exclude profanity — is pastorally transferable into 1689 theology without infant baptism.

This supplies a historical Reformed answer to:

> “Do not require Christian order from an unbelieving dependent.”

The correct distinction is not Christian order vs personal freedom; it is lawful household government vs counterfeit personal profession.

## 7. Public wording guard

Recommended:

> **Новозаветные “домашние крещения” показывают, насколько семейно-домашним было распространение Евангелия, но сами по себе не сообщают возраст каждого члена дома. В отдельных рассказах — особенно о темничном страже и доме Стефана — Лука и Павел связывают дом также со слышанием, верой и служением. Поэтому эти тексты разумнее использовать как часть совокупного спора о крещении, а не как фотографию младенца в купели или доказательство его отсутствия.**
