# 3. User’s controlling adult-child scenario — preserve exactly in substance

The difficult scenario is:

- child grew up in a believing home;
- there were discoveries/conversations concerning serious double life;
- hidden sexual/profane/deceptive life may continue;
- the child reaches approximately civil adulthood (example: 18);
- he/she **secretly leaves home**;
- parents may initially not know full facts;
- many counselors immediately say “let go,” “do not play God,” “stop controlling,” sometimes using the prodigal-son parable;
- user rejects a universal immediate no-contact formula;
- there may be a legitimate period of fact-finding, contact, meetings and admonition if the adult child remains willing to speak;
- sometimes ongoing contact may contribute to quick return/repentance;
- sometimes persistent deceit/defense shows that a different mode is needed;
- sometimes parents should stop subsidizing the far-country life and allow ordinary consequences they are not morally required to remove;
- no parent has authority to manufacture suffering as a conversion technique;
- no universal timeline exists;
- modern work/social media/phones make physical departure different from ancient complete geographic separation;
- blocking can happen and must be respected in ordinary safe adult situations; do not bypass using fake profiles absent safety cause;
- parents must distinguish love/contact from control/stalking;
- parents must distinguish non-enabling from abandonment;
- younger siblings may require protection from corrupting adult influence;
- return home may involve conditions;
- household mercy does not require the home to become infrastructure for sexual immorality, pornography distribution, drugs, violence, theft, corrupting minors, etc.

This scenario remains central to the **adult-companion portion** of the future project.

# 8. Module 72 — parental culpability vs adult-child guilt

File:

`72_PARENTAL_CULPABILITY_ADULT_CHILD_RESPONSIBILITY_ELI_SAMUEL_EZEKIEL18_CAIN_ISAIAH1_2026-09-07.md`

This is a major theological module.

## Eli

1 Sam. 2:29 / 3:13:

- adult priestly sons commit severe sacrilege/sexual sin;
- Eli knows;
- he has real overlapping jurisdiction: father/high priest/judge;
- verbal objection without effective use of retained authority is insufficient;
- God accuses him of honoring sons above Him.

Core:

> **A rebuke is not necessarily restraint. Eli spoke; God still charged him with culpable toleration.**

This strongly applies to:

- church/youth/choir access;
- ministry role;
- household resources;
- younger-child protection;
- actual institutional jurisdiction.

## Samuel

1 Sam. 8 records wicked adult sons but does **not** give Samuel the same explicit divine indictment as Eli.

Do not infer Samuel perfect parenting from silence.

But also do not infer:

`wicked adult child = parent necessarily guilty in same way as Eli`.

## Ezekiel 18

Personal moral responsibility:

- parent bears parent’s sin;
- son bears son’s sin.

This does not deny formative influence.

Core formula:

> **The parent bears guilt for the parent’s sin; the son bears guilt for the son’s sin. Their sins can interact causally without becoming the same moral act.**

## Cain

Perfect divine warning before murder does not mechanically produce obedience.

Therefore faithful admonition is a real means but does not possess regenerative power.

## Isaiah 1:2

God’s covenant father/son imagery includes real nurture and real rebellion; use analogically, never human-parent = God equivalence.

# 14. New master balance for adult-prodigal section

The adult-child companion/article must now hold all of these together:

### Parents do NOT own the adult child

- adulthood changes jurisdiction;
- no perpetual child-level surveillance/control;
- no coercive stalking of a safe competent adult.

### Parents are NOT morally irrelevant

- honor/family relation remains;
- truthful counsel can continue;
- prayer continues;
- doing good continues;
- relationship may continue;
- household/resources remain real stewardship.

### Parents may have real guilt

- neglect;
- hypocrisy;
- provoking;
- indulgence;
- enabling;
- failure to use actual entrusted jurisdiction.

### Parents do not bear the adult child’s personal guilt

- Ezek. 18 guard;
- adult child answers to God for own sexual/deceptive/rebellious actions.

### God alone regenerates

- means matter;
- warnings matter;
- consequences may matter;
- but no combination guarantees new heart.

### Relationship preservation is not ultimate

- child must not be honored above God;
- fear of loss can make parent permissive or controlling.

### Consequences are not vengeance

- do not finance sin;
- do not manufacture pain;
- never ignore acute danger to teach lesson.

### Return is not automatically repentance

- receive openings warmly;
- distinguish crisis need from confession/forsaking;
- do not require fake conversion;
- do not restore every sensitive trust merely because address changed.

### Protect third parties

- siblings;
- youth;
- sexual victims;
- church members.

A theology centered only on `parent + prodigal` can become unjust if Tamar/younger siblings disappear from the frame.
