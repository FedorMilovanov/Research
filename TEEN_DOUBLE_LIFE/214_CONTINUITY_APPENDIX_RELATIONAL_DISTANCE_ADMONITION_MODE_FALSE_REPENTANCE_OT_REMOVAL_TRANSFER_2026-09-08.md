## 3. Locked relational distinction

`RELATION ≠ VOLUNTARY CLOSENESS ≠ TRUST ≠ FELLOWSHIP ≠ ACCESS ≠ APPROVAL ≠ ROLE`.

A parent may continue:

- natural love;
- ordinary humanity;
- prayer;
- necessary care;
- willingness to hear truth;
- open door to repentance/reconciliation;

while reducing or withdrawing:

- ordinary intimate companionship;
- financial support that enables sin;
- use of parental property for sin;
- unsupervised access to vulnerable younger people;
- credibility-based privileges;
- church/ministry roles where qualifications are contradicted.

### P0

> **Do not make the sinner artificially miserable. Stop making defended sin artificially comfortable.**

This is not total family shunning.

Owen/Edwards controls remain mandatory: even ecclesial discipline does not erase natural parent-child duties.

## 4. Locked admonition rule

`MODE CHANGE, NOT TRUTH CHANGE`.

Scripture requires both:

- patient correction;
- avoidance of foolish quarrels.

Therefore:

> **Faithfulness is not measured by how many times or how many hours a parent can repeat the same argument.**

Mode may change from:

`long/frequent confrontation`

to

`short, clear standing testimony + openness to real questions + practical consequences + less re-litigation`.

### Titus 3 guard

Do not turn Titus 3:10 into:

> “warn an adult child twice, then cut off contact.”

The text addresses ecclesial handling of a divisive/factious person under church authority.

### Proverbs 26 guard

There is a time to answer folly and a time not to answer it in its own manner. Wisdom is situational, not a fixed warning count.

## 5. Locked repentance/credibility rule

`HOPE IN GOD ≠ CONFIDENCE IN THE PRESENT REPENTANCE CLAIM`.

`REPENTANCE FRUIT ≠ JUSTIFICATION-BY-PROBATION`.

Parents may simultaneously hold:

- very high theological hope that God can give repentance;
- low present confidence in a child's promise because of a history of deliberate deception.

This is not unbelief.

### Repentance is not authenticated merely by

- tears;
- shame over discovery;
- fear of losing phone/home/relationship;
- saying “I sinned”;
- quoting grace/forgiveness texts;
- short-term compliance under intense monitoring;
- one act resembling restitution.

### Credibility ordinarily strengthens through

- voluntary truth not yet forced by evidence;
- reduction of minimization/blame-shifting;
- forsaking means/opportunities of sin;
- concern for people harmed;
- acceptance of proportionate consequences;
- truthfulness over time;
- help-seeking before the next fall;
- costly structural change consistent with the professed repentance.

### Relapse guard

`RELAPSE ≠ AUTOMATIC PROOF OF FALSE REPENTANCE`.

The project must distinguish:

`WAR WITH SIN`

from

`PEACE / DEFENSE / PLANNED CONCEALMENT OF SIN`.

The person's relation to truth can be more diagnostic than a simple fall count.

## 6. Saul / Judas / Pharaoh case controls

Use these as narrative guards, never as labels pasted onto a living child.

### Saul

“I have sinned” can coexist with blame displacement, judgment avoidance, and reputation preservation.

### Judas

Remorse, admission, and returning money do not by themselves establish evangelical repentance toward Christ.

### Pharaoh

Concession under pressure can reverse when pressure lifts.

### P0

None of these proves:

> “a child who first confesses after consequences begins can never genuinely repent.”

Painful providence can be one of God's means. What follows matters.

## 7. OT removal transfer — mandatory actor/jurisdiction audit

Before using any OT removal text ask:

1. Who acts?
2. By what authority?
3. What covenant/institution is involved?
4. What exact offense?
5. What NT text controls transfer?

### Locked case results

#### Genesis 21 — Hagar/Ishmael

- specific Abrahamic covenant/inheritance revelation;
- explicit NT typological use in Galatians 4;
- **not** an ordinary household eviction rule.

#### Deuteronomy 21 — rebellious son

- rebellion is extremely serious;
- parents do not execute punishment privately;
- case goes to public elders/civil process;
- Mosaic civil sanction does not transfer to Christian parents;
- general equity supports seriousness + proper jurisdiction.

#### Ezra 10

- post-exilic covenant-Israel reform of prohibited marriages;
- NT 1 Corinthians 7 explicitly gives a different rule for an existing believer/unbeliever marriage;
- therefore **not** a modern family-expulsion or mixed-marriage template.

#### Nehemiah 13

- governor/covenant/temple-priestly reform;
- supports integrity of entrusted sacred/community access;
- not private household eviction legislation.

#### Absalom

- royal/family/civil jurisdictions overlap;
- narrative is not an ideal disciplinary manual;
- nevertheless vividly shows `PHYSICAL RETURN ≠ RECONCILIATION` and `ACCESS ≠ HEART RESTORATION`.

#### National exile

- divine covenant-national judgment;
- analogy can show warning + consequence + restoration;
- parents possess no exile/covenant-curse authority.

## 8. Modern separate-residence argument — required structure

A Christian parent should ordinarily justify requiring a competent adult to live elsewhere through a cumulative present-duty argument such as:

`ADULT STATUS`
+
`LAWFUL HOUSEHOLD ORDER`
+
`PERSISTENT REFUSAL`
+
`NON-ENABLING`
+
`PROTECTION OF OTHERS`
+
`PROPERTY/RESOURCE STEWARDSHIP`
+
`PROPORTIONALITY`
+
`CIVIL LAW / TENANCY COMPLIANCE`.

Do **not** argue:

`ISHMAEL / EZRA / DEUT 21 → EVICT`.

### P0

> **Separate residence may be a stewardship decision. It is not a sacrament of regeneration.**

## 9. Continued co-residence remains a live option

Serious sin does not automatically create a biblical eviction command.

Continued residence may remain prudent when:

- meaningful household order is accepted;
- vulnerable people are protected;
- deception is decreasing;
- truthful accountability is possible;
- the person is fighting rather than defending sin;
- disproportionate harm would result from immediate removal;
- work/education/counsel/treatment plans are substantive.

### P0

The question is not merely:

> “How sinful is the child?”

but also:

> **“What are the parents actually authorizing, enabling, protecting, and able lawfully to govern?”**
