### Saturation rule

Create a new substantive Research owner only if a proposed issue:

1. changes a material theological/pastoral/safeguarding category;
2. is not already directly owned;
3. is not safely resolvable by a documented combination of existing owners;
4. is not merely a current-law/platform lookup that belongs near publication;
5. is not merely Product architecture/editorial compression.

`NEW QUESTION ≠ NEW CATEGORY`.

`NEW EDGE CASE ≠ NEW MODULE`.

`NEW RESEARCH MODULE ≠ NEW PRODUCT ARTICLE`.

# 1. Classification vocabulary

## `OWNED`

A current module directly owns the mechanism and its P0 mirror guards.

## `COMPOSITE-COVERED / NO-NEW-MODULE`

No single file title names the exact hypothetical, but existing owners jointly resolve the moral/evidentiary problem without contradiction.

## `CURRENT-JURISDICTION HOLD`

The conceptual category is owned, but exact current legal duty depends on:

- country/state;
- age/status;
- custody/guardianship;
- role;
- offense;
- date;
- local reporting/privacy/recording/evidence law.

This should be verified at Product/publication time rather than converted into a permanent universal Research rule.

## `CURRENT-PLATFORM HOLD`

The conceptual category is owned, but exact platform product behavior, moderation, retention, takedown or reporting path can change.

## `PRODUCT PREFLIGHT HOLD`

Research is sufficient at category level, but publication still requires:

- live Product ownership/collision check;
- source-authority gate;
- current citations where needed;
- editorial compression;
- target audience/part selection;
- CI.

## `TRUE GAP`

A materially distinct category lacking safe ownership. None is currently identified in this late lane after the audit below.

# 10. What would reopen the Research lane?

The saturation verdict is defeasible.

A new substantive module is justified if a later requirement reveals a category such as:

- a genuinely distinct stewardship/jurisdiction relation not represented anywhere in the corpus;
- a new technological mechanism that materially changes the moral/evidentiary analysis rather than merely changing a platform name;
- a source correction that invalidates a current P0 proposition;
- a new owner instruction requiring a major theological question not already represented;
- evidence that two existing owners actually conflict rather than complement one another.

### Not enough to reopen

- one more hypothetical variation;
- a different app performing the same already-owned mechanism;
- a different wording of a rumor/screenshot case;
- a different family member occupying an already-owned role;
- a current-law question whose moral category is already controlled;
- desire to make the corpus numerically larger.
