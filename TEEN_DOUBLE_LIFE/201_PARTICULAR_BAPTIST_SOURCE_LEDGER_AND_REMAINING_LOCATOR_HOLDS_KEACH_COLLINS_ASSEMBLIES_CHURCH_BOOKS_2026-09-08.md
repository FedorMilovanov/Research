**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Mode:** research-only / evidence control / not publication approval

## 1. Exact / strong sources already located

### 1.1. Second London Baptist Confession, chapter 25

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY / CONFESSIONAL / EXACT TEXT.

Located propositions:

- marriage one man/one woman;
- mutual help / offspring / sexual purity;
- lawful for persons able with judgment to give consent;
- Christians marry in the Lord;
- restrictions against ungodly/idolatrous unions.

Sources:
- https://baptistconfession.org/
- https://the1689.org/chapters/25/paragraphs/1/

Reader-facing allowed:

> 1689 explicitly preserves competent marital consent and God's moral restrictions on marriage.

Not allowed:

> 1689 explicitly requires parental consent from every adult first marriage.

No such clause is located in ch. 25.

---

### 1.2. Hercules Collins, *An Orthodox Catechism* (1680)

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY PARTICULAR BAPTIST CATECHETICAL.

Fifth-commandment material is located: honour, love, loyalty, obedience/submission to father/mother and authorities, patience with their failings, God governing through authority.

Sources:
- https://anorthodoxcatechism.org/
- Oxford Angus original-edition landing: https://theangus.rpc.ox.ac.uk/treasures/an-orthodox-catechism-being-the-sum-of-christian-religion-contained-in-the-law-and-gospel/

Reader-facing allowed:

> a leading early Particular Baptist catechism retained Heidelberg's strong authority/parental-duty framework.

Guard: it does not specify a detailed post-18 daughter/courtship polity.

---

### 1.3. Benjamin Keach, *A Golden Mine Opened* (1694)

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY PARTICULAR BAPTIST PASTORAL/THEOLOGICAL.

Exact searchable text located:

- Christ observes duties in all relations;
- husbands/wives have differentiated duties;
- parents must nurture children in Lord, pray, exemplify holiness;
- parents must not provoke through harshness/bitterness;
- children honour/obey parents “in all things in the Lord.”

Primary text:
https://llds.ling-phil.ox.ac.uk/llds/xmlui/bitstream/handle/20.500.14106/A47542/A47542.html?isAllowed=y&sequence=5

Reader-facing allowed:

> early Particular Baptist pastoral theology joins serious child obedience with equally serious parental non-provocation/accountability.

## 3. Broader Reformed sources — strong, but authority level differs

### Westminster Directory for Public Worship

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY HISTORIC REFORMED / NON-1689 CONFESSIONAL.

Strong adult-first-marriage parental-consent procedure, with free-consent and anti-tyranny limits.

Source:
https://reformedstandards.com/westminster/directory-for-publick-worship.html

### Second Helvetic Confession

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY HISTORIC REFORMED CONFESSIONAL / NON-BAPTIST.

Marriage with consent of parents/those in their place.

Source:
https://ccel.org/creeds/helvetic.htm

### Luther

**Evidence class:** PRIMARY TITLE + ACADEMIC HISTORICAL INTERPRETATION.

Tract against children becoming engaged without parental consent and against parents compelling/hindering unjustly.

Sources:
- https://sites.google.com/luther.edu/index-lw/home
- relevant Cambridge Law and History Review articles already logged in `193`.

These strengthen historical plausibility but may not be cited as “the 1689 Baptist rule.”

## 5. Evidence ladder for Product

### Can state strongly now

1. 1689 requires competent consent and marriage in Lord.
2. Collins gives strong Particular Baptist fifth-commandment authority theology.
3. Keach gives strong child-obedience + parent-duty/non-provocation theology.
4. Particular Baptists treated marriage as ecclesially/publicly significant in practice.
5. Strong adult parental-consent practice has major Reformed precedent.

### Must state with attribution

1. Westminster Directory expected parental consent even for adults at first marriage.
2. Second Helvetic explicitly includes parental consent.
3. Gill/Poole/Calvin represent strong classical readings with their own limits.

### Must not state yet

1. Particular Baptist General Assembly mandated adult parental consent.
2. Keach explicitly required father's permission for every adult daughter's marriage.
3. 1689 chapter 25 itself teaches father-guardianship-until-marriage.

## 6. Final evidence-control thesis

> **The Baptist sources already destroy the claim that Reformed Baptist adulthood means authority-free self-definition. They do not yet authorize us to turn every strong broader-Reformed household practice into a clause of 1689 Baptist identity.**
