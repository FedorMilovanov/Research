# 1. `251` — favoritism and differentiated parenting

**Classification:** P0 Part II overlay.

Preserve:

- `IMPARTIAL LOVE ≠ IDENTICAL TREATMENT`;
- `DIFFERENTIATED STEWARDSHIP ≠ FAVORITISM`;
- `ACCOUNTABILITY FOR DIFFERENT CONDUCT ≠ DIFFERENT HUMAN WORTH`;
- one child may rightly receive different device/privacy/freedom conditions because age, maturity, danger or deception history differ;
- parental preference based on beauty, ease, talent, status, temperament match or embarrassment is a different moral category;
- Jacob/Joseph and Isaac/Rebekah narratives show parental favoritism can become part of a destructive family ecology without excusing siblings' sins;
- `CHILD SIN ≠ PARENTAL IMMUNITY` remains controlling.

**Product guard:** do not make “equal treatment” mean identical restrictions for unequal risk/history.

---

# 2. `252` — achievement/reputation idolatry

**Classification:** P0 Part II + Part III family/church-culture overlay.

Preserve:

- `HIGH STANDARDS ≠ CONDITIONAL LOVE`;
- `DILIGENCE ≠ ACHIEVEMENT IDOLATRY`;
- `PUBLIC TESTIMONY ≠ REPUTATION MANAGEMENT`;
- `SPIRITUAL FRUIT ≠ FAMILY TROPHY`;
- a child's grades, appearance, ministry visibility, musical gift or public spirituality must not become the parent's identity/justification;
- fear of man can govern parents as well as teenagers;
- visible Christian performance must not be confused with regeneration or Spirit-wrought holiness;
- a child's works cannot justify the child or the parents;
- train for useful service/stewardship, not family glory.

**Product guard:** do not turn this into anti-achievement or anti-excellence rhetoric.

# 3. `253` — family honor, shame and concealment

**Classification:** P0 Part II + Part III safeguarding/hypocrisy overlay.

Preserve:

- `HONOR ≠ IMAGE MANAGEMENT`;
- `LOYALTY ≠ CONCEALMENT OF SIN OR ABUSE`;
- `GOOD NAME ≠ FALSE NAME`;
- `FAMILY UNITY ≠ COMPELLED SILENCE`;
- `DISCLOSURE OF REAL EVIL ≠ DISHONORING THE FAMILY`;
- legitimate privacy/confidence remains good; embarrassment alone does not make a dangerous fact “private”;
- Eli is a strong control: a parent can verbally rebuke sin and still functionally honor children above God by preserving position/privilege when real correction is required;
- family/church reputation must not silence victims or protect ministry-family image.

**Product guard:** do not stereotype collectivist/family-honor cultures; family solidarity can also support disclosure and responsibility.

# 4. `254` — discipline / rod / corporal-punishment debate

**Classification:** high-risk P0 exegesis guard for Part II; mostly source-control rather than required reader-facing expansion.

Preserve together:

- Scripture clearly requires real parental discipline, instruction, correction and non-indulgent government;
- Proverbs rod texts must not be erased merely because modern readers dislike corporal punishment;
- `PAIDEIA ≠ SPANKING ONLY`;
- `HEBREWS 12 ≠ HOUSEHOLD TECHNIQUE MANUAL`;
- historic Puritan/Particular Baptist writers sometimes understood rod texts physically, but also condemned cruelty, anger, public humiliation and disproportionate severity;
- `DISCIPLINE ≠ ANGER DISCHARGE`;
- `NONVIOLENT DISCIPLINE ≠ PERMISSIVENESS`;
- a specific physical punishment technique must not become an orthodoxy test;
- empirical harm evidence informs prudential application but does not rewrite exegesis;
- civil/safeguarding law remains independently binding.

**Product routing:** use only if Part II discusses discipline method. Do not force a corporal-punishment subsection into the series merely because Research contains the debate.

# 5. `255` — voluntary disclosure and parental response

**Classification:** P0 Part I–II bridge / Part II response overlay.

Preserve:

`CAUGHT`

≠ `ADMITS AFTER PROOF`

≠ `VOLUNTARILY DISCLOSES WHAT COULD STILL BE HIDDEN`

≠ `SEEKS HELP BEFORE THE NEXT FALL`.

Add parent-side controls:

- `VOLUNTARY DISCLOSURE ≠ INNOCENCE`;
- `HONESTY ≠ AUTOMATIC CANCELLATION OF CONSEQUENCES`;
- `CONSEQUENCES ≠ LICENSE FOR THE SAME REACTION AS DECEPTION`;
- `CALM RESPONSE ≠ MORAL INDIFFERENCE`;
- `MONITORING ≠ KNOWLEDGE`;
- `PRIVACY ≠ RIGHT TO DECEPTION`;
- `DISCLOSURE ≠ ENTITLEMENT TO TOTAL PRIVACY AFTER SERIOUS MISUSE`;
- **truth should change the way parents respond even when it does not erase what must still be done**;
- do not promise “tell me and nothing will happen”;
- do not make voluntary confession functionally worthless through rage/public humiliation/indefinite universal distrust;
- parental knowledge often depends materially on voluntary disclosure; surveillance should not become a substitute for a truthful relationship;
- perceived legitimacy can affect cooperation without becoming the source of parental authority.

**Product guard:** no psychology-led parenting framework; Scripture defines truth, confession, parental authority and non-provocation.

# 6. Integrated correction

These modules jointly reject five false choices:

1. `same love → identical rules`;
2. `high standards → conditional belonging`;
3. `family honor → hide embarrassing evil`;
4. `real discipline → one mandatory physical technique`;
5. `real consequences → no distinction between confession and continued concealment`.

A stronger biblical synthesis is:

> **Parents may govern children differently for truthful stewardship reasons while loving impartially; demand excellence without making the child a trophy; protect a good name only through truth; discipline without cruelty or method-idolatry; and make voluntary truth pastorally meaningful without turning confession into immunity.**

# 7. Product reconciliation contract

Before Part II is called complete, audit:

- Are different restrictions explicitly tied to different maturity/risk/history rather than favoritism?
- Does the article guard against family reputation/achievement becoming a motive for concealment?
- Does parental response distinguish voluntary truth from being caught, without erasing consequences?
- Is parental discipline described broadly enough not to imply permissiveness or one mandatory technique?

Before Part III is called complete, audit:

- Is ministry/family reputation explicitly subordinate to truth and safeguarding?
- Can a pastor/leader's child lose role/access when necessary without the church treating exposure as an attack on the family's honor?

Missing P0s are reconciliation defects, not reasons to create five new chapters.
