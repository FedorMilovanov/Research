Both strong traditions can affirm present child duty. Paedobaptist polity gives believers’ children a formal ecclesial address earlier; 1689 Baptist polity reserves membership/baptism for credible professors but should still maintain intense household and pastoral responsibility before membership.

## 4. Key comparative findings

### Strong paedobaptist gain

- child is formally on the church’s map;
- elders/session/consistory have named duties;
- parents may be accountable to church for child nurture;
- wayward baptized children remain objects of church reclamation;
- URCNA gives a concrete exclusion process for mature baptized nonprofessors.

### Strong paedobaptist guard

- baptized child is **not** thereby certainly regenerate; Westminster 28.5 and Reformed anti-presumption controls matter.

### Strong Baptist gain

- baptism/member status follows credible personal profession;
- church keys remain tied to professed discipleship;
- inherited ecclesial status does not become a shortcut to assurance.

### Strong Baptist danger

- `not member` can be mishandled into `not our pastoral responsibility`;
- this creates an ecclesial vacuum around church-raised youth.

### Proposed Baptist cure

> **Do not solve the pre-membership gap by lowering baptism standards. Solve it by actually pastoring nonmember children.**

Use named catechesis, pastoral ownership, parent accountability, direct admonition, role/access stewardship and continued gospel summons without counterfeit church censure.
