# 1. Part I — Hidden life overlays

## A. Unpredictable enforcement can increase concealment payoff (`259`)

Preserve:

- `CONSISTENCY ≠ INFLEXIBILITY`;
- `MERCY ≠ RANDOMNESS`;
- `AUTHORITY ≠ LICENSE TO BLUFF`;
- repeated empty threats can train the child to discover when the parent's word becomes real;
- parental inconsistency does not justify deception but can make deception strategically attractive.

Do **not** turn this into parent-blaming causal determinism.

## B. Fast visible compliance is not the same as heart transparency (`260`)

Preserve:

- prompt/whole/sincere obedience is a real biblical aim;
- `FAST EXTERNAL COMPLIANCE ≠ HEART TRANSPARENCY`;
- a highly polished child can still maintain a hidden parallel life;
- `QUESTION ≠ REBELLION`; respectful appeal can coexist with authority;
- `SINCERE OBEDIENCE ≠ REQUIRED SMILE`.

## C. Cultural/subcultural appearance can become camouflage (`262`)

Preserve:

- correct clothes/music vocabulary/friends can create a convincing public Christian identity without heart holiness;
- permissive refusal to judge content/company can also facilitate secret sin;
- `VISIBLE SUBCULTURAL CONFORMITY ≠ HEART HOLINESS`;
- `ANTI-LEGALISM ≠ MORAL PASSIVITY`.

## D. Parental hypocrisy can teach the social logic of double life (`263`)

Preserve:

- true standard remains true despite parental failure;
- defended parent double standard can teach `public moral language + private exception`;
- parent recruiting child into lies is especially direct modeling of deception;
- `PARENTAL HYPOCRISY CAN MODEL DOUBLE LIFE; CHILD REMAINS RESPONSIBLE FOR CHOOSING DOUBLE LIFE`.

## E. Overbinding can make concealment the practical survival strategy (`264`)

Preserve:

- false classification of every preference as grave divine sin can raise incentive to hide ordinary differences;
- this never excuses actual lying/sexual sin/etc.;
- discovering false human overclaims must not collapse actual biblical morality.
