## 4. Numbers 30 — Gill vs Poole age/jurisdiction dispute

### Gill
URL: https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/numbers/30.htm

## 5. Later Jewish tradition — adult daughter not under father’s betrothal power

**Sources:**
- https://www.sefaria.org/Kiddushin.64
