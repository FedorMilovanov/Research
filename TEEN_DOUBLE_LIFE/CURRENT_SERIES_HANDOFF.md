## 3. Core subject that must not be compressed away

The series is about adolescents, especially children raised by believing parents (ДВР), who can develop a hidden/double life involving some combination of:

- desire for peer approval/status/being “cool”;
- profanity, obscene humor and sexualized speech;
- pornography and lust;
- sexualized chats/sexting;
- hidden accounts/groups/DMs;
- lying, selective truth and information management;
- church/home religious presentation separated from private conduct;
- repeated protected sin, deception and hardening;
- conscience becoming less practically sensitive;
- fear of exposure replacing fear of God;
- corrupting or recruiting peers into sin;
- modern digital access making the hidden peer world persistent, private and portable.

Digital technology does not create the fallen heart. It can radically reduce friction around access, secrecy, repetition, peer presence, recommendation, redistribution and concealment. Do not use the invented `internet ×20` as a factual multiplier.

AI-companion material remains reserve/structural evidence unless a concrete manuscript sentence needs it; it must not turn Part I into a general AI-risk article.

## 4. Correct model of advanced hidden sin / hardening

Do **not** force a cognitive progression such as:

> “Eventually the teenager decides pornography or sexual sin is normal.”

The user specifically corrected this.

A person can still know that the conduct is wrong and yet practically stop debating it. Desire arises → he yields → hides → repeats. Known truth may remain in memory while exerting progressively less practical government in the moment.

Prefer biblical categories:

- slavery to passions/pleasures;
- deceitfulness of sin;
- hardening;
- callousness/loss of sensitivity;
- giving oneself to sensuality;
- suppression of known truth;
- lust gaining habitual practical rule.

Do not use “brain switched off” as a technical explanation. Do not diagnose irreversible searing from ordinary teenage sexual sin.

Locked distinctions:

## 7. Discovery, confession, repentance and trust

Mandatory distinctions:

`caught` ≠ `admitted after proof` ≠ `voluntarily disclosed` ≠ `asked for help before falling`.

Voluntary accountability matters because it attacks secrecy as a strategy rather than only one detected episode.

Do not make confession an immunity card from consequences; do not make truth so dangerous relationally that lying becomes the rational strategy.

Tears, terror, vows, deleted apps and short perfect behavior are not automatic repentance.

Observable pastoral distinction to preserve without heart-reading:

`recurring struggle + truth + warfare + help-seeking`

is not the same as

`planned concealment + defense + recruitment + refusal of correction + settled peace with sin`.

Forgiveness, reconciliation, credibility, trust, access, role and safeguarding remain distinct. Time does not create repentance; it gives fruit or lack of fruit room to become visible.

Fruit is evidence of repentance; fruit is not the price of justification. Exposure, punishment and consequences do not atone. Christ alone grounds pardon and righteousness.

## 8. Children, worship, choir and church privileges — mandatory Part III material

This is a direct user requirement and must not be treated as optional decoration.

Churches must not act as though holiness requirements do not apply to children. A knowingly rebellious, profane, sexually corrupt or corrupting child must not be automatically granted every church privilege merely because “he is only a child” or “he may be unconverted.”

Locked distinction:

`PUBLIC HEARING OF THE WORD`
≠ `MEMBERSHIP / TABLE`
≠ `CONGREGATIONAL OR CATECHETICAL PARTICIPATION`
≠ `FORMAL CHOIR / PUBLIC PLATFORM`
≠ `LEADERSHIP / HELPER ROLE`
≠ `CONFIDENTIAL YOUTH ACCESS`
≠ `UNSUPERVISED ACCESS TO VULNERABLE CHILDREN`.

There is no explicit NT children's-choir admission statute. Therefore specific choir policies are prudential ecclesial applications, not divine law verbatim. But the church has strong biblical grounds to set conduct standards and remove representative/trusted access where known defended or corrupting conduct makes such access unwise.

**WORSHIP DUTY ≠ CHOIR ENTITLEMENT.**

Do not teach an unregenerate child simply to stop all prayer/singing until conversion. Instead expose the contradiction: religious song does not cover deliberate rebellion. The child remains commanded to worship God truly and is summoned to repentance and faith.

Psalm-numbering guard for Russian publication remains mandatory: historical English `Psalm 50:16–21` corresponds to Russian Synodal **Пс. 49:16–21**.

Another direct user requirement:

An unbelieving or nonmember teenager is not morally exempt. “He is unbelieving” is not a reason to leave known rebellion unaddressed.

If leaders know that a person is deliberately maintaining sexual/profane rebellion, lying, using church persona as cover, recruiting peers, sharing obscene material, teaching deception, or using youth/small-group access chiefly for romantic/sexual/corrupting purposes, they should admonish and protect the group proportionately.

Public hearing of the Word should remain open where safety permits. That does **not** require unrestricted access to close groups, trips, private chats, ministry roles or vulnerable youth.

Do not silently tolerate known deliberate hypocrisy merely from fear that, if confronted, the unbelieving teenager may stop attending. The aim is not to drive him away, and confrontation must be wise and proportionate; but “maybe he will repent someday” cannot justify teaching him and the rest of the youth that settled rebellion can coexist indefinitely with unquestioned religious participation.

Formal 1 Cor. 5 discipline applies to professing insiders and must not be juridically collapsed onto a never-member. Pastoral admonition, stewardship restrictions and safeguarding responsibilities still exist for outsiders.

## 12. Adult child / prodigal companion — controlling user scenario

This is a separate unnumbered companion after the three-part adolescent core unless the user reprioritizes it.

Preserve the concrete scenario:

- believing-home upbringing;
- prior discoveries/conversations about double life;
- approximately civil adulthood (example: 18);
- sudden/secret departure from home;
- possible continued deception/self-justification and hidden sexual/profane life;
- or impulsive departure with rapid reconsideration;
- or open contact;
- or progressive blocking.

Reject the universal formula:

> “He is eighteen; immediately let go, stop writing, stop meeting, stop asking, otherwise you are playing God.”

Also reject the opposite extreme of perpetual child-level control.

### Luke 15

Must be handled by historical-grammatical/literary exegesis. It is not a modern no-contact/contact-frequency manual. Narrative silence cannot prove zero contact; the father's seeing the son far off cannot prove digital tracking.

### Immediate post-departure clarification

After a secret sudden departure, a reasonable period of fact-finding may be necessary:

- safe?
- voluntary?
- coercion/blackmail/exploitation?
- serious drugs/violence/trafficking/self-harm risk?
- safe shelter?
- willing to speak/meet?

This is not automatically “playing God.”

### Relationship vs control

Adulthood changes parental jurisdiction, not God's moral law or the natural family relation.

`release child-level control ≠ release love/prayer/contact/admonition`.

No universal contact schedule exists. Open contact may continue; circular argument may require a **mode change, not truth change**. A competent safe adult's explicit block/no-contact request should ordinarily not be bypassed with fake accounts or stalking, absent extraordinary safety cause.

### Consequences / far country

Do not manufacture suffering as a conversion technique. Do not send a person to sin so he can “learn.” But parents may stop financing or repeatedly rescuing a competent adult from consequences they are not morally obligated to remove.

> Do not send the child into the far country; if he chooses it, parents may have to stop financing the far country.

### Israel analogy

Repeated warning, patience, discipline and eventual severe consequences may supply a guarded theological pattern. Parents are not Yahweh, the adult child is not covenant Israel in the theocratic role, and no direct household exile rule may be derived.

### Modern social media

Physical departure no longer guarantees informational separation. Public stories/posts may permit ordinary awareness, but the parent is not called to become an online detective. Distinguish responsible knowledge from obsession/control.

### Household return / co-residence

`relationship ≠ co-residence ≠ money ≠ trust ≠ ministry privilege`.

Questions can include:

- safety;
- sexual conduct in the home;
- pornography/corruption of minors;
- drugs/intoxication;
- theft/crime;
- respectful conduct;
- work/financial responsibility;
- truthfulness on matters affecting the household;
- influence on younger siblings;
- applicable civil/tenancy law.

Parents do not owe an adult child use of the home as infrastructure for gross rebellion. They also must not turn eviction/homelessness into a conversion technique. Jurisdiction-specific legal instructions require live legal verification.

Protect younger siblings. Love for one prodigal does not require knowingly exposing minors to pornography, sexual corruption, drugs, violence, predatory friends, secret-chat recruitment or systematic deception.

### Return is not automatically repentance

Coming home is a location; repentance is moral/spiritual turning. A child may return broke, lonely or frightened. Mixed initial motives do not justify shutting the door to conversation, but neither do they automatically restore trust/privileges.

Do not require the returning child to “suffer enough” before mercy becomes available. Do not make rock-bottom a sacrament.

No universal tablet/timeline exists. Decisions branch by age/jurisdiction, safety, contact openness, defended sin vs repentance, enabling, corruption of others, household conditions, church status and legal facts.
