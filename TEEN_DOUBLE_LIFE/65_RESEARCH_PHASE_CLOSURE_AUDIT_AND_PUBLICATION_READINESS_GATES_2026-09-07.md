# 9. Corrupting influence / protection of other youth

**Status: GREEN.**

This is now a distinct shepherding category.

A teen who personally struggles and seeks help is not the same group risk as one who:

- distributes porn;
- recruits peers;
- teaches lying/circumvention;
- sexually pressures others;
- mocks holiness;
- uses church status for access.

Church leaders have duties both to the rebellious teen **and to the children he may corrupt**.

Core line:

> **Love for one rebellious teenager is not love if it requires indifference to the teenagers he is teaching to rebel.**

Due process/safeguarding controls remain mandatory.
