## 1. Shared center

Both modules answer the same higher-order question:

> **Once truth needs to cross a relational boundary, how do we move it to the right person without creating new danger, false certainty, gossip or jurisdictional confusion?**

### Locked package

- `REPORTING DANGER ≠ BETRAYAL`;
- `ANTI-RETALIATION ≠ AUTOMATIC BELIEF OF EVERY ALLEGATION`;
- `PROTECTING A REPORTER ≠ DECLARING THE ACCUSED GUILTY`;
- `OTHER PARENT MAY NEED TO KNOW ≠ OTHER PARENT ALWAYS SAFE TO TELL FIRST`;
- `PARENT-TO-PARENT CONTACT ≠ SAFEGUARDING PROCESS`;
- `INFORMING ANOTHER FAMILY ≠ SENDING SEXUAL EVIDENCE`;
- `PRIVATE FAMILY AGREEMENT ≠ LEGAL / SAFEGUARDING CLEARANCE`;
- `UNSUBSTANTIATED ≠ PROVED FALSE`;
- `TRUE REPORTING CHANNEL ≠ PUBLIC BROADCAST`.

## 2. Apparent tension: protect reporter vs tell other family

No contradiction.

`294` says another family may need information.

`293` says exposing reporter identity can create retaliation.

### Reconciled rule

> **SHARE THE MATERIAL SAFETY/TRUTH INFORMATION NEEDED FOR THE NEXT DUTY; SHARE REPORTER IDENTITY ONLY WHEN THE legitimate process actually needs it.**

Do not promise impossible anonymity.

Do not expose a source merely to make an informal parent-to-parent confrontation feel more persuasive.

---

## 3. Apparent tension: parents normally matter vs unsafe-parent exception

No contradiction.

The project affirms real parental responsibility.

But `184`, `249`, `268`, `293`, `294` jointly establish a safeguarding exception where:

- parent/guardian is alleged abuser;
- notification could trigger serious retaliation;
- parent is part of concealment/exploitation;
- notification could compromise police/child-protection work.

### Locked

`PARENTAL AUTHORITY ≠ AUTOMATIC SAFE-RECIPIENT STATUS IN AN ABUSE CASE`.

This is not a general privacy doctrine for ordinary rule-breaking.

---

## 4. Apparent tension: direct communication can repair vs direct communication can contaminate

No contradiction.

For ordinary, established, non-criminal peer enablement, direct parent-to-parent communication may be wise.

For serious allegations involving abuse/coercion/illegal imagery, impulsive confrontation may:

- trigger deletion;
- coordinate stories;
- expose reporter;
- increase danger;
- interfere with competent investigation.

### Routing rule

`ORDINARY FAMILY STEWARDSHIP → DIRECT COMMUNICATION MAY FIT`.

`HIGH-RISK SAFEGUARDING / CRIMINAL EVIDENCE → COMPETENT PROCESS MAY NEED TO CONTROL CONTACT`.

---

## 5. Apparent tension: no retaliation vs accountability for false accusation

No contradiction.

Protecting the act of legitimate reporting does not immunize:

- knowingly fabricated accusation;
- malicious publication;
- separate coercion or blackmail;
- unrelated misconduct.

But:

> `not substantiated`

must not silently become:

> `proved fabricated`.

And even a proved false statement requires evidence before motive is labeled malicious.

Use `288`, `291`, `293`.

## 6. Other-minor role classification controls the route

Before contacting another family, classify only as far as evidence supports:

- potential victim/harmed child;
- mutual participant;
- active accomplice;
- witness/reporter;
- falsely implicated child;
- unclear/mixed role.

### P0

`MUTUAL PARTICIPATION ≠ NO VICTIMIZATION POSSIBLE`.

`VICTIMIZATION ≠ NO PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR SEPARATE VOLUNTARY CONDUCT`.

`ACCOMPLICE ≠ ENEMY FAMILY`.

---

## 7. Sexual-image minimization survives every reporting route

Whether information goes to:

- another parent;
- pastor;
- safeguarding lead;
- civil authority,

the project keeps:

`NEED-TO-KNOW ≠ NEED-TO-POSSESS`.

Do not send sexual images of minors around simply to make others believe the seriousness of the matter.

Use `283`, `290`.
