# 1. Why the old “one adult companion” is no longer sufficient

`140` correctly protected the core sequence and sent adult-child material **after** the trilogy. That remains controlling for sequencing.

But subsequent owner requirements and Research modules added several distinct pastoral questions:

- sudden departure after age 18;
- continued contact vs changed mode of contact;
- false repentance and repeated deception;
- long-term waiting;
- return and re-entrustment;
- living at home as a competent adult;
- when separate residence may become appropriate;
- money, debt, work, surety and inheritance;
- third-party housing and church-family intervention;
- mediation between parent and adult child;
- fifth-commandment continuity after childhood;
- status-sensitive adult jurisdiction;
- unmarried daughter / father / marriage consent;
- transition to husband headship;
- work, education, gifts and competence;
- modern autonomy/feminism and competing evangelical models.

These no longer form one coherent reader question.

### Architecture principle

> **ONE ARTICLE SHOULD HAVE ONE PRIMARY PASTORAL QUESTION, ONE PRIMARY STATUS FRAME, AND ONE CLAIM OWNER.**

A single “adult child / family government” article would force incompatible questions into one document and would predictably create:

- repetition;
- exegetical compression;
- overclaiming;
- legal/prudential details crowding out gospel/pastoral argument;
- confusion between son/daughter, resident/departed, dependent/independent, courtship/marriage, and church/family jurisdictions.

# 3. Core Part II — Parents

## Primary question

> **How should believing parents form, discover, confront and shepherd a minor/dependent teenager without either compromise or tyranny?**

## Primary reader

- parents of minors / dependent adolescents.

## Owns

- sovereignty of God + ordained parental means;
- warmth and real authority (`258`);
- parent as parent, not approval-seeking peer;
- real child obedience with lawful limits;
- `question ≠ rebellion`, `appeal ≠ veto`;
- consistent rules and no empty threats;
- discipline without anger discharge;
- parental unity without complicity;
- parental repentance without capitulation;
- conscience category map: divine law / wisdom / house rule / preference / civil law;
- culture/media/clothing/worldliness without legalistic overbinding;

- consequences, trust and gradual re-entrustment;

# 8. Companion D — Daughter, father, courtship and marriage

## Primary question

> **How should a conservative Christian understand an unmarried daughter’s relation to her father, real adult agency, parental consent/counsel, courtship, and the transition to her husband without importing either feminist atomism or paternal absolutism?**

## Primary reader

- fathers/mothers;
- adult daughters;
- suitors;
- pastors/elders.

## Owns

- Numbers 30 with status/transfer guards;
- Genesis 24 and Rebekah's real assent;
- Numbers 36 and bounded choice;
- 1 Cor. 7:36–38 competing readings;
- fifth-commandment continuity applied to daughter;
- `FATHERHOOD ≠ HUSBAND HEADSHIP`;
- fiancé ≠ husband;
- marriage as new one-flesh household;
- competent consent / no forced marriage;
- Gill/Stennett Particular Baptist consent evidence;
- explicit Assembly/Keach/Russian Baptist locator HOLDs;
- strong father-led model as `CUMULATIVE_INFERENCE` where appropriate;
- respect for faithful parental process / no secret bypass;
- unjust parental refusal and mediation;
- work, education, gifts, property competence and lawful female agency;
- widow/unmarried direct Godward calling controls;
- critique of feminism/expressive autonomy without caricature;
- competing evangelical models.

## Hard publication guard

Never write:

> `The New Testament explicitly gives every father husband-like government over every major decision of every adult unmarried daughter until marriage.`

That is stronger than the evidence class permits.

Equally, do not erase the substantial biblical/historical cumulative case for continuing family authority, honour, parental involvement and non-atomistic courtship simply because modern culture prefers private adult autonomy.

# 9. Cross-article repetition policy

Every piece may briefly repeat the following because they are gospel/ethical anchors:

- God sees the hidden life;
- Christ/justification, not consequences, grounds acceptance with God;
- repentance has fruit but fruit does not purchase justification;
- human authority is delegated and bounded by God;
- real love neither lies about sin nor delights in punishment.

Everything else should have a **primary owner** and be cross-linked rather than re-proved at full length.

### Examples

- Luke 15 full exegesis → Companion A.
- money/debt/surety/inheritance → Companion B.
- fifth-commandment adulthood taxonomy → Companion C.
- Numbers 30 / Gen. 24 / 1 Cor. 7 daughter-marriage dossier → Companion D.
- choir/platform/youth access → Part III.
- warm authority / discipline / conscience map → Part II.
