# 8. Part II — Parents

Do **not** insert a long sacramental controversy into the parent article unless architecture later authorizes it.

Integrate these reader-facing conclusions:

- conversion does not create moral duty;
- unregenerate child still lives under Christian household order;
- parents seek faith now, not after an arbitrary age threshold;
- external restraint has real moral/providential value without becoming regeneration;
- no false Christian persona;
- development changes method/degree of authority but not God’s basic claim;
- stronger authority requires truthful evidence and proportionate consequences.

### Historical Baptist recovery box — recommended

A compact box can use **Collins / Keach / Gill** to show:

> credobaptist parents historically prayed, catechized, instructed, corrected, guarded company, brought children under Scripture/means, and sought real conversion while refusing inherited baptism/member status.

This would answer the user’s concern from **inside Baptist history**, not merely by borrowing a Presbyterian critique.

### Particularly strong Gill sentence (paraphrase in Product)

Teach children sin, their natural condition, Christ’s salvation, Scripture, prayer and duty — but do not train them to rest in an outward form or profess faith they do not possess.

Exact quote/locator required if published as quotation.
