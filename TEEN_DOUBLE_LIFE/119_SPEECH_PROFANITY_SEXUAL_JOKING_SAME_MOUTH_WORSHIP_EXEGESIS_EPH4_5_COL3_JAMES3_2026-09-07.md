# 0. Governing claim

The Bible does not reduce speech holiness to:

> “never pronounce a fixed blacklist of syllables.”

But it equally refuses:

> “words are morally neutral sounds; only physical sexual acts matter.”

New Testament speech ethics asks:

- what the mouth expresses;
- what it celebrates;
- whether it corrupts or builds;
- whether it sexualizes/degrades;
- whether it blesses God while cursing God’s image-bearers;
- what kind of heart/wisdom the speech reveals.

# 1. James 3:1–12 — the “same mouth” contradiction

## TEXT

James gives an extended warning about the tongue.

He compares it to:

- a bit directing a horse;
- a rudder directing a ship;
- a small fire setting a great forest ablaze.

He describes the tongue as profoundly dangerous and difficult to tame.

Then comes the exact project-relevant contradiction:

> with the tongue we bless the Lord and Father;
> with it we curse human beings made in God’s likeness;
> from the same mouth come blessing and cursing;
> **“these things ought not to be so.”**

James then uses spring/tree imagery to expose the incoherence.

## Why this is stronger than a generic “no swearing” verse

The problem is **worship-language contradiction**.

The same person may use his mouth in two social worlds:

### Church/public register

- hymns;
- prayer;
- Scripture;
- respectful Christian speech.

### Hidden-peer register

- cursing;
- humiliation;
- obscene joking;
- sexual objectification;
- vicious mockery.

James directly says the blessing/cursing contradiction is morally intolerable.

## Calvin

Calvin emphasizes the hypocrisy of pretending to bless God while the same tongue attacks human beings who bear God’s image.

This is an especially valuable Part III source because it is:

- New Testament;
- directly about speech and worship;
- addressed within the professing Christian community;
- severe without requiring us to declare every offender unregenerate.

## GREEN publication line

> **Иаков не разрешает разделить рот на “воскресный” и “чатовый”: тем же языком благословлять Бога и уничтожать человека, носящего Его образ, — противоречие, которое церковь обязана называть грехом.**

Synthesis.

## Guard

James writes corrective exhortation to “brothers.”

Therefore the passage itself does **not** prove:

> “any professing Christian who curses once is certainly unregenerate.”

It proves:

> the contradiction is real sin and must not be normalized.

This is extremely helpful for the project’s struggling-sinner vs defended-rebellion distinction.

# 2. James 3:13–18 — speech belongs to a wider wisdom system

Immediately after the tongue section James contrasts two kinds of wisdom.

Earthly/unspiritual/demonic “wisdom” is associated with:

- bitter jealousy;
- selfish ambition;
- disorder;
- every vile practice.

Wisdom from above is:

- pure;
- peaceable;
- gentle;
- open to reason;
- merciful;
- full of good fruits;
- impartial;
- sincere.

## Project use

A peer circle’s speech is not merely vocabulary.

It can reveal a whole social wisdom:

- what is admired;
- what earns status;
- who may be mocked;
- whether purity is despised;
- whether sexual aggression is funny;
- whether deceit is clever.

### Strong line

> **Язык группы часто выдаёт её мудрость: что здесь считается смешным, сильным, взрослым и достойным одобрения.**

This is pastoral synthesis.

# 3. Ephesians 5:1–7 — sexual impurity and corrupt speech belong in the same holiness paragraph

## Context

Paul calls believers:

- beloved children;
- imitators of God;
- walkers in love after Christ’s self-giving pattern.

Then he says sexual immorality, impurity and covetousness must not characterize saints and immediately adds:

- filthiness/shameful speech;
- foolish talking;
- `εὐτραπελία` in its improper/jesting sense;
- thanksgiving as the fitting alternative.

The paragraph continues with kingdom/wrath seriousness and warns against deceptive empty words.

## Project significance

The text destroys a narrow purity model:

> “I did not physically have sex, therefore sexual holiness has nothing to say about the way I talk.”

Paul treats speech as part of the same saintly/non-saintly moral environment.

### Modern relevant forms

- sexual double entendre;
- pornographic joking;
- reducing another person’s body to a punchline;
- obscene sexual boasting;
- forwarding explicit memes as humor;
- verbally normalizing adultery/promiscuity.

## Guard — do not ban humor itself

`εὐτραπελία` has a semantic history that can include wit/clever pleasantry in other Greek usage.

In Eph. 5:4 it stands in a clearly negative triad beside filthiness/foolish talk.

Classic commentary tradition commonly recognizes that the target is improper, indecent, scurrilous or morally corrupt joking — not the proposition:

> “Christians may never laugh or make any joke.”

Therefore article wording should prefer:

- “непристойные/грязные шутки”;
- “двусмысленный сексуальный юмор”;
- “шутка, которая делает нечистоту развлечением”

rather than:

> “Paul forbids humor.”

---

# 4. Thanksgiving as a counter-form, not merely silence

Eph. 5:4 gives an alternative:

> rather thanksgiving.

The Christian answer to obscene speech is not merely:

> “say fewer words.”

It is a reoriented mouth.

This fits James 3:

- blessing God;
- speaking consistent with His image/holiness.

## Project application

A child can learn a “clean vocabulary” under surveillance and still retain:

- contempt;
- sexual objectification;
- cruelty;
- cynical worldview.

Biblical speech transformation is deeper than replacing swear words with euphemisms.

# 5. Ephesians 4:25–32 — speech should put away falsehood and corruption and build

## Context

Paul’s new-man ethics include:

- put away falsehood;
- speak truth;
- righteous handling of anger;
- no stealing;
- no corrupt/rotten speech;
- speech that builds according to need and gives grace to hearers;
- put away bitterness/wrath/anger/clamor/slander/malice;
- kindness/tenderhearted forgiveness.

## Project significance

This is almost a speech code for dismantling the double life.

The problem is not only obscenity.

The hidden life frequently needs:

- lies;
- partial truths;
- manipulative speech;
- rage at correction;
- degradation of peers;
- corrupt sexual talk.

### Strong synthesis

> **Библейская чистота речи — это не просто “не матерись”. Это правда вместо лжи, созидание вместо гниения, милость вместо унижения и речь, которая не делает грех развлечением.**

# 6. Colossians 3:5–10 — sexual immorality and obscene speech inside “put to death / put off” ethics

Paul commands mortification of:

- sexual immorality;
- impurity;
- passion;
- evil desire;
- covetousness/idolatry.

Then he commands putting away:

- anger;
- wrath;
- malice;
- slander;
- obscene/filthy speech from the mouth;
- lying.

## Project importance

Again, sexual desire, dirty speech and falsehood are not sealed moral compartments.

They can coexist in one “old self” pattern.

This is highly relevant to the double-life cluster:

`lust + obscene group speech + lying to parents/church`.

## Guard

Do not imply the list proves one behavior causally produces all the others.

Paul gives a moral cluster to put off, not a statistical causal model.

# 7. Speech and heart — avoid simplistic reverse diagnosis

Jesus teaches elsewhere that speech proceeds from the heart and that words matter before God.

This supports the broad principle:

> recurring speech reveals something morally meaningful.

But article must avoid:

> “He said one obscene word; therefore we know the entire secret state of his heart.”

Speech is **evidence**, not omniscience.

More serious patterns include:

- habitual obscene register;
- delight in degrading speech;
- hidden-vs-public code switching used deliberately to conceal a moral life;
- hostility to correction;
- sexual speech that pressures/corrupts others.

# 8. Profanity vs blasphemy vs obscenity vs abuse — do not collapse categories

The Russian word `мат` can cover socially taboo profanity, but biblical speech categories are broader/different.

Future article should distinguish where useful:

## A. Blasphemy / misuse against God

Direct Godward irreverence.

## B. Obscene/filthy sexual speech

Especially relevant to Eph. 5 / Col. 3.

## C. Cursing/abusive speech toward people

James 3 / image-of-God concern.

## D. Slander / false accusation

Truth/neighbor concern.

## E. Foolish/corrupt speech

Speech that rots, degrades or makes evil socially attractive.

### Why this matters

The article should not create a magical vocabulary list where one Russian swear word carries the entire theology while:

- cruel humiliation;
- sexual objectification;
- lying;
- slander

are treated as secondary.

# 9. Sexual joking as social initiation

Module `15` contains empirical/qualitative evidence that obscene/sexual humor can:

- trivialize harmful content;
- signal belonging;
- make objection socially costly.

Scripture adds the moral level:

Eph. 5 does not permit sexual impurity to be laundered into harmlessness merely by formatting it as humor.

### Strong line

> **Шутка меняет форму высказывания; она не обязательно меняет нравственную природу того, чем человек развлекается.**

Guard:

Not every joke about marriage/body is obscene.

Ask:

- what is being celebrated?
- who is being degraded?
- does laughter invite lust/cruelty?
- does the joke normalize what Scripture calls shameful?

# 10. This directly strengthens the children’s choir concern

James 3 provides an important Part III distinction.

A child singing to God and later using obscene/cursing speech should not receive the message:

> “The two uses of your mouth have nothing to do with each other.”

James explicitly says they do.

## But do not overreach

The text does not say:

> “one curse automatically removes a child from choir.”

Role response remains governed by:

- seriousness;
- persistence;
- defended posture;
- influence on others;
- repentance;
- role classification;
- `WORSHIP DUTY ≠ CHOIR ENTITLEMENT`.

### Excellent Part III line

> **Церковь не должна требовать от детей безгрешного рта. Но она не имеет права учить их, будто рот, которым они славят Бога, нравственно не связан с тем, как они говорят о людях после собрания.**

# 11. Struggling child vs corrupting speech culture

## Struggling/confessing case

- fell into profanity;
- confesses it;
- dislikes it;
- asks friends not to drag conversation there;
- seeks help;
- sometimes relapses.

This should not be treated like settled corrupting leadership.

## Defended/corrupting case

- obscene sexual speech is identity/status;
- recruits younger/cleaner peers;
- sends explicit memes/content;
- mocks holiness;
- curses people habitually;
- refuses correction;
- shifts instantly into church language before adults.

This is materially more serious for youth/choir access.

Observable conduct, not mind-reading, supports the distinction.

---

# 12. Parent response — do not solve language by surveillance-only code switching

If the only intervention is:

> “Never say that word in my house,”

the child may simply learn:

> “Use another register in front of parents.”

A fuller biblical conversation asks:

- why is this funny?
- what does it say about sex/people?
- why do you speak differently with this group?
- whom does the speech make small?
- what are you proving to the group?
- what does James 3 say about blessing God and cursing His image?
- what would repentance in speech look like inside the actual chat, not only at home?

External rule remains legitimate; heart/worldview instruction must accompany it.

---

# 13. Positive speech counter-formation

Do not leave the teenager with:

> “holy people are silent and boring.”

Scripture supplies positive speech:

- truth;
- thanksgiving;
- edification;
- grace;
- praise;
- kindness;
- wise humor that does not degrade impurity into entertainment;
- courageous refusal of corrupt group language.

The replacement is **better speech**, not no personality.

# 15. Final locked synthesis

> **Подростковая двойная жизнь слышна не только в том, что ребёнок скрывает, но иногда и в том, как резко меняется его язык, когда взрослые исчезают. Новый Завет не сводит эту проблему к списку запрещённых слов. Павел ставит непристойную и глупую речь рядом с половой нечистотой; он велит снять с уст грязную речь и ложь, а вместо гнилого слова говорить то, что созидает. Иаков идёт прямо в церковное противоречие: тем же языком благословлять Господа и проклинать созданного по образу Божию человека — “так не должно быть”. Поэтому мат и пошлый юмор не являются магической причиной развращения, но и не являются нравственно нейтральным шумом. Они могут стать языком той жизни, в которой нечистота уже не стыд, а развлечение и знак принадлежности.**
