## B. `110_FEAR...` — parent-control limit

Part II should explicitly distinguish:

1. fear of consequences;
2. fear of people/exposure;
3. fear of God.

### Core parental line

> **Родительский контроль может закрыть дверь. Только страх Божий делает грех страшным и тогда, когда дверь никто не охраняет.**

This is not an argument against filters/boundaries.

Parents must use lawful external restraint where needed while admitting that surveillance cannot become omniscience/new birth.

### Important discovery principle

Harshness can train **fear of being caught**; permissiveness can communicate that God’s holiness carries little practical weight.

Keep Eph. 6:4 / Col. 3:21 and real consequences together.

# 5. Part III mandatory late deltas

Canonical base remains:

`91_PART_III... + 104 deltas from 100 + 95/96 where needed`.

But Part III has the largest late correction set.

## A. `105/107` — Psalm correction

**P0:** Russian body uses **Пс. 49**, not Пс. 50, for the “что ты проповедуешь уставы Мои” text.

## B. `108` — WORSHIP DUTY ≠ CHOIR ENTITLEMENT

This is now a required central distinction.

### Strong

- unregenerate child is still under God’s moral claim;
- 1689 16.7 does not create moral neutrality;
- 19.5 says moral law binds all;
- 22.3 explicitly says prayer is required of all men;
- 22.5 treats singing as holy religious worship;
- 26.2/26.6 connect visible church profession with visible walk.

### But

None of those texts says:

> every unregenerate/rebellious child has a divine entitlement to remain in a formal children’s choir or representative platform.

### Locked Part III formula

> **Невозрождённость не освобождает ребёнка от власти Бога. Но и обязанность человека поклоняться Богу не создаёт права на любую церковную роль.**

### Therefore

A church may:

- keep public gospel hearing open;
- call child to prayer/repentance/faith;
- **and** temporarily remove formal choir/platform/trusted access when known defended serious conduct makes the role spiritually false or unsafe.

Choir policy remains pastoral inference, not an inspired qualification list.

## C. `109` — conscience language in church section

If Part III mentions 1 Tim. 4:2, state false-teacher context.

Better general hardening center remains Heb. 3 / Eph. 4.

Do not let church leaders label a difficult child “seared forever” to justify abandonment.

## D. `110_FEAR...`

Part III may use one sharp institutional question:

> Does church practice teach children chiefly to fear **exposure before adults**, or to fear God and hate evil even when no adult sees?

Guard: church cannot produce regenerating fear by procedure; it can testify truthfully or falsely about God’s holiness.
