# Baptized Minor Church Member — Discipline, Parental Authority, and Dual Stewardship

**Status:** REFORMED BAPTIST ECCLESIOLOGY / FAMILY AUTHORITY / RESEARCH ONLY / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Depends on:** `35`, `41`, `50`, `58`, `96`, `108`, `151`, `154–158`.

---

# 0. The missing case

Most prior modules distinguished:

- nonmember church-raised youth;
- adult child;
- professing church member.

But a very practical case remained underdeveloped:

> **What if the teenager is both a minor under parental authority and a baptized church member under church government?**

Examples:

- baptized at 14–16;
- formally admitted as member;
- later enters defended sexual sin/deception;
- parents know;
- elders know;
- youth remains legally/developmentally dependent.

Two real authorities now overlap without becoming identical.

### Governing thesis

> **Baptized minor membership does not cancel parental authority; minority does not cancel real church membership. Family discipline and church discipline are distinct stewardships that should ordinarily cooperate without one swallowing the other.**

---

# 1. 1689 26.12 — church privileges carry church government/censure

Primary:

- https://baptistconfession.org/

1689 26.12 states that those admitted to the privileges of a church are also under its censures/government according to Christ's rule.

### Consequence

If a teenager has actually been admitted as a member, the church cannot say:

> “He is only a child, therefore formal church accountability does not apply.”

The confession does not create a child-membership category exempt from church government.

### Guard

Local church constitution may define membership processes/ages differently. Publication should speak to the principle, not assume every Baptist church admits minors identically.

---

# 2. Ephesians 6:1–4 — church membership does not erase parent-child duty

Primary:

- Eph. 6:1–4;
- Col. 3:20–21.

Paul addresses children within the Christian congregation and commands obedience to parents while also binding fathers not to provoke but to bring children up in the Lord's discipline/instruction.

### Consequence

A baptized 15-year-old does not become ecclesially autonomous from parents merely because he is a church member.

Parents may still:

- regulate devices;
- set curfews;
- choose household rules;
- restrict dangerous friendships/access;
- require ordinary family duties;
- impose proportionate consequences.

Church membership is not emancipation from parental government.

---

# 3. Two authorities, different objects

## Parents ordinarily govern household/child life

Examples:

- bedtime;
- phone access;
- school/work expectations;
- transportation;
- household conduct;
- ordinary discipline;
- access to family resources.

## Church governs ecclesial standing/privileges

Examples:

- membership good standing;
- Lord's Table according to church polity;
- church discipline/censure;
- ministry/platform roles;
- pastoral admonition;
- membership restoration.

### Core distinction

> **Parents should not excommunicate; elders should not become substitute parents.**

---

# 4. Cooperation is ordinarily wise

Where safety permits, serious discipline of a minor member should ordinarily involve truthful coordination among:

- parents/guardians;
- elders/pastors;
- relevant youth leaders only as needed.

Reasons:

- parents observe daily conduct;
- elders steward church standing;
- mixed messages can undermine both;
- the child may play one authority against the other.

### Example of destructive inconsistency

Parents:

> “This is serious rebellion; phone/night access is removed.”

Church leader privately:

> “Your parents are too strict; we don't think it matters.”

Or reverse:

Church:

> “We are beginning formal correction because you profess Christ and defend scandalous sin.”

Parents:

> “He's our child; elders have no right to address it.”

Both can destroy ordered stewardship.

---

# 5. Parents cannot veto Christ's church simply because the member is their child

If the church has biblically admitted the youth as member, parents should not demand:

> “You may never correct or discipline him without our permission.”

1689 26.12 treats church government as real.

### But

Church leaders must still respect:

- lawful parental authority;
- minor safeguarding;
- privacy;
- legal requirements;
- the fact that pastoral access to a minor is not an unlimited entitlement.

### Rule

> **Real church authority does not authorize ecclesial parental replacement.**

---

# 6. Church discipline should not be outsourced to parents

A church can fail by saying:

> “His parents already punished him, so the church has nothing to consider.”

Household consequences and church standing answer different questions.

Example:

A baptized member repeatedly distributes pornography to youth peers and refuses repentance.

Parents may:

- remove devices;
- restrict contacts;
- impose home consequences.

Church may separately need to consider:

- membership profession;
- pastoral process;
- youth access;
- Table/privileges under its polity.

### Locked line

> **A grounded phone is not church discipline; church discipline is not a substitute for parenting.**

---

# 7. Church discipline should not become leverage for family quarrels

Parents can misuse church authority:

> “He disobeyed our preferred career plan; discipline him.”

or

> “He is disrespectful at home; announce it publicly.”

Not every parental conflict is a church-discipline case.

Church leaders must establish:

- actual biblical sin;
- relevant facts;
- impenitence where discipline is contemplated;
- proper process.

### Guard

Do not let church discipline become enforcement of every household preference.

---

# 8. If parents are themselves unsafe or abusive

Normal parent-elder cooperation is not absolute.

If allegations involve:

- abuse;
- coercion;
- sexual harm;
- credible safety threat;

then safeguarding/legal obligations may require a different information pathway.

Do not automatically disclose a minor's report to an alleged abuser merely because the person is a parent.

This is a legal/safeguarding implementation issue requiring jurisdiction-specific policy.

---

# 9. The Lord's Table requires separate ecclesial judgment

Parents should not independently decide church sacramental standing merely by household mood.

Likewise a church should not communicate:

> “Because you are under 18, your professed membership and Table participation have no accountability consequences.”

Exact practice varies by church constitution.

### General principle

> **If the church grants a privilege as a consequence of membership/profession, it must be willing to steward that privilege honestly when the profession is materially contradicted.**

---

# 10. Restoration should coordinate without collapsing spheres

Credible repentance may lead to:

- immediate pastoral welcome/forgiveness;
- progressive rebuilding of home trust (`130–132`);
- church restoration under church process;
- continued device/safety restrictions for a time.

These timelines need not be identical.

Example:

Church may conclude:

> profession is now credible / discipline restored.

Parents may still wisely say:

> night-time private smartphone access remains restricted for now because that particular trust domain has not yet been rebuilt.

That is not necessarily contradiction.

---

# 11. Article-ready synthesis

> **Крещёный подросток может одновременно быть ребёнком под реальной властью родителей и членом под реальной ответственностью церкви. Эти власти не конкурируют за собственность над ним: родители отвечают за дом и воспитание, церковь — за исповедание, общение и церковные привилегии. Телефонное ограничение не заменяет церковную дисциплину, а церковная дисциплина не заменяет отцовство.**

---

# 12. Guards

Do not write:

- baptized minor = adult autonomy;
- parents control church discipline by veto;
- elders control every household decision;
- every parent-child conflict is discipline-worthy;
- church restoration requires instant restoration of every household trust domain;
- safeguarding is suspended because both family/church have internal processes.

---

# 13. Final locked rule

> **Если церковь достаточно уверена в исповедании подростка, чтобы принять его в членство, она не должна потом делать вид, будто его исповедание не имеет церковной ответственности только потому, что он несовершеннолетний. И если родители согласились, что он член церкви, они не перестали быть родителями. Библейская зрелость требует согласования двух реальных, но разных stewardships.**
