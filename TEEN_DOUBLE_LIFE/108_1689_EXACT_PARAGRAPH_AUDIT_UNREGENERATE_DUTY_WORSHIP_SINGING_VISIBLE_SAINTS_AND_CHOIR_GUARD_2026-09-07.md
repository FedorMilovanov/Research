# 1689 Exact-Paragraph Audit — Unregenerate Duty / Worship / Singing / Visible Saints / Choir Guard

**Status:** CONFESSIONAL SOURCE VERIFICATION / PRE-DRAFT CORRECTION  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Scope:** Parts II–III, especially the user’s concern about unregenerate/rebellious children singing in church and being retained in choir/visible roles.  
**Sources checked:** `the1689.org`, `baptistconfession.org`, and independent 1689 text mirrors for cross-verification.

---

# 0. Why this audit matters

Several Research modules correctly argued against two errors:

1. `the child is unregenerate, therefore God has no moral claim on him`;
2. `the child does outward religious things, therefore all is spiritually well`.

But a third distinction needed tighter wording:

> **A universal creaturely/moral duty toward God is not the same thing as entitlement to a particular church role or platform.**

This is especially important for children’s choir.

---

# 1. 1689 16.7 — works of unregenerate people

Verified substance:

The confession says works done by unregenerate people may, **as to their matter**, be things God commands and may be useful to themselves/others; yet because they do not proceed from a heart purified by faith, are not done in the right manner according to the Word or to the right end (God’s glory), they are sinful and cannot please God or merit/precondition grace; nevertheless **neglect of such duties is more sinful and displeasing to God**.

## What this directly supports

**GREEN:**

- unregenerate status does not create a morally neutral zone;
- external conformity is not regeneration;
- a commanded outward act can be materially right while spiritually unacceptable because faith/right end are absent;
- “he is unsaved, therefore obedience/duty no longer matters” is confessionally false.

## What it does NOT independently establish

**RED:**

> “Every particular religious activity available in a church is therefore a duty that every unregenerate child must continue performing.”

16.7 is conditional/general: **works which God commands**.

The paragraph does not determine whether a specific institutional role such as:

- children’s choir;
- worship team;
- Scripture reading platform;
- youth leadership;
- testimony slot

is itself commanded of every individual child.

That question belongs to ecclesiology/pastoral prudence.

---

# 2. 1689 19.5 — the moral law binds all

Verified substance:

> The moral law forever binds **all**, justified persons and others, to obedience, because of both its moral content and the authority of God the Creator; Christ strengthens rather than dissolves this obligation.

## Strong project use

This is the cleanest confessional answer to:

> `He is unregenerate — what can you demand from him?`

Answer:

> Regeneration is necessary for spiritual life and acceptable obedience, but lack of regeneration does not remove the Creator’s authority or moral law.

## Guard

19.5 does not itself specify every household/church prudential rule.

Do not turn:

> moral law binds all

into:

> every parent/church policy I prefer therefore binds all.

---

# 3. 1689 22.3 — prayer is explicitly required of all men

Verified exact substance:

> Prayer with thanksgiving, as one part of natural worship, is **required by God of all men**.

The paragraph then says acceptable prayer is made:

- in the Son’s name;
- by the Spirit’s help;
- according to God’s will;
- with understanding, reverence, humility, fervency, faith, love and perseverance.

## Project consequence

This decisively blocks the crude counsel:

> `You are probably unregenerate, therefore stop praying until you get converted.`

The right call is:

> **Pray truly; seek God; repent; do not use prayer as a religious mask while defending rebellion.**

## Important limit

This paragraph speaks explicitly of **prayer** and “all men.”

Do not silently expand the exact phrase “required of all men” to every church office/platform/activity.

# 4. 1689 22.5 — singing is part of religious worship

Verified substance:

The confession lists among parts of religious worship:

- reading Scripture;
- preaching and hearing the Word;
- teaching/admonishing one another in psalms, hymns and spiritual songs;
- singing with grace in the heart to the Lord;
- baptism;
- Lord’s Supper;
- other stated acts.

These are to be performed:

- in obedience to God;
- with understanding;
- faith;
- reverence;
- godly fear.

## What this supports strongly

**GREEN:**

> Singing in gathered Christian worship is not morally/spiritually neutral stage entertainment.

**GREEN:**

> A church should not teach children that the only thing that matters is hitting the notes while their known defended life is irrelevant to the holiness of worship.

**GREEN:**

> The internal disposition described by the confession makes “sing and preserve your rebellion; no one will address it” an incoherent pastoral message.

## What this does NOT say

22.5 does not state:

> “Every unregenerate child is individually commanded to belong to a formal children’s choir.”

Nor:

> “Every child who commits serious sin must permanently cease all congregational singing.”

Nor:

> “Choir participation equals church membership.”

# 5. Critical distinction — WORSHIP DUTY ≠ CHOIR ENTITLEMENT

This is now a locked Part III distinction.

## A. Godward moral duty

The child remains God’s creature and is called to:

- repent;
- believe;
- pray;
- hear God’s Word;
- honor lawful authority;
- cease evil;
- worship God truly rather than live as though God has no claim upon him.

## B. Church-granted / institutionally stewarded role

A particular church may steward access to:

- children’s choir;
- public solo;
- worship-leading platform;
- youth-team role;
- closed/confidential group;
- trip leadership;
- unsupervised ministry access.

These are **not identical to universal creaturely duty**.

### Therefore

It is confessionally coherent to say to an openly rebellious/corrupting child:

> **“Бог не перестал иметь право на тебя. Тебе нужно каяться, молиться, слышать Слово и искать Христа. Но мы не будем делать вид, будто твоя защищаемая жизнь совместима с этой представительской ролью; поэтому сейчас ты не будешь петь в хоре/нести эту функцию.”**

This does not tell him:

> “God no longer wants you to worship.”

It tells him:

> “Church platform is not camouflage and not an unconditional entitlement.”

# 6. 1689 26.2 — visible saints and profession not destroyed by unholiness

Verified substance:

The confession says those throughout the world who:

- profess gospel faith;
- profess/manifest obedience to God through Christ according to it;
- do not destroy their profession by foundation-destroying error or **unholiness of conversation/life**

may be called visible saints, and particular congregations ought to be constituted of such.

## Strong use

Visible conduct genuinely matters to visible ecclesial profession.

The church need not claim to read the secret decree in order to say:

> a public Christian profession can be materially contradicted by persistent unholiness of life.

## Guard

This paragraph directly governs the **constitution/visible sainthood of churches**, not the exact policy of a pedagogical children’s choir.

Choir application remains inference.

---

# 7. 1689 26.6 — profession AND walking

Verified substance:

Church members are saints by calling who visibly manifest/evidence their obedience to Christ’s call **in/by their profession and walking**, willingly consenting to walk together under Christ’s ordinances.

## Strong use

The Baptist ecclesiological model is not:

> `correct words alone = indefinitely sufficient visible profession regardless of life`.

It explicitly holds together:

> profession + walking.

## Guard

Again:

- church member standing;
- choir role;
- visitor access;
- public preaching attendance;
- safeguarding access

are distinct categories.

Do not collapse them.

# 8. Correction to an earlier possible overstatement

Earlier Research sometimes used a broad formula like:

> `Do not tell the unconverted child to stop singing; he is still obligated to worship.`

This needs refinement.

## Safe final form

> **Do not tell an unregenerate child that God has no claim on him until conversion. Call him to repentance, prayer, hearing the Word, faith and true worship. But none of this grants him an unconditional right to remain in a formal choir or representative role while known serious rebellion is defended.**

This better preserves both:

- 16.7 / 19.5 / 22.3 universal duty;
- 22.5 holiness of worship;
- 26.2 / 26.6 visible profession-and-walk;
- church stewardship of particular roles.

# 9. Application matrix for Part III

| Case | Hearing public Word | Prayer / call to God | Ordinary congregational presence | Formal children’s choir / representative role | Sensitive youth/minor access |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unconverted child, ordinary immaturity, no serious corrupting pattern | OPEN | CALL/TEACH | ordinarily OPEN | policy-dependent | ordinary safeguards |
| child struggling with serious sin, confesses/fights/seeks help | OPEN | ENCOURAGE | OPEN | case-specific; do not punish weakness mechanically | case-specific |
| known defended serious hypocrisy, refuses correction | OPEN to gospel unless independent safety/order reason | CALL TO REPENTANCE; do not validate mask | usually may hear Word under appropriate order | **may/should be restricted where role becomes spiritually incoherent** | restrict where risk/influence warrants |
| child distributing porn / sexual pressure / recruiting deception / grooming-like conduct | OPEN hearing only as safety/order permits | direct admonition + gospel | supervised/context-dependent | REMOVE from trusted/representative role while issue persists | **strong safeguarding restriction** |
| credible repentance after serious pattern | OPEN | ENCOURAGE | OPEN | restoration assessed by role, fruit, time, safety | trust/access may rebuild separately |

This matrix is pastoral application, not the text of the confession.

# 10. Final locked formulation

> **Невозрождённость не освобождает ребёнка от власти Бога. Но и обязанность человека поклоняться Богу не создаёт права на любую церковную роль. Церковь должна звать грешного ребёнка к Слову, молитве, покаянию и Христу — и одновременно вправе отнять представительскую или доверительную привилегию, если известная защищаемая жизнь делает её ложной или опасной.**

This is the confessional bridge needed for the user’s original “children sing while openly living rebelliously” concern.
