# 0. Decision criterion

Do **not** choose architecture because a number sounds elegant.

Choose the smallest structure that can preserve:

- biblical anthropology;
- modern digital reality;
- parenting/formation;
- trust/accountability;
- church/youth/choir responsibility;
- repentance/hardening distinctions;
- sovereign grace/human responsibility;
- adult-child material if retained;
- enough narrative energy that readers actually finish it.

The architecture must also avoid repeating the same framework in every installment.

# 7. Theological-density test

A single article must carry too many high-density distinctions:

- total depravity vs responsibility;
- sovereignty vs means;
- restraint vs regeneration;
- struggling believer vs settled hypocrite;
- hearing Word vs ministry privileges;
- child authority vs adult jurisdiction;
- discipline vs safeguarding;
- forgiveness vs trust;
- love vs enabling.

Too many distinction pairs in one narrative can make prose feel like a legal memorandum.

Three-part/hybrid architectures distribute them more naturally.

# 8. Journalistic-energy test

The project should read like serious theological journalism/pastoral longform, not systematic-theology notes.

Strongest narrative arcs:

### Arc A

**A child you think you know → secret world → sweetness → concealment → hardening → exposure.**

### Arc B

**Parents discover they cannot regenerate → nevertheless must shepherd → must distinguish control from care → truth from concealment → consequences from rejection.**

### Arc C

**Church says holiness → institutional practices may contradict it → what should change.**

### Arc D

**Adult leaves → parental jurisdiction collapses → love and moral truth remain → non-enabling and hope.**

These are four distinct journalistic arcs.

That observation favors **hybrid** or **3 + companion adult-child**.

# 10. Decision gate before drafting

Before selecting architecture, build one more artifact:

- `KEEP / CUT / APPENDIX / COMPANION` matrix;
- estimate evidence density and narrative necessity per theme;
- identify which themes can disappear from main prose without theological loss;
- identify quote/fact budget per piece.

Only then lock the product architecture.
