# Edge-Case Stress Test — Child Status, Membership, Discipline, Evidence, and Transitions

**Status:** RESEARCH / ADVERSARIAL CASE MATRIX / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Depends on:** `277`, `284–287`, `299`, parent evidence routing `281–297`, existing owners `150`, `159–161`  
**Purpose:** test whether the comparative model still works when pastoral cases do not fit a neat `believing child / unbelieving child` binary.

> Governing rule: **status changes the available ecclesial tools; it does not change the truth of God’s moral law, manufacture regeneration, or relax evidence standards.**

---

# 1. Case A — Minor openly says: “I do not believe”

## Facts

- raised in Christian home;
- understands basic gospel substantially;
- attends church under parental authority;
- gives no credible profession;
- does not want to lie about faith;
- may nevertheless be outwardly orderly or may be resisting some house rules.

## Strong 1689 Baptist response

Do **not** force a profession.

Do **not** baptize to solve social belonging.

Do **not** infer:

> `unbelief -> no moral duty`.

Maintain:

- truthfulness;
- lawful parental authority;
- ordinary household worship/attendance expectations within existing project guards;
- direct gospel summons;
- pastoral conversation;
- role/access distinctions;
- no false Christian testimony.

### Locked

`HONEST UNBELIEF > COUNTERFEIT PROFESSION`.

But:

`HONEST UNBELIEF ≠ MORAL EXEMPTION`.

## Strong paedobaptist response

If baptized/noncommuning, the child remains an objective church member under polity-specific oversight and discipline.

The church should press personal faith/repentance precisely because baptized status does not itself settle regeneration.

### Comparative difference

The Baptist child can be truthful nonmember under organized pastoral care.

The paedobaptist child can be truthful nonprofessing member under formal church government.

The moral summons is present in both; formal jurisdiction differs.

# 2. Case B — Baptized paedobaptist adolescent persists in serious known sin but has never professed faith

## Facts

- baptized in infancy/childhood;
- noncommuning member;
- persistent serious conduct established by adequate evidence;
- no credible profession;
- no right to assume regeneration.

## PCA

Current BCO clearly provides:

- membership;
- broad discipline;
- instruction/government;
- Chapter 28 reclamation;
- parent accountability to church.

But exact technical judicial pathway must respect the current BCO distinction between broad discipline and `offense` as object of judicial process in 29-1.

### Product guard

Do not promise a generic `trial/excommunication` sequence for every noncommuning child.

## OPC

Current Book of Discipline places baptism-only members under administrative and judicial discipline and permits erasure of a persistently resistant noncommunicant after sustained exhortation/notice under the specified conditions.

## URCNA

For a **mature** baptized member delinquent in doctrine/life, Article 59 supplies admonition and eventual exclusion with classis advice if persistence continues.

### Locked

> **The strongest paedobaptist model has real constitutional tools before profession; it is not merely “parents handle it privately.”**

# 3. Case C — Baptist child wants baptism mainly to belong socially

## Facts

- church-raised;
- friends are baptized/members;
- feels excluded from Table, membership language, trusted roles or peer identity;
- gives correct doctrinal answers;
- motive may be mixed;
- personal repentance/faith not yet clearly credible.

## Failure mode

Use baptism to solve the church’s pre-membership pastoral vacuum.

This creates:

`SOCIAL BELONGING NEED`
→ `LOWERED PROFESSION THRESHOLD`
→ `BAPTISM AS IDENTITY CERTIFICATE`
→ later false assurance.

## Correct Baptist response

- give real pastoral belonging without counterfeit membership;
- distinguish attendance, care, catechesis, service opportunities and friendships from sacramental/member privileges;
- examine faith age-appropriately without demanding adult rhetorical polish;
- allow mixed human motives to be explored without assuming all mixed motives invalidate faith;
- do not rush merely because the child feels left behind.

### Locked

> **The cure for Baptist under-pastoring is not premature baptism.**

Use `161`, `277`, `298`, `299`.

# 4. Case D — Baptized Baptist minor later enters defended serious sin

## Facts

- previously made credible profession;
- baptized/admitted as member;
- still under lawful parental authority as minor/dependent;
- serious current conduct established;
- repentance absent or disputed.

## Two real stewardship spheres

### Parents

Own:

- household rules;
- protection;
- device/access boundaries;
- ordinary consequences;
- sibling safety;
- dependence/residence stewardship.

### Church

Owns:

- member oversight;
- admonition;
- discipline according to church constitution;
- role/sacramental/member questions;
- restoration.

### Anti-collision guard

Parents must not say:

> `He is our minor, therefore elders have no church authority.`

Church must not say:

> `He is a member, therefore Session/elders replace parents in household government.`

### Locked

`PARENTAL AUTHORITY + MEMBER DISCIPLINE = DUAL STEWARDSHIP, NOT COMPETING SOVEREIGNTIES`.

Use `159`.

# 5. Case E — Adult paedobaptist nonprofessor remains on roll

## Facts

- baptized as covenant child;
- now legally adult;
- never made public profession;
- may still live at home or may be independent;
- relation to church varies by polity.

## Key distinction

Civil adulthood does not automatically erase ecclesial status.

PCA explicitly addresses adult non-communing members and warns concerning covenant obligations.

OPC can maintain jurisdiction over a noncommunicant on the roll and provides an erasure pathway for persistent rejection.

URCNA has mature baptized-member exclusion procedure.

### Do not collapse

- adult civil status;
- parental household authority;
- residence/dependence;
- church membership;
- Table privilege;
- profession.

### Product implication

The paedobaptist model exposes a useful Baptist question:

> **What happens pastorally when a church-raised Baptist turns 18 but has never joined?**

Answer cannot simply be:

> `Now he is an ordinary outsider.`

But Baptist leaders also cannot counterfeit member jurisdiction over someone never admitted.

# 6. Case F — Serious allegation against a child, but evidence remains incomplete

## Facts

- prior deception may exist;
- parent has screenshots or another person’s report;
- allegation could affect peers/safety;
- child denies or refuses to answer;
- evidence is credible enough to warrant concern but not yet adjudicatively complete.

## Error 1 — authority inflation

`We have jurisdiction / parental authority -> therefore our suspicion is the verdict.`

Forbidden.

## Error 2 — evidence paralysis

`Not proved beyond all doubt -> no temporary protective action is allowed.`

Also forbidden where credible risk justifies proportionate precaution.

## Correct model

Separate:

1. established fact;
2. strongly supported inference;
3. unresolved allegation;
4. precautionary action;
5. final disciplinary finding.

### Locked

`PRECAUTION ≠ CONVICTION`.

`PAST DECEPTION ≠ AUTHENTICATION OF NEW EVIDENCE`.

`SILENCE ≠ CONFESSION`.

`FORMAL JURISDICTION ≠ LOWER PROOF THRESHOLD`.

Use parent modules `281–292`, `296–297`.

# 7. Case G — Parents neglect spiritual formation but demand church intervention once crisis appears

## Facts

- little household Scripture/prayer/catechesis;
- weak supervision or inconsistent discipline;
- church-raised child enters serious hidden pattern;
- parents now want elders to “fix” the child.

## Paedobaptist insight

PCA BCO 28 explicitly makes parents primarily responsible for spiritual nurture and accountable to the church.

This prevents:

> `child membership -> church replaces household formation`.

## Baptist transfer

A 1689 church need not accept infant membership to hold **member parents** accountable for:

- neglect;
- hypocrisy;
- refusal to protect;
- failure to bring children under ordinary means;
- sabotaging or refusing appropriate pastoral help.

### Jurisdiction precision

Church discipline, if warranted, falls directly on the **member parent’s conduct**, not on fictional member status of the unbaptized child.

### Locked

> **A Baptist church can increase child accountability partly by increasing parent accountability to the church.**

Use `287`, `298`, existing parent owners.

# 8. Case H — Child/teen truthfully reports wrongdoing by parent, elder, youth leader, or respected church member

## Facts

- reporting person may be a minor;
- alleged wrongdoer may possess family/church authority;
- social pressure may call the report disloyalty, rebellion, gossip or “snitching”;
- facts still require fair evaluation.

## Dangerous authority error

Use covenant/fifth-commandment/submission language to suppress truthful disclosure.

Forbidden.

## Required controls

- authority does not create immunity from investigation;
- anti-retaliation protection does not turn allegation into automatic guilt;
- exact words/paraphrase/inference must be separated;
- cross-family disclosure may require competent safeguarding judgment;
- church reputation is not a higher good than truth/protection;
- a minor’s subordinate status does not negate witness competence.

### Locked

`SUBMISSION ≠ COMPLICITY IN CONCEALMENT`.

`REPORTING ≠ AUTOMATIC REBELLION`.

`ANTI-RETALIATION ≠ AUTOMATIC CREDIBILITY`.

Use parent modules `293–297`.

# 9. Case I — Other minors are implicated

## Facts

- peer receives sexual material;
- romantic partner is involved;
- another child may be victim, witness or accomplice;
- one set of parents wants private settlement.

## Guard

Family/church authority over one child does not create jurisdiction over another family’s child.

Possible duties may require:

- notifying appropriate parents/guardians where safe and appropriate;
- safeguarding escalation;
- avoiding distribution of explicit material;
- separating victim/witness/accomplice categories;
- avoiding private agreements that suppress legitimate safety reporting.

### Locked

`OUR CHILD'S CONFESSION ≠ OUR AUTHORITY OVER EVERY OTHER MINOR INVOLVED`.

Use `294–295`.

# 10. Case J — Child reforms outwardly under consequences but gives no evidence of faith

## Facts

- behavior improves;
- lies stop or reduce;
- device restrictions work;
- grades/routine stabilize;
- parent longs to see conversion.

## Error

Call external reform `regeneration` because it is emotionally relieving.

## Better categories

External reform can be:

- genuinely good in civil/household terms;
- evidence that consequences and formation matter;
- a reduction of harm;
- preparation for listening;
- not proof of saving union with Christ.

### Historical Baptist control

Keach/Gill material in `298` is especially useful here: outward reformation is not new birth, but that does not make outward obedience worthless.

### Locked

`EXTERNAL REFORM ≠ REGENERATION`.

`EXTERNAL REFORM ≠ NOTHING`.

# 11. Case K — Child professes faith very young

## Two opposite errors

### Automatic certification

`He used the right words -> baptize immediately`.

### Automatic age skepticism

`A child cannot have credible faith until an arbitrary later age`.

## Better model

Assess age-appropriate credibility:

- personal awareness of sin;
- actual trust in Christ rather than family identity;
- repentance in kind appropriate to development;
- truthfulness rather than rehearsed performance;
- persistence over a prudent period where local polity requires;
- no demand for adult vocabulary or emotional intensity.

### Spurgeon control

Historic Baptist child-conversion preaching seeks genuine faith now, not at a magic age, while refusing sacramental shortcut.

### Locked

`SAME SAVIOR / SAME FAITH IN KIND ≠ SAME ADULT MATURITY IN EXPRESSION`.

# 12. Case L — Eighteenth birthday

## Before birthday

Possible realities:

- moral responsibility;
- parental minor authority;
- household rules;
- church member/nonmember status;
- gospel-light responsibility.

## After birthday

Some civil/parental authority changes by jurisdiction and circumstance.

But the birthday does not automatically change:

- truth of God’s moral law;
- history of gospel light;
- baptism/member status;
- credibility of profession;
- existence of serious pastoral concern.

### Locked

`18 = CIVIL/DEVELOPMENTAL TRANSITION`.

`18 ≠ THEOLOGICAL CONVERSION OF STATUS BY ITSELF`.

Use `285` and adult/prodigal owners.

# 13. Cross-case decision grid

For any difficult child/youth case, answer in this order:

## A. What is established?

- facts;
- evidence strength;
- safety risk;
- unknowns.

## B. What is the person’s actual status?

- age/capacity;
- dependence/residence;
- profession;
- baptism;
- membership;
- communicant status;
- church roll/jurisdiction.

## C. Who has which stewardship?

- parents;
- church officers;
- civil/safeguarding authorities;
- other families;
- school/employer where applicable.

## D. What action is proportionate now?

- instruction;
- household consequence;
- pastoral admonition;
- role/access restriction;
- temporary safeguard;
- formal member discipline;
- referral/escalation;
- continued gospel invitation.

## E. What must not be claimed?

- hidden motive as certain;
- regeneration/reprobation as infallibly known;
- allegation as fact before adequate proof;
- nonmembership as moral neutrality;
- membership as saving certificate.

# 14. Adversarial verdict

The comparative model survives the edge cases only if five separations remain intact:

1. `MORAL DUTY ≠ MEMBER STATUS`;
2. `PASTORAL OWNERSHIP ≠ CHURCH KEYS`;
3. `BAPTISM/MEMBERSHIP ≠ REGENERATION`;
4. `AUTHORITY/JURISDICTION ≠ PROOF`;
5. `DEVELOPMENTAL TRANSITION ≠ MAGIC MORAL RESET`.

If any of these collapse, either paedobaptist presumption or Baptist ecclesial vacuum reappears.

---

# 15. Final locked synthesis

> **A strong child-accountability theology must be able to say simultaneously: this child is really accountable; this child’s developmental capacity matters; this child’s ecclesial status must be named accurately; no one may force a false profession; formal church tools depend on real membership/jurisdiction; serious risk can justify proportionate protection before final adjudication; and no amount of parental or ecclesial authority turns weak evidence into truth.**

This is the minimum adversarial standard for Product transfer.
