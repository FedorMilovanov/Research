# 1. Financial stewardship — locked distinctions

Never collapse:

`HELP ≠ CASH ≠ DEBT RESCUE ≠ SURETY ≠ CURRENT SUPPORT ≠ INHERITANCE`.

Preserve:

- real provision duties toward minors/dependents;
- `UNABLE TO WORK ≠ UNWILLING TO WORK`;
- direct help can be different from unrestricted cash;
- ordinary Christian good to a sinful adult does not require financing defended sin;
- Scripture seriously warns against rash surety/guaranteeing others' debts;
- repeated debt rescue can erase consequences without changing the moral structure;
- `DEBT PAYMENT ≠ REPENTANCE`;
- Luke 15's advance share is narrative architecture, not a modern early-inheritance command;
- Scripture values intergenerational provision but does not supply a simple universal modern equal-share estate rule;
- `EQUAL LOVE ≠ IDENTICAL CASH MECHANICS`;
- `NO FUNDING ≠ UNIVERSAL MORAL PROHIBITION`;
- `FINANCIAL HELP ≠ PURCHASE OF TOTAL ADULT JURISDICTION`;
- adult independence includes increasing responsibility for the costs/consequences of adult choices;
- `BOUNDARY ≠ REVENGE` applies to financial limits;
- never use manufactured poverty/`rock bottom` as a conversion technology.

# 2. Third-party housing / rescue — locked distinctions

Preserve:

- `REFUGE FROM ABUSE ≠ REFUGE FROM RIGHTEOUS CORRECTION`;
- `HOSPITALITY ≠ SECRET COALITION`;
- `MEDIATE BEFORE SUBSIDIZE` where facts/jurisdiction remain live and safe to verify;
- `FIRST STORY HEARD ≠ FINAL FACT PATTERN`;
- temporary safety/hospitality does not itself adjudicate the whole family dispute;
- `LEGAL ADULT ≠ LONG-INDEPENDENT ADULT`;
- recent departure does not instantly erase a still-live family relation;
- do not build a Christian-looking alternative household whose function is to neutralize righteous parental correction;
- boyfriend/girlfriend support never creates spouse jurisdiction (`SUITOR SUPPORT ≠ SPOUSAL JURISDICTION`);
- grandparents/extended family can undermine parents by supplying consequences away without examining truth;
- church families should not become private unaccountable housing systems in a live conflict;
- where parents are actually abusive/violent/forcing sin, intervention/refuge may be righteous;
- `REPENT / RECONCILE ≠ AUTOMATIC PHYSICAL RETURN IN EVERY CASE`;
- resource transfer can take a side even when the helper claims neutrality;
- third-party mercy must not become third-party subsidy of the contested sin.

---

# 3. Third-party sequence

Where safe and lawful, an ordinary sequence is:

`SAFETY → FACTS → REAL JURISDICTION → MORAL ASSESSMENT → MEDIATION → FORM OF HELP → REVIEW`.

Do not invert it into:

`EMOTIONAL STORY → SECRET COALITION → MONEY/HOUSING → LATER MAYBE FACTS`.

### Guard

Where acute danger exists, safety can rightly precede complete fact resolution.

# 4. Money and family authority — two-way guard

Against permissive autonomy:

> An adult cannot demand every benefit of parental dependence while declaring every legitimate condition of those resources morally irrelevant.

Against financial domination:

> Parents do not purchase total jurisdiction over an adult conscience merely because they provide money/housing.

If a subsidy requires inappropriate control to maintain, reduction/end of subsidy may be cleaner than permanent financial leverage.
