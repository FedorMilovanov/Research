- Numbers 36 illustrates real choice within divinely bounded constraints.

### Locked synthesis

> **The classic alternative to modern autonomy is continuing-but-changing relational authority, not a proof that adulthood is fictitious.**

# 4. P0 — adult under parents' roof is neither hotel guest nor minor child

`202` locks this distinction.

Civil adulthood, natural filial relation, household membership, economic dependence, marriage and church authority are different axes.

A competent adult living in parents' home can be legitimately bound by household order concerning:

- sexual conduct brought into the home;
- overnight guests;
- substances/violence/theft;
- protection of younger siblings;
- reasonable common-work/financial contribution;
- security/visitor rules;
- truth about matters directly affecting the household.

But:

> `HOUSEHOLD ORDER ≠ OWNERSHIP OF ADULT CONSCIENCE`.

### Four-level requirement model

1. God's explicit moral law;
2. lawful household order;
3. parental counsel carrying special filial weight;
4. illegitimate overreach/tyranny.

# 6. P0 — parental consent to marriage is serious; parental refusal is morally judgeable

`203` controls this.

Westminster Directory provides an unusually strong historic Reformed precedent:

- parental consent expected even for adults at first marriage;
- free consent of those marrying preserved;
- parents may not force marriage;
- parents may not refuse consent without just cause.

Baxter is highly deferential to parental authority but allows categories where parental prohibition in a specific matter may cease to bind because authority is given for edification, not destruction.

Traditional Calvin/Poole father/daughter readings of 1 Cor. 7 contain similar anti-tyranny limits.

### P0 sequence if parents oppose a marriage

Do not jump to:

`secret romance`

or

`parental no ends all inquiry`.

Ordinary path:

`truth → specific reasons → character/safety audit → church/wise mediation → time → distinguish just delay from indefinite veto`.

### Mandatory question

> **Why are the parents saying no?**

The moral quality of the reason matters.
