## 2. `193` — historic Reformed parental consent is stronger than many modern evangelicals assume

`193_REFORMATION_PARENTAL_CONSENT_CLANDESTINE_MARRIAGE_LUTHER_CALVIN_SECOND_HELVETIC_WESTMINSTER_AND_LIMITS_2026-09-08.md`

Locked findings:

- Luther opposed secret engagement/marriage without parental consent while also opposing parents who compel or unjustly hinder marriage;
- Calvin treats parental consent as morally/juridically serious in his criticism of canon-law treatment of minors' marriages;
- Second Helvetic Confession explicitly expects marriage with parental consent / those standing in parents' place;
- Westminster Directory for Public Worship is especially important: for **first marriage**, the parental-consent procedure is to be observed even for those **of age** whose parents are living;
- the same Directory requires genuine consent of the parties and forbids parents to force marriage or deny consent without just cause;
- this is substantial historic Reformed evidence against atomistic/secret couple formation;
- it is **not** itself a clause of the 1689 Baptist Confession and may not be silently promoted into a Baptist confessional article.

P0:

> **Historic Reformed Christianity can give parental consent serious post-adulthood weight without treating adult consent as fiction.**

## 3. `194` — “adult” is not one jurisdictional status

`194_STATUS_JURISDICTION_MATRIX_MINOR_DEPENDENT_ADULT_AT_HOME_RECENT_DEPARTURE_INDEPENDENT_ADULT_ENGAGED_MARRIED_WIDOWED_2026-09-08.md`

Before categorical counsel, classify at least:

- minor child living at home;
- legally adult but resident/dependent in parents' home;
- recently/secretly departed adult after active family conflict;
- competent adult independently established for years;
- unmarried adult daughter living with parents;
- engaged/serious courtship but not married;
- married son/daughter;
- widow;
- divorced/separated adult (`HOLD` for separate doctrinal treatment);
- adult child in defended gross sin.

Locked distinctions:

- `GROWN => LESS PARENTAL POWER` does not entail `NO FILIAL DUTY`;
- recent physical departure does not instantly erase a still-live family relation;
- long-term independent adulthood materially changes ordinary direct household government;
- `ENGAGED ≠ MARRIED`; no husband-headship exists before marriage;
- marriage creates a new primary household relation;
- widowhood does not simply return a woman to child-level paternal jurisdiction;
- co-residence creates real household obligations without automatically creating ownership of every adult decision.

P0:

> **Biblical adulthood is a transition in jurisdiction and responsibility, not emancipation from every inherited obligation.**

### Connivance control

“Love/patience” may become connivance when parents knowingly preserve the infrastructure, role, access, concealment or protection that allows serious defended evil to continue in their actual jurisdiction.

P0:

> **A Christian parent cannot save a rebel by controlling the house. A Christian parent also cannot evade responsibility for the house merely by saying the rebel is an adult.**

And:

> **“My house, my rules” is not yet a Christian argument. The stronger question is what God has actually entrusted to the household head and whether that sphere is governed lawfully, proportionately, truthfully and without retaliation.**

## 7. Confessional control — do not Presbyterianize the 1689

This pass also opens an explicit Particular Baptist verification task.

Known already:

- 1689 LBCF ch. 25 says people able **with judgment to give their consent** may marry, with Christian restrictions (`in the Lord`, no wicked/idolatrous/infidel union);
- ch. 25 does not itself contain the Westminster Directory's explicit adult-first-marriage parental-consent rule;
- Hercules Collins' 1680 *Orthodox Catechism* preserves strong Heidelberg fifth-commandment language about honoring/loving/being loyal to father/mother and authorities and submitting to proper correction;
- Benjamin Keach explicitly exhorts children to honor/obey parents “in all things in the Lord” and rejects indulgence of children's evil desires.

Therefore:

> **Strong parental authority is genuinely Particular Baptist-compatible, but a specific Westminster marriage-consent procedure must be labeled historical Reformed evidence unless separately demonstrated from Particular Baptist sources.**

Next source pass should search Keach/Collins/Kiffin/General Assembly/church-book material specifically on courtship/marriage/family government.
