# Do Not Confuse Responsibility with Equal Culpability
## Child Capacity and Discipline Proportionality

**Status:** RESEARCH / P0 WORDING GUARD / NOT PUBLICATION
**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Depends on:** `285`, `273–292`

> This file narrows the user-language “полная ответственность” so the final article does not accidentally claim equal culpability, identical church process, or adult-level capacity for every baptized/unbaptized child.

## 1. Four propositions that may all be true

1. A child is **really** morally accountable before God.
2. A child’s culpability can be **graded** by understanding, intent, knowledge, coercion, maturity and received light.
3. Parents possess **real** authority but must discipline proportionately and non-provocatively.
4. Formal church jurisdiction depends on actual ecclesial status and polity; it is not identical merely because moral responsibility is real.

## 2. Unsafe equation

`REAL RESPONSIBILITY = SAME CULPABILITY AS ADULT = SAME CONSEQUENCES = SAME CHURCH CENSURE`.

This equation is false.

## 3. Better language

Use:

- `реальная ответственность`;
- `настоящая обязанность`;
- `возрастающая ответственность по мере света и понимания`;
- `не нравственная нейтральность`;
- `пропорциональная дисциплина`.

Avoid unqualified:

- `полная ответственность`;
- `полностью как взрослый`;
- `крещение делает полностью ответственным`.

## 4. Paedobaptist relevance

When PCA says children at the age/years of discretion are subject to covenant obligations of faith, repentance and obedience, this should not be paraphrased as:

> “A baptized toddler is judicially treated exactly as an adult communicant.”

The polity itself uses capacity/discretion distinctions.

## 5. Baptist relevance

When 1689 says the moral law binds all, this should not be paraphrased as:

> “Every child possesses adult-level comprehension of every command.”

Universal obligation and developmental capacity are different questions.

## 6. Project line

> **Правильная противоположность нравственной нейтральности — не взрослый приговор ребёнку, а реальная, возрастно осмысленная ответственность под Божьим Словом.**
