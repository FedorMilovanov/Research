### Core formulas

> **Цифровая эпоха не создала греховное сердце, но дала подростковой двойной жизни новую инфраструктуру: почти непрерывный доступ, приватность, peer presence, social reward, повторение, масштаб и технически лёгкое сокрытие.**

> **Родители не возрождают ребёнка, но воспитание не нейтрально: Бог суверенно действует через реальные средства, а характер, нормы, отношения и opportunity structure действительно формируются.**

> **Невозрождение ≠ невиновность. Fallen inability не отменяет обязанности человека перед Божьим моральным законом.**

> **External restraint ≠ regeneration — и именно поэтому нельзя ни обожествлять ограничения, ни объявлять их бесполезными.**

> **Attendance ≠ membership ≠ Lord’s Supper ≠ choir/ministry ≠ leadership ≠ trusted youth access.**

> **Публичное слышание Слова и unrestricted close fellowship — не одно и то же. Известное развращающее влияние можно и иногда нужно ограничивать, продолжая призывать человека слушать Слово и каяться.**

> **Дети не находятся в моральной “приёмной” до обращения. Их нужно не принимать за возрождённых по наследству и не оставлять без закона до будущего обращения, а учить, обличать, ограждать и сейчас призывать ко Христу.**

> **Нам нужна не статья, где Библия украшает психологический отчёт стихами, а библейское исследование сердца, где эмпирические данные помогают прежде всего измерять современные обстоятельства, масштабы и технические механизмы.**

## Evidence / theology guards

1. `religiosity / attendance / identity` ≠ regeneration.
2. Peer influence ≠ removal of personal responsibility.
3. Parenting influence ≠ parental determinism.
4. Sovereign regeneration ≠ upbringing irrelevance.
5. Fallen inability / unregeneration ≠ innocence.
6. External restraint ≠ heart change; external restraint can still be useful and required.
7. Legitimate privacy ≠ active secrecy around serious moral/risk behavior.
8. Victimization/coercion ≠ voluntary sexual sin; mixed cases must separate the child’s action from what was done to the child.
9. Pornography exposure ≠ universal addiction; self-perceived addiction ≠ automatically CSBD.
10. No invented `internet ×20` coefficient or universal dopamine/brain-damage story.
11. No automatic diagnosis `conscience permanently seared`.
12. Modern adolescents do not show increases in every offline sexual-behavior indicator; digital sexual opportunity may expand while some offline measures decline.
13. Psychological control and behavioral boundaries are distinct.
14. Matthew 5:29–30 supports radical dealing with occasions of sin, not self-harm or parental abuse.
15. First-smartphone age is not a universal scientific threshold; powers/use/night access/readiness matter.
16. Family religious practice is a real means/formative context but empirical religiosity does not prove regeneration.
17. Safeguarding is not replaced by church discipline.
18. Boredom/loneliness can be vulnerabilities without excusing voluntary sin.
19. Being caught ≠ repentance; apology/tears/vows ≠ restored trust or proven heart change.
20. Repeated relapse ≠ automatically settled impenitence; settled defense/concealment/refusal of correction is a different pattern.
21. Deut. 21 judicial penalty is not a household sanction today; 1689 19.4 general-equity control applies.
22. Excommunication ≠ universal ban from hearing public preaching.
23. Unbaptized/unprofessing teen ≠ automatically a 1 Cor. 5 “brother” discipline case.
24. For minors, homelessness/abandonment must never be used as discipline; adult household boundaries are separate prudential/legal category.
25. “Hidden persona feels more real” is a phenomenological/pastoral description, not scientific proof of a metaphysical `true self`.
26. Biblical `flesh` is not identical with body/sexuality; Spirit-enabled mortification must not be reduced to generic self-control psychology.
27. Sin may provide real short-term pleasure; temporary reward does not negate guilt/destructive end.
28. `Knows it is wrong` does not imply active inner debate at every episode; habitual lust may coexist with retained doctrinal knowledge and greatly reduced practical resistance.
29. Do not equate biblical hardening/callousness with neurological “brain switched off.”
30. Do not teach unregenerate people that cessation of prayer/song is the cure. Outward duty cannot cover rebellion; call them to repent and worship truly.
31. Public hearing of Word does not create entitlement to every confidential/small-group/youth/ministry context.
32. Restrictions on an outsider for concrete corrupting/safeguarding risk are prudential shepherding, not fictional 1 Cor. 5 membership discipline.
33. A known struggling/confessing sinner must not be collapsed into the same category as one who defends, recruits others into, and refuses correction regarding sin.
34. Rumor/suspicion is insufficient for punitive exclusion; facts, conversation, due process and safeguarding distinctions matter.
35. Scripture contains no inspired children’s-choir eligibility code. Choir/ministry standards must be labeled pastoral/ecclesial application.
36. Child-appropriate holiness ≠ adult-level maturity; childhood also ≠ moral exemption.
37. Educational/catechetical singing may reasonably have a lower access threshold than formal representative worship ministry.
38. Genuine repentance does not automatically restore sensitive/ministry access immediately; forgiveness, trust, role fitness and safeguarding are distinct questions.
39. Puritan paedobaptist assumptions (especially Baxter) are not controlling for the 1689 Reformed Baptist ecclesiology of the future article.
40. Satan’s devices never erase the sinner’s own lust/responsibility; technology is infrastructure, not a fourth source of sin.
