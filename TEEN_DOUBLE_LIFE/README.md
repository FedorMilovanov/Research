## 2. Locked project theses

- Digital technology did not create the sinful heart; it can radically alter **access, privacy, persistence, peer presence, recommendation, scale and concealment**.
- Sovereign regeneration is not random grace and does not make upbringing/means irrelevant.
- Godly upbringing is genuinely formative but does not mechanically regenerate.
- `unregenerate ≠ innocent`; God’s moral law binds all.
- childhood is not a moral exemption zone and not a neutral waiting room before conversion.
- external restraint is not regeneration, but can still be wise, useful and required.
- parents must combine real discipline with Eph. 6:4 / Col. 3:21 non-provocation; indulgence and arbitrary domination are both rejected.
- child-specific weakness/temptation can justify child-specific boundaries.
- Matt. 5:29–30 supports radical removal of occasions of sin, not self-harm or parental abuse.
- a teen may still know an act is wrong while practically ceasing to debate it; use biblical categories of lust, yielding, deceit, hardening and callousness rather than pseudo-neuroscience.
- sin may provide real temporary sweetness while hiding the end (Heb. 11:25; Prov. 9:17–18).
- `caught ≠ confessed ≠ voluntarily disclosed ≠ asked for help before falling`.
- repentance is not proved by tears, panic, temporary compliance or one apology.
- repeated fall does not automatically prove false conversion; 1689 ch. 17 permits grievous falls of true believers.
- recurring struggle with truth/warfare must be distinguished from planned concealment, defense, recruitment and refusal of correction.
- public hearing of the Word ≠ membership ≠ Table ≠ choir/platform ≠ leadership ≠ trusted access to youth.
- church must remain open to sinners hearing the gospel while refusing to normalize known, settled, corrupting rebellion as acceptable Christian life.
- children’s choir/ministry standards are **prudential ecclesial applications**, not an inspired NT admission statute.
- known corrupting influence may justify restricting close-group/platform/youth access even for an outsider who remains welcome to hear public preaching.
- safeguarding/victimization must never be collapsed into moral/church-discipline categories.
- Deut. 21 judicial penalties are not modern household sanctions; 1689 19.4 general-equity guard applies.
- minors must never be made homeless as discipline; adult household boundaries are a separate prudential/legal category.
- psychology no longer governs the main architecture; `dopamine damage`, universal pornography escalation, generic reward-neuroscience and metaphysical “false self” claims are rejected/demoted.
