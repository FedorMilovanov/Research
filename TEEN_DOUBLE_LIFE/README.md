## 2. Locked project theses

- Digital technology did not create the sinful heart; it can radically alter **access, privacy, persistence, peer presence, recommendation, scale, concealment and conversational availability**.
- AI assistants/companions add a new private conversational pathway; they are not a fourth spiritual enemy beside world/flesh/devil and their use is not automatically sinful.
- Sovereign regeneration does not make upbringing/means irrelevant.
- Godly upbringing is genuinely formative but does not mechanically regenerate.
- `unregenerate ≠ innocent`; childhood is not a moral waiting room.
- External restraint is not regeneration, but can be wise/required; strong boundaries must coexist with Eph. 6:4 / Col. 3:21 non-provocation.
- A teen may retain correct moral knowledge while repeatedly yielding until truth exerts less practical rule; use biblical categories of lust, deceit, hardening and callousness rather than pseudo-neuroscience.
- Sin may provide real temporary sweetness while hiding its end (Prov. 9:17–18; Heb. 11:25).
- `caught ≠ confessed ≠ voluntarily disclosed ≠ asked for help before falling`.
- Repentance is not proved by tears, panic, temporary compliance or one apology.
- Repeated fall does not automatically prove false conversion; recurring struggle + truth/warfare is not identical to planned concealment + defense + recruitment + refusal of correction.
- Greater gospel light can aggravate guilt without proving regeneration or making God less able to save.
- A baptism candidate is not retrospectively a member; a baptized minor can simultaneously be under parental authority and church discipline.
- Formal excommunication and pastoral/stewardship restrictions for a never-member are not juridically identical.
- Public hearing of the Word ≠ membership/Table ≠ choir/platform ≠ leadership ≠ confidential/trusted youth access.
- Known corrupting conduct may justify restricting trusted/representative access while keeping the gospel summons open.
- Safeguarding/victimization must never be collapsed into moral/church-discipline categories.
- Voluntary sexual sin, romantic/peer coercion, nonconsensual redistribution, grooming and sextortion are distinct moral/safeguarding states; one person’s earlier sin does not make another person’s later abuse his fault.
- Adulthood changes parental jurisdiction, not God’s moral law or the family relation.
- Luke 15 is not a modern no-contact or contact-cadence manual; do not finance the “far country,” but do not manufacture suffering as a conversion technique.
- Forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ credibility ≠ trust ≠ access ≠ role ≠ safeguarding.
- Time does not create repentance; it allows fruit or lack of fruit to become visible.
- Conscious/repeated sin ≠ automatically Heb. 10:26 final apostasy.
- Blasphemous speech ≠ automatically the unpardonable sin.
- Accountability ≠ compulsory exhaustive sexual disclosure.
- Marriage is not a cure for protected lust/pornography.
- Radical removal of occasions must be followed by positive `put off → put on / flee → pursue` sanctification.
- **Fruit is evidence of repentance; fruit is not the price of justification.** Consequences do not atone. Christ alone is the ground of pardon/righteousness.
- No invented `internet ×20`, universal dopamine damage, universal pornography escalation or automatic `seared conscience forever` diagnosis.
