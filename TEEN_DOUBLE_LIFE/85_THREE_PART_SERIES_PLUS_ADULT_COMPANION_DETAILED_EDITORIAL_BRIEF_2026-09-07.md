# 0. Product concept

Build a **three-part core series** about adolescent double life, followed by a **separate adult-child/prodigal companion**.

Why separate adult-child piece:

- civil adulthood changes jurisdiction;
- Luke 15 / household / non-enabling / contact cadence introduce a new pastoral problem;
- forcing that lane into the adolescent series overloads the core argument;
- concurrent research agent has already built enough material for a substantial independent article.

Core series progression:

> **heart and hidden life → home and shepherding → church and holiness**

Companion:

> **when the child becomes an adult and walks out of parental jurisdiction**

---

# 1. Series identity

The series should not be marketed primarily as:

> “internet dangers for Christian teenagers.”

Better identity:

> **A biblical anatomy of the double life of children raised around the faith—how desire, secrecy, digital opportunity, family formation, and church culture interact, and what repentance and faithful shepherding require.**

The digital age is a major structural theme, not the root doctrine.

# 2. Part I editorial brief

## Provisional promise

> **Why can a teenager who knows Christian language, attends church, and appears normal at home build a second life of pornography, profanity, sexualized conversation, secret accounts, and deception—and why can that life become increasingly easy to inhabit?**

### Candidate title families

Do not finalize yet.

- `Второй ребёнок в вашем доме`
- `Две жизни одного подростка`
- `Украденные воды сладки: анатомия тайной жизни подростка`
- `Когда церковный ребёнок живёт второй жизнью`

### Opening scene strategy

Use a **composite pattern**, explicitly not a claimed real case.

Example structure:

- Sunday: child sings, talks normally, knows answers;
- same evening: phone/secret chat/sexual joke/pornography/lying;
- parents see first role;
- peers see another;
- question: which world is training him more often?

Avoid lurid sexual detail.

Core:

- Mark 7;
- James 1;
- total depravity;
- technology is not root.

Prevent article from becoming generational panic.

Core:

### Section 4 — The pocket infrastructure of secrecy

### Section 5 — Pornography: serious without fake neuroscience

Core:

Use only strongest evidence.

### Section 6 — How the second life learns to hide

Core:

Core:

This prevents reckless assurance and reckless condemnation.

Do not fully teach parenting yet; set up Part II.

# 3. Part II editorial brief

## Provisional promise

> **If only God gives a new heart, what can parents actually do—and how can they combine serious protection, discipline, trust, and gospel hope without either abandoning the child to the world or trying to control regeneration?**

### Candidate title families

- `Родители не спасают — но они не зрители`
- `Как ограждать ребёнка, не выращивая тюрьму`
- `Дом, где правду не выгоднее скрывать`
- `Воспитать нельзя возродить — но нельзя и не воспитывать`

### Section 1 — Sovereignty is not parental fatalism

Core:

- God alone regenerates;
- means are real;
- 1689 10/14;
- Deut. 6;
- Eph. 6.

Reject:

> “He is unregenerate, let him live.”

### Section 2 — No moral waiting room for the unregenerate child

Core:

- Eph. 6 direct commands to children;
- 1689 19.5;
- law/gospel distinction.

Important:

Do not coerce false profession.

### Section 3 — Upbringing really forms

Core:

- Prov. 22:6 wisdom pattern;
- example;
- discipline;
- church;
- peers;
- habits;
- faith conversation.

Use empirical faith transmission lightly, as corroboration not theology.

### Section 4 — Father and mother must know the child they actually have

Core:

- presence;
- conversation;
- peculiar weaknesses;
- Owen on occasions;
- children differ.

Avoid deterministic father-blame/mother-blame.

### Section 5 — Do not throw a child into the world to make him strong

Core:

- graded responsibility;
- no exposure-as-training to pornography/predation;
- discernment built under increasing freedom.

### Section 6 — Pluck out the eye: radical boundaries

Core:

- Matt. 5:29–30;
- 2 Tim. 2:22;
- 1 Cor. 6:18;
- Rom. 13:14.

Applications:

- phone outside bedroom;
- app/account removal;
- restricted browser;
- friendship boundaries;
- trip/access changes.

Case-specific, not universal.

### Section 7 — Strong rules without provoking the child

Core:

- Eph. 6:4;
- Col. 3:21;
- Baxter/Watson;
- indulgence vs arbitrary harshness.

Key line:

> love is not on probation; freedoms can be.

### Section 8 — Build a truth channel before the scandal

Core:

- ordinary conversation about lust/sex/porn;
- ask, listen, admonish;
- voluntary disclosure;
- accountability before fall.

### Section 9 — When the parent discovers it

Decision tree:

1. safety/victimization?
2. facts?
3. voluntary conduct?
4. corrupting others?
5. current access?
6. initial boundaries?
7. direct biblical naming?

Avoid interrogation frenzy.

### Section 10 — Being caught is not repentance

Core:

- 2 Cor. 7;
- Watson;
- 1689 15;
- tears/fear/deleted apps ≠ proof.

### Section 11 — What repentance begins to look like

Core:

- truth;
- no defense;
- light;
- removal of occasion;
- accepted consequences;
- restitution;
- help;
- time.

### Section 12 — Parents also come into the light

Examine:

- hypocrisy;
- harshness;
- absence;
- neglect;
- reputation idolatry;
- inconsistent rules;
- failure to apologize.

Do not use parent sin to excuse child sin.

### Part II ending

> **A family can confront sin seriously and still teach the child that confession opens the door to help rather than annihilation. But the family is not the only formative community. What happens when the church itself sees the hypocrisy and decides not to see it?**

# 4. Part III editorial brief

## Provisional promise

> **How should a church treat a child or teenager who is welcome to hear the gospel but is known to live in defended, corrupting rebellion—and what do choir, youth group, small group, membership, and discipline teach about holiness?**

### Candidate title families

- `Что церковь учит своим молчанием`
- `Греши и пой? Дети, святость и церковная платформа`
- `Слышать Слово — не значит иметь право на любую церковную роль`
- `Когда молодёжка становится укрытием двойной жизни`

### Section 1 — “He is only a child” is not theology

Core:

- Eph. 6;
- moral responsibility;
- unregenerate ≠ exempt.

### Section 2 — Singing does not cover rebellion

Central:

- Ps. 50:16–21.

Supporting:

- Isa. 1;
- Amos 5;
- Matt. 15.

Important 1689 16.7/22 guard:

not “never pray/sing until saved,” but “do not hide behind form; repent and worship truly.”

### Section 3 — Open the Word, not every door

Taxonomy:

- public preaching;
- congregational singing;
- catechesis;
- small group;
- choir/platform;
- leadership;
- access to vulnerable minors.

### Section 4 — The children’s choir problem

Core:

- no explicit NT choir statute;
- visible ministry can communicate trust/representation;
- serious known conduct can justify temporary removal;
- not every childhood sin triggers suspension.

### Section 5 — Struggling child vs corrupting child

Distinguish:

- weakness/confession/warfare;
- pornography distribution;
- sexual pressure;
- recruitment into deception;
- mocking correction;
- using church for romantic/sexual access.

### Section 6 — Youth group is not morally neutral territory

Bad company + holy company.

- 1 Cor. 15:33;
- 2 Tim. 2:22;
- mentors/godly peers.

Church should build positive belonging, not merely police danger.

### Section 7 — Outsider vs “called a brother”

Core:

- 1 Cor. 14 outsider hearing;
- 1 Cor. 5 professing insider discipline.

Do not pretend every unbelieving teen is formally excommunicable.

### Section 8 — Safeguarding is not discipline

Core:

- exploitation/coercion/victimization distinctions;
- protect other children;
- forgiveness does not immediately restore sensitive access.

### Section 9 — Restoration

Core:

- repentant weak child not permanently branded;
- role can pause;
- trust rebuilds;
- church should rejoice in credible repentance.

Use Sibbes as tenderness guard.

### Section 10 — The theology children learn from procedures

Closing challenge:

> church policy can teach holiness or practical hypocrisy.

### Part III ending

Return to gospel:

> holiness is not admission price paid by sinless children; it is not optional decoration either. The church must call sinners to Christ without making peace with the sin Christ calls them to leave.

# 5. Adult-child companion editorial brief

## Provisional promise

> **What changes when the child is now an adult: he leaves home, refuses parental control, may continue serious sin, but remains a son or daughter whom parents love, admonish, sometimes support, sometimes must stop enabling, and cannot regenerate?**

### Candidate title families

- `Когда блудный сын уже взрослый`
- `Отпустить контроль — не отпустить сына`
- `Не финансировать дальнюю страну`
- `Взрослый ребёнок ушёл: любовь, границы и надежда без контроля`

### Required sections

1. adulthood changes jurisdiction, not morality;
2. Luke 15 in context — not no-contact manual;
3. initial safety/fact clarification after secret departure;
4. contact can continue without coercive control;
5. limits of repeated admonition;
6. Rom. 12 truthful love / no revenge / real good;
7. James 5 restorative initiative;
8. non-enabling;
9. Israel analogy with severe guards;
10. adult co-residence rules;
11. protect younger siblings;
12. church discipline does not erase family duties — Owen/Gill/Edwards;
13. conditions of return;
14. joyful mercy without naive immediate trust restoration;
15. sovereign grace / prayer.

### Important editorial rule

This article must not become an eviction manual.

Country-specific legal advice stays out unless separately verified.

# 6. Shared series doctrine capsule

Every installment may include a short version of this idea:

> Human beings do not become sinners because the internet or their friends corrupt an originally neutral heart. Scripture locates evil desire within the fallen person. Yet God also treats parents, churches, companionship, habits, and opportunities as real means of formation and restraint. Only God gives a new heart; that does not make upbringing or discipline irrelevant. And because inability does not erase responsibility, an unregenerate child remains accountable to God and to lawful authority.

This is a **drafting concept**, not finalized prose.

Keep concise in publication.

# 9. Editorial verdict

This architecture is strong because each piece answers a distinct pastoral question while sharing one anthropology.

The adult-child article should be treated as a **companion**, not “Part IV,” unless later product strategy strongly favors numbered continuity.

Reason:

> the original series concerns the formation and shepherding of the adolescent double life; the adult-child piece concerns what happens when parental jurisdiction has already materially changed.
