# 3. Module 249 — obedience, body boundaries and safeguarding

## Classification

**P0 safety overlay:** Part II and Part III.  
This is not optional merely because the main series critiques autonomy.

## P0 material that may not disappear

- `BIBLICAL OBEDIENCE ≠ UNLIMITED ADULT ACCESS TO CHILD'S BODY`;
- `BODY BOUNDARIES ≠ CHILD SOVEREIGNTY`;
- no human authority may command sexual touching, sexual images, concealment of abuse, lying to protect an offender, dangerous isolation or coercive intimate contact;
- `HONOR ≠ MANDATORY INTIMATE TOUCH`;
- a known/respected/church adult is not therefore automatically safe;
- children need lawful routes to tell a safe adult about sexual contact, coercion, threats, violence or abuse even when the offender is respected or inside the family/church;
- “don't gossip,” “submit,” “forgive,” “keep confidence” and similar Christian categories must never be weaponized to conceal predation;
- pastoral confidentiality does not create an absolute command for a child to conceal harm;
- legitimate medical/hygiene/safety care must be distinguished from sexualized access;
- body-safety teaching must preserve God's ownership and parental stewardship without creating human sexual ownership of the child.

# 4. Module 250 — positive belonging / replacement

## Classification

**P0 positive-formation overlay:** Part II and Part III.  
**Empirical mentoring/prosocial-peer effect sizes:** reserve unless reader-facing prose needs a current evidential claim.

## P0 material that may not disappear

2 Tim. 2:22 gives a controlling threefold movement:

`FLEE youthful passions`

+ `PURSUE righteousness, faith, love, peace`

+ `WITH those who call on the Lord from a pure heart`.

Therefore:

- `REMOVE ≠ COMPLETE FORMATION`;
- after cutting corrupt chats, pornography access, a sinful romantic relationship or a corrupt peer circle, parents/church must ask what truthful and holy social world replaces it;
- Heb. 3:12–13 treats mutual exhortation as a real anti-hardening means without making community a regeneration technology;
- Christian formation should not become a peer-only youth subculture; intergenerational discipleship is biblically normal;
- `INTERGENERATIONAL CHURCH ≠ UNSUPERVISED ADULT-TEEN INTIMACY` — safeguarding from `249` remains controlling;
- mentors are not substitute parents, private saviors or secret parallel authorities;
- good company can form toward good; peer influence is not intrinsically sinful;
- the goal is not to destroy the adolescent desire to belong, but to reorder belonging around truth, holiness and service.

## Product placement

Part II: positive replacement after access restriction / corrupt-company separation.  
Part III: church as truthful, intergenerational, safeguarded community rather than mere attendance venue.

# 5. Cross-module synthesis

These four modules together correct four opposite simplifications:

1. `strong feelings = covenant` — false;
2. `rebellion = maturation` — false;
3. `obedience = unlimited adult bodily access` — false;
4. `removing bad influence = complete discipleship` — false.

The integrated conservative position is stronger:

> **Adolescents should grow in real agency without moral sovereignty; romantic attachment remains below covenant; lawful authority remains bounded by God; and separation from corruption should be joined to positive pursuit and safeguarded belonging.**
