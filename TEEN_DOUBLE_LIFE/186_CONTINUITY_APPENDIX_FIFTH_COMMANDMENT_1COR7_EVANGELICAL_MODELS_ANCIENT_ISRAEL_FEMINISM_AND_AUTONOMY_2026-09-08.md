## Locked finding 1 — fifth commandment is relational, not atomistic

Westminster Larger Catechism 124–132 gives a particularly useful grammar:

- real superiors/inferiors/equals exist;
- duties correspond to **several relations**;
- lawful commands/counsels deserve obedience/deference;
- authority is received from God and bounded by the nature of the relation;
- superiors themselves sin through unlawful commands, tyranny, provocation, unjust rigor or sinful remissness.

Therefore reject both:

`adult birthday => no special parental moral claim`

and

`parental status => unlimited command jurisdiction forever`.

## Locked finding 2 — adulthood changes authority without abolishing filial honour

Calvin presses parental subjection much harder than modern evangelical culture and requires deference even when lawful commands are inconvenient/difficult; God remains the ceiling.

Gill explicitly says parental power is less when children are grown while observance, gratitude and reverence continue.

Modern Reformed witnesses such as Sinclair Ferguson similarly distinguish childhood obedience from lifelong honour after a new household is formed.

P0:

> **Reduced power is not dissolved relationship; enduring honour is not perpetual childhood.**

## Locked finding 3 — 1 Corinthians 7 complicates every simplistic model

The chapter contains real evidence in both directions:

- unmarried woman/virgin is a direct servant of the Lord, concerned with holiness and the Lord's affairs;
- vv. 36–38 have a substantial traditional father/guardian reading and can support strong parental marriage authority;
- the same father-reading in Calvin/Poole still limits tyranny and requires regard for the daughter's welfare/desire;
- an alternative fiancé reading is exegetically substantial;
- v. 39 explicitly describes a widow as free to marry whom she wishes, `only in the Lord`.

Therefore:

> 1 Cor. 7 cannot honestly sustain either `parental involvement is unbiblical` or `every adult unmarried woman requires a male human governor for all major decisions`.

## Locked finding 4 — “male covering” is not a controlling biblical phrase

Use direct biblical relations instead:

- child/parent;
- wife/her own husband;
- member/elders;
- citizen/magistrate;
- believer/Christ.

If a stronger father-guardianship-until-marriage model is argued, it must be labelled as a cumulative household inference, not smuggled in as the direct NT doctrine of husband headship.

## Locked finding 5 — three modern evangelical streams must be distinguished

### Egalitarian/mutualist

Usually mutualizes Eph. 5 authority, rejects role hierarchy and emphasizes direct female agency/equality.

### Mainstream complementarian

Maintains husband headship / wife submission and male eldership while typically treating adult parental blessing as morally weighty but not absolute.

### Strong household / father-guardianship

Often teaches unmarried daughter remains under father until marriage and strongly father-governed courtship.

No future agent may collapse these into one binary `Bible vs feminism` without showing the actual claims and texts.

## Locked finding 6 — critique feminism precisely

Distinguish at least:

- civil/legal women's rights;
- cultural individualism;
- sexual-revolution feminism;
- evangelical egalitarian theology;
- radical anti-patriarchal ideology.

The project may strongly oppose egalitarian exegesis and modern autonomous selfhood without claiming that education, property ownership, paid work or economic competence are inherently feminist rebellion.

## Locked finding 7 — ancient Israel is corporate and patriarchal, but not one-dimensional

Historical scholarship strongly supports:

- patrilineal/patrilocal kinship;
- household as primary economic/social unit;
- marriage as family/kinship transition;
- real paternal authority;
- substantial female household labour/economic contribution;
- diverse father-daughter relations;
- age/status distinctions that matter legally;
- female agency alongside hierarchy.

Use feminist scholarship for descriptive reconstruction where sound; do not import its normative theology automatically.

## Locked finding 8 — presentism can be liberal or conservative

Liberal presentism:

> ancient hierarchy differs from modern equality, therefore it is morally obsolete.

Conservative presentism:

> ancient Israel had this civil/household form, therefore Christians must replicate the whole form.

Both are methodologically unsafe.

Every transfer requires category/jurisdiction/covenant analysis.

## Locked cultural synthesis

The project's strongest defensible cultural critique is not:

> `feminism invented disobedient daughters`.

Prefer:

> **Modern Western individualism, reinforced by important feminist currents, often treats inherited dependence and asymmetrical authority as presumptively suspect in a way foreign to Scripture's relational and covenantal social imagination.**

This avoids monocausal history and keeps the critique theological rather than rhetorical.

## Strong final synthesis

> **The biblical alternative to feminist individualism is not paternal absolutism. It is ordered creaturely dependence under God: real parental authority, real adult responsibility, real female agency, real husband headship in marriage, real church authority, real limits on every human authority, and lifelong obligations of honour and love.**
