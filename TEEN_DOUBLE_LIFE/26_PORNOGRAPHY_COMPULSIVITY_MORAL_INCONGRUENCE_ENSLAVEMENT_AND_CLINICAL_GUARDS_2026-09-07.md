# Порнография: компульсивность, moral incongruence, привычка и библейское рабство греху

**Статус:** RESEARCH / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Дата:** 2026-09-07

---

## 1. Почему этот модуль нужен

В церковной речи слово **«зависимость»** часто используется очень широко:

- «смотрел несколько раз»;
- «не может остановиться»;
- «чувствует сильную вину»;
- «возвращается годами»

могут быть названы одним словом.

Но future article должна различать:

1. moral sin;
2. repeated habit;
3. subjective feeling «я addicted»;
4. problematic pornography use (PPU);
5. possible compulsive sexual behavior disorder (CSBD);
6. distress from moral incongruence;
7. biblical category of slavery/mastery by sin.

Эти категории пересекаются, но **не идентичны**.

---

## 2. Clinical diagnosis is narrower than «I feel addicted»

Current ICD-11-oriented reviews describe CSBD as persistent failure to control intense/repetitive sexual impulses or behavior, typically over at least months, with:

- repeated unsuccessful control attempts;
- priority over responsibilities;
- continued behavior despite negative consequences;
- significant distress/impairment.

High sexual frequency alone is insufficient.

Distress caused **only by moral disapproval** is also insufficient.

Sources:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5775124/
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13132360/

### Critical adolescent guard

ICD-oriented guidance specifically warns against diagnosing common adolescent sexual interest/behavior as CSBD merely because it produces distress.

This is essential protection against clinical overreach.

---

## 3. Moral incongruence is real — but does not mean the moral standard is false

`Moral incongruence` means conflict between behavior and moral convictions.

Research shows it can contribute to **self-perceived addiction** independently of actual use frequency/dysregulation.

Adult studies:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32955140/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32027146/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42013354/

### Very important theological distinction

A clinician may correctly say:

> moral distress alone is not proof of psychiatric disorder.

That does **not** imply:

> therefore the behavior is morally harmless.

Psychiatric diagnosis and Christian moral judgment answer different questions.

### Strong formula

> **Не всякая сильная вина доказывает клиническую зависимость; и отсутствие клинической зависимости не делает pornography допустимой.**

## 4. Adolescents can show genuinely compulsive patterns

Longitudinal study of male Croatian adolescents found that boys reporting compulsive-use features tended to have higher pornography-use levels; among more religious compulsive users, use increased over time from lower initial levels.

Источник:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29676698/

The authors note implications both for real compulsivity and moral-incongruence theory.

### Guard

This does not establish prevalence of clinical CSBD among church boys.

It shows that **some adolescents reporting compulsivity also demonstrate behavioral patterns consistent with real dysregulation**, so dismissing everything as «religious guilt» would also be wrong.

---

## 5. Recent adolescent research finds heterogeneous problem profiles

2025 study among Israeli-Arab adolescents examined profiles involving:

- alcohol-use disorder patterns;
- problematic pornography use;
- compulsive sexual behavior;
- mental health;
- moral disapproval;
- thought suppression;
- self-concealment.

Источник:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40551074/

The importance for our project is heterogeneity:

> two teenagers saying «I cannot stop pornography» may not have the same mechanism or severity.

Possible dominant patterns:

- frequent use + loss of control;
- lower use + severe shame/moral conflict;
- pornography as distress coping;
- peer-driven use;
- established sexual compulsivity;
- episodic experimentation.

---

## 6. Pornography and psychopathological symptoms: association, not simple one-way causality

2026 systematic review/meta-analysis:

- adolescent online pornography use;
- 14 studies in review / 12 meta-analysis;
- moderate positive association with psychopathological symptoms.

Authors suggest problematic use may sometimes serve as maladaptive coping for underlying distress.

Источник:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41081900/

### Vicious-cycle model

Potential pattern:

`distress/loneliness → pornography for relief → shame/secrecy/sleep loss → more distress → return for relief`.

This is plausible maintenance model, not universal causal sequence.

---

## 7. Thought suppression can be part of the problem

A child may respond to sexual temptation with:

> «I must never think about this thought.»

Paradoxically, aggressive suppression can increase salience for some people.

Future article should distinguish:

- **mortification** — rejecting desire/action and redirecting to righteousness;
- **panic suppression** — obsessive internal checking that may amplify distress.

This is especially important in highly scrupulous/religious adolescents.

Do not confuse clinical scrupulosity/OCD with tender conscience without assessment.

---

## 8. Biblical «enslavement» is morally deeper and diagnostically broader than CSBD

John 8:34 / Rom. 6 describe sin as slavery/mastery.

This is not a DSM/ICD diagnosis.

A person can be **biblically enslaved to sin** without meeting psychiatric criteria for CSBD.

Conversely, a clinical compulsive pattern does not remove moral responsibility, though impaired control and mental-health factors matter pastorally.

### Therefore

Do not translate:

`slave of sin` = `behavioral addict`.

And do not translate:

`not clinically addicted` = `spiritually free`.

---

## 9. «Sex drags deeper» should be described carefully

We can support several mechanisms:

- immediate sexual reward;
- repetition/habit;
- cue-triggered desire;
- secrecy;
- novelty seeking;
- peer normalization;
- use as coping;
- opportunity architecture.

We cannot state as universal law:

> every teen necessarily needs more extreme content over time because dopamine receptors become damaged.

Current evidence does not justify that rhetoric.

### Stronger formulation

> **Повторяемый сексуальный грех может становиться всё привычнее, всё быстрее запускаться знакомыми сигналами и всё глубже встраиваться в способы справляться со скукой, стрессом и одиночеством — даже если конкретная траектория контента у разных подростков различается.**

---

## 10. Frequency alone is not severity

Two users might both view pornography weekly.

Person A:

- no failed attempts to stop;
- no functional impairment;
- strong moral regret.

Person B:

- repeatedly fails to stop;
- loses sleep;
- bypasses safeguards;
- escalates time spent;
- neglects responsibilities;
- continues despite major consequences.

Same frequency does not mean same clinical severity.

### Christian moral assessment

Both may require repentance from pornography.

But intervention intensity and clinical evaluation may differ substantially.

---

## 11. Conversely, low frequency does not mean morally trivial

One episode can involve:

- child sexual abuse material;
- coercion;
- nonconsensual images;
- distribution of peer intimate image;
- predatory contact.

Thus risk/severity must consider **content and harm**, not merely count.

---

## 12. Signs suggesting need for professional assessment

Not a diagnostic checklist, but red flags for referral can include:

- repeated failed attempts to control;
- marked functional impairment;
- major sleep disruption;
- severe depression/anxiety;
- suicidal ideation;
- trauma history;
- compulsive sexual behavior across contexts;
- significant escalation in time/risk;
- self-harm/shame crisis;
- exploitation/coercion.

Professional help should supplement, not replace, pastoral/parental moral care.

---

## 13. The church must not weaponize clinical language

Avoid:

> «You are an addict for life.»

Especially with minors.

Such identity language can:

- foster hopelessness;
- confuse behavior with identity;
- overstate diagnosis;
- reduce perceived agency.

Better to describe observed pattern precisely:

> «Ты много раз возвращался, обходил ограничения, терял сон и не смог остановиться, несмотря на последствия. Это серьёзный pattern; нам нужна и духовная, и, возможно, профессиональная помощь.»

---

## 14. Nor should moral-incongruence research be weaponized against conscience

Opposite abuse:

> «You only feel bad because religion taught you pornography is wrong.»

That may pathologize moral conviction.

The scientific point is narrower:

- moral conflict can increase subjective distress/self-labeling;
- clinical diagnosis must require dysregulation/impairment beyond moral distress alone.

It cannot adjudicate whether pornography is morally permissible.

---

## 15. Repentance and recovery overlap but are not identical

A teenager may:

- genuinely repent and still struggle with strong habit/cues;
- reduce behavior without repenting;
- feel enormous shame without changing;
- clinically improve while still morally rationalizing pornography.

Therefore track multiple dimensions:

### Moral/spiritual
- confession;
- forsaking;
- truthfulness;
- willingness to cut off access;
- restitution where harm occurred.

### Behavioral
- frequency/time;
- circumvention;
- high-risk content;
- sleep/function.

### Psychological
- distress;
- coping;
- shame;
- trauma;
- compulsivity.

### Relational
- secrecy;
- trust;
- accountability;
- peer context.

---

## 16. Gospel hope must not become minimization

Christian message is not:

> «Grace means it’s no big deal.»

Nor:

> «You have ruined your brain/soul permanently.»

It is:

- sin is serious enough for Christ’s cross;
- forgiveness is real;
- mortification is costly;
- patterns may require long repair;
- consequences can remain;
- no clinical label can define final spiritual destiny.

## 17. Publication-ready claims

**Claim A:** self-perceived pornography addiction and clinically meaningful dysregulation are not identical.  
**Strength:** strong clinical/research consensus.

**Claim B:** moral incongruence can amplify self-perceived addiction/distress, but does not itself establish or refute moral wrongdoing.  
**Strength:** robust adult literature; adolescent application guarded.

**Claim C:** some adolescents show longitudinal patterns consistent with genuine compulsive pornography use.  
**Strength:** limited but direct adolescent evidence.

**Claim D:** pornography may function as maladaptive coping for distress in some adolescents.  
**Strength:** recent meta-analytic synthesis, non-universal.

**Claim E:** biblical slavery to sin is not a psychiatric diagnosis and should not be collapsed into CSBD.  
**Strength:** theological-category distinction.

---

## 18. Strong conclusion

> **Нельзя утешать подростка ложью: “это только чувство вины, потому что ты верующий”. Но нельзя и пугать ложью: “раз ты возвращался к pornography, ты теперь навсегда клинический зависимый”. Писание требует назвать грех грехом; наука требует назвать диагноз диагнозом только тогда, когда для него есть основания. Именно эта двойная точность даёт место и ответственности, и реальной помощи, и надежде.**
