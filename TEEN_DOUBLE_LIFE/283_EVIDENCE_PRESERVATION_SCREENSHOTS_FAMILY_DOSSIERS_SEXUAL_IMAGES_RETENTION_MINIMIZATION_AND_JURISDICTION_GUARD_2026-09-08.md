## 0. Executive verdict

Digital discovery creates a special temptation:

> `If evidence exists, save everything forever and show it to everyone who needs convincing.`

That is not a sound universal rule.

The opposite error is also dangerous:

> `Delete everything immediately so the family can move on.`

In exploitation/sextortion/abuse cases, deleting accounts/messages can destroy information useful to competent authorities.

### Locked center

> **PRESERVE WHAT DUTY REQUIRES; DO NOT TURN PRESERVATION INTO CIRCULATION.**

> **EVIDENCE PRESERVATION ≠ FAMILY OWNERSHIP OF SEXUAL MATERIAL.**

> **DOCUMENTING A FACT ≠ COPYING EVERY EXPLICIT FILE.**

> **NEED-TO-KNOW ≠ NEED-TO-POSSESS.**

> **A SAFETY CASE MAY REQUIRE PRESERVATION; A FAMILY DISCIPLINE CASE DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY REQUIRE A PERMANENT DOSSIER.**

> **EXACT RETENTION / DELETION / REPORTING RULES ARE JURISDICTION-DEPENDENT.**

# 1. First distinguish the evidence purpose

Before saving/copying anything, ask what duty the evidence serves.

Possible legitimate purposes:

1. immediate safety;
2. showing that a material lie occurred;
3. identifying an active account/contact;
4. preserving information for competent safeguarding/law-enforcement process;
5. correcting a public falsehood;
6. documenting a temporary church/household decision;
7. proving restitution/property harm.

Bad or weak purposes include:

- proving to extended family how shocking the child was;
- keeping graphic material as leverage in future arguments;
- satisfying curiosity;
- repeatedly viewing images/messages to maintain anger;
- building a permanent character dossier after the stewardship purpose ended.

### P0

`EVIDENCE SHOULD HAVE AN OWNER, A PURPOSE, AND A BOUNDARY`.

This is `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`, not a universal data-retention statute.

---

# 2. Scripture values truthful witness without sanctifying unlimited exposure

Canonical controls:

- Ex. 20:16 — truthful witness;
- Prov. 18:13, 17 — hear/examine rather than answer prematurely;
- Eph. 4:25 — put away falsehood, speak truth;
- Matt. 18 — proportionate relational/church process;
- Eph. 5:11–12 — expose darkness without making shameful detail itself virtuous.

Existing `165`/`266` own the central application:

> **truth must be specific enough to destroy the lie, but not graphic enough to reproduce the sin unnecessarily.**

### Locked

`TRUTHFUL RECORD ≠ MAXIMUM RECORD`.

`EXPOSURE OF DARKNESS ≠ REPRODUCTION OF DARKNESS`.

# 3. Ordinary household evidence and safeguarding evidence are different lanes

## Ordinary household lane

Examples:

- hidden social account;
- bypassed device rule;
- repeated lie about contact;
- pornography history without evidence of exploitation/other victims.

A parent may reasonably preserve limited non-graphic proof such as:

- account name;
- date/time relevant to contradiction;
- relevant text line or activity log;
- note of what was found and what decision followed.

This does not mean copying the entire sexual archive.

## Safeguarding / criminal / exploitation lane

Examples:

- sextortion;
- grooming;
- adult-minor contact;
- coercion;
- sexual image distribution;
- abuse;
- credible threats.

Now evidence preservation may be important for external investigation.

Exact procedure must follow competent current jurisdiction guidance.

### Locked

`ORDINARY PARENTAL DOCUMENTATION ≠ FORENSIC EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT`.

# 4. Current FBI/NCMEC-linked sextortion guidance: do not simply delete the evidence

Current FBI guidance for financially motivated sextortion tells victims/families to:

- report the predator account;
- block contact;
- preserve profile/messages because they can help identify the offender;
- seek trusted-adult/law-enforcement help rather than paying or sending more material.

Sources:
- https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/common-frauds-and-scams/sextortion/financially-motivated-sextortion
- https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/fbi-and-partners-issue-national-public-safety-alert-on-financial-sextortion-schemes

### Safe project use

When exploitation is plausible:

> **do not impulsively erase the account/message trail merely to remove shame before competent advice/reporting.**

### Guard

This is U.S. public-safety guidance, not a universal legal instruction for every jurisdiction.

Evidence class: `CURRENT SAFEGUARDING/PUBLIC-SAFETY SOURCE`.

# 5. “Preserve messages” does not mean “download and circulate explicit images”

This distinction is crucial.

APSAC's 2025 Practice Guidelines explicitly oppose showing child sexual abuse material and sexted images to parents, guardians and other adults.

Source:
- https://apsac.org/practice-guidelines/
- https://apsac.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/APSAC-Opposing-Showing-CSAM-and-Sexted-Images-to-Parents_October2025.pdf

### Project synthesis

If a sexual image exists:

- parents/pastors do not need to view it merely to feel convinced;
- do not forward it to spouse/elders/friends;
- do not save extra copies into ordinary photo backups;
- do not make the child display it as a confession ritual;
- do not project/read/show explicit material to family/youth group.

### P0

`PRESERVE A CASE ≠ MULTIPLY THE IMAGE`.

`KNOW THAT AN IMAGE EXISTS ≠ EVERY RESPONSIBLE ADULT MUST SEE IT`.

Exact technical handling: `CIVIL_OR_SAFEGUARDING_DEPENDENT`.

# 6. Minimize duplication

Digital systems automatically proliferate copies through:

- cloud photo sync;
- messaging backups;
- family-shared albums;
- screenshots;
- email forwarding;
- shared drives;
- pastor/team chat;
- phone-to-PC backup.

Therefore a parent who thinks:

> `I am only keeping evidence`

may unintentionally create many durable copies.

### P0

Where sexual/minor imagery may be involved:

> **DO NOT CREATE NEW COPIES MERELY FOR CONVENIENCE OR FAMILY REVIEW.**

Use competent authority/platform guidance for actual preservation/reporting.

### Evidence class

`SAFEGUARDING PRUDENCE`; exact operational/legal rules remain jurisdiction-dependent.

---

# 7. Non-graphic evidence is often enough for ordinary accountability

Suppose parents need to establish:

> `contact continued after the child said it ended`.

The relevant proof may be:

- account identity;
- message timestamp;
- non-explicit contact line;
- activity log.

There may be no stewardship need to preserve:

- erotic image;
- whole explicit conversation;
- exact sexual description.

### Locked

> **PRESERVE THE PROPOSITION YOU NEED TO ESTABLISH, NOT EVERY SENSATIONAL DETAIL SURROUNDING IT.**

This is a prudential minimization principle.

---

# 8. A “family evidence folder” can become a permanent punishment technology

After repeated deception, parents may accumulate:

- screenshots;
- timelines;
- saved voice notes;
- copied chats;
- written confessions;
- old account exports.

Some temporary documentation can be useful.

But a dossier can later become:

- ammunition in unrelated conflicts;
- material shown to relatives;
- a way to freeze the child in the worst period of life;
- a source of voyeuristic re-exposure;
- proof that no restoration is ever allowed to become real.

### Locked pair

`GRACE DOES NOT REQUIRE AMNESIA`.

And:

`MEMORY OF REAL HISTORY ≠ PERMANENT ARCHIVE OF EVERY PRIVATE ARTIFACT`.

---

# 9. Define a review point for ordinary non-forensic records

For ordinary family records, ask periodically:

1. Does this item still serve a current safety/repair/trust decision?
2. Is a church/civil/safeguarding process still relying on it?
3. Would deleting it now create a real risk or merely reduce parental reassurance?
4. Can the necessary fact be retained in a less intrusive/non-graphic form?
5. Who still needs access?
6. Is the record being used outside its original purpose?

### P0

`NO FIXED UNIVERSAL RETENTION PERIOD`.

The project must not invent a one-year/three-year/five-year biblical timetable.

Evidence class: `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`.

---

# 10. Record facts, not loaded character labels

Bad family note:

> `He is a manipulative sexual liar.`

Better factual note:

> `On 8 Sept, account X showed contact after he had stated contact ended on 4 Sept.`

Why this matters:

- facts can be reviewed;
- labels globalize identity;
- factual records distinguish established history from interpretation;
- future restoration can coexist with accurate past documentation.

### Locked

`DOCUMENT THE EVENT ≠ ESSENTIALIZE THE PERSON`.

This aligns with person-shame guards in `28`, `243`, `266`.

---

# 11. Distinguish evidence from confession notes

A written confession may itself contain:

- sensitive third-party information;
- sexual detail;
- victim information;
- allegations requiring safeguarding.

Do not automatically:

- email it to all elders;
- place it in general youth-leader files;
- keep it in shared family cloud;
- quote it in future sermons/testimonies.

### P0

`PASTORAL NEED-TO-KNOW ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL ALL-ACCESS`.

Church record policy is denominational/jurisdictional governance and should be current-policy checked before Product gives operations.

---

# 12. Third-party privacy can constrain family “evidence ownership”

A teenager's chat may contain private information about:

- another minor;
- victim;
- peer's family;
- mental health;
- sexual content;
- allegations against another person.

The fact that parents lawfully inspected their child's device does not mean every third-party detail becomes family social property.

### Locked

`PARENTAL ACCESS TO A DEVICE ≠ LICENSE TO PUBLISH EVERY PERSON IN THE DEVICE`.

Use `268` need-to-know/confidentiality controls.

---

# 13. Church evidence sharing should be role-bound

If elders need to assess:

- member discipline;
- role eligibility;
- safeguarding,

the information circle should be no broader than actual governance requires.

Possible distinctions:

- lead safeguarding person needs precise risk facts;
- elder body may need sufficient moral/jurisdiction facts;
- youth volunteers may only need operational restriction (`X is not to be alone with minors`);
- congregation may need only proportionate discipline/public correction information.

### P0

`NEED TO ACT ≠ NEED TO KNOW EVERY DETAIL`.

Use `167`, `282`.

---

# 14. Evidence must not become a threat

Corrupt uses:

- `If you disobey again, I will show everyone the screenshots.`
- `I still have your sexual messages and can prove what kind of person you are.`
- `If you leave our church, we will send these to your new church` without a legitimate safeguarding/discipline basis.

### Locked

> **EVIDENCE PRESERVED FOR TRUTH/SAFETY MUST NOT BECOME EXTORTIVE FAMILY OR ECCLESIAL LEVERAGE.**

This does not prohibit legitimate need-to-know safeguarding communication.

`WARNING ≠ BLACKMAIL`.

`SAFEGUARDING COMMUNICATION ≠ RETALIATORY DISCLOSURE`.

---

# 15. Evidence destruction can itself be morally relevant

The minimization guard must not become:

> `Delete evidence so nobody can hold you accountable.`

If a child knowingly destroys:

- messages;
- hidden account data;
- financial records;
- material needed to protect another person,

for the purpose of preserving deception, that destruction may itself be part of the covering pattern.

### Guard

Do not assume every missing/deleted digital item was intentionally destroyed:

- apps auto-delete;
- accounts expire;
- sync/storage fails;
- user may have ordinarily deleted content before discovery.

### Locked

`DELIBERATE EVIDENCE DESTRUCTION ≠ ORDINARY DATA LOSS`.

---

# 16. Restoration should eventually change who can access old records

Suppose the child demonstrates sustained truthfulness and current safety improves.

Even if some historical documentation remains legitimately retained, access can narrow.

Examples:

- parents no longer revisit it in routine arguments;
- siblings never had access and still do not;
- ordinary youth leaders do not retain copies;
- a safeguarding record remains with the responsible role rather than family circulation.

### Locked

`RECORD RETENTION ≠ PERMANENT ACTIVE USE`.

`HISTORICAL FACT ≠ PERMANENT PRESENT IDENTITY`.

---

# 17. The child may need truthful assurance about records

Where safe and appropriate, parents can clarify:

- what was preserved;
- why;
- who has access;
- what was not copied;
- whether a formal safeguarding/legal process controls it;
- that it will not be used for public humiliation.

Do not promise deletion if law/policy may require preservation.

Do not promise secrecy if safeguarding requires disclosure.

### P0

> **TRUST RESTORATION IS HELPED BY HONEST DATA GOVERNANCE, NOT SECRET PERMANENT ARCHIVES.**

This is prudential, not an empirical causal claim.

---

# 18. Product-ready decision split

## Ordinary deception / household rule case

Goal:

- establish material fact;
- minimal non-graphic documentation if needed;
- use present boundaries/time for trust restoration;
- review whether old records remain necessary.

## Exploitation / sextortion / abuse case

Goal:

- protect child/others;
- do not pay/comply with predator merely to stop threat;
- preserve relevant account/message trail;
- involve competent reporting/authority;
- avoid multiplying/showing sexual images.

## Church discipline case

Goal:

- enough reliable evidence for the actual ecclesial decision;
- role-bound access;
- proportionate public communication;
- no pornography-by-proof.

# 19. Product overclaim kill-switch

Reject or rewrite:

- `Always delete explicit material immediately.`
- `Always screenshot every message for evidence.`
- `Parents should view sexual images so they know what happened.`
- `Every elder should inspect the evidence personally.`
- `If evidence is real, keeping it forever is prudent.`
- `Restoration requires deleting all historical records.`
- `Keeping a factual record means the child is unforgiven.`
- `A parent may share screenshots with relatives because it is their child.`
- `Safeguarding confidentiality means no other church should ever be warned.`
- `Evidence preservation permits redistribution.`

Preserve:

> **Keep only what real truth, safety, repair or competent process requires; do not multiply intimate material merely because technology makes copying easy.**

# 21. Final locked synthesis

> **После обнаружения двойной жизни семье иногда нужно сохранить доказательство, а при шантаже, grooming или ином safeguarding-риске — не уничтожить полезный для компетентного расследования след. Но “сохранить факт” не значит “размножить сексуальный материал”. Родителям и служителям обычно не нужно владеть каждой фотографией и всей перепиской, чтобы знать, что серьёзный факт установлен. Цель — сохранить ровно то, что требуется правде, защите, repair и законному процессу, ограничить круг доступа и не превращать цифровые артефакты худшего периода жизни ребёнка в вечное семейное досье.**
