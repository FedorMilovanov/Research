# 4. David / Absalom guard

2 Samuel 18–19 is not a simple parenting manual.

Safe transfer:

- parental grief can remain intense even toward a rebel;
- the parent's own failures may intensify grief;
- grief can become disordered if it absorbs other real God-given duties.

Prohibited transfer:

- `David grieved → parents should preserve every practical privilege`;
- `Joab confronted David → grieving parents should simply get over it`.
