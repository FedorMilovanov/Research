# 1. `256` — parental unity without complicity

### P0

`PARENTAL UNITY ≠ COMPLICITY`.

`SPOUSAL LOYALTY ≠ FALSE WITNESS`.

`DISAGREEMENT ≠ UNDERMINING`.

`UNDERMINING ≠ RIGHTEOUS INTERVENTION`.

A child should not be recruited into parental coalitions, and parents should ordinarily avoid competing for the child's loyalty or casually undoing each other's lawful correction.

But `united front` must never mean affirming something known to be false, sinful, cruel or unsafe merely to preserve visible adult solidarity.

### Product use

One compact Part II guard near parental unity/consequences/mixed-fault. Cross-link `215` and `242/249` where safety is implicated.

# 4. `259` — consistent discipline without empty threats

### P0

`CONSISTENCY ≠ INFLEXIBILITY`.

`MERCY ≠ RANDOMNESS`.

`AUTHORITY ≠ LICENSE TO BLUFF`.

Parents may revise a decision because facts changed, because a threat was unwise, or because mercy is appropriate. That differs from repeatedly announcing consequences they do not intend/cannot lawfully carry out.

### Product use

Part II consequences paragraph. Emphasize truthful, predictable, proportionate government rather than a larger number of threats.

Do not psychologize child behavior as determined by parental inconsistency; responsibility remains bilateral.

# 6. `261` — conscience category map is a cross-cutting P0

This is one of the strongest late drafting controls.

Never collapse:

1. `DIRECT DIVINE MORAL COMMAND`;
2. `BIBLICAL WISDOM / PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`;
3. `LAWFUL HOUSEHOLD RULE`;
4. `PARENTAL PREFERENCE`;
5. `CIVIL LAW / EXTERNAL JURISDICTION`.

### Locked synthesis

> **The duty to obey a lawful parental rule may be divine even when the specific rule is not itself a universal divine moral law.**

And:

> **Parental authority can bind conduct within its lawful sphere; it cannot convert a human preference into God's universal command.**

### Example

A minor may be required to obey a family rule that phones charge in the kitchen at night. The specific kitchen/clock rule is not thereby God's universal command to all Christians.

### Product use

Mandatory whenever Part II discusses clothing, music, phone rules, curfew, courtship procedures, privacy, church schedule or other prudential domains.

Cross-link adult claim-evidence ladder `231` for changed jurisdiction after adulthood.

# 7. `262` — clothing/music/entertainment/peers/worldliness

### P0

`ADIAPHORA ≠ MORAL VACUUM`.

`LAWFUL ≠ ALWAYS WISE OR HELPFUL`.

`PARENTAL DISLIKE ≠ PROOF OF SIN`.

`NOT INTRINSICALLY SINFUL ≠ PARENT MUST ALLOW IT TO A DEPENDENT MINOR`.

`WORLDLINESS ≠ WHATEVER MY SUBCULTURE DISLIKES`.

`GENRE LABEL ≠ MORAL VERDICT`.

Cultural form, content, function, company, sexual/profane meaning and actual use must be distinguished.

### Product use

Part I peer/cultural-world overlay and Part II concrete-rule overlay. Keep it subordinate to the central hidden-life thesis.

The article may strongly condemn explicit sexual degradation, blasphemy, cruelty or idolatrous content without pretending a music genre, garment measurement or family taste is automatically universal revelation.

# 8. `263` — parental example, hypocrisy and repentant modeling

### P0

`TRUE STANDARD ≠ FALSE BECAUSE PARENT FAILS IT`.

`PARENTAL AUTHORITY ≠ HYPOCRISY IMMUNITY`.

`IMPERFECT PARENT ≠ DEFENDED HYPOCRITE`.

`MORAL FAILURE + REPENTANCE ≠ DEFENDED DOUBLE STANDARD`.

`ASYMMETRY OF ROLE/STATUS ≠ HYPOCRISY`.

A parent may legitimately have permissions a minor does not have because of role, ownership, covenant/status or responsibility. But adult status cannot excuse the same underlying moral evil (for example sexual impurity or lying) that the parent condemns in the child.

### Product use

Part II near parental repentance (`215`) and achievement/reputation controls (`252–253`).

Parent confession is not loss of authority. It can model what the parent is demanding: truthful repentance under the same Lord.
