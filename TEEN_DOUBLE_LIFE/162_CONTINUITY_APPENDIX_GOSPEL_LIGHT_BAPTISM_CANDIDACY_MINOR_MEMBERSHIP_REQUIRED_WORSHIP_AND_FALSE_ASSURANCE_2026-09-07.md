- later severe sin does not infallibly prove the entire past profession false;

Mandatory line:

> **A past profession matters; present fruit matters too.**

# 3. New P0 publication guards

## P0-A — no fatalistic probability claims

Do not say:

> “Such hardened church children almost never convert later.”

unless publication intentionally introduces valid data for a carefully defined population — and even then do not turn population data into election diagnosis.

Biblical warning severity ≠ statistical conversion probability.

## P0-B — no fake excommunication of never-members

Do not say:

> “Treat every rebellious church child exactly as excommunicated.”

Use:

> **pastoral analogy where appropriate, juridical distinction always.**

## P0-C — no moral waiting room for unregenerate minors

Do not say:

> “Until regeneration, parents should stop requiring worship/attendance/moral obedience.”

Nor:

> “Force a Christian profession to maintain household order.”

## P0-D — no childhood-conversion amulet

Do not use:

- old prayer;
- baptism request;
- choir;
- tears;
- childhood testimony

as a permanent trump card against present contradicted life.

## P0-E — no retrospective certainty

Do not write:

> “Because he later rebelled, we now know with certainty that nothing real ever happened earlier.”

The eventual pastoral concern is present relation to Christ, not always reconstructing the exact chronology of regeneration.
