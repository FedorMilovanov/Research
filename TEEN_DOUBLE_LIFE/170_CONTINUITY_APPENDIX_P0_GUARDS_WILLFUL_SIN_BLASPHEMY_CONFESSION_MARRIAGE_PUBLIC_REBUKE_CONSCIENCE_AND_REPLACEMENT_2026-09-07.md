# 1. `163_HIGH_HANDED_SIN_NUM15_HEB10...`

### P0 correction

Do **not** equate:

> any knowingly committed/repeated sexual sin

with:

> the apostasy-pattern of Heb. 10:26–31.

Numbers 15 “high hand” background includes deliberate defiance/contempt for God's word.

Heb. 10 itself defines the extreme trajectory in v.29:

- trampling Son of God;
- treating covenant blood as unholy;
- insulting Spirit of grace.

### Locked balance

> **Deliberateness can aggravate sin without making it final Hebrews-10 apostasy.**

For ordinary progressive hardening prefer:

- Heb. 3;
- Eph. 4;
- Rom. 6;
- Prov. 29:1.

Never tell a youth:

> “You knowingly watched pornography again, therefore no sacrifice remains for you.”

# 2. `164_BLASPHEMY_AGAINST_SPIRIT_MATT12_MARK3...`

### P0 correction

Do not equate every:

- profanity involving God;
- blasphemous joke;
- rage statement;
- atheist statement

with the specific unpardonable blasphemy of Matt. 12 / Mark 3.

Mark 3:30 explicitly anchors the warning:

> they were saying Christ had an unclean spirit.

Gill also explicitly rejects “every knowingly/wilfully committed sin” as the definition.

### Locked balance

> **Blasphemous speech may be a grave sign of hardening without giving parents infallible warrant to declare forgiveness impossible.**

Use ordinary speech/reverence texts for ordinary blasphemy; use Matt.12/Mark3 only with the narrative guard.

---

# 3. `165_CONFESSION_SCOPE_ACCOUNTABILITY_PRIVACY...`

### New balance

Accountability must not become compulsory sexual voyeurism.

Confession should be:

- truthful;
- specific enough to kill the lie;
- sufficient for reconciliation/safeguarding/help;
- proportional to who actually needs to know.

### Strong formulation

> **Исповедание должно быть достаточно конкретным, чтобы убить ложь, но не настолько графическим, чтобы воспроизводить грех в воображении слушателя.**

### P0 guards

- James 5:16 ≠ universal exhaustive sexual inventory to parents/leader;
- privacy ≠ right to protect deception/harm;
- public confession ≠ default for private sin;
- safeguarding may require precise facts, but not repeated amateur forensic interviewing.

---

# 4. `166_MARRIAGE_IS_NOT_A_CURE_FOR_UNREPENTANT_LUST...`

### Locked balance

1 Cor. 7 genuinely presents marriage as lawful/good and relevant to sexual continence.

But:

> **marriage is not a substitute for repentance or a therapy in which another person's body is used to manage protected lust.**

### P0 guards

Never write:

- marriage cures pornography;
- sexually tempted youth should simply marry quickly;
- former pornography struggle permanently disqualifies marriage.

Do maintain:

- repentance/truth;
- holiness;
- no deceptive courtship;
- no false spiritual certification of a known double-life youth to a prospective Christian spouse.

---

# 5. `167_PRIVATE_PUBLIC_REBUKE_MATT18_1TIM5...`

### P0 correction

1 Tim. 5:20 is immediately in the elder/presbyter accusation/correction context.

Do not use it as:

> “publicly rebuke every sinful teenager before the whole church.”

Calvin's disciplinary distinction is controlling:

- private/secret fault → graduated private correction ordinarily;
- open scandal → may require wider/public correction.

### Locked rule

> **Make correction as broad as truth, restoration and protection genuinely require—and no broader merely to satisfy outrage.**

For minors, protect confidentiality and avoid graphic public disclosure.

Corporate truthfulness ≠ mob confrontation.

---

# 6. `168_CONSCIENCE_RESTORATION_CLEANSING_HEB9...`

The conscience story must not end with hardening.

### Gospel center

- Heb. 9:14 — Christ's blood cleanses conscience from dead works to serve living God;
- Heb. 10:19–22 — draw near with heart cleansed;
- 1 John 1–2 — light/confession/cleansing/Advocate;
- 1 Tim. 1 — good conscience + sincere faith; rejecting conscience risks shipwreck;
- Acts 24:16 — active exercise toward conscience without offense.

### Strong formulations

> **The goal is not a louder conscience but a truer conscience.**

> **The goal after repentance is not a permanently bleeding conscience before parents.**

Do not turn conscience restoration into merely increasing shame.

---

# 7. `169_PUT_OFF_PUT_ON_FLEE_PURSUE_REPLACEMENT_SANCTIFICATION...`

Radical boundaries are necessary but incomplete.

### NT pattern

- Eph. 4 — put off / renew / put on;
- lie → truth;
- theft → labor/generosity;
- corrupt speech → edifying speech;
- Col. 3 — mortify old practices + put on compassion/kindness/love;
- 2 Tim. 2:22 — flee youthful desires + pursue righteousness/faith/love/peace **with** pure-hearted believers.

### Locked line

> **“Убегай” без “стремись” оставляет пустоту; “стремись” без “убегай” оставляет открытой дверь искушению.**

A home fighting hidden sin must remain more than an interrogation/crime scene.
