# Editorial HOLD resolution — lawful life feels “flat”: appetite, affections, sinful pleasure and replacement

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Mode:** RESEARCH-ONLY / editorial hold resolution / Scripture-first  
**Series:** `TEEN_DOUBLE_LIFE` / Part I–II  
**Verdict:** `KEEP WITH CONTROLLED WORDING`

---

## 0. Owner concern

The owner repeatedly raised a real pastoral observation:

> after repeated pornography, sexualized messaging, profane entertainment, status-seeking and intense secret-group stimulation, ordinary lawful life can begin to feel increasingly “presnaya” / flat / uninteresting, while the sinful hidden world becomes more attractive.

The project correctly refused to publish speculative mechanisms such as:

- `dopamine receptors are burned out`;
- `ordinary life becomes objectively incapable of giving pleasure`;
- `porn always causes irreversible escalation`;
- a universal numerical `internet ×20` effect.

The question remained an `EDITORIAL_HOLD` rather than being silently deleted.

### Resolution

**KEEP the concern, but state it as a biblical/pastoral claim about trained affections, appetite, rival love, habituated provision for lust and the subjective attractiveness of sin — not as a universal neuroscience claim.**

---

# 1. Scripture openly acknowledges the attractiveness of sin

### Proverbs 9:17–18

The seduction is presented as:

- stolen waters being sweet;
- secret bread being pleasant;
- while the end/death is hidden from the participant.

The deception is therefore not necessarily:

> `sin promises pleasure but produces literally none`.

A more accurate biblical formulation is:

> **sin can offer real immediate sweetness while hiding the character, slavery and end of the path.**

### Hebrews 11:25

Moses refuses the **temporary pleasure of sin**.

This is a direct guard against moralistic overstatement.

### James 1:14–15

Desire draws and entices; conceived desire gives birth to sin, and sin matures toward death.

The sequence is appetite/enticement → consent → sin → end, not merely lack of information.

---

# 2. 2 Timothy 3:4–5 gives the strongest governing category: rival love

The text contrasts:

> lovers of pleasure

with

> lovers of God,

and places this near a retained **form of godliness** without its power.

This is especially important to the teen-double-life series because it joins:

- pleasure;
- rival allegiance/love;
- religious exterior;
- practical denial.

### Product center

Do not write merely:

> `the teenager needs stronger stimulation`.

Write closer to:

> **The problem is not only that some experiences feel intense. It is that the heart can learn to prize the pleasure it repeatedly serves above communion with God and above the ordinary duties and joys in which obedience is lived.**

### Classical support

Gill links 2 Tim. 3:4 with sinful pleasures, serving various lusts/pleasures and making the belly a god.

Locator:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/2_timothy/3-4.htm

Evidence class:
- biblical contrast = `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE`;
- application to a teenager's comparative felt attractiveness of ordinary life = `CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS / PASTORAL_APPLICATION`.

---

# 3. Titus 3:3 / Romans 13:14 — desire can become a served master and a provisioned appetite

Titus 3:3 describes the pre-conversion pattern as being enslaved to various passions and pleasures.

Romans 13:14 warns against making provision for the flesh to gratify its desires.

These texts support a crucial project concept:

> **desire is not merely an isolated flash; it can be served, provisioned and obeyed as a pattern of life.**

Therefore repeated access matters morally without needing a speculative brain model.

A teenager may repeatedly provide for lust through:

- private nighttime phone access;
- secret accounts;
- pornography;
- sexualized chats;
- a peer group whose shared good is transgression;
- profane/sexualized entertainment used precisely to feed appetite;
- rehearsed fantasy and concealment.

This does not prove identical effects in every person.

It does explain why radical removal of occasions can be a biblical act of wisdom rather than an admission that holiness is powerless.

---

# 4. Owen supplies the strongest Puritan mechanism: sin entangles the affections by presenting itself as satisfying

John Owen, *The Nature, Power, Deceit, and Prevalency of the Remainders of Indwelling Sin in Believers*, describes sin's deceit as:

- drawing the mind away;
- presenting sinful objects as desirable and especially suited to satisfy corrupt affections;
- using imagination to present a false-painted beauty / pretended satisfactoriness;
- entangling and inflaming the affections;
- taking advantage when love/delight become attached to worldly or fleshly objects.

Primary stable locator:
- https://ccel.org/ccel/owen/indwellingsin/indwellingsin.i.xv.html
- related discussion: https://ccel.org/ccel/owen/indwellingsin/indwellingsin.i.x.html

### Evidence class

`CLASSICAL_EXEGESIS_STRONG / PURITAN PRACTICAL THEOLOGY`.

### Project implication

Owen gives us a much better account than pseudo-neuroscience:

> **What is repeatedly represented to the imagination as desirable and repeatedly welcomed by corrupt affection can acquire a powerful practical attractiveness.**

This is not the claim that every lawful pleasure literally stops being pleasurable.

It is a claim about **comparative affection and allegiance**.

# 5. “Ordinary life becomes flat” — exact wording boundary

## Prohibited wording

Do not say as universal fact:

> `Porn/sexualized media rewires the brain so normal life becomes boring.`

Do not say:

> `Once this happens, the teen permanently loses the ability to enjoy ordinary things.`

Do not say:

> `Every teenager escalates to harder content because tolerance inevitably develops.`

These are stronger causal/neuroscientific claims than the current evidence and biblical argument require.

## Allowed reader-facing claim

> **Когда человек снова и снова кормит похоть, тайное возбуждение, статус и запретное удовольствие могут занять в его желаниях всё больше места. Тогда тихая законная жизнь — семья, труд, чтение Писания, обычная дружба, служение, чистая радость — способна субъективно казаться всё менее привлекательной не потому, что в ней стало меньше добра, а потому, что сердце приучается искать наслаждение в другом месте.**

This is `CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS / PASTORAL_APPLICATION`, not a measured universal effect.

### Shorter formula

> **Не обязательно мир становится пресным; аппетит может стать испорченнее.**

This is potentially one of the strongest formulations for Product.

---

# 6. The point must remain moral/spiritual, not merely intensity management

The article must not imply:

> `Problem = too many exciting stimuli; solution = lower stimulation baseline.`

The biblical question is:

- what do I love?
- what do I serve?
- what do I repeatedly provide for?
- what does my imagination present as beautiful/desirable?
- what pleasures am I unwilling to lose for Christ?
- what company trains those loves?

The issue is therefore not only intensity but **lordship / rival love / trained appetite**.

---

# 7. Positive replacement is mandatory

The project already uses 2 Tim. 2:22:

`FLEE youthful passions`

+ `PURSUE righteousness, faith, love, peace`

+ `WITH those who call on the Lord from a pure heart`.

This is the needed positive counterpart.

Removing pornography/chat/app/company without a new pursuit can leave a social and affectional vacuum.

### Chalmers — useful later Reformed witness, not Puritan authority

Thomas Chalmers' *The Expulsive Power of a New Affection* argues that a wrong affection is not effectively displaced by mere demonstrations of worldly vanity; another superior affection must take its place.

Stable locator:
- https://www.unionpublishing.org/resource/the-expulsive-power-of-a-new-affection/

Evidence class:
- `LATER_REFORMED_PASTORAL_WITNESS`, not Puritan and not confessional authority.

Use only as a supporting historical formulation if needed; the controlling biblical center remains 2 Tim. 2:22, 1 John 2 and the gospel.

---

# 8. Regeneration / sanctification guard

Do not reduce recovery to retraining preferences by habit.

For the Christian:

- God gives the new heart;
- Christ is not merely a competing stimulus;
- the Spirit mortifies sin;
- faith beholds a better treasure;
- sanctification genuinely redirects affections and conduct;
- ordained means include Word, prayer, fellowship, discipline, fleeing occasions and active obedience.

For the unregenerate teenager:

- external restraint remains lawful/wise;
- better habits and company may restrain/shape;
- none of these means should be mislabeled regeneration.

`FORMATION ≠ REGENERATION` remains locked.

### Publication-safe thesis

> **Грех не обязан сначала убедить подростка, что зло стало добром. Достаточно, чтобы он снова и снова кормил желание, которое обещает быструю сладость. Со временем вопрос может звучать уже не “правильно ли это?”, а просто “хочу ли я этого?”. И тогда проблема не в том, что Божья добрая жизнь стала беднее, а в том, что испорченное желание всё сильнее ищет насыщения не там.**

### Mandatory guard immediately nearby

Do not imply:

- inevitability;
- irreversible brain damage;
- universal escalation;
- that one severe relapse proves this advanced state;
- that regenerated believers cannot experience lawful pleasure normally after repentance.

The Christian answer is not merely less stimulation. It is repentance, mortification, reordered love, removal of occasions, renewed mind and positive pursuit in Christ and His people.
