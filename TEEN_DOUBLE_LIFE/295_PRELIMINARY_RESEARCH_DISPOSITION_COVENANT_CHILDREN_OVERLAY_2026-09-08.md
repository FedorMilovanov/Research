# Preliminary Research Disposition — Covenant Children Overlay

**Status:** RESEARCH / PRE-CLOSURE / NOT PUBLICATION
**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Depends on:** `273–294`

## What is already strong enough to lock provisionally

1. Moral responsibility is not created by baptism.
2. No biblical/civil age-18 moral switch exists.
3. Paedobaptist and credobaptist systems differ principally over pre-profession visible-church/covenant status and jurisdiction.
4. Serious Westminster/Reformed doctrine does not identify every baptized child with certain regeneration.
5. 1689 doctrine has ample resources for high child accountability before conversion/membership.
6. Weak Baptist `what can you expect from an unbeliever?` practice is not confessionally necessary.
7. Paedobaptist polity offers a real institutional lesson: named church ownership/catechesis/oversight/reclamation of children.
8. Baptist churches can receive that lesson without infant baptism by distinguishing membership from sustained pastoral care.
9. 1 Cor 7:14 supports at least a serious argument for special set-apart household status; baptismal entitlement remains disputed.
10. Eph 6 directly destroys the moral waiting-room concept.
