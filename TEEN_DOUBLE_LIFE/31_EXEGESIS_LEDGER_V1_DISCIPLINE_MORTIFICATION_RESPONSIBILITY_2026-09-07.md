# Exegesis Ledger v1 — mortification, responsibility, discipline, repentance

**Статус:** RESEARCH CONTROL / NOT PUBLICATION  
**Дата:** 2026-09-07  
**Задача:** не использовать библейские тексты как slogans; для каждого текста фиксировать контекст, допустимый вывод и запрещённое применение.

---

## X01 — Марк 7:20–23 — источник нравственной нечистоты

**Context:** Jesus locates defiling evils in the human heart, not merely external ritual contamination.

**Supports:**
- technology/peers do not create sinful desire ex nihilo;
- external controls cannot regenerate heart;
- personal responsibility remains.

**Does NOT support:**
- environment is irrelevant;
- parents need no boundaries;
- temptation/opportunity structures do not matter.

---

## X02 — Иакова 1:13–15 — желание, зачатие, грех, смерть

**Context:** temptation is not attributable to God; person is drawn by own desire.

**Supports:**
- desire is morally serious before public act;
- sin has developmental sequence;
- external bait works because internal desire responds.

**Guard:** do not pretend James gives a neuroscientific addiction model.

---

## X03 — Матфея 5:27–30 — lust and radical amputation

**Context:** Sermon on the Mount; Jesus intensifies adultery command to the heart and immediately commands radical dealing with occasions of sin.

**Supports:**
- lust itself matters;
- spiritually lethal temptation may require costly removal of access;
- “useful” thing can be surrendered to avoid sin;
- proactive avoidance is biblically legitimate.

**Does NOT support:**
- literal self-harm;
- abusive parental punishment;
- same restriction for every child;
- external amputation = heart renewal.

**Research resonance:** modern self-control literature often finds proactive avoidance/environment design more effective than waiting for maximal temptation.

---

## X04 — 2 Тимофею 2:22 — flee youthful passions

**Supports:**
- avoidance/fleeing is not cowardice;
- positive replacement matters: pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace **with those** who call on Lord from pure heart.

**Important:** text contains both **flight** and **counter-community**.

Working formula:
`flee harmful cue + pursue good + pursue with godly people`.

---

## X05 — 1 Коринфянам 6:18–20 — flee sexual immorality

**Context:** Christian sexual ethics grounded in union with Christ/body belonging to Lord.

**Supports:**
- sexual sin is not a toy for exposure-training;
- fleeing is legitimate strategy;
- embodied holiness matters.

**Guard:** primary address is believers; do not infer unregenerate child has no moral duty. Moral law binds all, while specifically Christian identity arguments have covenant force for believers.

---

## X06 — Римлянам 13:14 — make no provision for flesh

**Supports:**
- do not deliberately engineer access/circumstances that feed lust;
- “provision” logic fits device/access/environment boundaries.

**Does NOT support:**
- every technology is provision for flesh;
- purity through isolation alone.

---

## X07 — Евреям 3:12–15 — deceit, hardening, daily exhortation

**Supports:**
- hardening can be progressive;
- deceit is central mechanism;
- mutual exhortation is not optional decoration;
- hidden isolation is dangerous.

**Research resonance:** moral disengagement, normalization, secrecy and peer validation can describe human-level mechanisms consistent with sin’s deceitfulness without “proving” Hebrews psychologically.

---

## X08 — Ефесянам 4:17–24 — callousness and sensuality

**Supports:**
- moral-spiritual desensitization/callousness is biblical;
- giving oneself over to sensuality is active moral language;
- old-self/new-self identity contrast matters.

**Guard:** no dopamine receptor claims.

---

## X09 — Ефесянам 6:1–4 — child obedience and fatherly discipline

**Supports:**
- children have real obligations, not suspended until regeneration;
- fathers/parents have real authority/duty;
- discipline and instruction belong together;
- provoking/exasperating children is forbidden.

**Critical balance:**
`authority without instruction/warmth` is not the biblical ideal; `warmth without authority` is not either.

**Age guard:** text addresses children within household order. Adult offspring continue to owe honor, but the exact command structure of dependent-child obedience should not be simplistically mapped onto independent adults.

---

## X10 — Притчи 22:6 — formative path

**Genre:** wisdom proverb, not covenant formula guaranteeing regeneration.

**Supports:**
- early formation has durable significance;
- upbringing is not neutral.

**Does NOT support:**
- every apostate adult proves parental failure;
- every godly parent is guaranteed a converted child.

---

## X11 — Второзаконие 21:18–21 — stubborn rebellious son

**Context:** Old Covenant Israel judicial law. Parents bring case to **elders/city gate**; parents do not execute privately. The offense is hardened, public, stubborn rebellion after discipline, not ordinary adolescent imperfection.

**1689 control:** ch.19.4 — judicial laws expired with Israel’s polity; only general equity remains morally useful.

**General-equity observations:**
- rebellion against rightful authority is serious;
- parental authority itself had judicial limits;
- grave disorder involved community/public justice;
- persistent rebellion was not treated as morally neutral because “the son is sinful by nature.”

**Forbidden application:**
- corporal or lethal imitation;
- using the passage as direct warrant to expel a minor from home;
- parents functioning as Israel’s magistrates.

---

## X12 — 1 Коринфянам 5:1–13 — professing brother in flagrant unrepentant sin

**Context:** sexual immorality tolerated inside church; offender claims inside status.

**Supports:**
- church must judge those inside/professing brother;
- scandalous unrepentant sin cannot be normalized as good-standing membership;
- exclusion has covenant/community meaning;
- Paul quotes Deuteronomic purge formula.

**Important distinction:** Paul explicitly says he is **not** calling church to withdraw from sexually immoral outsiders generally.

**Does NOT support:**
- banning unbelievers from hearing public preaching;
- treating every struggling believer as excommunicated;
- immediate discipline without process/repentance assessment in every case.

---

## X13 — Матфея 18:15–20 — process and goal of discipline

**Supports:**
- escalating process;
- aim is “gain your brother”;
- refusal to listen changes ecclesial recognition;
- discipline is remedial and protective, not revenge.

**Guard:** exact application depends on nature/publicity of sin and church polity; criminal/safeguarding cases cannot be reduced to private Matt 18 process when reporting/protection is required.

---

## X14 — 2 Фессалоникийцам 3:6,14–15 — withdrawal without enemy-hatred

**Supports:**
- fellowship boundaries can be real;
- shame can have restorative purpose;
- disciplined person is not to be treated with vindictive hatred.

Key line: `do not regard him as enemy, but warn him as brother`.

---

## X15 — 1 Коринфянам 14:23–25 — unbeliever entering assembly

**Supports:**
- NT assembly can include unbelieving visitors;
- public Word is intelligible/convicting toward outsiders.

**Therefore:** excommunication ≠ universal building ban.

Safety/disruption/trespass questions are separate prudential matters.

---

## X16 — 1 Коринфянам 11:27–32 — Lord’s Supper and judgment

**Supports:**
- participation can incur divine discipline/judgment;
- table is not spiritually casual;
- self-examination matters.

**Guard:** “unworthy manner” must not be reduced to “only perfect Christians may commune.” Calvin and other Reformed interpreters distinguish repentant weakness from presumptuous unrepentant scandal.

**Application:** church should not teach a knowingly unrepentant professing believer that he may remain in good standing and commune as though nothing is wrong.

---

## X17 — 2 Коринфянам 7:8–11 — godly grief and repentance

**Supports:**
- distress after being caught is not automatically repentance;
- godly grief produces earnest moral movement/fruit;
- worldly grief can remain self-focused.

**Publication question:** distinguish remorse over consequences/image loss from turning against sin itself.

---

## X18 — Притчи 28:13 / 1 Иоанна 1:5–10 — concealment vs confession

**Supports:**
- concealment is spiritually dangerous;
- confession belongs to walking in light;
- confession cannot be equated with instant restoration of all trust/privileges.

---

# Exegesis audit rules

1. Never use an OT judicial penalty as direct household policy.
2. Never collapse attendance, membership, communion and leadership into one category.
3. Never infer regeneration from external church participation.
4. Never infer final reprobation from a discovered sin pattern.
5. Never make external restraint equivalent to sanctification.
6. Never make sovereign grace equivalent to moral irresponsibility.
7. Never call ordinary struggling with repented sin “living in open rebellion” without criteria.
8. Always distinguish weakness + repentance from settled defense + refusal to repent.
