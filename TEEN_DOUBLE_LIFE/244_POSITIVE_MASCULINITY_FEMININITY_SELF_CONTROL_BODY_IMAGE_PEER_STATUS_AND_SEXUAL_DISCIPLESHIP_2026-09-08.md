## 0. Purpose

The project now has strong material on:

- lust;
- pornography;
- sexual double standards;
- modesty;
- coercion;
- purity-culture shame;
- parental boundaries;
- church discipline;
- safeguarding.

A remaining risk is **negative-only sexual formation**.

If boys hear only:

> `do not watch porn / do not masturbate / do not fornicate / do not lust`,

and girls hear only:

> `be modest / do not tempt boys / protect purity`,

then the church may leave secular peers, pornography, influencers, fitness culture, romance media and social platforms to provide the positive definition of:

- manhood;
- womanhood;
- beauty;
- strength;
- attractiveness;

- status;
- sexual maturity.

This module asks:

> **What positive sexual and gender formation can Scripture actually support for adolescents without baptizing cultural stereotypes?**

# 1. The first positive category for young men is self-government, not conquest

## Titus 2:6–8

Paul's strikingly compact direct instruction to younger men is:

> be self-controlled / sober-minded.

Titus is also to model good works, integrity, dignity and sound speech.

This is an important corrective to both secular and church caricatures.

Biblical young manhood is not established by:

- sexual experience;
- ability to get girls;
- physical intimidation;
- vulgar humor;
- alcohol capacity;
- gym status;
- risk-taking;
- rebellion from parents;
- money/display;
- emotional numbness.

### Ligonier control

A Reformed devotional on Titus 2:6–7 rightly centers self-control and notes that sexual restraint is one application of a broader refusal to become enslaved by created goods.

Source:
- https://learn.ligonier.org/devotionals/self-controlled-younger-men

### P0

> **The New Testament's positive answer to macho sexual status is not weaker masculinity but governed masculinity.**

# 2. 1 Timothy 5:1–2 gives an unusually practical male-female relation category

Paul tells Timothy to treat:

- older women as mothers;
- younger women as sisters;
- **in all purity**.

Gill understands this as chastity in looks, words and actions.

Source:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/1_timothy/5.htm

### High-value adolescent transfer

A Christian teenage boy should be taught to see a girl not primarily as:

- sexual opportunity;
- body rating;
- girlfriend candidate;
- social-status proof;
- pornographic category;

but as a human person and, in the Christian community, in a family-like moral relation requiring purity and honor.

### Guard

Do not use `sister` language to erase possible future romantic/marital interest.

The point is the **moral mode of present relation**, not denial that attraction exists.

---

# 3. 1 Thessalonians 4 — male sexual maturity is learned holiness and honor

Paul demands that each believer know how to possess/acquire/control his vessel in sanctification and honor rather than passion of lust.

The exact meaning of `skeuos` is disputed:

- body;
- wife;
- possibly sexual-member euphemism in some interpreters.

Gill discusses multiple senses and still converges on chastity/honor.

Sources:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/1_thessalonians/4.htm
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/1_thessalonians/4-4.htm

### P0

Do not use a disputed lexical reading as a dogmatic proof of one modern masculinity program.

The secure center is:

> **sexual desire is to be governed in holiness and honor, not treated as an uncontrollable male force.**

# 4. Strength in Scripture is not reducible to physical dominance

Biblical strength includes:

- courage;
- endurance;
- self-control;
- work;
- protection;
- truth;
- sacrificial responsibility;
- resistance to temptation.

Useful trajectories:

- Prov. 16:32 — ruling one's spirit compared favorably with taking a city;
- 1 Cor. 16:13–14 — courage/strength joined to love;
- Eph. 6:10ff — strength in the Lord for spiritual warfare;
- 2 Tim. 2 — endurance as soldier/athlete/farmer imagery;
- 1 Pet. 3:7 — male marital strength/status does not license harshness; wife must be honored.

### Product-safe line

> **A boy who can overpower another person but cannot govern his own eyes, speech and appetites is not displaying biblical mastery.**

# 5. Male peer culture can reward exactly the opposite traits

A meta-analysis of peer norms and adolescent sexual behavior found:

- descriptive norms had a substantial association with sexual activity (`r ≈ .40` in the fixed-effect summary reported);
- injunctive norms and direct peer pressure were also associated, though less strongly;
- age, gender, peer type and sociocultural context moderated associations;
- longitudinal analyses suggested selection effects can be stronger than socialization effects.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25217363/

### Interpretation

Teenagers are not passive victims of peer norms.

They both:

- are shaped by groups;
- select groups resembling their existing interests.

This fits the project's anti-determinism.

### Positive formation implication

A boy needs not only prohibition of corrupt friends but a community where:

- self-control has status;
- sexual boasting is embarrassing rather than admirable;
- truth is respected;
- women/girls are treated as persons;
- courage includes refusing the group;
- adult men model non-vulgar masculinity.

# 6. Pornography can become a masculinity curriculum

Long-standing reviews report associations between adolescent pornography use and:

- permissive sexual attitudes;
- stronger gender-stereotypical sexual beliefs;
- some sexual behaviors/aggression outcomes;

while repeatedly warning against simple causal inference.

Sources:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27105446/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36853749/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40631652/

A 2024 systematic review found adolescent SEIM use strongly associated with being male and connected peer/status variables among studied correlates.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39424699/

### Project synthesis

Pornography can catechize boys toward scripts such as:

- male entitlement;
- performance;
- body comparison;
- endless novelty;
- female availability;
- sex without covenant/responsibility.

### Guard

Do not claim every pornography user adopts every script.

Use:

> `available/normalized script + association + repeated exposure`

not deterministic language.

# 7. The male body can also become an idol and commodity

Male body-image concerns require more attention in Christian parenting.

A 2025 study of boys/men (`N = 1,553`) found viewing muscularity-oriented social-media content strongly associated with probable muscle dysmorphia, independent of total social-media time.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40367828/

Systematic review of adolescent masculinities also found stereotypical male-role norms, including emotional restriction and other conformity dimensions, connected in the literature to internalizing problems/social support patterns.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33417737/

### Church danger

A conservative church may criticize female appearance-idolatry while quietly baptizing male appearance/status idolatry through:

- obsessive muscularity;
- steroid/supplement culture;
- ridicule of physically smaller boys;
- equating masculinity with visual dominance;
- contempt for vulnerability.

### P0

> **Created male strength is good; worship of the male body is not.**

# 8. Emotional self-control is not emotional nonexistence

The secular critique of male emotional restriction sometimes overreaches into a generalized rejection of masculine restraint.

But the church can overcorrect the other way and teach:

> `real men do not fear, grieve, confess weakness or ask help`.

Scripture gives masculine examples of:

- David grieving;
- Jeremiah lamenting;
- Paul describing fear/weakness/tears;
- Christ weeping;
- men confessing sin;
- brothers bearing burdens.

### Distinction

`SELF-CONTROL ≠ EMOTIONAL NUMBNESS`.

`COURAGE ≠ ABSENCE OF FEAR`.

`MASCULINITY ≠ INABILITY TO ASK FOR HELP`.

This matters directly for secret pornography/sexual sin: macho secrecy can make early help-seeking feel humiliating.

# 9. Young women: Scripture gives purity and strength without making appearance the center

Titus 2:3–5 describes older women teaching younger women in a framework including:

- love of husband/children;
- self-control;
- purity;
- kindness;
- household faithfulness;
- marital submission.

### Major context guard

The `younger women` in this passage are substantially described as **wives/mothers**.

Therefore it is wrong to directly convert every item into an immediate duty of a 13-year-old girl.

Valid adolescent transfer is broader:

- self-control;
- purity;
- kindness;
- intergenerational female discipleship;
- preparation for possible adult vocations.

Do not tell an unmarried teen that Titus 2 already gives her an existing husband's authority.

# 10. Proverbs 31 — female strength is not sexual display and not passive fragility

The Proverbs 31 woman is:

- economically active;
- industrious;
- strong;
- wise in speech;
- generous;
- responsible for household welfare;
- feared/recognized for Godward character.

### Genre/status guard

This is an adult wife/household figure and wisdom poem, not a literal daily checklist for teenage girls.

### Positive adolescent formation

It does destroy two caricatures:

1. femininity = sexual attractiveness;
2. femininity = helpless passivity.

### Product-safe line

> **Biblical womanhood is not measured by how desirable a girl can make herself to boys, nor by how small she can make herself in the world.**

---

# 11. 1 Peter 3 — beauty is morally decentered, not body-denied

Peter contrasts external adornment with the hidden person/imperishable inner character.

The text should not be flattened into:

> `physical beauty is bad`.

The canonical tradition includes appreciation of created beauty.

The point is priority and character.

### Teen application

A girl's body/image should not become:

- her principal worth;
- family honor-token;
- social currency;
- main route to attention;
- proof of femininity.

Nor should parents communicate:

> `your body is dangerous and must disappear`.

# 12. Social media places girls under intense appearance pressure, but boys are not exempt

Systematic review of adolescent social-media use/body image found a robust association between frequency/appearance-related content and negative body image across male and female samples, with substantial methodological limitations and predominantly observational designs.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34801229/

Another systematic review covering adolescents/young adults likewise identifies body-image/well-being concerns related to social-media use.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37256748

2026 review of visual-social-media interventions found modest benefits for some body-image intervention approaches but high heterogeneity and mostly high-income/female samples.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41661648/

### P0

Do not diagnose social media as the sole cause of body dissatisfaction.

But parents/churches should recognize a real formation environment around:

- filters;
- metrics;
- sexualized presentation;
- fitness ideals;
- cosmetic comparison;
- male muscularity;
- female thinness/curves/sexual attractiveness.

# 13. Biblical femininity must not mean “manage male temptation for them”

Girls can be taught:

- modesty;
- wisdom;
- boundaries;
- sexual holiness;
- humility;
- non-manipulative self-presentation.

But the curriculum becomes distorted when the central female task is:

> `keep boys from lusting`.

### Better structure

Teach the girl:

1. your body belongs to God;
2. beauty is a gift, not a god;
3. sexual attention is not equivalent to love;
4. you are responsible for your own conduct;
5. boys are responsible for theirs;
6. danger-awareness is wisdom, not guilt-transfer;
7. no boy receives a right to your body because you attracted him;
8. marriageability is not your ultimate identity.

---

# 14. Biblical masculinity must not mean “sexual desire is uncontrollable”

Teach the boy:

1. desire can be strong without becoming lord;
2. attraction is not permission;
3. a girl's body does not create your guilt;
4. pornography trains consumption rather than covenant love;
5. sexual boasting is not maturity;
6. coercion/manipulation are incompatible with honor;
7. strength is ordered toward service/protection, not exploitation;
8. help-seeking can be courage;
9. marriage is not entitlement to a sexual service-provider;
10. self-control is explicitly New Testament young-man formation.

---

# 15. Romance does not make a teenage boy a husband or a teenage girl a wife

One cultural script grants teenage romantic attachment near-covenantal status:

> `she is my girl / he is my man / we belong to each other`.

The project must distinguish:

- attraction;
- dating/courtship;
- engagement;
- marriage.

### P0

`BOYFRIEND ≠ HUSBAND`.

`GIRLFRIEND ≠ WIFE`.

Therefore neither teenager receives marital sexual rights, marital headship/submission rights, or a moral claim to bypass parents/church simply because a romance feels intense.

This links with existing `secret romance` and family-government dossiers.

---

# 16. Adult models matter more than slogans

Titus 2 uses intergenerational teaching/modeling.

Teen formation is weakened if adult church culture says:

- boys: self-control;
- fathers: sexual jokes/porn secrecy;
- girls: modesty;
- mothers: status/body obsession;
- teens: tell truth;
- adults: hide scandal;
- boys: honor women;
- men: publicly demean wives/women.

### Locked principle

> **A church cannot plausibly disciple adolescents into a sexual ethic it refuses to embody.**

This is not a claim that adult hypocrisy removes teen responsibility.

It is a formation/integrity claim.

---

# 17. The grace center in Titus 2 must stay visible

Titus 2 does not end with behavioral rules.

Verses 11–14 ground the entire section in the appearing grace of God that:

- brings salvation;
- trains believers to renounce ungodliness/worldly passions;
- trains self-controlled/upright/godly life;
- directs hope to Christ's appearing;
- connects redemption with purification of a people zealous for good works.

### P0

This is essential for Product.

Do not turn:

> `young men be self-controlled / young women be pure`

into detached moralism.

The theological grammar is:

> **grace saves and trains.**

# 18. Hard myths to reject

## Male myths

- `Real men need sexual experience.`
- `Porn is inevitable male biology.`
- `Sexual conquest proves status.`
- `A man cannot control himself if a woman dresses sexually.`
- `Strength means intimidation.`
- `Emotions/help-seeking are feminine weakness.`
- `Muscularity equals masculinity.`
- `Marriage will fix lust.`

## Female myths

- `Beauty/sexual attention proves worth.`
- `Good femininity means making no one uncomfortable.`
- `A girl's job is to prevent male lust.`
- `Modesty means body shame.`
- `Victimization destroys purity.`
- `Sexual history permanently lowers human worth.`
- `Submission means no moral agency or right to report harm.`
- `Being desired is the same as being loved.`

## Church counter-myths

- `Because secular masculinity is distorted, sex distinctions are meaningless.`
- `Because feminism critiques abuse, every concern about power is feminist contamination.`
- `Because complementarity is biblical, every traditional gender stereotype is biblical.`

### Locked distinction

`BIBLICAL SEX DISTINCTION ≠ CULTURAL STEREOTYPE PACKAGE`.

# 21. Product-safe formulations

> **Церковь проиграет сексуализированной культуре, если предложит подросткам только запреты, а положительное представление о мужчине, женщине, красоте, силе и любви оставит TikTok, порно и сверстникам.**

# 22. Locked final synthesis

> **Biblical male and female formation should be both differentiated and morally symmetrical. Scripture does not erase sex, but neither does it create two levels of holiness. Boys must be formed away from conquest, entitlement, pornography and performative dominance toward self-control, purity, courage and sacrificial responsibility. Girls must be formed away from body-as-worth, sexual-attention status and shame toward purity, wisdom, strength, kindness and Godward identity. Both require grace, truth, community and embodied adult models.**

And:

> **The alternative to secular gender scripts is not a baptized stereotype package. It is discipleship under Christ.**
