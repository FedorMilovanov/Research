## 1. Shared center

The two modules answer one higher-order problem:

> **How should adults preserve and evaluate a disclosure when the account may be incomplete, delayed, corrected, retracted, anonymous, or later remembered differently?**

### Locked package

- `DISCLOSURE ≠ AUTOMATIC FINAL PROOF`;
- `DELAYED DISCLOSURE ≠ PROVED FABRICATION`;
- `PARTIAL DISCLOSURE ≠ AUTOMATIC DECEPTION`;
- `RECANTATION ≠ AUTOMATIC DISPROOF`;
- `ANONYMOUS REPORT ≠ FINAL VERDICT`;
- `ANONYMOUS REPORT ≠ NO SAFETY SIGNAL`;
- `CHILD'S WORDS ≠ ADULT'S PARAPHRASE`;
- `PARAPHRASE ≠ VERBATIM QUOTE`;
- `FACT ≠ INFERENCE`;
- `CONTEMPORANEOUS NOTE ≠ INFALLIBLE RECORD`;
- `LATER MEMORY ≠ ORIGINAL NOTE`;
- `ACCURATE RECORDING ≠ AMATEUR FORENSIC INTERVIEWING`.

## 2. Apparent tension: changing story vs evidence-bounded confession

No contradiction.

`273` warns about a person whose confession repeatedly expands exactly to the boundary of what is already proved.

`296` warns that a victim/witness may disclose harm gradually, minimize, delay or recant for many possible reasons.

### Reconciled rule

> **DO NOT TRANSFER A CONFESSION-OF-OWN-WRONGDOING MODEL DIRECTLY ONTO A DISCLOSURE-OF-HARM MODEL.**

The same surface pattern — more detail later — can arise from different moral/evidentiary mechanisms.

Ask what role the person occupies:

- confessing own misconduct;
- reporting harm suffered;
- witnessing another's conduct;
- repeating secondhand information;
- mixed role.

### Locked

`MORE DETAIL LATER ≠ ONE UNIVERSAL MORAL DIAGNOSIS`.

---

## 3. Apparent tension: recantation vs anti-retaliation

No contradiction.

`293` shows that retaliation/coercion after disclosure can be real.

`296` says recantation can occur for many reasons, including pressure — but pressure must not be assumed without evidence.

### Reconciled rule

> **RECANTATION SHOULD TRIGGER BETTER FACT-FINDING, NOT A PRESELECTED SCRIPT ABOUT WHY IT HAPPENED.**

Possible states include:

- retraction under threat;
- retraction from shame/loyalty;
- correction of a genuine mistake;
- retraction of exaggeration;
- retraction of an intentionally false report;
- unresolved reason.

`POSSIBLE MECHANISM ≠ ESTABLISHED MECHANISM`.

---

## 4. Apparent tension: exact words vs child-led disclosure

No contradiction.

`297` says exact words should be preserved where material.

That does **not** mean adults should force exhaustive exact wording or interrogate until every ambiguity disappears.

### Reconciled rule

> **PRESERVE WHAT THE CHILD ACTUALLY SAID; DO NOT manufacture MORE SPEECH TO IMPROVE THE RECORD.**

Good evidence hygiene may include:

- note date/time/context;
- quote exact words when known;
- distinguish paraphrase/inference;
- record materially important adult prompts/questions;
- preserve later correction as later correction;
- avoid leading/probing where safeguarding process should take over.

### Locked

`BETTER RECORD ≠ LONGER INTERROGATION`.

---

## 5. First disclosure and later correction must coexist in the record

A serious error is silent replacement:

Original:

> `It happened Friday.`

Later:

> `I remembered wrong; it was Thursday.`

Bad record:

> quietly edit the first note so it now says Thursday.

### Better structure

- preserve original statement as historical fact about what was said;
- add later correction;
- distinguish which statement is currently believed/supportable;
- do not treat the mere existence of correction as proof of lying.

### Locked

`CORRECTION OF CONTENT ≠ REWRITING HISTORY OF THE DISCLOSURE`.

This matters for parents, pastors, church safeguarding leads and third-party reports.

## 6. Anonymous report routing

`296` owns the core distinction:

`ANONYMOUS REPORT ≠ FINAL VERDICT`  
`ANONYMOUS REPORT ≠ NO SAFETY SIGNAL`.

### Reconciled with `291`

Anonymous information may be:

- one source with unknown identity;
- several copied reports from the same source;
- independent reports;
- rumor;
- documentary lead.

Do not inflate source count merely because several anonymous messages appear.

### Reconciled with `282`

Church may take proportionate precaution from credible unresolved risk without publicly declaring guilt.

### Locked

`ANONYMITY AFFECTS VERIFIABILITY; IT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY ERASE SAFETY RELEVANCE`.

---

## 7. Initial adult questions belong to the evidence context

`297` correctly distinguishes:

> spontaneous detail

from

> detail introduced by an adult question.

This does not make answers to direct questions invalid.

It means later readers should know how the information arose.

### Example

Adult:

> `Did he threaten to share the image?`

Child:

> `Yes.`

is not evidentially identical to:

Child spontaneously says:

> `He told me he would send the image to everyone.`

Both may be true.

### Locked

`ANSWER ≠ QUESTION-FREE NARRATIVE`.

`QUESTIONED DETAIL ≠ FALSE DETAIL`.

The purpose is transparency about elicitation, not automatic discounting.

---

## 8. Do not turn family notes into pseudo-clinical diagnoses

Adults may record observable behavior:

- crying;
- silence;
- shaking;
- flat affect;
- anger;
- delayed answer.

They should not silently convert observation into diagnosis:

- `traumatized`;
- `dissociating`;
- `lying because no emotion`;
- `manipulating because crying`.

Use `275` affect/repentance guard and safeguarding professionalism where needed.

### Locked

`OBSERVED AFFECT ≠ SELF-INTERPRETING DIAGNOSIS`.

## 10. Safeguarding override

When disclosure concerns:

- abuse;
- exploitation;
- grooming;
- sextortion;
- illegal sexual imagery;
- serious violence;
- credible self-harm danger,

ordinary family truth-gathering must not become repeated improvised interviewing.

### Safe sequence

`LISTEN / RECORD MATERIAL WORDS / STABILIZE SAFETY / REFER OR ESCALATE AS REQUIRED`.

Not:

`REPEAT QUESTIONING UNTIL STORY BECOMES PERFECTLY CONSISTENT`.

Exact civil/legal procedure remains jurisdiction-dependent.

## 14. Final locked synthesis

> **Раскрытие может быть поздним, неполным, исправленным, отозванным или анонимным — ни одна из этих форм не является магическим переключателем доказанности. Поэтому взрослым нужна двойная дисциплина: не объявлять меняющийся рассказ автоматически ложным или автоматически истинным и одновременно сохранять то, что действительно было сказано, не заменяя слова ребёнка собственной интерпретацией. Чем серьёзнее дело, тем важнее точность записи и тем меньше места для самодельного допроса: услышать, зафиксировать материальное, защитить и передать вопрос компетентному процессу.**
