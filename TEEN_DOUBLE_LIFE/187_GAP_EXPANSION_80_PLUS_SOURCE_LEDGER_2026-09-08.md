## Evidence semantics used here

Repository classes only:
- **A1** — primary legal, judicial, governmental, or participant-created record.
- **A2** — official independent investigation, transcript, report, or primary institutional publication.
- **A3** — official event-specific statement, board decision, or institutional document.
- **B1** — high-quality secondary corroboration; never sole support for a disputed quote-safe claim.
- **C** — context/discovery/unverified; never quote-safe.
- **D** — excluded/unreliable/irrelevant.

**Important:** peer-reviewed systematic reviews/meta-analyses are normally **B1** in this repository schema even when academically strong. Official regulators/government guidance may be A1/A2/A3 depending on object type. Reader-facing direct quotations still require the repository quote-safe contract and exact locators.

Most web-discovered items below remain `PARTIAL_OBJECT` or `LINK_ONLY` and `COARSE_LOCATOR_ONLY` until selected for Product. Bibliographic presence is not full-object verification.
