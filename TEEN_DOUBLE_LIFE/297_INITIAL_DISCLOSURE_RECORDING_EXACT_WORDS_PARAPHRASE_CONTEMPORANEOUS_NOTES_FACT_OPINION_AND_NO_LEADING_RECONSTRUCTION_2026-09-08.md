## 0. Executive verdict

A first disclosure often changes while travelling through adult memory:

Child says:

> `He made me send something.`

Adult later remembers:

> `She said he threatened her into sending nude photos.`

That interpretation may later prove accurate.

But it is not the same evidentiary statement.

### Locked distinctions

> **CHILD'S WORDS ≠ ADULT'S PARAPHRASE.**

> **PARAPHRASE ≠ VERBATIM QUOTE.**

> **FACT ≠ INFERENCE.**

> **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR ≠ DIAGNOSED MOTIVE / EMOTION.**

> **CONTEMPORANEOUS NOTE ≠ INFALLIBLE RECORD.**

> **LATER MEMORY ≠ ORIGINAL NOTE.**

> **ACCURATE RECORDING ≠ AMATEUR FORENSIC INTERVIEWING.**

> **NOTE-TAKING SHOULD PRESERVE WHAT WAS SAID, NOT CREATE A BETTER STORY.**

## 1. Biblical control — do not bear witness beyond what you know

Relevant controls:

- Ex. 20:16 — false witness;
- Prov. 18:13 — answering before hearing;
- Prov. 18:17 — first case may require examination;
- Prov. 14:5, 25 — faithful vs false witness;
- Eph. 4:25 — truthful speech.

### Safe synthesis

A Christian adult should be able to say:

> `These are the child's words.`

and separately:

> `This is what I understood them to mean.`

without collapsing the two.

Evidence class: `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE → CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS`; exact safeguarding record practice is `PROFESSIONAL / PRUDENTIAL`.

## 2. Current safeguarding guidance strongly supports exact-word recording

### Department for Education participant-safeguarding guidance

DfE guidance instructs staff to report soon after disclosure while details are fresh, record date/time and the participant's words, avoid paraphrase/assumption, and pass the record securely to the safeguarding lead.

Source:
- https://user-research.education.gov.uk/guidance/ethics-and-safeguarding/participant-safeguarding

### Ofsted safeguarding concerns guidance

Ofsted instructs inspectors to let a child disclose at their own pace, avoid interruption/leading/probing, refer rather than investigate, and not promise confidentiality.

Source:
- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ofsted-safeguarding-policy/safeguarding-concerns-guidance-for-inspectors

### NSPCC Learning

NSPCC safeguarding guidance says records should be accurate/detailed, include the child's exact words where a verbal disclosure occurred, and distinguish factual information from opinion.

Sources:
- https://learning.nspcc.org.uk/safeguarding-child-protection/safeguarding-examples-scenarios-issues-concerns
- https://learning.nspcc.org.uk/child-abuse-and-neglect/recognising-and-responding-to-abuse

### Evidence boundary

These are professional/institutional safeguarding practices, not a biblical command that every family keep formal case records.

Their safe transfer is narrow:

> **where serious disclosure may later require other responsible adults or authorities to understand what was said, preserve the child's actual words and separate them from interpretation.**

## 3. Four layers should be separated in any serious note

### Layer A — direct quotation / exact words

Example:

> Child said: `He kept asking after I said no.`

### Layer B — observed fact

Example:

> Child paused for approximately one minute before continuing.

### Layer C — adult paraphrase

Example:

> I understood the child to be describing repeated pressure.

### Layer D — later inference / hypothesis

Example:

> This may indicate coercion, but that is my inference and was not the child's wording.

### P0

> **THE MORE SERIOUS THE CONSEQUENCE, THE MORE IMPORTANT IT IS NOT TO PRESENT C/D AS A.**

---

## 4. Do not silently clean up sexual or embarrassing language

A child may use:

- slang;
- explicit sexual terms;
- imprecise body words;
- profanity;
- unusual phrasing.

Adults may be tempted to sanitize:

> `He touched my ___`

into a more clinical anatomical formulation.

That can change meaning.

### Safe rule

Where exact words matter, record the wording actually used, even if awkward or offensive; explain separately if clarification later becomes necessary.

### Guard

Do not create a transcript-like sexual inventory where no safeguarding need exists.

`EXACT WORDS WHERE MATERIAL ≠ MAXIMAL SEXUAL DETAIL`.

Use `165`, `283`.

---

## 5. Questions asked by the adult are part of the evidentiary context

Compare:

Adult: `Did he threaten you with the photo?`

Child: `Yes.`

versus:

Adult: `Tell me what happened next.`

Child: `He said he'd send the photo to everyone.`

Both are statements.

They have different elicitation contexts.

### P0

> **ANSWER ≠ QUESTION-FREE NARRATIVE.**

If a serious record is made, important adult prompts/questions should not disappear from the account.

### Why

Otherwise later readers may think a detail originated spontaneously with the child when it was introduced by the questioner.

---

## 6. Do not reconstruct exact words from memory and put quotation marks around them

Bad pattern:

> two days later, pastor writes a memo with polished quoted sentences from memory.

If exact wording was not recorded contemporaneously, say:

> `My best recollection is...`

or:

> `I understood her to say...`

rather than fabricating transcript certainty.

### Locked

`REMEMBERED SUBSTANCE ≠ VERBATIM RECORD`.

Truthfulness includes honest uncertainty about one's own memory.

---

## 7. Later correction should not rewrite the original record invisibly

Suppose parent initially records:

> `Child said it happened Friday.`

Later child says:

> `I got the date wrong; it was Thursday.`

The safe record model is:

- preserve what was first said;
- add the later correction/date/context;
- do not silently edit history so it appears the first statement always said Thursday.

### P0

> **CORRECTION SHOULD UPDATE THE RECORD WITHOUT FALSIFYING THE RECORD'S HISTORY.**

This is especially useful alongside `296` recantation/detail-change control.

Exact institutional/legal record-retention rules remain jurisdiction/policy dependent.

---

## 8. Parent/pastor notes are evidence of what the adult recorded — not automatic proof of the underlying event

A contemporaneous note can strengthen confidence that:

> the adult recorded a particular statement at a particular time.

It does not automatically prove:

> every underlying allegation in the statement occurred.

### Locked

`CONTEMPORANEOUS NOTE ≠ INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION OF THE EVENT`.

If three elders read one parent's note, there are not three independent witnesses.

Use `291`.

---

## 9. Audio/video recording is a separate legal/privacy question

Do not jump from:

> accurate notes are helpful

to:

> secretly record every disclosure on a phone.

Audio/video recording can implicate:

- privacy;
- consent;
- data protection;
- local law;
- institutional policy;
- safeguarding process;
- later forensic interviewing.

### Locked

`ACCURATE RECORDING PRACTICE ≠ UNIVERSAL SECRET-RECORDING LICENSE`.

Exact law must be verified by jurisdiction.

---

## 10. Do not show notes to suspected offender for informal “cross-examination” in a safeguarding case

Bad sequence:

1. child discloses;
2. parent writes exact note;
3. parent hands note to alleged offender;
4. asks offender to respond directly to child;
5. parties begin coordinating/pressuring.

Where serious abuse/coercion is possible, competent safeguarding process should control.

Use `293`, `294`, `296`.

### Locked

`PRESERVE THE RECORD ≠ CIRCULATE THE RECORD`.

---

## 11. Emotion/body language must be described, not overinterpreted

Potentially safe:

> `Child was speaking quietly and looking down.`

Unsafe conversion:

> `Child displayed trauma behavior proving the allegation was true.`

Or reverse:

> `Child was calm, therefore nothing serious happened.`

### P0

`OBSERVED AFFECT ≠ TRUTH DETECTOR`.

Use `275`, `279` for affect-asymmetry controls.

---

## 12. Parents/pastors should not become evidence editors for family/church reputation

Examples:

- deleting the sentence that makes a respected member look worse;
- adding a theological interpretation the child never used;
- changing `I was scared` to `I feared for my life`;
- omitting the child's correction because it weakens the case;
- sanitizing a parent's own threatening question from the note.

### Locked

> **A SAFEGUARDING RECORD SHOULD SERVE TRUTH, NOT THE CASE THEORY OF THE PERSON WRITING IT.**

This applies whether the adult leans toward accusation or exoneration.

---

## 13. Ordinary family discipline does not need a bureaucratic dossier

Mirror guard:

Do not turn every:

- broken screen-time rule;
- ordinary sibling quarrel;
- minor lie;
- household correction

into formal incident documentation.

### P0

`SERIOUS DISCLOSURE RECORDING ≠ PERMANENT FAMILY CASE-FILE CULTURE`.

Use `283` on retention/minimization and `131` on no permanent stigma.

---

## 14. When serious disclosure and own-sin confession coexist

A teen may say:

> `I lied about where I was, but while there he threatened me.`

Adults must preserve both categories:

- child's admitted deception can be addressed;
- alleged coercion/harm must not be downgraded because the child also sinned;
- exact words should show which part was self-confession and which part was allegation/report.

### Locked

`CHILD'S REAL SIN ≠ EVIDENTIARY ERASER FOR HARM REPORTED`.

And:

`HARM REPORTED ≠ ERASER FOR SEPARATE VOLUNTARY SIN`.

---

## 15. Church intake discipline

When a pastor/elder receives a serious report, useful minimum distinctions are:

- reporter identity / source lineage where available;
- date/time received;
- exact material words where remembered/recorded;
- questions/prompts asked;
- factual observations;
- adult interpretation separately labeled;
- immediate safety action;
- where/when the concern was referred;
- what sensitive material was **not** duplicated.

### Guard

This is not a universal ecclesial form template.

The church should follow competent policy/law where applicable.

### P0

`PASTORAL NOTE ≠ FORENSIC FINDING`.

## 16. Case matrix

| Record pattern | Evidentiary problem | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| `She said he assaulted her` when child said `he kept asking after no` | interpretation presented as quote | preserve exact words; label inference |
| polished quotations written days later | false verbatim certainty | label as recollection/paraphrase |
| note omits leading question | origin of detail hidden | record material prompt/question |
| child corrects date; original overwritten | history falsified | append/update, preserve original statement |
| three leaders read one note | false witness multiplication | one recorded source, multiple readers |
| child calm during disclosure | affect overread | describe behavior; do not infer truth/falsity |
| parent records every minor conflict | dossier culture | reserve formal practice for material need |
| explicit image copied into note/evidence folder | needless possession/distribution | record fact/context; follow `283` |

## 18. Product overclaim kill-switch

Reject or rewrite:

- `A contemporaneous note proves the allegation.`
- `Parents should record every conversation with a deceptive teen.`
- `If a pastor remembers the substance, it is fine to put it in quotation marks.`
- `Body language can show whether the child is telling the truth.`
- `Exact wording means adults should ask for every sexual detail.`
- `All serious disclosures should be secretly audio-recorded.`
- `If a child later corrects a detail, edit the original note to keep the record clean.`
- `Three elders who read one parent's note are three witnesses.`
- `A note should summarize what the adult believes really happened rather than preserve ambiguous child wording.`

Preserve:

> **Сильная запись не делает рассказ “убедительнее”; она делает происхождение информации честнее. Нужно отличить слова ребёнка от взрослого пересказа, факт от вывода, первоначальную запись от поздней памяти и исправление от тайного переписывания истории. Такая точность помогает и защите ребёнка, и справедливости к обвиняемому.**

## 20. Final locked synthesis

> **После серьёзного disclosure взрослый легко начинает помнить не то, что ребёнок сказал, а то, что взрослый понял. Поэтому точность означает не недоверие к ребёнку, а верность истине: сохранить существенные слова, отметить вопросы, отделить наблюдение от вывода и не выдавать позднюю память за стенограмму. Но запись остаётся записью сообщения, а не автоматическим доказательством самого события; серьёзное расследование должно оставаться у компетентного процесса.**
