## 0. Executive verdict

A common late-stage family fracture looks like this:

1. months of deception are established;
2. new disclosure occurs;
3. some corroboration exists;
4. one parent says, `We know enough to stop digging and watch fruit`;
5. the other says, `No, I still believe there is another material layer`;
6. one parent begins returning privileges while the other continues searching;
7. the child learns that each parent operates a different evidence threshold.

The project already owns general coparental unity and triangulation in `256`. The distinct problem here is **epistemic divergence after proven deception**.

### Locked center

> **PARENTAL UNITY ≠ PRETENDING BOTH PARENTS HAVE THE SAME LEVEL OF CONFIDENCE.**

> **DIFFERENT CONFIDENCE LEVELS ≠ TWO COMPETING HOUSEHOLD VERDICTS.**

> **ONE PARENT'S SUSPICION ≠ PROVED FACT.**

> **ONE PARENT'S HOPE ≠ RESTORED CREDIBILITY.**

> **STOPPING ACTIVE INVESTIGATION ≠ DECLARING FULL TRUST.**

> **CONTINUING A BOUNDARY ≠ CONTINUING AN ACCUSATION.**

> **THE CHILD MUST NOT BECOME THE REFEREE BETWEEN PARENTAL EVIDENCE THRESHOLDS.**

## 1. Scripture does not require false certainty

Relevant controls:

- Ex. 20:16 — truth/factual integrity;
- Prov. 18:13 — do not answer before hearing;
- Prov. 18:17 — first presentation can look persuasive until examined;
- Eph. 4:25 — put away falsehood and speak truth;
- Eph. 6:1–4 — real parental authority remains morally accountable;
- James 1:19 — listening and restraint are Christian duties, not signs of weak authority.

### Safe synthesis

Parents are not required to say:

> `We both know exactly what happened and feel exactly the same confidence.`

when they do not.

They are required not to make uncertainty into falsehood.

Evidence class: `DIRECT_SCRIPTURE → CANONICAL_SYNTHESIS` for truthfulness; exact family procedure is `PRUDENTIAL_APPLICATION`.

---

## 2. Four questions must be separated

After repeated lying, parents often collapse four distinct judgments.

### A. What facts are established?

Example:

- hidden account existed;
- contact continued after a prohibition;
- a false statement was made.

### B. What remains unresolved?

Example:

- whether another account exists;
- whether contact ended on the claimed date;
- whether a third person received material.

### C. What present boundary is justified by established risk?

Example:

- nighttime device restriction;
- no private contact with a named person;
- periodic inspection.

### D. How much credibility/trust has returned?

This is usually slower and domain-specific.

### P0

> **DISAGREEMENT ABOUT D DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN DISAGREEMENT ABOUT A.**

And:

> **A BOUNDARY UNDER C MAY REMAIN EVEN WHEN ACTIVE SEARCH UNDER B STOPS.**

Use `281`.

## 3. Parent A may be ready to stop searching before Parent B

That fact alone does not prove either parent is morally wrong.

Possible Parent A errors:

- relief-driven premature closure;
- wanting the family crisis to be over;
- mistaking tears/apology for sufficient evidence;
- fear of relational rupture;
- fatigue from investigation.

Possible Parent B errors:

- betrayal-driven hypervigilance;
- requiring certainty no child can provide;
- treating every ambiguity as proof;
- moving the completeness goalpost after each answer;
- continuing search because anxiety remains rather than because a stewardship question remains.

### Mirror guard

Do not moralize temperament:

> cautious parent = faithful;

or

> hopeful parent = merciful.

Either posture can be wise or distorted depending on evidence and duty.

---

## 4. The parents need a shared evidence language before a shared verdict

Use a compact ladder such as:

1. `ESTABLISHED`;
2. `STRONGLY SUPPORTED`;
3. `PLAUSIBLE / UNRESOLVED`;
4. `SPECULATIVE`;
5. `DISPROVED / MATERIALLY CORRECTED`.

This is not inspired terminology.

Its function is to prevent sentences like:

> `Dad knows there is more`;

when Dad actually means:

> `I still think there may be more.`

Or:

> `Mom believes me now`;

when Mom actually means:

> `I do not think we presently have a concrete reason to keep interrogating.`

### Locked

> **CALIBRATED LANGUAGE CAN PRESERVE PARENTAL UNITY WITHOUT MANUFACTURING AGREEMENT.**

---

## 5. A shared stopping rule can exist before shared trust

`281` is controlling.

Parents can jointly decide:

> `We are stopping active investigation because no unresolved question currently changes safety, repair, stewardship or jurisdiction.`

while one parent still says:

> `I remain less confident than you that the account is fully truthful.`

That is coherent.

### P0

> **STOPPING RULE = DECISION ABOUT THE NEXT INVESTIGATIVE ACT.**

It is not necessarily:

> **VERDICT THAT EVERY HISTORICAL FACT IS KNOWN.**

---

## 6. Do not run parallel secret investigations

A particularly corrosive pattern:

- one parent tells the child the investigation is over;
- the other secretly continues broad device/account searches;
- each parent privately offers different conditions for restoring access;
- the child later discovers the contradiction.

This teaches that the parents' words do not have a stable meaning.

### Exception guard

Safety-driven discreet evidence preservation can still be legitimate under `270` where disclosure of the inquiry could materially increase danger.

But ordinary post-crisis distrust is not automatically a safety emergency.

### Locked

`PARENTAL DISAGREEMENT ≠ LICENSE FOR EACH PARENT TO RUN A PRIVATE STATE WITH SEPARATE RULES`.

---

## 7. Do not ask the child to prove which parent is right

Bad patterns:

- `Tell your father there is nothing else.`
- `Explain to your mother why she is naive.`
- `Which of us do you think handled this better?`
- `If you were truly repentant, you would convince Dad.`

The child owes truth.

The child does **not** owe the impossible task of producing emotional certainty in both parents.

### P0

> **THE CHILD'S DUTY IS TRUTHFULNESS; THE PARENTS' DUTY IS TO GOVERN THEIR OWN EVIDENTIARY DISAGREEMENT.**

---

## 8. No parent may become the child's appeal court against the other

Suppose current rule is:

> no private phone use after 22:00.

If one parent thinks trust has improved faster, the answer should not ordinarily be:

> `Ask me when your father is not around and I will give it back.`

That recreates the infrastructure of selective disclosure.

Use `256`.

### Better structure

- parents discuss the rule directly;
- identify the evidence/risk the rule addresses;
- decide whether it changes;
- communicate one operative household rule;
- if they remain unresolved, choose a temporary proportionate rule rather than competing permissions.

Exact headship/coprocess mechanics depend on the project's broader family-government framework; this module does not create a new universal marital procedure.

---

## 9. Temporary default under unresolved parental disagreement

Where no emergency exists and parents cannot yet agree, the safest ordinary default is not necessarily the most severe imaginable restriction.

A defensible temporary boundary should be:

- connected to already established risk;
- reversible;
- clear;
- time/review bounded rather than indefinite;
- not dependent on declaring an unresolved allegation true;
- not a punishment for the parents' disagreement.

### Locked

> **PRECAUTION MAY BE JUSTIFIED BY RISK WITHOUT BEING DESCRIBED AS PUNISHMENT FOR AN UNPROVED ACT.**

Evidence class: `PRUDENTIAL APPLICATION`.

---

## 10. Review criteria must be shared before privileges move

Parents should try to agree on what evidence would justify changing a boundary.

Examples:

- no new material contradictions for a defined review period;
- truthful reporting of a same-domain lapse before discovery;
- compliance with a contact restriction;
- stable use of a device under current rules;
- voluntary help-seeking;
- completion of a concrete repair obligation.

### Guard

This is not a mechanical salvation score.

No checklist proves regeneration.

But a boundary with no shared review criteria makes split-parent restoration highly likely.

### P0

`NO FIXED TIMER ≠ NO REVIEW STANDARD`.

---

## 11. One parent's verified error must update the shared model

Use `288`.

If one parent strongly promoted an allegation and later evidence disproves it, the family must not preserve the allegation merely because the other parent still feels suspicious.

Likewise, if one parent prematurely dismissed a concern and new evidence establishes it, the family must update upward.

### Locked

> **EVIDENCE MUST BE ABLE TO MOVE THE FAMILY'S CONCLUSION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.**

That is a truth-channel requirement.

---

## 12. One parent's private confidence in repentance is not a household privilege grant

A parent may personally think:

> `I believe the repentance claim is probably sincere.`

That is not the same as:

- restored domain credibility;
- restored device access;
- restored church role;
- closed safeguarding concern;
- proof that every material disclosure is complete.

Use `130`, `131`, `278`.

### Locked

`PERSONAL HOPE ≠ ADMINISTRATIVE RESET`.

And:

`LOWER PERSONAL CONFIDENCE ≠ PERMANENT ADMINISTRATIVE FREEZE`.

## 13. Coparenting evidence is supportive, not theological authority

`256` already controls the empirical layer:

- 2022 coparenting meta-analysis: 93 studies / 41,207 participants;
- 2025/2026 triangulation meta-analysis: 49 studies / 23,336 participants / 15,137 families;
- 2026 meta-analysis linking feeling caught with poorer parent-child relationship quality.

The safe use here is narrow:

> **placing children between parents and creating competing coparental systems is associated with poorer family/child relational outcomes.**

It does not establish:

- a biblical headship model;
- which parent should win a factual dispute;
- that disagreement itself is sinful;
- that parents must display artificial unanimity.

Evidence class: `EMPIRICAL_SECONDARY`.

## 14. Safeguarding changes the rule

If the unresolved disagreement concerns:

- abuse;
- grooming;
- sextortion;
- credible violence;
- illegal sexual imagery;
- self-harm danger;
- another vulnerable person at risk;

then `we parents will compromise on a middle rule` may be inadequate.

The case may require:

- immediate safety action;
- competent external investigation;
- reporting according to jurisdiction;
- evidence preservation;
- separation from alleged danger.

### P0

`PARENTAL AGREEMENT ≠ SAFEGUARDING CLEARANCE`.

`PARENTAL DISAGREEMENT ≠ REASON TO DELAY REQUIRED PROTECTION`.

Use `242`, `249`, `281`, `283`.

## 15. Church mediation should not simply choose the more confident parent

Pastoral helpers should distinguish:

- established facts;
- unresolved claims;
- current safety issues;
- current household boundaries;
- credibility/trust judgments;
- marital/coprocess conflict.

Bad pastoral shortcut:

> `Dad is head, therefore Dad's factual inference is true.`

Also bad:

> `Mom is more relational, therefore her belief in the child is spiritually superior.`

### Better role

- restore calibrated fact-language;
- help parents define the stewardship question;
- prevent child triangulation;
- call either parent to repentance for falsehood, rage, complicity or unilateral undermining where present;
- defer safeguarding/legal questions to competent jurisdiction where required.

Use `256`, `282`.

## 16. Case matrix

| Case | Parent divergence | Safe response |
|---|---|---|
| one parent thinks disclosure complete; other has no concrete new evidence | confidence differs | stop ordinary archaeology under `281`; retain justified boundaries; review fruit |
| one parent has specific new contradictory evidence | materially new fact | reopen bounded question; do not call it mere anxiety |
| one parent wants full phone restoration; other wants permanent ban | stewardship disagreement | tie rule to established risk + review criteria; no competing secret permissions |
| child tells different versions to each parent | possible active triangulation/deception | parents compare exact facts directly; do not infer manipulation until mismatch is established |
| parents disagree over motive but conduct is established | heart-reading dispute | govern established conduct; do not make motive certainty a privilege condition |
| safeguarding allegation divides parents | protection/jurisdiction | immediate safety + competent process; family compromise is not controlling |
| one parent made disproved accusation | evidentiary correction | apply `288`; correct fact and consequences specifically |

## 17. Product overclaim kill-switch

Reject or rewrite:

- `Good parents always agree about whether the child is telling the truth.`
- `The stricter parent should control because deception already happened.`
- `The more trusting parent should control because forgiveness requires believing.`
- `Stopping investigation means both parents must now trust the child.`
- `If parents disagree, the child should explain until both are convinced.`
- `One parent may secretly restore a privilege the other parent lawfully withheld because the other is too strict.`
- `One parent may secretly investigate after telling the child the investigation ended, merely because suspicion remains.`
- `Parental agreement can substitute for safeguarding clearance.`

Preserve:

> **After real deception, two faithful parents may temporarily differ in confidence. Their task is not to manufacture identical feelings but to agree on what is established, what remains unknown, which present boundaries are justified by known risk, what would reopen inquiry, and what evidence would permit re-entrustment. The child must tell the truth; the child must not be made responsible for resolving the parents' epistemic conflict.**

## 19. Final locked synthesis

> **Родители могут по-разному оценивать достоверность нового признания, особенно после долгой лжи. Библейское единство не требует притвориться, будто сомнений нет. Оно требует не делать из разных уровней уверенности две конкурирующие реальности. Нужно отдельно назвать установленное, неизвестное, текущую границу и критерий пересмотра. Можно прекратить расследование, не объявляя доверие восстановленным; можно сохранить границу, не объявляя недоказанное доказанным. И ребёнок не должен становиться судьёй, который обязан убедить одного родителя против другого.**
