# 0. The missing positive architecture

The project strongly teaches:

- flee temptation;
- cut off access;
- remove the phone/app/account if necessary;
- leave corrupting company;
- restrict dangerous opportunity.

All of that can be biblically necessary.

But a parent can still make a serious mistake:

> **remove the sinful infrastructure and leave the child's life empty.**

Then holiness is experienced almost entirely as:

- no phone;
- no friends;
- no music;
- no internet;
- no privacy;
- no pleasure.

That is not the full NT pattern of sanctification.

### Governing thesis

> **Biblical change is not only subtraction. The NT repeatedly joins putting off old conduct with putting on new conduct, fleeing evil with active pursuit of righteousness, and leaving corrupt company with fellowship among those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.**

---

# 1. Ephesians 4:20–24 — put off, renewed mind, put on

Primary:

- Eph. 4:20–24.

The structure is not merely:

> stop behaving badly.

It includes:

- learning Christ;
- putting off old man/former manner of life;
- renewal in the spirit of the mind;
- putting on the new man created according to God in righteousness/holiness of truth.

### Project significance

Christian parenting cannot regenerate the child.

But Christian discipleship should present holiness as a **new manner of life**, not an indefinite quarantine from bad stimuli.

---

# 2. Ephesians 4:25–32 — Paul repeatedly gives replacements

Calvin:

- https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom41/calcom41.iv.v.vii.html

Examples:

## Lie → truth

Not merely:

> stop lying.

But:

> speak truth to one another.

## Theft → labor + generosity

Not merely:

> stop stealing.

But:

> work honestly so there is something to share.

## Corrupt speech → edifying speech

Not merely:

> stop swearing/sexual jokes.

But:

> speak what gives grace/builds others according to need.

## Bitterness/malice → kindness/tenderhearted forgiveness

The new life develops opposite practices.

### Strong line

> **The Christian answer to a sinful habit is often not an empty space but an obedient replacement.**

---

# 3. Colossians 3 — mortification and putting on belong together

Primary:

- Col. 3:1–17.

Calvin/CCEL:

- https://www.ccel.org/study/Colossians_3%3A5-10

Paul commands:

- put to death earthly members/desires;
- put away anger, malice, slander, obscene speech, lying;
- put on the new man;
- put on compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, patience, forgiveness, love;
- let word of Christ dwell richly;
- sing/teach/admonish;
- do all in Lord's name.

### Project consequence

The replacement is not merely:

> “Find a harmless hobby.”

It is a Christ-centered reordering of:

- speech;
- relationships;
- desires;
- worship;
- service.

Healthy hobbies/work/sport can serve this, but they are not themselves regeneration/sanctification.

---

# 4. 2 Timothy 2:22 — flee AND pursue AND change company

Primary:

- 2 Tim. 2:22.

Commentary:

- https://www.biblehub.com/commentaries/2_timothy/2-22.htm

Paul's sequence:

- flee youthful desires;
- pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace;
- **with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart**.

### This is nearly a complete youth-discipleship architecture

1. negative distance from corrupt desire;
2. active positive pursuit;
3. godly companionship.

### Strong line

> **“Убегай” без “стремись” оставляет пустоту; “стремись” без “убегай” оставляет открытой дверь искушению. Павел требует обоих.**

---

# 5. Radical amputation is therefore one part of a larger pattern

Matthew 5 / 1 Cor. 6 / 2 Tim. 2 support strong removal of occasions.

But after removing:

- private browser;
- secret account;
- corrupt chat;
- dangerous friendship,

parents/child should ask:

> What righteous good will occupy this time, attention, body and relationship space?

Not as distraction technique alone.

As formation toward a life fit for God's service.

---

# 6. Concrete replacements for the teen-double-life context

These are prudential applications, not direct verse-to-activity commands.

## Secret sexualized chats → truthful fellowship

- family conversation;
- mature peer friendship;
- accountable church relationships;
- group activity without sexualized secrecy.

## Endless passive stimulation → useful labor/skill

- household work;
- paid work where age-appropriate;
- craft;
- study;
- serving others.

Eph. 4:28 especially grounds labor not only as occupation but as capacity to give.

## Profanity / sexual joking → speech that builds

Eph. 4:29 / 5:4.

## Solitary temptation windows → embodied/shared routines

- device outside bedroom;
- family/common-room time;
- exercise/sport;
- early sleep;
- service.

These are prudential supports, not holiness by schedule.

## Corrupt peer belonging → godly peer belonging

2 Tim. 2:22 directly includes pursuing virtues **with others** who call on the Lord.

---

# 7. Work matters, but do not turn busyness into salvation

Biblical work can train:

- responsibility;
- usefulness;
- stewardship;
- service/generosity.

But parents can misuse it:

> “Keep him exhausted so he cannot sin.”

That may suppress opportunity without touching heart.

### Correct category

Useful structure can:

- reduce idle opportunity;
- cultivate responsibility;
- reconnect body/time to good purposes.

It cannot:

- regenerate;
- justify;
- replace gospel.

---

# 8. Healthy pleasure is not the enemy

A reaction to sexualized/digital pleasure can become ascetic:

> “All pleasure is suspicious.”

Scripture does not teach this.

God gives legitimate goods to receive with thanksgiving.

The issue is:

- idolatry;
- lawlessness;
- domination by appetite;
- pleasures loved above God (2 Tim. 3:4 context).

### Project consequence

Parents should help youth rediscover lawful delight:

- friendship;
- beauty;
- food/family celebration;
- music consistent with holiness;
- exercise;
- craft/achievement;
- creation;
- service;
- marriage in its season.

### Guard

Do not promise lawful pleasures will always subjectively feel stronger than sinful stimulation immediately.

Holiness is not justified because it wins a pleasure contest every evening.

---

# 9. Family restoration needs positive shared life

After discovery, families can become:

> interrogation headquarters.

Every conversation is about:

- phone checks;
- pornography;
- lying;
- rules;
- suspicion.

Even when boundaries are necessary, relationship cannot be rebuilt only through inspection.

### Positive family work

- ordinary conversation;
- meals;
- shared responsibilities;
- worship;
- projects;
- humor without filth;
- service;
- appropriate recreation.

### Strong line

> **A home fighting hidden sin must remain more than a crime scene.**

---

# 10. Church counter-formation should be substantive, not merely entertaining

Bad response to worldly attraction:

> make youth group more exciting than TikTok.

Church cannot outbid every sinful stimulus by spectacle.

Better:

- serious Scripture;
- respected godly adults;
- truthful friendships;
- meaningful service;
- doctrinal depth;
- responsibility;
- prayer;
- singing/worship;
- belonging without sexualized status games.

### Strong line

> **The church's alternative to the world's intensity is not necessarily greater entertainment, but a truer and weightier life.**

---

# 11. For the unregenerate child, positive formation is still meaningful

Do not reason:

> “Since he is not regenerate, positive habits are hypocrisy.”

Parents may teach:

- truth;
- work;
- restraint;
- kindness;
- Scripture;
- church attendance;
- useful service appropriate to non-representative roles.

But they should not certify these as saving fruit automatically.

### Locked distinction

> **Formation is a real parental duty and providential means; regeneration remains God's sovereign work.**

---

# 12. For the regenerate child, replacement belongs to sanctification

For one credibly professing Christ:

- mortification is by the Spirit (Rom. 8:13);
- new practices flow from union/new identity;
- accountability/boundaries serve rather than replace grace.

### Guard

Do not turn replacement habits into secular habit-hacking with Christian vocabulary.

The controlling reality is:

> learn Christ / new man / Spirit / Word.

---

# 13. A simple Part II framework

After exposure:

1. **STOP / CUT** — remove access/occasion proportional to danger;
2. **TELL TRUTH** — end concealment;
3. **TURN** — repentance/faith/forsaking;
4. **PUT ON** — replace old conduct with concrete obedience;
5. **WITH WHOM** — rebuild godly companionship;
6. **REVIEW** — progressively restore trust where fruit appears.

### Guard

This is an editorial framework, not a six-step regeneration formula.

# 14. Article-ready formulations

> **Отрезать путь ко греху иногда необходимо; но пустота сама по себе не является святостью.**

> **Павел говорит не только “убегай от юношеских похотей”, но сразу “держись правды, веры, любви и мира с призывающими Господа”.**

> **Дом, который борется с тайным грехом ребёнка, не должен навсегда превратиться в место досмотра.**

> **Церковь не обязана быть развлекательнее мира; она обязана показывать более истинную и весомую жизнь.**

> **Христианское изменение часто выглядит не как “перестал делать”, а как “перестал делать зло и начал делать противоположное добро”.**

---

# 15. Final locked synthesis

> **Радикальное отсечение соблазна — библейская мудрость, но не полная картина изменения. Новый Завет соединяет “снять” с “облечься”, “убежать” со “стремиться”, отказ от лжи — с правдой, отказ от гнилой речи — с созидающим словом, отказ от корысти — с трудом и щедростью. Поэтому после закрытого браузера ребёнку нужен не только более пустой день, а более правдивая жизнь: отношения, труд, служение, Слово, здоровая компания и конкретное добро. Для невозрождённого это остаётся воспитанием и средствами; для верующего — частью освящения во Христе.**
