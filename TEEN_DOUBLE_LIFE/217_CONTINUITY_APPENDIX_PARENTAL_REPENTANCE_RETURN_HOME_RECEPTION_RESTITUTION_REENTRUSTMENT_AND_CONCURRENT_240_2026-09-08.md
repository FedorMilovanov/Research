## 3. Mixed fault — locked P0

`PARENTAL REPENTANCE ≠ MORAL CAPITULATION`.

`CHILD'S REBELLION ≠ PARENTAL IMMUNITY`.

`PARENTAL SIN ≠ CHILD'S LICENSE`.

Scripture itself places child obedience and parental non-provocation together (Eph. 6:1–4; Col. 3:20–21).

Therefore a valid judgment can be:

> **The child's lying/sexual rebellion was sin, and the father's rage/humiliation/false accusation was also sin.**

Do not force mixed cases into a single innocent party / single guilty party narrative.

---

## 4. Parent repentance — required form

A parent who sinned should ordinarily:

- name the exact wrong;
- correct false facts;
- ask forgiveness without an “if you were offended” pseudo-apology;
- make restitution/correction where appropriate;
- change the sinful method;
- leave lawful moral/household boundaries intact;
- avoid using the apology as a manipulation device to extract the child's immediate confession.

### Locked synthesis

> **Confess exactly what is yours; do not confess what is not yours merely to buy peace.**

Likewise:

> **Do not use the child's greater sin to excuse your smaller/different sin.**

---

## 5. Authority under Christ

The project rejects the idea:

> “If the father says ‘I was wrong,’ authority collapses.”

Christian authority is derivative.

A superior's repentance can display that he is himself under Christ rather than claiming personal sovereignty.

But do not promise that parent repentance will restore influence or cause the prodigal to return. Regeneration/repentance remains God's work and the child's responsibility.

---

## 6. Third-party mixed-case guard

Pastors/friends/relatives must investigate claim by claim.

Do not conclude:

- `parents sinned → child should ignore all parental correction`;
- `child sexually rebelled → everything parents did was justified`.

Distinguish:

- real abuse/danger;
- unlawful overreach;
- ordinary painful but lawful correction;
- child manipulation of abuse language;
- parental misuse of authority language;
- cases where both sides sinned.

Existing rule remains:

> **Choose what God calls true before choosing parents or child as a party.**

## 7. Return home — category distinctions

`RECEIVE THE PERSON ≠ RESTORE EVERY PREVIOUS PRIVILEGE IMMEDIATELY`.

`RETURN HOME ≠ REPENTANCE`.

`REPENTANCE ≠ INSTANT RESTORATION OF ALL ACCESS`.

`MERCY MAY BE IMMEDIATE; RE-ENTRUSTMENT MAY BE GRADUAL`.

`FRUIT ≠ PRICE OF FORGIVENESS`.

### Luke 15 control

The father eagerly receives the returning son and does not require additional arbitrary suffering to prove sincerity.

But Luke 15 is not a modern housing/property/phone/vehicle/safeguarding manual.

Do not infer an unconditional right to return to the exact former co-residence arrangement.

---

## 8. 2 Corinthians 2 anti-severity guard

Church discipline must not become permanent punishment after repentance has become sufficiently evident.

Analogical family application:

> **A restriction once justified by protection or damaged credibility can become sinful severity if it is retained after its stewardship purpose disappears merely so the offender continues to “pay.”**

This does not mean all safeguards have the same duration.

---

## 9. Restitution — locked theological category

Zacchaeus / Watson control:

`RESTITUTION = FRUIT / DUTY WHERE ACTUAL WRONG CAN BE REPAIRED`

not

`RESTITUTION = PURCHASE PRICE OF FORGIVENESS`.

Possible family forms:

- return stolen money/property;
- repair damage;
- correct lies told about parents/siblings;
- repay legitimate debt where able;
- apologize to harmed people;
- undo practical harm where reasonably possible.

Do not make restitution impossible, theatrical, or unrelated to actual wrong.

## 10. Philemon/Onesimus reconciliation pattern — use with slavery guard

Do not equate Roman slavery with parent-child authority.

Allowed reconciliation pattern:

`REAL TRANSFORMATION + GRACIOUS RECEPTION + PRIOR WRONG/DEBT NOT PRETENDED AWAY`.

Calvin's key control is useful: Paul does not lightly certify Onesimus but does so after substantial evidence of change, while the letter still acknowledges possible loss/debt.

## 11. Residential return — lawful-condition domains

Potentially legitimate co-residence conditions for a competent adult may concern:

- sexual activity/partner lodging in the home;
- pornography distribution/exposure to minors;
- drugs/drunkenness/violence/theft/illegal material;
- protection of younger siblings/vulnerable persons;
- reasonable truth about matters directly affecting household safety/property;
- work/job-seeking/education where competent and contextually appropriate;
- reasonable financial/household contribution;
- a defined conflict-resolution/counsel process where needed;
- respect for other residents;
- civil tenancy/legal requirements.

### Conditions that require very strong caution

Do not make ordinary housing mercy depend on:

- a coerced saving-faith profession;
- forced baptism/church membership;
- scripted emotional performance;
- fake confession of beliefs the adult does not hold.

The unregenerate remain under God's moral law; parents cannot force regeneration.

## 12. Trust is domain-specific

Avoid a single global “trust restored / not restored” switch.

Different histories may justify different safeguards in:

- money;
- device access;
- younger children;
- driving/property;
- sexual safety;
- truth about whereabouts where still household-relevant;
- ministry/choir/youth roles.

`FORGIVENESS ≠ UNIVERSAL IMMEDIATE RE-ENTRUSTMENT`.

But equally:

`PAST DECEPTION ≠ PERMANENT UNIVERSAL DISTRUST`.

Restoration must have a real possible direction.

## 13. Two opposite restoration failures

### Premature false normalcy

- one emotional confession becomes total credibility;
- all safeguards disappear;
- harmed people are pressured to embrace immediately;
- younger-person risk is ignored;
- restitution disappears;
- church discipline is cancelled merely to remove family shame.

### Older-brother severity

- suffering becomes a required payment;
- goalposts continually move;
- every past sin is weaponized forever;
- no fruit is ever sufficient even for limited re-entrustment;
- parents resent grace because the child “got off too easily.”

### Locked center

`JOY ≠ NAIVETY`.

`SAFEGUARD ≠ VENGEANCE`.

## 14. Jurisdictions remain distinct

`FAMILY RECEPTION ≠ RESIDENTIAL ACCESS ≠ ECCLESIAL RESTORATION ≠ MINISTRY ROLE`.

- family deals with natural relation/love and household stewardship;
- church deals with profession, membership, Table, discipline, ministry qualification;
- civil authorities deal with law/crime/tenancy/protection;
- no office should silently absorb the others.
