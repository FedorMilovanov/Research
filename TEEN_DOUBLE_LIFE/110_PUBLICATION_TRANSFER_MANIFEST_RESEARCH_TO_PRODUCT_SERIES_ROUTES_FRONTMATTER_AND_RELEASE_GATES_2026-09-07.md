# 9. Source-transfer rule

Research files are **evidence control**, not reader bibliography.

The product article must cite the underlying source, not `Research/TEEN_DOUBLE_LIFE/*.md`, for reader-facing claims.

Examples:

- Scripture claim → Bible text / translation convention.
- Puritan claim → exact primary/archival locator.
- 1689 claim → selected confession edition/paragraph.
- Pew/WHO/UNICEF/NCMEC fact → direct institutional/publication URL.
- peer-reviewed empirical claim → DOI/PubMed/journal source.

Research may remain the audit trail explaining why wording was permitted.

---

# 10. Direct-quotation rule

Before a historical direct quotation enters product prose:

1. reopen exact primary/archival source;
2. verify wording in context;
3. identify work/chapter/sermon where practical;
4. translate quoted thought into Russian for the Russian article according to product policy;
5. keep original English out of body prose unless current product policy explicitly permits it;
6. preserve direct-source link in bibliography/source note;
7. do not quote a modernized paraphrase as if it were the historical wording.

A quote candidate in Research is not publication authority by itself.

# 11. Modern-data freshness rule

Part I contains time-sensitive contemporary facts. At actual drafting/release:

- use Pew 2025 rather than older 2024 internet-frequency figures when the same structural claim is being made;
- recheck WHO/HBSC because the underlying 2022 dataset may still be the latest comparable multi-country measure;
- use September 2026 UNICEF `Through Children's Eyes` only with explicit victimization framing;
- use NCMEC report counts as report volume, never prevalence;
- recheck Children’s Commissioner figures and keep England/sample scope visible.

If a fact changes between draft and release, update the fact rather than preserving a Research-era number for consistency.

# 12. Publication wording classes

Every nontrivial claim should be mentally classified before prose is finalized.

## A. Direct biblical claim

Can be stated strongly if historical-grammatical context is respected.

## B. Confessional synthesis

State as confessional/theological synthesis, not as if the confession were Scripture.

## C. Historical pastoral witness

Use as subordinate witness/illustration; do not let Owen/Baxter/Spurgeon create a command Scripture does not give.

## D. Empirical association/prevalence

Preserve:

- country/sample;
- age;
- study year;
- design;
- association vs causation;
- report volume vs prevalence.

## E. Pastoral inference

Use `может`, `разумно`, `церковь вправе`, `в некоторых случаях`, rather than `Библия требует именно...` unless the step is actually direct.

## F. Prudential/local policy

Must never masquerade as universal divine statute.
