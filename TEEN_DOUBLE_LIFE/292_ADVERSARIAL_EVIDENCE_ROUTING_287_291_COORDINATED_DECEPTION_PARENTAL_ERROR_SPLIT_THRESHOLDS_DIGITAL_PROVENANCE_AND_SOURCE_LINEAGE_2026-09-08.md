# 2. Master evidentiary sequence after serious deception

For ordinary family/church fact-finding, do not begin with:

> `How can we force certainty?`

Use:

### Step A — define the material stewardship question (`281`)

What answer could change:

- safety;
- repair;
- boundary;
- jurisdiction?

### Step B — classify the source

Is it:

- direct observation;
- child admission/denial;
- digital artifact;
- secondhand report;
- independent corroboration;
- rumor;
- inference?

### Step C — test provenance/source lineage (`290–291`)

What is original?

Who actually knows what?

Are multiple reports independent or repetitions?

### Step D — account for known deception architecture (`287`)

Was collusion/proxy use actually established?

Do not infer it merely because possible.

### Step E — calibrate language (`288–289`)

Use:

- established;
- strongly supported;
- unresolved/plausible;
- speculative;
- disproved/corrected.

### Step F — choose action proportional to established risk (`281–282`)

A precaution can sometimes precede final adjudication.

### Step G — stop ordinary search when further intrusion has no defined stewardship yield (`281`)

Residual distrust can remain without infinite investigation.

# 3. Apparent tension: coordinated deception vs source-lineage humility

No contradiction.

`287` says peers **can** fabricate corroboration.

`291` says similar/repeated reports do not prove collusion and may simply share one source innocently.

### Reconciled rule

> **DO NOT COUNT DEPENDENT SOURCES AS INDEPENDENT; DO NOT CALL DEPENDENCE COLLUSION WITHOUT EVIDENCE OF COORDINATION.**

Possible states:

1. independent corroboration;
2. dependent but honest repetition;
3. uncertain lineage;
4. established coordinated deception.

Product/church language must distinguish them.

# 4. Apparent tension: child has low credibility vs screenshot may be weak

No contradiction.

A child can have a proven history of lying **and** a particular screenshot can be ambiguous, wrongly attributed or manipulated.

### Reconciled rule

`LOW CREDIBILITY` is evidence about the reliability of future unsupported claims.

It does not perform technical authentication of every external artifact.

### Locked

> **BAD CREDIBILITY DOES NOT AUTHENTICATE EVIDENCE AGAINST THE PERSON.**

And:

> **WEAK ARTIFACT DOES NOT ERASE SEPARATE PROVEN DECEPTION.**

Use `288` if parents overstate the artifact and later must correct themselves.

# 9. Evidence and heart-reading remain separate

- whether relief means worldly sorrow;

### Locked

Moral evaluation can include observable patterns and stated motives, but Product should not turn inference into heart-reading certainty.

- sextortion;

- credible violence;

ordinary family evidence logic becomes subordinate to competent safety process.

It means:

> enough credible risk can require protective action before the family possesses complete adjudicative certainty.

`SOURCE-LINEAGE CAUTION ≠ EXCUSE TO SUPPRESS A SAFETY REPORT`.
