**Status:** `CURRENT QUESTION-QUALITY SUPPLEMENT / RESEARCH ONLY / NOT PRODUCTION`

## New candidates 091–096

### `w3q_091` — 4:11

Direct-text question on the telos of speaking and serving: both are framed so that God may be glorified through Jesus Christ.

The question must not imply that speaking/service is an exhaustive taxonomy of all gifts.

### `w3q_092` — 4:14

This deliberately sits next to the existing 4:14 textual HOLD.

Safe base-text content:
- reproached in Christ's name;
- called blessed;
- the Spirit of glory and of God rests on them.

Unsafe:
- importing an expanded variant into the base text;
- claiming the whole verse is unteachable merely because one textual unit is disputed.

Principle:

```text
DISPUTED_DETAIL != WHOLE_VERSE_HOLD
```

### `w3q_093` — 4:14 morphology

MorphGNT freezes `ἀναπαύεται` as `3PMI-S--`, lemma `ἀναπαύω`.

This is a morphology question only. It cannot answer the apparatus question.

### `w3q_094` — 5:7

Direct-text reason for casting anxieties on God: `ὅτι αὐτῷ μέλει περὶ ὑμῶν`.

The keyed answer stays with the explicit causal clause rather than turning the verse into a prosperity promise or mental-health prohibition.

### `w3q_095` — 5:11

Direct doxology after 5:10:

`αὐτῷ τὸ κράτος εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων· ἀμήν.`

This protects the chapter from jumping directly from restoration verbs to Silvanus and missing the doxological response.

### `w3q_096` — 5:5 morphology

MorphGNT freezes `ἐγκομβώσασθε` as `2AMD-P--`, lemma `ἐγκομβόομαι`.

The item tests the imperative form without pretending the verb supplies a certain archaeological reconstruction of one garment.

## Verse-coverage rule

For future chapter banks:

1. every verse should have a safe direct-text or morphology route unless the **entire verse** is genuinely unusable without unresolved evidence;
2. a textual HOLD on one phrase should coexist with READY material for stable wording in the same verse;
3. no verse should be represented only by a high-level synthesis question when a simple direct observation is available;
4. deep/research questions add distinctions; they do not replace basic textual literacy.

## Distractor blueprint contract

Wave 3.0 candidates still contain keyed concepts, not final four-option cards. Before authoring, each card should draw wrong answers from **nearby misconception families**.

Good distractors:
- same grammatical category, wrong parsing feature;
- same OT-reuse category, wrong strength/classification;
- serious competing interpretation, clearly wrong for the question's epistemic level;
- plausible historical reconstruction that exceeds the evidence;
- project-systematic conclusion incorrectly presented as lexical/textual fact.

Bad distractors:
- unrelated book/place/person;
- absurd modern object;
- obvious caricature of another theological position;
- correct answer is the only long/technical option;
- one option says “all of the above” when the bank never uses it elsewhere;
- distractor contradicts the verse in a way no attentive learner would choose.

## High-value misconception families

### 4:1 `πέπαυται`

Wrong families:
- perfect morphology = automatic sinless perfection;
- suffering earns forgiveness;
- `πέπαυται` means physical death only.

Correct distinction:
- form is secure; the theological force requires context/exegesis.

### 4:6 `νεκροῖς / εὐηγγελίσθη`

Wrong families:
- dative plural proves postmortem preaching;
- aorist passive proves preaching before death;
- 4:6 must be identical to 3:19 because both mention dead/spirit themes.

Correct distinction:
- morphology does not settle chronology, location, or identity.

### 4:8 / Prov 10:12

Wrong families:
- mechanically verbatim LXX quotation;
- no scriptural relation at all;
- direct translation from MT established by wording alone.

Correct distinction:
- strong proverbial/scriptural reuse with visible LXX wording differences.

### 4:14

Wrong families:
- variant expansion treated as base SBLGNT wording;
- textual variation makes the whole verse unusable;
- `ἀναπαύεται` morphology proves a particular manuscript reading.

Correct distinction:
- teach stable base text while holding the unresolved apparatus detail.

### 4:18 / Prov 11:31

Wrong families:
- merely thematic echo;
- identical category to the looser 4:8 reuse;
- proverb proves salvation by difficulty/works.

Correct distinction:
- very close LXX reproduction, with contextual theology handled separately.

### 5:2 elders

Wrong families:
- `ποιμάνατε` alone proves a complete denominational polity;
- disputed `ἐπισκοποῦντες` is safe as an unqualified original-text premise;
- anti-domination means elders have no leadership authority at all.

Correct distinction:
- stable shepherding command + ethical constraints; apparatus-sensitive claims remain HOLD.

### 5:5 / Prov 3:34

Wrong families:
- completely verbatim, including the subject;
- only thematic similarity;
- change `κύριος` -> `ὁ θεός` proves a known authorial motive.

Correct distinction:
- very close reuse with visible subject adaptation, motive unclaimed.

### 5:8 roaring lion

Wrong families:
- one OT lion text is grammatically mandatory;
- arena/ad bestias is established as the one source;
- lion imagery is irrelevant because διάβολος is already named.

Correct distinction:
- metaphor is explicit; exclusive background identification is interpretive and multiple backgrounds are seriously discussed.

### 5:12 Silvanus

Wrong families:
- διὰ Σιλουανοῦ grammatically proves secretary/amanuensis;
- polished Greek proves Silvanus composed the letter;
- carrier reading is impossible because διά marks agency.

Correct distinction:
- competing epistolary-role analyses; Richards and NET can be attributed specifically without converting them into consensus.

### 5:13 Babylon

Wrong families:
- Βαβυλών morphologically means Rome;
- feminine `συνεκλεκτή` necessarily denotes Peter's wife;
- listing three historical theories means equal probability.

Correct distinction:
- text, grammar, historical referent, and history-of-interpretation are separate layers.

## Answer-position requirement for later production authoring

Research candidates do not yet assign option indices. When four-option cards are finally authored:

- all four correct positions must be used;
- target near-even distribution;
- no long runs of one correct index;
- no simple rotating `0-1-2-3` cycle;
- reordering must preserve semantic keyed answers;
- tests must calculate the distribution independently.

This requirement comes directly from the Chapter-3 answer-key leakage findings and is now a general course-authoring rule.
