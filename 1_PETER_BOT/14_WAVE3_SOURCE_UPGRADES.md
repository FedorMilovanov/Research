## 2. Exact Proverbs comparisons

### 4:8 / Proverbs 10:12 LXX

LXX:

`μῖσος ἐγείρει νεῖκος, πάντας δὲ τοὺς μὴ φιλονεικοῦντας καλύπτει φιλία.`

1 Peter:

`ἀγάπη καλύπτει πλῆθος ἁμαρτιῶν.`

Safe classification:

`STRONG_PROVERBIAL_SCRIPTURAL_REUSE`

Unsafe:

`MECHANICAL_VERBATIM_LXX_QUOTATION`

The UBS Handbook independently notes the saying's difference from the LXX and possible detached-maxim transmission. That transmission explanation remains interpretation; the wording difference is direct observation.

### 4:18 / Proverbs 11:31 LXX

LXX:

`εἰ ὁ μὲν δίκαιος μόλις σῴζεται, ὁ ἀσεβὴς καὶ ἁμαρτωλὸς ποῦ φανεῖται;`

1 Peter reproduces essentially the same sequence with the opening `καί`.

Safe classification:

`VERY_CLOSE_LXX_QUOTATION_OR_REPRODUCTION`

This is deliberately stronger than 4:8.

### 5:5 / Proverbs 3:34 LXX

LXX:

`κύριος ὑπερηφάνοις ἀντιτάσσεται, ταπεινοῖς δὲ δίδωσιν χάριν.`

1 Peter:

`ὁ θεὸς ὑπερηφάνοις ἀντιτάσσεται, ταπεινοῖς δὲ δίδωσιν χάριν.`

Safe classification:

`VERY_CLOSE_SCRIPTURAL_REUSE_WITH_VISIBLE_SUBJECT_ADAPTATION`

No motive for `κύριος` -> `ὁ θεός` is claimed.

## 3. Forbes / Storms / UBS triangulation

This permits a strong Deep question:

## 6. Silvanus in 5:12

Three controls now keep the question calibrated.

### Richards

Official JETS issue identity plus the author's publication summary support attribution of Richards's thesis: the `γράφω διά + person` formula points to a letter carrier rather than Peter's secretary.

Full article was not audited end-to-end in this wave.

### NET note

Exact note inspected. NET allows secretary/amanuensis or carrier and regards carrier as more probable.

### Storms

Inspected 5:12 passage section explicitly leaves Silvanus's exact role unresolved.

Safe candidate:

> What does `διὰ Σιλουανοῦ ... ἔγραψα` not prove by itself?

Unsafe:

> Silvanus certainly composed Peter's Greek as secretary.

The separate `στῆτε / ἑστήκατε` unit remains apparatus HOLD.

## 7. Babylon in 5:13

The source map now deliberately separates:

- the written toponym `Βαβυλών`;
- the referent of feminine `συνεκλεκτή`;
- the historical identification of Babylon;
- Mark as Peter's “son”.

Storms prefers Rome and a congregation as the co-elect referent. Those are evangelical exegetical judgments, not morphology.

A DavarLogos article supplies a useful methodological counter-control: beware circularly proving Babylon=Rome by borrowing Revelation's interpretation and then using 1 Peter back in the opposite direction. This is a critique of method, not proof of literal Mesopotamian Babylon.

A 1954 Concordia thesis maps three historical location families — Rome, Mesopotamian Babylon, Egyptian Babylon. It is used only for history of interpretation, not as a modern probability ranking.
