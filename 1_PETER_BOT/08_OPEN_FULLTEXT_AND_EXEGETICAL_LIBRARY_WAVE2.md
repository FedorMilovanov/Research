# A. Large public-domain / openly downloadable historical works

## F. J. A. Hort — 1 Peter 1:1–2:17

**Title:** *The First Epistle of St Peter I.1–II.17: The Greek Text with Introductory Lecture, Commentary, and Additional Notes*  
**Year:** 1898  
**Extent:** about 188 pages in hosted bibliographic record  
**Access:** public-domain PDF route at BiblicalStudies.org.uk; readable digital edition also surfaced.

# C. Open scholarly articles / theses worth promotion

## Christoph Stenschke 2009 — mission and audience

**Title:** “Reading First Peter in the Context of Early Christian Mission.”  
**Venue:** Tyndale Bulletin 60.1, 107–126.  
**Access:** open full text.  
**Claims represented on article page:** predominantly Gentile audience; Israel-status language; exile/stranger identity; conduct and word as witness; suffering does not negate calling.

**Use:** audience/mission interpretation control.  
**Caution:** Israel/church systematic conclusions remain a separate layer.

## Gene L. Green 1990 — OT and Christian ethics

**Title:** “The Use of the Old Testament for Christian Ethics in 1 Peter.”  
**Venue:** Tyndale Bulletin 41.2, 276–289.  
**Access:** open full text.  
**Use:** OT/LXX ethical function across 1 Peter, including Chapter 3.

## Katie Marcar 2016 — Isaiah quotations

**Title:** “The Quotations of Isaiah in 1 Peter: A Text-Critical Analysis.”  
**Venue:** TC: A Journal of Biblical Textual Criticism 21, 1–22.  
**Access:** official journal issue/index openly exposes the article identity and abstract/conclusion summary; direct body route should be separately pinned before line-level claims.  
**Use:** six Isaiah quotation units, textual form/Vorlage analysis.

## Douw G. Breed 2025 — Exodus 19–24 in 1 Peter 1:2

**Venue:** In die Skriflig 59.1.  
**Access:** full HTML/PDF, CC BY 4.0.  
**Use:** serious proposed intertext at 1:2.  
**Status:** proposal, not established quotation.

## John H. Elliott 2008 — elders

**Title:** “Elders as leaders in 1 Peter and the early Church.”  
**Venue:** HTS 64.2, 681–695.  
**Access:** full PDF; journal page marks CC BY 4.0.  
**Use:** 5:1–5 leadership vocabulary/background; independent social/exegetical control.

## Gert Breed 2016 — diakonia of elder

**Venue:** In die Skriflig 50.3.  
**Access:** open full text, CC license.  
**Use:** 4:7–11 / 5:1–4 connection and elder ministry.

## Peter J. Gurry 2017 — CBGM Catholic Epistles

**Venue:** Tyndale Bulletin dissertation summary.  
**Access:** open.  
**Use:** methodological critical control for understanding what CBGM can/cannot establish.  
**Not:** a reason to discard ECM.

## Chu Luan Poh 1998 — social world of 1 Peter

**Type:** KCL doctoral thesis.  
**Access:** repository exposes a 21.8 MB PDF object.  
**Use:** large socio-historical research source; body requires targeted inspection before promotion.

## William J. Bynum 2026 — ongoing exile

**Type:** Liberty University PhD dissertation.

**Use:** current evangelical exposition-level research on exile motif; new source, not consensus.

## Ian James Hales 2021 — opposition and people-of-God identity

# E. Language acquisition / Greek bridge sources

## Abbott-Smith 1922

**Title:** *A Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament*.  
**Extent:** roughly 500+ pages in public-domain scans.  
**Access:** Open Library/Wikimedia/TEI project.  
**Role:** lawful entry-level lexical control and citation bridge.  
**Warning:** old lexicography; not equal to BDAG.

## A. T. Robertson — large grammar

**Title:** *A Grammar of the Greek New Testament in the Light of Historical Research*.  
**Extent:** public-domain editions over 1400 pages.  
**Role:** historic large reference grammar, useful for syntax-history and explanatory examples.  
**Warning:** not a modern grammar consensus by itself.

## Robertson — Short Grammar

~240-page public-domain introductory grammar. Useful learner bridge.

## Moulton & Milligan

**Title:** *The Vocabulary of the Greek Testament Illustrated from the Papyri and Other Non-Literary Sources*.  
**Public-domain early-edition route:** roughly 700 pages.  
**Role:** documentary-papyri lexical background.  
**Warning:** semantic parallel != meaning in 1 Peter automatically.
