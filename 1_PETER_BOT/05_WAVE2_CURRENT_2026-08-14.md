# 2. Manuscript layer

Priority manuscript teaching should begin with actual witnesses to 1 Peter rather than abstract sigla memorization.

High-value examples include:

- **P72 / Papyrus Bodmer VIII** — early witness containing 1 and 2 Peter; digitized Vatican material is available.
- **P81** — fragmentary witness including portions in the 1 Peter 2–3 range.
- **P125** — fragmentary witness with portions of 1 Peter 1–2.
- major codices should be used where an exact 1 Peter variant requires them, but only after the manuscript/object route is pinned.

The bot should never teach:

> “oldest manuscript = automatically correct reading.”

Instead:

```text
MANUSCRIPT_AGE = ONE_EVIDENCE_DIMENSION
ONE_WITNESS != TEXTUAL_DECISION
APPARATUS_LIST != EXPLANATION_OF_EDITORIAL_DECISION
SBLGNT_EDITION_COMPARISON_APPARATUS != ECM_MANUSCRIPT_APPARATUS
```

# 3. Variant curriculum principle

Textual criticism belongs in the bot only where it teaches something real.

Four levels:

### SIMPLE
“Some manuscripts/editions differ here. The main meaning of the passage is/is not substantially affected.”

### STANDARD
“Which wording is printed by the selected base text, and what is the main alternative?”

### DEEP
“Which witnesses/edition decisions support the alternatives, and what internal/external considerations matter?”

### RESEARCH
“Compare ECM/GNT6/Houghton/NTVMR evidence, record uncertainty, and explain why an editor may prefer one reading without pretending certainty where the evidence remains divided.”

Do not make manuscript trivia competitive by default.

# 5. Authorship/date/persecution must be taught as argument maps

The project may teach Petrine authorship as its preferred/confessional-historical position, but the scholarship map must remain honest.

Current serious discussion includes:

- Petrine authorship / early 60s and Rome/Babylon readings;
- pseudonymous composition / later first-century proposals;
- secretary/amanuensis proposals associated with Silvanus;
- debate over whether the readers faced local social hostility, more formal legal pressure, or mixed forms.

Duane Watson's 2024 Oxford Handbook chapter explicitly surveys authorship, date, place, ethnic/social background, occasion, Scripture use and theology as contested issues. It is a catalog/abstract control unless lawful full text is inspected.

Travis Williams' 2012 article documents the modern consensus around localized/sporadic hostility and challenges the history of how that consensus formed. Publisher abstract inspection is not enough to rank his full alternative model.

Therefore:

```text
UNOFFICIAL_PERSECUTION = NOT_DIRECT_BIBLICAL_TEXT
EMPIRE_WIDE_OFFICIAL_PERSECUTION = NOT_PROVED_BY_1_PETER
PETRINE_AUTHORSHIP = PROJECT_POSITION_WITH_ARGUMENTS
PSEUDONYMITY = SERIOUS_SCHOLARLY_ALTERNATIVE
SILVANUS_EXPLAINS_ALL_STYLE_ISSUES = NOT_PROVED
BABYLON_EQ_ROME = LEADING_INTERPRETATION / NOT_MORPHOLOGY
```

# 7. Project-theology architecture

TMS remains the project-position anchor because its current doctrinal statement explicitly distinguishes the church from Israel, teaches a future restoration of Israel, a future earthly millennial kingdom, and uses 1 Pet 5:1–4 in its local-church/elder doctrine.

This is a theological control, not a lexical authority.

The TMS Journal archive also provides a documented hermeneutical family: grammatical-historical interpretation, concern for original authorial meaning, criticism of readings that allow later revelation to erase original OT meaning, and dispensational treatment of the Bible's storyline.

For 1 Peter, the most sensitive application is 2:9–10:

```text
PETER_APPLIES_ISRAEL_SCRIPTURE_TO_CHRISTIAN_READERS = TEXT_INTERTEXT_FACT
THEREFORE_CHURCH_REPLACES_ISRAEL = NOT_TEXTUAL_FACT
THEREFORE_NO_CONTINUITY_BETWEEN_ISRAEL_AND_CHURCH = ALSO_NOT_TEXTUAL_FACT
PROJECT_SYSTEMATIC_SYNTHESIS = TMS_DISTINCT_CHURCH_AND_ISRAEL + REAL_SHARED_SALVATION_PRIVILEGES_IN_CHRIST
```

The bot should show the text first, then the project synthesis, then serious alternatives where relevant.

# 8. Learning-science upgrade

Wave 1 proposed spaced retrieval and confidence calibration. Wave 2 adds analytics requirements.

Record for each question after enough attempts:

```text
attempts
correct_rate
first_attempt_correct_rate
median_response_time
option_selection_counts
correct_position_counts
discrimination_proxy
confidence_vs_accuracy
repeat_after_error_success
repeat_after_guess_success
```

Editorially:

- answer positions must be balanced;
- distractor selection must be monitored;
- a distractor almost never selected is a candidate for rewrite;
- right-but-guessed should not equal mastery;
- high-confidence wrong answers are misconception signals;
- short-term rereading success must not be mistaken for long-term retrieval strength;
- adaptive delivery must preserve content coverage and not drill only easy/high-success topics.

Research on spacing shows that the best gap depends on the desired retention interval; therefore the bot should not advertise a universal “scientific” fixed schedule.
