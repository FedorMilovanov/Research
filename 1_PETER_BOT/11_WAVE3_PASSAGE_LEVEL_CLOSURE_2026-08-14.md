# 12. 1 Peter 4:6 — new disputed-passage queue

Wave 3 adds 4:6 to the formal dispute architecture.

Direct Greek observations:

- `νεκροῖς` — “to the dead” at the text layer;
- `εὐηγγελίσθη` — aorist passive indicative, gospel/good news was proclaimed;
- `κριθῶσι` / `ζῶσι` form the judgment/life contrast;
- `σαρκί / πνεύματι` requires contextual interpretation and must not be collapsed into the 3:18 dispute automatically.

Major interpretation families to map before production:

1. people who heard the gospel while alive but are now dead;
2. proclamation to the dead after death / Hades-related readings;
3. historically less persuasive “spiritually dead” readings;
4. narrower contextual proposals tied to persecuted believers or the judgment of 4:5.

A recent 2025 CC BY article argues for those who heard/responded while alive and had died by the time of writing. It is a useful **recent published position**, not consensus and not sufficient alone to rank the interpretations.
