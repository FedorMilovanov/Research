
Katie Marcar explicitly includes 2:22 and 2:25 among six Isaianic quotations studied text-critically.


# Source hierarchy for intertext cards

1. 1 Peter Greek base text.
2. Exact LXX passage.
3. MT/Hebrew where the claim depends on differences.
4. Other ancient textual witnesses where relevant.
5. Peer-reviewed intertext/text-critical work.
6. Commentary synthesis.
7. Project theological application.

Never reverse this order by starting with a systematic conclusion and searching backward for a lexical similarity.
