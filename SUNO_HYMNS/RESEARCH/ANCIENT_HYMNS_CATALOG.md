# Catalog of the ancient-hymn lane

The Dutch lane has its own catalog (`DUTCH_HYMN_CATALOG.md`) and its own backlog. This file is the catalog of works that are not Dutch and not modern: the pre-Reformation Greek and Latin hymns, taken as whole texts with a named witness.

The evidence rule is the same as everywhere in this project: a work enters this catalog with a source that was opened and read, and every state below names itself rather than implying more.

## PHOS-001 — Φῶς ἱλαρόν (*Phos Hilaron*, «Свет Отрадный»)

- **Russian release title (approved by the owner 2026-10-26):** **«Свет Отрадный»** — capital О as the owner wrote it; the verse line keeps the lowercase «отрадный». The title «Свете тихий» is **not** available: it is the name of the Church Slavonic liturgical text, and using it would present a new Russian translation as that text.
- **Author:** unknown. Basil of Caesarea, *De Spiritu Sancto* 29.73 (c. 375), states explicitly that he cannot name the author. The attributions to Athenogenes of Sebasteia († c. 305/311) and to Sophronius of Jerusalem (560–638) are later and are not supported: the first arose from conflating two separate testimonies in Basil, the second is contradicted by a papyrus older than Sophronius (see `SRC-PHOS-PRAVENC`).
- **Text category:** six-line Greek hymn sung at the lighting of the lamps (*ἐπιλύχνιος εὐχαριστία*); three parts — Christological address, Trinitarian doxology, Christological close.
- **Dating:** date of composition unknown; the hymn is already called ancient by Basil c. 375. The popular "200 AD or earlier" is **not verified** in this project and is not used in copy. The oldest physical witness held in the record is papyrus P. Lond. 2037B (6th–7th c.), known here only through the encyclopedia article — `ARCHIVE_HOLD`.
- **Dating claim that is held, not made:** "first recorded in the *Apostolic Constitutions* VIII.35" — repeated across the web, absent from the encyclopedia article read here, and not confirmable from a primary text in this pass. `EVIDENCE_HOLD`; see `CLM-PHOS-AC-HOLD`.
- **Structural note:** the three adjectives `οὐρανίου, ἁγίου, μάκαρος` are genitive singular and formally agree with `Πατρός`, while most modern translations attach them to Christ. The Greek — and the Church Slavonic — leave this suspended; the Russian translation keeps it suspended too.
- **Russian translation:** `PHOS-001-RU v0.4` in `../SUNO/PHOS-001_LYRICS.md` — a verse translation of the six lines, not an adaptation, word for word where Russian allows (one added auxiliary «быть», articles dropped, one pair of words reordered). **No rhyme and no metre:** the source has neither — six different line-endings, syllables 15·15·18·16·16·18 — so none is added. Russian **prose** translations exist (see `SRC-PHOS-AZBYKA`, `SRC-PHOS-SDAMP`); a singable Russian verse translation from the Greek was **not found** in the sources opened in this pass.
- **Production:** `LYRIC_DRAFT` — text, stage text and five style directions exist in this repository; **nothing has been generated**.
- **Publication status:** `UNKNOWN_NOT_VERIFIED`; there is nothing to publish yet.
