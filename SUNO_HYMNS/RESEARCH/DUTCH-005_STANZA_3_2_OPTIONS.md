# DUTCH-005, stanza 3, line 2 — the defect and three ways to close it

**Status:** decision needed from the owner. **Nothing is changed in the lyric yet.** Revision affected: `DUTCH-005-RU v0.9` → v0.10.

## 1. What is wrong

Russian, stanza 3, lines 1–4:

```
Жизнь без конца, во славе,
После земных невзгод,
Лишь она, душа, желанна
И влечёт вперёд.
```

Line 2 reads «После земных невзгод» — "after earthly hardships". The Dutch stanza it stands in reads:

```
' t Leven sonder eynden Heerlijck /
Na dit leven crijgelijck /
Is, mijn siel, alleen begeerlijck /
En beweegt onsigtbaarlijck:
```

Locator: *Uyt-spanningen* (2005), item 56, stanza III, lines 18–21, [p. 263]; the same lines in the 1676 print, pp. 155–158.

**The modern editors' gloss on that exact line**, [p. 263], note 19–20:

> Het heerlijke, eeuwige leven, dat **na dit leven** te verkrijgen is

— the glorious eternal life **to be obtained after this life**. And `crijgelijck` itself means "obtainable". The line is about *when* the life is obtained — after death — not about *what precedes it* in this world.

Why the difference matters, in one sentence: «после земных невзгод» makes the stanza say that heaven follows hardship, which the print does not say; the print says that the glorious life is the one obtained after this life. That is a claim about the next life, not a consolation about this one, and the whole stanza runs on it — the next two lines ("close your fleshly eye") only make sense as "look past death".

## 2. What the fix is allowed to touch

Rhyme standard **B** (cadence rhyme): lines 2 and 4 rhyme, the closing line rhymes with the call line, lines 1 and 3 are free. So line 2 cannot move alone — it is the rhyme partner of line 4, «И влечёт вперёд». Three ways to keep the pair intact:

| | Line 2 | Syllables | Rhyme with «вперёд» | What it says |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Та, что за смертью ждёт, | 6 | ждёт / вперёд | "the life that waits beyond death" |
| **B** | Та, что после смерти ждёт, | 7 | ждёт / вперёд | same, with the Dutch's own «после» in the same place |
| **C** | Та, что за смертью придёт, | 7 | придёт / вперёд | "the life that will come after death" |

Current line 2 has 6 syllables; the Dutch line has 7. **B** and **C** match the Dutch count, **A** keeps the present count.

The rhyme is of the ordinary masculine type (`-дёт / -рёд`): the stressed vowel and the final consonant agree, the preceding cluster differs — the same quality as the standard pair «идёт — вперёд». It is not an identical rhyme and not an assonance.

## 3. The three variants in place

**A** — fewest changes, shortest line:

```
Жизнь без конца, во славе,
Та, что за смертью ждёт,
Лишь она, душа, желанна
И влечёт вперёд.
```

**B** — closest to the Dutch word order (`Na dit leven` → «после смерти» stands where the print puts it):

```
Жизнь без конца, во славе,
Та, что после смерти ждёт,
Лишь она, душа, желанна
И влечёт вперёд.
```

**C** — future tense, closest to `crijgelijck` as "to be obtained":

```
Жизнь без конца, во славе,
Та, что за смертью придёт,
Лишь она, душа, желанна
И влечёт вперёд.
```

For the SUNO-ready text a stress mark is unnecessary: «ждёт» and «придёт» carry `ё`, so the stress is unambiguous.

## 4. Recommendation

**C.** It keeps the Dutch count (7), keeps the future orientation of `crijgelijck` ("to be obtained"), and `придёт` is the least ambiguous of the three for a singer — no risk of singing «ждёт» as a flat monosyllable where the Dutch line has four stresses.

**B** is the safest if the owner prefers the plainest diction; **A** if the melody, once chosen, wants a shorter line.

## 5. What is *not* on the table

- Keeping «невзгод» with a footnote. The gloss is explicit, and a note under a hymn line is not a hymn line.
- Adding a word to explain the change in a neighbouring line: line 3 is already a rhyme-free line and would start carrying a gloss.
- Touching stanza 3's call line («Закрой плотское око») or the closing line («Эта жизнь — высоко»). The defect is in one line; the fix is in one line.
