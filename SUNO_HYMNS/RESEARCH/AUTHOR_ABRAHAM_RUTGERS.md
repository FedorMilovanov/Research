# Author / reviser dossier — Abraham Rutgers

## Identity and role

Abraham Rutgers (1751–1809) was a Dutch Reformed pastor and poet/hymn writer. The historical biographical-dictionary entry describes several roles in *Evangelische Gezangen*: composing new hymns, translating and revising older texts. It explicitly says hymns 68 and 62 derive from Lodenstein's *Uyt-spanningen* and were revised by Rutgers.

Source: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/aa__001nieu03_01/aa__001nieu03_01_0176.php

## DUTCH-002 — *Zelfverloochening*, no. 68 (1806)

- The 1806 hymn title is *Zelfverloochening* (“self-denial”).
- The seven-stanza hymn begins *Zalig! zalig, niets te wezen, / In ons eigen oog, voor God…*
- Source relationship: Lodenstein → Rutgers revision → *Evangelische Gezangen* (1806).
- The original 1674 *Hert-sterckte in Jehova* and 1806 seven-stanza hymn are not identical texts.
- The Russian version should be credited as a modern literary/song adaptation on this source chain, not a direct 1674 transcription.

Primary songbook locator: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_eva005evan01_01/_eva005evan01_01_0070.php

## South African / Afrikaans transmission

Modern Missio pages document an Afrikaans Lied 276 chain with Jodocus van Lodenstein, Abraham Rutgers (1806), G.B.A. Gerdener (1931), E.C. Pienaar / AGB (1944), Gerjo van der Merwe (1978) and Andries Breytenbach (2001). Lied 271 is another Lodenstein-derived item. Treat the modern page as corroborating evidence and verify individual editions before publishing detailed claims about exact wording, roles or dates.
