# Author dossier — Jacobus Revius

## Identity

- **Name:** Jacobus Revius; name variant Jacob Reefsen.
- **Life:** 1586–1658; born in Deventer, died in Leiden.
- **Roles:** Dutch Reformed pastor, poet, historian and participant in the Dutch Bible translation/revision culture of the seventeenth century.
- **First publication relevant here:** *Over-Ysselsche sangen en dichten* (Deventer, 1630).

DBNL author record: https://www.dbnl.org/auteurs/auteur.php?id=revi001

## DUTCH-003 — *Hy droech onse smerten*

- **Form:** fourteen-line Passion sonnet.
- **First-publication anchor:** *Over-Ysselsche sangen en dichten*, Deventer, 1630.
- **Title meaning:** “He bore our sorrows”; the title echoes Isaiah 53:4.
- **Composition date:** not established in the current record. Do not infer it from 1630.
- **Original melody:** not verified. Do not attribute a modern Suno arrangement or melody to Revius.
- **Textual movement:** the opening eight lines describe people involved in the Passion. From line 9 the speaker confesses “I am the one”; the ending states that it happened because of “my sins”. The shift from *onse* (“our”) to *mijne* (“my”) is a key interpretive point in the specialist study.

## Editorial caution

The accessible DBNL poem page belongs to W.A.P. Smit's 1930–1935 scholarly edition of the 1630 collection. It is a precise locator for the sonnet, not a direct scan of the 1630 leaf. If a claim depends on original typography, punctuation, physical features or edition variants, inspect an original scan.

## Candidates for further work

The DBNL author record provides a discovery path for *Het hoghe liedt Salomons* (1621), Revius's revised metrical Psalter (1640) and other religious poems. Check each work's original classification before marking it a hymn. Metrical psalms, sonnets and devotional songs are different types.
