# Source register — Dutch hymn project

This registry is the human-readable view of data/source-registry-v1.json. The repository root evidence policy remains authoritative. The underlying historical source and modern digital object used to access it may have different evidence and rights states.

- A1/A2/A3/B1/C/D retain the exact global meanings from AGENT_RULES.md.
- Access, locator, rights and publication state are separate fields.
- RIGHTS_UNKNOWN means the rights question remains open, not that permission is granted.
- A link to a source is not automatically quote-safe approval or a license to republish its full text.
- The 1676 digital text is described as a diplomatic rendering with omissions and editorial corrections. The 2005 critical edition is explicitly copyright-protected.
- The 1615 Utrecht hymnbook is encountered through a 1967 facsimile. The 1930–1935 Revius text is a scholarly edition, not the verified 1630 leaf.

Only **Jugie and Simon Mimouni** present significant exceptions, the former because of his immortalist theological leanings, and latter out of a belief that the Dormition traditions issue from the concerns of the Fourth Council, **an assumption that unfortunately is not in evidence**».; **Место:** традиции «first took shape in Palestine, where they developed in close association with **the emergent veneration of Mary in the Hagiopolite liturgies** and mounting pilgrimage traffic to holy sites associated with her life and death» — то есть в том же иерусалимском богослужебном контексте, что и наш Иадгари.; **Наш текст назван в числе ранних следов:** «the famous *Sub Tuum Praesidium* papyrus» — но этих следов, по автору, «seem insufficient to explain the tidal wave that was to follow». Он держится ранней датировки папируса и остаётся в меньшинстве.; **Древнейшее сказание — «Liber Requiei Mariae»:** сирийские фрагменты конца V в. переведены с греческого, внутренние признаки ведут в III в. или раньше (Христос как «Great Angel»; гностические термины; невоздержанные подробности про Иосифа; Мария признаётся, что однажды согрешила, — мысль, известная Иринею и Тертуллиану). **И при этом:** «the apostles do not afford Mary any special reverence when they first greet her, nor are there any indications of a formal cult of the Virgin. Mary does not work any miracles and expresses doubts and a fear of dying».
