## Rights and reuse decision

No source in this registry is automatically PROMOTE for copying full text, editorial notes, images or modern translations into a public product. Underlying historic works may be public domain, but rights in hosted transcriptions, scans, critical apparatus, modern translations and artwork must be checked separately. Keep rights/publication HOLDs when uncertain.
