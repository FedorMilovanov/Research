## Selection gate before a candidate becomes an active SUNO work

- Original text and edition identified.
- Historical genre classified correctly.
- Composition date and first-publication date not conflated.
- Author, adapter and translator roles separated.
- Direct text locator and independent contextual source captured.
- Any melody claim supported by a primary source.
- Russian adaptation is not drafted before the historical text is checked.
- Candidate is distinct enough from existing releases.
- Rights checked separately for source text, modern edition, translation, image and any existing recording.
