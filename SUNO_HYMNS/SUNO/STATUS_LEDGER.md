## DUTCH-002 — «Блаженно, блаженно — быть ничем»

- **Historical source author:** Jodocus van Lodenstein.
- **Source chain:** Lodenstein's *II. Hert-sterckte in Jehova. Op 1 Sam. 30:6*, printed in the 1676 first edition on pp. 82–84, is dated 4 January 1674 at Fort Nieuw Rees; Abraham Rutgers later reworked Lodenstein material for *Evangelische Gezangen* (1806), hymn no. 68, *Zelfverloochening*. The Russian lyric follows the later seven-stanza hymn tradition, not a word-for-word translation of the full poem.
- **Do not confuse with I.** A different poem, *I. Hert-sterckte in Jehova*, stands on pp. 78–81 (28 December 1673) and has its own tune names. II has no tune of its own and is sung to I's. See `../RESEARCH/DOSSIER_HERT_STERCKTE_I_II.md`.
- **Date caveat:** the 1676 print is reported to misprint 1664; the 1727 edition and both modern editions read 1674; the 1676 leaf has not been inspected. `EVIDENCE_HOLD` is narrowed to that literal reading only.
- **Related later transmission:** Afrikaans Lied 276 and another Lodenstein-derived text listed as Lied 271; exact spellings and intermediate editions remain to be checked.
- **Production state:** ASSETS_PREPARED for versions 1 and 2 (reported).
- **Version 1:** WAV 433.240 sec; YouTube MP4 1920×1080/30 fps; Telegram MP3 320 kbps/48 kHz with cover.
- **Version 2:** WAV 438.569521 sec; YouTube MP4 1920×1080/30 fps; Telegram MP3 320 kbps/48 kHz with cover.
- **Previously reported tags:** versioned title; artist Йодокус ван Лоденштейн; album Господь Бог — Сила Моя; genre Христианский гимн; generation-source comments removed from output. Read back actual files before relying on this state in a new release.
- **Stanza counts (collated, fifth pass):** 1676 = **14**; 1806 *Evangelische Gezangen* LXVIII = **seven** (an earlier statement of five was wrong); 1938 Hervormde Bundel **Gezang 205** = **five**, now read in the printed bundel (image pp. 724–725): it omits 1806 stanza 4 and stanza 5 lines 1–4 (keeping that stanza's closing couplet), and changes stanza 2 in two places (`zielverdervend`→`trots, hoogmoedig`; `Werp die trotsheid dan`→`werp dien hoogmoed dan`). Only 1938 stanza 1 rests on web transcriptions. The bundel is a protected object; its melody credit is Johann Christoph Bach, which is **not** the 1674 tune.
- **Production decision recorded as per-work only:** male monastic-style polyphony, powerful organ, epic; syllabic delivery; **112–120 BPM**; short intro and transitions; no long interludes; no stretched vowels — adopted because earlier generations came out too slow and whispered. This is **not** a project default and it has **not** been demonstrated to describe either existing version; duration alone proves nothing.
- **Suno style prompts:** NOT CAPTURED verbatim in this repository, so compliance with the tempo decision cannot be shown.

## DUTCH-003 — «Он понёс наши скорби»
