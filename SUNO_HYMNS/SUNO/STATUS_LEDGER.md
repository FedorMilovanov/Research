## PHOS-001 — «Свет Отрадный» (Φῶς ἱλαρόν) — the ancient-hymn lane

- **What it is:** the six-line Greek hymn sung at the lighting of the lamps, the earliest complete Christian hymn outside Scripture that is still in use. Checked on 2026-10-26: that phrase is the only defensible form of "oldest" — see the dossier § 3.4, where the hymn is shown **not** to be the oldest by manuscript or by date of composition. The owner asked for the oldest hymn after the Gospels, a Russian translation and a SUNO package (2026-10-26).
- **Historical author:** unknown. Basil of Caesarea, *De Spiritu Sancto* 29.73 (c. 375), says he cannot name the author and calls the hymn an ancient form. The ascriptions to Athenogenes and to Sophronius are recorded as attributions only.
- **Source anchor:** the received six-line Greek text, read in an open choral library; **a printed Horologion has not been collated** (`LOCATOR_HOLD`).
- **Checked and dropped (2026-10-26):** "first recorded in the *Apostolic Constitutions* VIII.35" is false — VIII.35–37 contain the evening rite and the bishop's thanksgiving, not this hymn (`CLM-PHOS-AC-CHECKED`). Also false: "oldest text in the Codex Alexandrinus" (`CLM-PHOS-NOT-ALEXANDRINUS`). Still not to be used in copy: any second- or third-century date of composition.
- **Russian text:** `PHOS-001-RU v0.5` in `PHOS-001_LYRICS.md` — lines 1–2 carry the address first («Иисус Христос, свет отрадный святой славы / бессмертного Отца, небесного, святого, блаженного»), because the Greek order with a nominative address is ungrammatical in Russian — a verse **translation**, six lines into six lines, no refrain, no repeated or added line; no rhyme because the Greek has none — its six line-endings do not rhyme and its syllable counts are 15·15·18·16·16·18. The text is word-for-word where Russian allows: one added auxiliary «быть», the articles dropped, one pair of words reordered in line 3. Russian prose translations exist and were used as a sense check only; the Church Slavonic «Свете тихий» is not Russian and is not the source of our wording.
- **Arrangement:** `PHOS-001-ARR-R1` — the owner authorised repeats from the hymn's own words on 2026-10-26. Line 4 is the refrain, repeated verbatim after verses 2 and 3; line 6 is reprised at the end (line 1 stopped working as a reprise when the address moved to its head). Nine sung lines instead of six, **no new words**; the six-line translation is unchanged. Alternative `R2`: the whole hymn twice, no refrain.
- **Handoff:** `PHOS-001_SUNO_HANDOFF.md` — five ready pairs of blocks (Style + Lyrics) to paste into SUNO, with no prose between them.
- **Style:** five directions in `PHOS-001_STYLE.md`, `STYLE-PHOS-001-A`…`E`; A sits in the primary slot only because the registry must carry one prompt. None submitted.
- **Production state:** `LYRIC_DRAFT`. **No generation, no audio, no cover, no export, no metadata readback.**
- **Publication status:** `UNKNOWN_NOT_VERIFIED` — there is nothing to publish yet, and channel copy is deliberately not written before a take exists.
- **Closed by the owner 2026-10-26:** the title **«Свет Отрадный»**; the suspended reference of the three genitive adjectives — «как в греческом»; and, on a later instruction («без православной стилистики, сделай ровнее»), the vocatives gave way to the nominative — «Иисус Христос», «Сын Божий». The Greek vocative case is therefore not transmitted, and with it the suspended reference of the three genitive adjectives: both are named losses, chosen for a level register. «Иисуса Христа» was rejected — as a genitive it would join the chain and make Christ a second name for the Father. The reading of ἱλαρόν as «отрадный» is the owner's choice; the dictionary-literal «радостный» is named in the translation dossier, not hidden.
- **Open:** the choice between arrangement R1 and R2 if R1 does not suit, the choice of style A–E and the first generation; collation of the Greek text; the *Apostolic Constitutions* check; P. Lond. 2037B seen in a catalogue.
- **Package:** `PHOS-001_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE.md`; research: `../RESEARCH/DOSSIER_PHOS-001_PHOS_HILARON.md`; translation decisions: `../RESEARCH/DOSSIER_PHOS-001_TRANSLATION_DECISIONS.md`.
