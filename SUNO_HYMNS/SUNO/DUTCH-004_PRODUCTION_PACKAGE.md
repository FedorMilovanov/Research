## 1. Form decision

| Option | Decision |
|---|---|
| Reproduce the 1676 Dutch text as a sung Dutch lyric | **rejected** — the target channel is Russian-language, and the historical tune is not available |
| An "exact translation" | **rejected** — an exact verse translation is not achievable without inventing a metre, and calling it a translation would misrepresent the method |
| Historical Dutch text + literal gloss + **independent Russian literary adaptation** | **CHOSEN** |
| Free original composition "after the theme" | rejected — the source text is strong enough to follow closely stanza by stanza |

**Declared form:** `ORIGINAL_RUSSIAN_LITERARY_ADAPTATION_AFTER_A_PUBLIC_DOMAIN_DUTCH_SOURCE`.

It follows the source **stanza by stanza in order**, keeps the source's argument and imagery, keeps the internal doubling device of lines 5–6, and does **not** claim to reproduce Dutch metre, rhyme scheme or the tune *Prendete la*. It is not a translation, and no line of it should be presented as a literal rendering.

**Rights basis for adapting at all:** the 1676 poem is a public-domain historical work (author d. 1677). The modern critical edition (2005) and the DBNL transcription were used only to check sense and to locate readings; no wording, note or editorial expansion from those editions is reproduced. See §7.

## 3. Russian draft — `DUTCH-004-RU-DRAFT v0.1`

**Status:** working draft. Rhythm deliberately regularised; rhyme only where it came without forcing, so a **poetic revision by the owner is a required gate** before `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. Fidelity is to be judged against §2, not against the Dutch word order.

> **Отец, Ты видишь боль мою** — 1
> Отец, Ты видишь боль мою,
> Ты видишь сердце,
> Ты правишь сердцем
> И знаешь все повадки в нём.
> Оно, мне кажется, легло,
> Легло смиренно ниц;
> И если я собьюсь — проверь,
> Не выше ли оно.
>
> **Глянь, Отче** — 2
> Глянь, Отче: не глядят ли ввысь
> Мои глаза,
> Не в высях ли?
> Ты видишь: я иду согбен.
> Мой разум — точка, малый прах,
> Мой суд — одно мгновенье:
> Он не доходит до глубин,
> Он их не видит вглубь.
>
> **Когда я вижу** — 3
> Когда я вижу, как к Тебе
> Я приклоняюсь,
> И, оробев,
> Тебе служение несу, —
> Да кто я? Червь или моль,
> Чтоб Твой совет,
> Тайну прощенья и любви,
> До дна исследовать?
>
> **Как дитя** — 4
> Не положил ли я себя
> В покорность, в тихость,
> В смиренность, в кротость —
> Так низко, низко пред Тобой!
> Как малое дитя, дитя,
> Что всем довольно,
> Коль только любит мать свою —
> И боле ничего.
>
> **Ребёнок** — 5
> Ребёнок, что лишь сосать
> И гнуться может,
> Что плачем скажет
> О недовольстве своём:
> Отторгнут от груди — он может
> Заплакать о любви,
> Но спорить с матерью своей
> Не смел он никогда.
>
> **Итак, мой Господь** — 6
> Итак, мой Господь,
> Придёт ли мне Твоя благодать,
> Придёт ли поздно,
> Или уйдёт:
> О, горестный уход!
> О, горестный уход!
> Душа моя всё ж замолчит,
> Затем что так Отец велит.
>
> **О зрящие, цари, жрецы** — 7
> О зрящие, цари, жрецы,
> Кто дом свой и венец
> Приял из рук
> Господа земель!
> Ждите ещё Его даров,
> Ждите ещё:
> Коль благость без дна,
> То и конца ей нет.

**Stanza numbering note:** the draft uses a short descriptive label per stanza for reading. In the SUNO lyric file these become `[Verse 1]` … `[Verse 7]` (see §6).

**Point of decision recorded:** stanza 4 follows the **1676 exclamation** (`Ootmoedig neergeleydt!`), not the 1727 question mark. If the owner prefers the questioning reading, stanza 4 line 1 becomes "Не положил ли я себя?" and the fourth line loses its exclamation — that is a legitimate alternative and must then be recorded as `DUTCH-004-RU-DRAFT v0.2`.

## 4. Significant deviations from the source — declared

| # | Place | Dutch source | Russian draft | Why | Fidelity risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whole text | 7 stanzas × 8 printed lines, aaa-rhyme in the first three lines, seven sung lines with a split second line | 7 stanzas × 8 lines, regular iambic, no aaa-rhyme | Russian cannot carry Dutch *slagrijm* without distortion; the syllable scheme cannot be reproduced anyway because the tune is not acquired | low for sense, **high for form** — this is the clearest place where the draft is an adaptation, not a rendering |
| 2 | I.4 | *al sijn grepen* — literally "all its grips/seizures" | "все повадки" ("all its ways/habits") | *grepen* is a harsh, semi-technical word about the heart's evasions; a literal Russian equivalent ("ухватки") sounds comic in a hymn | low |
| 3 | II.5–6 | *Mijn oordeel is een stip* — "my judgement is a dot" | "Мой суд — одно мгновенье" | "точка" twice in two lines read as a typo; the image (a point with no extension) is kept in line 5 ("точка, малый прах") | **medium** — the "dot" metaphor is weakened in line 6 |
| 4 | III.4 | *bie ick u mijn dienst* — archaic "I offer you my service" | "Тебе служение несу" | register, not sense | none |
| 5 | IV.7–8 | *Als 't maar de Moeder mint* — "if it but loves the Mother" (the Mother as object of the child's love) | "Коль только любит мать свою — / И боле ничего" | Russian needs a predicate, and the line needs an ending; "и боле ничего" is added | **medium** — an interpretive addition. The Dutch says only "loves the Mother"; the exclusivity is implied by *maar*, but it is stated here, not translated |
| 6 | V.5–6 | print abbreviates the repeat as `En treckt men etc.` | the repeat is **not** written out; the draft gives the line once ("Отторгнут от груди — он может") and lets the music repeat it | the print's `etc.` is a repeat instruction, not new wording | none if the repeat is performed; the section is marked for repetition in §6 |
| 7 | VII.1 | *Sienders* — "seers" (prophets) | "зрящие" | literal but archaic; kept deliberately | low — may read as obscure; owner may prefer "пророки" |
| 7b | VII.2–4 | *Die woningen, / En kroningen / Hebt van Israels Heer* | "Кто дом свой и венец / Приял из рук / Господа земель" | *woningen* (dwellings, most likely the sanctuary) and *kroningen* (crownings) are compressed into "дом свой и венец"; "из рук" is added to carry the genitive | **medium** — a compression of two nouns into a hendiadys-like phrase |
| 8 | VII.5–6 | `Wagt verder op sijn gunst, / Wagt etc.` | "Ждите ещё Его даров, / Ждите ещё:" | the doubling is reproduced | none |
| 9 | VII.7–8 | *Want is sijn goedheyd grondeloos, / So is sy eyndeloos* | "Коль благость без дна, / То и конца ей нет" | keeps the groundless→endless wordplay | none |
| 10 | Title | *Den nedrigen christen, Op Psalm 131* | working title «Смиренный христианин» (Psalm 131) | — | none; the display title is not final until the owner approves it |

**Not a deviation, but must not be confused with one:** the draft is *not* a translation of Rutgers's 1806 *Zelfverloochening*. That hymn belongs to DUTCH-002. This is a different poem, a different century of use, and a different Russian text.

## 5. Technical brief for the musical setting

| Element | Specification | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Form** | Through-composed strophic: 7 verses to one harmonic plan, with a 4-bar instrumental intro, a 4-bar link before verse 4, and a 6–8 bar outro. No refrain is invented — the doubling inside each verse does the work a refrain would do. | source has no refrain; inventing one would add text that is not in the source |
| **Tempo** | ♩ = 63–68; a slow 3/4 that breathes on the short lines | the short half-lines need time; the project's prior tracks run 6–7 minutes |
| **Duration target** | 5:30–6:30 | 7 verses at ~45 s |
| **Mode** | Aeolian, cadencing to the relative major only in verse 7 | verse 7 turns from the first person to a plural, doxological address — the one legitimate harmonic turn in the poem |
| **Lead vocal** | one male baritone, close-miked, low vibrato, speech-adjacent diction; **no belting** | project style rules: restrained, dark, clear diction |
| **Choir** | absent in verses 1–3; two voices (alto + tenor) from verse 4; four-part only in verse 7 | verse 4 is where the "child" image enters and the poem warms; verse 7 is the communal turn |
| **Instruments** | gut-string cello (sustained pedal tones), contrabass (arco, sparse), theorbo or archlute (plucked, only on cadences), small pipe-organ pad (no mixture stops), single low wooden flute line in verse 5 | matches the owner's stated preference; **explicitly a modern arrangement**, not a claim about 1676 practice |
| **Excluded** | drum kit, electric bass, synth pads, strings-section swell, EDM elements, harsh or "smoker-like" bass voice, tremolo organ | project style rules |
| **Dynamics** | verse 1 p, verse 2 pp (self-examination goes quieter, not louder), verses 3–4 mp, verse 5 mf with the flute, verse 6 dim. to pp on "замолчит", verse 7 mf → p on the final line | follows the rhetorical arc rather than a generic build |
| **Audio** | 48 kHz stereo, natural room, no plate reverb; target −16 LUFS for streaming | house delivery pattern |
| **Language handling** | Russian lyric; the Dutch title and author are metadata only, never sung | — |
