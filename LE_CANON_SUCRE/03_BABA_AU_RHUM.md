# 03 — Baba au Rhum

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать о Nicolas Stohrer, Stanislas Leszczyński, первоначальном алкоголе и появлении rum; чем исторически различаются baba и savarin?

## Краткий статус

`CONFIRMED 1873 rum-baba/savarin distinction / HOUSE TRADITION for 18th-c origin / exact early chain unresolved`

## Participant sources

Stohrer house history:
https://stohrer.fr/notre-maison/

Current Maison states that Nicolas Stohrer created the baba. Importantly, the page itself preserves divergent versions: the dry kouglof was soaked with Tokay **or** Malaga depending on the account, and rum came later.

This is valuable participant evidence because it does not pretend the origin story is uniform.

Current product:
https://stohrer.fr/produit/baba-au-rhum-individuel/

Current savoir-faire:
https://stohrer.fr/notre-savoir-faire/

These verify house continuity and current attribution, not the eighteenth-century event independently.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Wave 1 can prove that by 1873 professional French pastry literature treats rum baba and savarin as distinct named forms.

Wave 1 cannot yet prove:

- exact year Nicolas Stohrer first made a baba;
- which alcohol was used first;
- exact moment rum replaced wine;
- every element of the Stanislas anecdote.

The current Stohrer page itself supports caution by acknowledging divergent wine accounts.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Stohrer today preserves Nicolas Stohrer as the house creator of baba, while acknowledging divergent Tokay/Malaga versions and a later arrival of rum. By 1873 Gouffé directly documents a `baba moderne glacé au rhum` and separately documents savarin.

Unsafe:

> Stanislas ordered a dry kouglof, Stohrer poured exactly X wine on it in year Y, then immediately invented rum baba.

## Хронология

- 1730 — Stohrer house foundation according to current Maison history.
- 18th c. — house origin tradition; exact event chain unresolved.
- 1873 — direct Gouffé witnesses for rum baba and savarin.

## Anatomy / technique

Research layers to distinguish:

- fermented enriched dough;
- soaking/syrup;
- alcohol choice and chronology;
- mold/form;
- baba vs savarin geometry and garnish.

Do not treat current rum percentage or garnish as proof of the original recipe.

## Myth vs Document

**DOCUMENT:** Gouffé 1873 rum-baba and savarin pages.  
**PARTICIPANT TRADITION:** Stohrer/Nicolas/Stanislas lineage.  
**INTERNAL CONFLICT ACKNOWLEDGED BY HOUSE:** Tokay vs Malaga.  
**UNRESOLVED:** exact first rum use.
