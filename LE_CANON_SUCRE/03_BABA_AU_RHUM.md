# 03 — Baba au Rhum

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать о Nicolas Stohrer, Stanislas Leszczyński, первоначальном алкоголе и появлении rum; когда `baba` и `savarin` уже являются профессионально именованными формами; чем исторически различаются baba и savarin?

## Краткий статус

`BAILLEUX 1860 PROFESSIONAL NAMED BABA+SAVARIN ENTRIES CONFIRMED VIA INSTITUTIONAL TOC / GOUFFE 1873 EXACT RUM-BABA+SAVARIN PAGES CONFIRMED / HOUSE TRADITION FOR 18TH-C ORIGIN / FIRST RUM-SPECIFIC DATE STILL OPEN`

## Bailleux 1860 — professional named entries before Gouffé

SPbPU institutional record / open machine-readable table of contents:
https://elib.spbstu.ru/dl/2/ed-3262_0000697628bx.pdf/en/info

Object identity exposed by the institution:

- Louis Bailleux;
- *Le patissier moderne ou Traité élémentaire et pratique de la patisserie française au dix-neuvième siècle*;
- Paris, **1860**;
- DOI `10.18720/SPBPU/2/ed-3262`;
- scan reading restricted to authorized/SPbPU access, but the table of contents is openly exposed.

The TOC directly lists, among professional pastry entries:

- `Petits Savarins`;
- `Babas parisiens`;
- later in the entremets section, separate `Savarin` and `Baba` entries.

Evidence decision:

- named professional `Baba` / `Babas parisiens` and `Savarin` in the **1860 edition** = `A2 INSTITUTIONAL TOC VERIFIED`;
- exact 1860 recipe pages, geometry, soaking liquid and alcohol = `ACCESS/LOCATOR_HOLD` because scan reading is restricted;
- presence of `Baba` in 1860 does **not** prove the entry is rum-soaked;
- whether the same entries already occur in the **1856 first edition** must be established by edition comparison rather than inferred.

This materially improves the chronology while preserving the key distinction: **named Baba/Savarin is documented by 1860; exact rum-baba formulation is directly inspectable in Gouffé 1873.**

Evidence decision:

- by 1873, professional French pastry literature directly and exactly documents a **rum-specific baba**;
- Gouffé also treats baba and savarin as separate named forms;
- this does not establish 1873 as the first-ever use of rum, only the earliest exact rum-baba page directly inspected in the current corpus.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Current corpus can establish:

- Bailleux 1860 already names Baba/Babas parisiens and Savarin in a professional pastry structure;
- Gouffé 1873 directly documents `baba moderne glacé au rhum` and savarin with exact pages;
- current Stohrer tradition attributes baba to Nicolas Stohrer but itself acknowledges divergent Tokay/Malaga stories and later rum.

Current corpus cannot yet prove:

- exact year Nicolas Stohrer first made a baba;
- which alcohol was used first;
- exact date rum entered the formula;
- whether Bailleux 1860 Baba was already rum-soaked;
- whether the 1856 first edition already contains the same named Baba/Savarin entries;
- every element of the Stanislas anecdote.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Stohrer today preserves Nicolas Stohrer as the house creator of baba while acknowledging divergent Tokay/Malaga versions and a later arrival of rum. Bailleux's 1860 professional table of contents already lists Baba/Babas parisiens and Savarin, while Gouffé 1873 provides directly inspectable pages for a `baba moderne glacé au rhum` and for savarin.

Unsafe:

> Stanislas ordered a dry kouglof, Stohrer poured exactly X wine on it in year Y, then immediately invented rum baba.

Unsafe:

> Rum baba first appeared in 1873.

1873 is the earliest **directly inspected rum-specific page in the current corpus**, not an absolute first-use claim.

## Хронология

- 1730 — Stohrer house foundation according to current Maison history.
- 18th c. — house origin tradition; exact event chain unresolved.
- **1860** — Bailleux professional edition openly exposes named `Babas parisiens`, `Baba`, `Petits Savarins`, `Savarin` in institutional TOC; exact pages/formula restricted.
- **1873** — direct Gouffé exact pages for `baba moderne glacé au rhum` and savarin.

## Anatomy / technique

Research layers to distinguish:

- fermented enriched dough;
- soaking/syrup;
- alcohol choice and chronology;
- mold/form;
- baba vs savarin geometry and garnish;
- named-form continuity across Bailleux editions 1856/1860 and Gouffé 1873.

Do not treat current rum percentage or garnish as proof of the original recipe.

## Myth vs Document

**DOCUMENTED NAMED PROFESSIONAL FORMS:** Bailleux 1860 institutional TOC.  
**DOCUMENTED EXACT RUM-BABA:** Gouffé 1873.  
**PARTICIPANT TRADITION:** Stohrer/Nicolas/Stanislas lineage.  
**INTERNAL CONFLICT ACKNOWLEDGED BY HOUSE:** Tokay vs Malaga.  
**UNRESOLVED:** first rum-specific recipe/date; 1856-vs-1860 edition delta; exact 1860 Baba/Savarin pages.
