## STRONG RESERVE

### Macaron parisien

**Why strong:** enormous contemporary influence, technique, house competition and international transmission.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** Ispahan already carries the macaron language; separate study must show that a generic macaron article adds more than it duplicates.

### Mille-feuille classique

**Why strong:** foundational laminated pastry architecture and historical transmission.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** `2000 Feuilles` is intentionally a modern transformation of the lineage. Research should determine whether the classical form deserves its own core slot or is better a genealogy layer inside 2000 Feuilles.

### Savarin

**Why strong:** direct Gouffé 1873 attribution to the Julien brothers and close relation to baba.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** likely best handled as a contrast dossier inside Baba unless independent influence warrants a slot.

### Pithiviers

**Why strong:** strong architectural/lamination identity and relevance to Galette/frangipane history.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** needs evidence showing distinct canonical transmission rather than acting only as a comparator.

### Madeleine de Commercy

**Why strong:** regional identity, literary/cultural transmission, recognizable form.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** less central to haute-pâtisserie architecture than several current candidates; should be scored after source pass.

### Flan pâtissier

**Why strong:** old professional lineage and modern revival.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** everyday canonical significance is high, but current core already has broad choux/lamination/ritual coverage.

### Fraisier

**Why strong:** recognizable layered architecture and professional classic status.  
**Why not auto-promoted:** exact historical/influence case not yet researched.

## INTERESTING BUT NOT YET CANON

- Croissant — culturally canonical but primarily viennoiserie rather than pâtisserie; would distort the section boundary unless the project explicitly expands.
- Brioche regional families — important but category definition is too broad for the current 15.
- Financier — strong recognizable technique but current evidence/influence comparison not yet done.
- Clafoutis — culturally important, less central to the professional architecture thesis.

## Selection tests for replacement

A reserve candidate should replace a core object only if research demonstrates a materially stronger combination of:

1. documented historical reach;
2. technique transmitted across professional generations;
3. form that became a reusable pastry language;
4. cultural/house/territorial significance;
5. distinctiveness from the other 14;
6. enough evidence for an intellectually honest dossier.

Popularity alone is insufficient.

## Current editorial recommendation

Retain all 15 during evidence collection. Do **not** remove a controversial item simply because its legend weakens: Tatin, Canelé and Religieuse become more valuable when their uncertainty is documented accurately. The final Canon can distinguish `canonical influence` from `origin certainty` rather than pretending every classic has a clean inventor/date story.
