# 04 — Tarte au Citron / Jacques Genin

## Проверяемый вопрос

Как отделить многовековой жанр tarte au citron от конкретного современного языка Jacques Genin; что документируется о его citron-basilic и технике крема?

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Wave 1 supports a modern Genin chapter, not a generic origin claim.

Do not write:

> Jacques Genin invented the tarte au citron.

Do write, with attribution:

> In modern Parisian pâtisserie, Genin's 2009 citron-basilic version became a distinctive reinterpretation built around sharp acidity, minimal sugar and a very fluid creamy texture.

The broader history of lemon curd/crème citron, meringue/no-meringue forms and tart-shell evolution remains a separate research lane.

## Sensory markers

Supported modern markers: pronounced acidity, aromatic purity, restrained sweetness, unusually fluid cream texture.

## Typical failures — research hypotheses only

Future technical sources should test: over-sweetening, overcooked/curdled cream, weak citrus aroma, thick rubbery texture, soggy shell. These are not yet source-closed claims.
