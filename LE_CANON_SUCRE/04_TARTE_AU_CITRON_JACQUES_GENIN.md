# 04 — Tarte au Citron / Jacques Genin

## Проверяемый вопрос

Как отделить историю жанра tarte au citron от конкретного современного языка Jacques Genin; когда во французской pâtisserie появляется именно сочетание baked tart shell + lemon cream/curd; что документируется о Genin citron-basilic и его технике крема?

## Краткий статус

`GENRE HISTORY OPEN / PROFESSIONAL HISTORICAL SYNTHESIS WARNS AGAINST SIMPLE FRENCH-ORIGIN STORY / GENIN 2009 VERSION PROBABLE / DO NOT ATTRIBUTE INVENTION OF TARTE AU CITRON TO GENIN`

## Genre-history falsification lead — Relais Desserts 2026

Relais Desserts professional association:
https://www.relais-desserts.net/2026/01/19/il-etait-une-fois-la-tarte-au-citron/

This recent professional historical synthesis is useful **not as primary origin proof**, but because it directly challenges a simplistic narrative that the modern lemon tart has always existed as a French classic in its present form.

The article argues that:

- older French pastry uses of lemon often concern juice/zest, syrups, confectionery and acidity/aroma rather than the later familiar lemon-cream tart;
- Alexandre Dumas's 1873 culinary dictionary discusses lemon preparations without, according to the article, supplying the modern lemon-cream core;
- it points toward an **English lemon-curd / lemon-cream genealogy** before the modern French tart form.

Evidence decision:

- this is `PROFESSIONAL HISTORICAL SYNTHESIS / LOCATOR GENERATOR`, not a primary document;
- the claim `modern tarte au citron is simply an old native French invention` is now an explicit falsification target;
- Dumas 1873 and the proposed eighteenth/nineteenth-century English sources must be directly acquired before the article's historical chain is promoted;
- do not replace one national-origin myth with another merely because a professional association states it.

This source substantially improves the **research question** even though it does not close the chronology.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Current evidence supports a modern Genin chapter, not a generic origin claim.

Do not write:

> Jacques Genin invented the tarte au citron.

Do write, with attribution:

> In modern Parisian pâtisserie, Genin's 2009 citron-basilic version became a distinctive reinterpretation built around sharp acidity, restrained sweetness and a fluid creamy texture.

For the older genre, keep separate:

1. use of lemon in pastry/confiserie;
2. lemon cream / lemon curd as a preparation;
3. tart shells filled before baking vs blind-baked shells filled afterward;
4. cooked/baked lemon custard vs emulsified butter-rich cream;
5. meringued vs unmeringued forms;
6. British/European transmission vs specifically French codification.

The modern object can be canonical even if its component genealogy is transnational.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

## Anatomy / technique

Current Genin evidence supports research into:

- pâte sucrée / tart shell;
- citrus cream;
- acidity vs sugar balance;
- basil as aromatic extension;
- butter incorporation / emulsion technique from published method.

Historical research must separately classify:

- baked custard fillings;
- curd-like preparations;
- emulsified creams;
- meringue additions;
- blind-baked shell assembly.

Do not infer the exact current shop formula from journalism alone.

## Sensory markers

Supported modern markers: pronounced acidity, aromatic purity, restrained sweetness, unusually fluid cream texture.

## Typical failures — research hypotheses only

Future technical sources should test: over-sweetening, overcooked/curdled cream, weak citrus aroma, thick rubbery texture, soggy shell. These are not yet source-closed claims.

## Myth vs Document

**MODERN DOCUMENTARY LAYER:** Genin 2009/reception through strong French press.  
**PROFESSIONAL HISTORY HYPOTHESIS:** Relais Desserts points toward a transnational/English lemon-cream genealogy.  
**PRIMARY GAPS:** exact Dumas 1873 lemon entries; early English lemon-curd sources; earliest French recognizable tarte-au-citron recipes; meringue transition.  
**PROHIBITED SHORTCUT:** `Genin invented tarte au citron` or `France invented the modern lemon-curd tart in year X` without primary chain.
