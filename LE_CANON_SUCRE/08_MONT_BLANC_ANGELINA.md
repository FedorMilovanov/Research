# 08 — Mont-Blanc / Angelina

## Проверяемый вопрос

Как отделить более раннюю европейскую историю Mont-Blanc / Monte Bianco от конкретного signature Angelina; что можно доказать о Rumpelmayer, 1903 и современной тройке meringue–cream–chestnut?

## Краткий статус

`ANGELINA HOUSE LINEAGE CONFIRMED / PRE-1903 EUROPEAN GENEALOGY OPEN / DO NOT CLAIM CATEGORY INVENTION`

## Participant sources

Angelina savoir-faire:
https://www.angelina-paris.fr/notre-savoir-faire

Current Maison treats Mont-Blanc as its signature and associates it with Anton Rumpelmayer at the beginning of the twentieth century. The current house description centers on:

- meringue;
- light whipped cream;
- chestnut-cream vermicelli.

Angelina history:
https://www.angelina-paris.fr/notre-histoire

Current Maison dates the establishment to **1903** and identifies Anton Rumpelmayer in its founding history.

The house also tells a hairstyle-inspired form story with hedged wording. Preserve that as participant tradition rather than historical fact.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

The current Angelina sources are strong for:

- house identity;
- signature status;
- present/claimed early-20th-century lineage;
- founding chronology.

They are not sufficient to prove that no Mont-Blanc/Monte Bianco chestnut dessert existed before Angelina.

A proper dossier therefore needs two timelines:

A. `EUROPEAN MONT-BLANC / MONTE BIANCO BEFORE ANGELINA`  
B. `ANGELINA SIGNATURE FROM 1903 ERA ONWARD`

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Angelina, founded in 1903 according to its house history, presents Mont-Blanc as a signature associated with Anton Rumpelmayer and built around meringue, cream and chestnut vermicelli.

Unsafe:

> Anton Rumpelmayer invented the Mont-Blanc dessert in 1903.

That second claim remains blocked until French/Italian/Swiss/German historical cookbooks are searched.

## Хронология

- pre-1903 — European chestnut-dessert genealogy `UNRESOLVED`;
- 1903 — Angelina house founding milestone;
- early 20th c. — current Maison associates signature Mont-Blanc with Rumpelmayer.

## Anatomy / technique

Current signature anatomy:

`meringue base/core → light cream → chestnut vermicelli`.

Future work must check whether this exact stacking is stable across historical Angelina menus/recipes or is a modern house description.

## Myth vs Document

- `DOCUMENTED CURRENT HOUSE`: signature/status/composition and 1903 house history.
- `HOUSE TRADITION`: hairstyle inspiration.
- `UNRESOLVED`: earliest European Mont-Blanc/Monte Bianco and exact Rumpelmayer creation date.
