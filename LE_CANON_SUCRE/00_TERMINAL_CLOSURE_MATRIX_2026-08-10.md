## Closure contract

A row is `CLOSED` when the corpus has enough evidence for the bounded editorial proposition stated in `safe wording`. This does **not** imply absolute invention priority unless explicitly stated.

`TERMINAL_NON_BLOCKING_HOLD` means:

- targeted public/institutional/participant/independent routes were attempted;
- no authentication, paywall or access control is bypassed;
- negative search coverage is never converted into proof of absence;
- the missing leaf, number or exact date is omitted or attributed at its actual evidence level;
- the hold may be reopened only by a newly acquired primary/authorized object, not by another generic retelling.

`VISUAL_CUSTODY_HOLD` is never a historical-fact blocker by itself. It blocks facsimile reuse/quotation at page-image grade, not an otherwise evidence-closed bounded claim.
