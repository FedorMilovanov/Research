# 12 — Tarte Tropézienne

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать об Alexandre Micka, 1955, Brigitte Bardot, съёмках *Et Dieu… créa la femme* и регистрации марки; где заканчивается house legend и начинается документируемая история?

## Краткий статус

`1955 STRONG HOUSE CREATION TRADITION / FILM SHOOT DOCUMENTED MAY–JULY 1956 / BARDOT NAMING HAS STRONG RETROSPECTIVE TESTIMONY BUT YEAR-CONFLATION CONFLICT / 18 AUG 1972 STRONG SECONDARY PATENT LOCATOR / EXACT INPI OBJECT OPEN`

## Participant source — Maison

La Tarte Tropézienne official Maison:
https://www.latartetropezienne.fr/fr/

Current Maison attributes the pastry to Alexandre Micka in Saint-Tropez in **1955** and emphasizes the protected/secret cream recipe and continuing house identity.

Official Bardot tribute page:
https://www.latartetropezienne.fr/fr/blog/le-coffret-bb-trop-en-lhonneur-de-brigitte-bardot-n18

The Maison preserves a retrospective Brigitte Bardot quotation identifying herself as the pastry's `marraine` and tying the story to the film. This is valuable participant/retrospective testimony, but the page itself frames the episode as `En 1955`.

That framing conflicts with independent film-production chronology below.

## Film-production chronology — independent institutional evidence

Film France / CNC:
https://www.filmfrance.net/films/et-dieu-crea-la-femme/

The official Film France/CNC record identifies *Et Dieu… créa la Femme* as a **1956** film and lists Saint-Tropez among the shooting locations.

Radio France gives exact shooting dates:
https://www.radiofrance.fr/franceinter/et-dieu-crea-la-femme-8528446

`3 May 1956 – 7 July 1956`.

German filmportal independently gives the same exact Saint-Tropez shooting period:
https://www.filmportal.de/en/movie/et-dieu-crea-la-femme_

`03.05.1956 – 07.07.1956`.

La Cinémathèque française independently catalogues the film as **1956**:
https://www.cinematheque.fr/film/51587.html

This creates a real chronology correction:

- `Micka creates pastry / Maison milestone` = 1955 tradition;
- `Bardot encounters/names/popularizes it during filming` = cannot literally be a 1955 filming event if the documented shoot ran May–July 1956.

## Strong retrospective Bardot testimony

La Toque reproduces a retrospective Bardot statement in which she recalls discovering the still-unnamed pastry, sharing it with the film crew and becoming its `marraine`:
https://www.latoque.fr/tarte-sucree/article/832844/la-tarte-tropezienne-star-des-brioches

This materially strengthens the **Bardot connection itself**, but it does not turn the house's `1955 during filming` wording into a chronologically exact event date.

Le Monde also preserves the standard Micka/Bardot narrative:
https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/2012/08/11/la-tarte-tropezienne_4324351_1819218.html

Again, this is later journalism, not a 1956 source.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Popular retellings often compress four distinct claims:

1. Micka created the pastry in 1955;
2. the film project reached Saint-Tropez;
3. Bardot encountered the pastry during filming;
4. Bardot proposed or inspired the name `Tarte Tropézienne`.

Wave 1 now requires those claims to be split.

### 1. Micka / 1955

`STRONG HOUSE TRADITION`, supported by current Maison and modern Saint-Tropez institutional commemoration.

### 2. Film shooting

`DOCUMENTED 1956`, with exact dates 3 May–7 July 1956 from Radio France/filmportal and 1956 institutional film records from CNC/Cinémathèque.

### 3. Bardot connection

`STRONG RETROSPECTIVE PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY` — Bardot's later quoted recollection supports discovery/promotion/marraine status.

### 4. Exact naming date and mechanism

`PROBABLE / NOT CONTEMPORARY-CLOSED`.

The current evidence makes `Bardot named it in 1955 while shooting the film` internally inconsistent. A better working hypothesis is: **pastry/house creation tradition in 1955; Bardot/film encounter in 1956**, unless a period source reveals a pre-shoot encounter or different naming sequence.

This is an inference from the combined sources and must be labeled as such.

## Trademark / brevet / INPI

The legal-history lane is now narrower but **not closed**.

Several independent secondary sources converge on **18 August 1972** as the date on which the production process (`procédé permettant la réalisation de l’appareil`) was the subject of a French patent filing. A useful explicit locator is:

https://www.provence7.com/portails/gastronomie/tarte-tropezienne/

La Toque separately states that in 1972 the recipe and the name were deposited with INPI:
https://www.latoque.fr/tarte-sucree/article/832844/la-tarte-tropezienne-star-des-brioches

A current indexed INPI opposition decision, reproduced by a French legal-data service, independently proves that the present company owns later French marks including `LA TARTE TROPEZIENNE 1955 SAINT-TROPEZ` (No. 4 046 450, filed 12 Nov 2013) and `LE CREATEUR DE LA TARTE TROPEZIENNE A. MICKA` (No. 4 046 444):
https://justice.pappers.fr/decision/585fe174b7400b63a5beba64449269c0dc8a4d69

This **does not** prove the 1972 filing; it only helps separate later trademark continuity from the historical patent claim.

One modern article instead says **1973**, demonstrating why the primary INPI patent object is still mandatory before the exact legal chronology is published as closed fact.

Current decisions:

- `18 August 1972` = `STRONG SECONDARY LOCATOR` for a French patent/process filing;
- exact historical patent number, applicant, title, claims and publication = `LOCATOR_HOLD`;
- historical trademark/name filing in 1972 = `PROBABLE`, exact mark object still needed;
- later 2013 French marks = independently documented, but they are not substitutes for 1972;
- `marque`, `brevet`, trade name, logo and secret recipe must remain distinct legal objects.

Do **not** write simply `the recipe was patented in 1972` until the primary patent record is acquired and its scope is read.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> The current Maison dates Alexandre Micka's Saint-Tropez creation to 1955. The film *Et Dieu… créa la femme* was shot in Saint-Tropez in 1956, with independent sources giving 3 May–7 July 1956; later Bardot testimony strongly supports her role as the pastry's famous `marraine`. Secondary sources converge on a French patent/process filing dated 18 August 1972, but the exact historical INPI patent and mark records remain to be acquired.

Unsafe:

> Brigitte Bardot named the pastry in 1955 during the shooting of *Et Dieu… créa la femme*.

The documented shoot occurred in 1956.

Also unsafe:

> The whole secret recipe and the brand were definitively patented on 18 August 1972 under a known legal scope.

The exact primary patent/mark records have not yet been acquired, and `brevet`/`marque` must not be conflated.

## Хронология

- **1955** — current Maison creation milestone for Micka / pastry.
- **3 May–7 July 1956** — documented filming period for *Et Dieu… créa la femme*.
- **1956** — Bardot naming/discovery episode is chronologically compatible with the film evidence, but exact date/mechanism remains retrospective rather than contemporaneously closed.
- **18 Aug 1972** — strong secondary locator for French patent/process filing; primary patent number/object pending.
- **1972** — secondary sources also describe INPI name/mark protection; exact historical mark object pending.
- **1985** — modern press describes Dufrêne succession/recipe transfer; company records should still be acquired for exact transaction history.
- **12 Nov 2013** — later French marks including Nos. 4 046 450 and 4 046 444 are documented in an INPI opposition decision reproduced in legal-data sources; this is a modern rights-continuity milestone, not the origin filing.

## Anatomy / technique

Do not reverse-engineer the secret cream recipe. Research only publicly documented structural characteristics:

- brioche-like base/top;
- cream filling;
- sugar topping;
- regional/house service form.

Exact formula remains proprietary.

## Polski research lane

A genuine Polish-language source is now present:

Dzień Dobry TVN:
https://dziendobry.tvn.pl/przepisy-kulinarne/tarta-tropezienne-czyli-tarta-z-saint-tropez-jaki-jest-przepis-da315891-ls5322597

The Polish TV article preserves an interview-derived description from Albert Dufrêne, who worked with Micka from 1985 and later took over the business. It describes Micka's character and gives a public structural description of the cake (yeasted/brioche dough with butter/pastry-cream combination) while stressing that the true formula is known only to a few people.

This is `B1` evidence for Polish reception/biographical testimony and later participant memory. It is **not** a primary source for Micka's prewar Polish training or his grandmother's exact recipe.

A French-language publication based in Warsaw also provides a useful Polish-context synthesis and correctly places the film episode in **1956**:
https://lepetitjournal.com/varsovie/alexandre-micka-polonais-tarte-tropezienne-recette-358718

Still required for the Polish biography:

- spelling variants `Alexandre/Aleksander Micka`;
- birth/migration records;
- Polish pastry training/context;
- wartime and postwar movement to Provence;
- independent records of the grandmother-recipe lineage.

## Myth vs Document

**HOUSE TRADITION:** Micka / pastry creation in 1955.  
**DOCUMENTED FILM FACT:** Saint-Tropez filming in May–July 1956.  
**STRONG RETROSPECTIVE TESTIMONY:** Bardot as discoverer/promoter/marraine.  
**CONFLICT:** house/popular 1955-film wording vs documented 1956 shoot.  
**UNRESOLVED:** exact naming date/mechanism.  
**STRONG SECONDARY LEGAL LOCATOR:** 18 Aug 1972 patent/process filing.  
**LOCATOR HOLD:** exact 1972 patent number/object and historical mark record.  
**DOCUMENTED LATER RIGHTS:** 2013 French mark Nos. 4 046 450 / 4 046 444.  
**PROPRIETARY:** secret cream formula.
