# 12 — Tarte Tropézienne

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать об Alexandre Micka, 1955, Brigitte Bardot, съёмках *Et Dieu… créa la femme* и регистрации марки; где заканчивается house legend и начинается документируемая история?

## Краткий статус

`1955 STRONG HOUSE TRADITION / BARDO​T NAMING NEEDS CONTEMPORARY EVIDENCE / INPI LOCATOR OPEN`

## Participant source

La Tarte Tropézienne official Maison:
https://www.latartetropezienne.fr/fr/

Current Maison attributes the pastry to Alexandre Micka in Saint-Tropez in **1955** and emphasizes the protected/secret cream recipe and continuing house identity.

This is strong participant evidence for current house history, but a 1955 newspaper/menu/company record would be stronger for the historical event itself.

## Archive lane

Wave 1 found an INA catalogue-level lead to later television material about the pastry with house interviews/archive images. A catalogue record is useful navigation but remains `CATALOG_ONLY` until exact programme/item metadata and relevant segment locator are captured.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Popular retellings often compress three distinct claims:

1. Micka created the pastry in 1955;
2. Brigitte Bardot encountered it during the filming of *Et Dieu… créa la femme*;
3. Bardot proposed the name `Tarte Tropézienne`.

These claims must be tested separately. The current house history supports the general lineage but Wave 1 has not yet acquired contemporaneous film-production/press evidence for the naming episode.

The reported `1972` mark-registration milestone also needs exact INPI record acquisition before being promoted.

Keep this as participant/popular tradition until a contemporaneous source or strong direct testimony is acquired.
