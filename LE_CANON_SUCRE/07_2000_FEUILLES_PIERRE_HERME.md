# 07 — 2000 Feuilles / Pierre Hermé

## Проверяемый вопрос

Когда создан `2000 Feuilles`; почему он так называется; чем он технически отличается от классического mille-feuille; какие элементы можно подтвердить напрямую у Hermé?

## Краткий статус

`CONFIRMED current signature/composition / PROBABLE millennium naming context / EVIDENCE_HOLD on first launch date and exact name origin`

## Official source

Pierre Hermé — L'Art de la Pâtisserie:
https://www.pierreherme.com/fr/art-de-la-patisserie

Current house material places 2000 Feuilles among signature/fetish creations.

## Direct participant interview

Galeries Lafayette Le Gourmet — Pierre Hermé:
https://gourmet.galerieslafayette.com/b/pierre-herme-et-pierre-sang-une-rencontre-gourmande-entre-deux-univers/

Hermé directly describes the 2000 Feuilles logic through caramelized puff pastry, Piedmont hazelnut praline feuilleté, praline mousseline and the addition of crisp elements for contrast. This is strong participant evidence for composition/concept, not necessarily for the first launch year.

## Independent secondary lead

Gault&Millau:
https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/pierre-herme-en-cinq-desserts

The article connects the creation/name to the turn of the millennium. This is plausible and useful, but under Research policy it remains `B1` until confirmed in a period catalogue or direct Hermé statement.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Do **not** infer that `2000` means literally two thousand pastry layers.

Do **not** turn the millennium explanation into fact solely because it sounds obvious.

Current evidence securely supports:

- the object is a Hermé signature/fetish;
- it is deliberately related to mille-feuille;
- praline and textural contrast are central;
- the exact launch/name rationale still requires period evidence.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Pierre Hermé's own materials define 2000 Feuilles through caramelized puff pastry, hazelnut-praline feuilleté and praline mousseline, using crispness as a deliberate textural counterpoint.

Conditional:

> Created around the millennium and named for the year 2000.

This remains attributed to Gault&Millau until a primary launch source is obtained.

## Anatomy / technique

Research-ready layers:

`caramelized feuilletage → praline feuilleté / crisp layer → praline mousseline`.

Exact counts, formula and current proprietary recipe are outside scope unless openly published by the rightsholder.

## Industry influence

The dossier should compare 2000 Feuilles to classical mille-feuille as an example of a modern chef converting a canonical technique into an authored signature. Influence claims beyond Hermé's own status still need independent professional sources.
