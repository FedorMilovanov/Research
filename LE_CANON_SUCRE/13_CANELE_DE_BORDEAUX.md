# 13 — Canelé de Bordeaux

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что действительно известно о происхождении canelé; есть ли доказательства монастырской истории, связи с винным осветлением/желтками и портом; когда фиксируются современная форма, написание и коллективная марка?

## Краткий статус

`CONVENT ORIGIN NOT ESTABLISHED / REGIONAL MYTH-CORRECTION STRONG / 1985 PROFESSIONAL INSTITUTIONALIZATION STRONGLY CORROBORATED / PROFESSIONAL PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY CONFIRMS ONE-N INPI FILING / INPI HISTORICAL COVERAGE CONFIRMS A 1985 FR MARK SHOULD BE SEARCHABLE IN PRINCIPLE / EXACT MARK RECORD AND HISTORICAL HOLDER STILL TO ACQUIRE`

## Regional institutional source

Région Nouvelle-Aquitaine:
https://www.nouvelle-aquitaine.fr/le-territoire/produits-de-nouvelle-aquitaine/le-cannele-bordelais

This remains the strongest myth-correction source. The regional page reports that archival/archaeological research conducted in 1995 did **not** find evidence supporting the modern canelé convent story and states that no trace of the modern cake was found before the twentieth century in the research it cites.

The page separates older `canaule/canauliers` vocabulary from the later modern pastry rather than assuming identity.

It reports:

- a 1937 written milestone associated with Jean Balde;
- the 1985 choice of `canelé` with one `n` by the Confrérie;
- a collective-mark deposit.

The exact underlying 1937 page, 1995 research object and INPI record remain acquisition targets.

## Professional participant source — strongest current 1985 testimony

**Pâtisserie Française** / French professional pastry federation site, interview published 18 November 2020:
https://www.patisseriefrancaise.fr/website/page/actualites_regionale?__ws=YToyOntzOjQ6InBhZ2UiO2k6OTA7czo0OiJhcmVhIjtzOjk6Im1haW5fbWVudSI7fQ%3D%3D

This is stronger than a generic modern retelling because the professional source speaks from inside the Gironde pastry trade initiative. It states that:

- Daniel Antoine of Bordeaux launched the idea of promoting the departmental specialty;
- in **1985**, the Union départementale des pâtissiers de la Gironde decided to bring the recipe back into prominence by creating the Confrérie du Canelé;
- M. Marquet was named Grand Maître;
- members used a ring/logo and a cahier-des-charges style professional commitment;
- crucially, the interview uses first-person professional wording that **in 1985 they removed one `n` and filed the one-`n` name with INPI to distinguish it from the generic two-`n` spelling**.

Evidence decision:

- `1985 professional Confrérie initiative` = `STRONG A3 PARTICIPANT/PROFESSIONAL INSTITUTIONAL EVIDENCE`;
- `intent/action to file one-n CANELE at INPI` = `STRONG A3 PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY`;
- exact historical mark number, formal legal type, filing date, registration/publication date, classes and present legal status = **still `LOCATOR_HOLD` until the historical INPI/BOPI record itself is acquired**.

## Official INPI coverage — the 1985 record is not outside the database horizon

INPI Base Marques guidance:
https://www.inpi.fr/ressources/propriete-intellectuelle/rechercher-une-marque-base-marques

Official INPI data/API documentation:
https://www.inpi.fr/ressources/propriete-intellectuelle/acces-aux-api-et-ftp

Data INPI industrial-property data documentation:
https://data.inpi.fr/content/editorial/lien-serveur-ftp-PI

The official coverage materially changes the acquisition diagnosis:

- French marks, **in force or no longer in force**, filed at INPI are covered **from 1976**;
- bibliographic, administrative and legal data are available;
- BOPI pages citing French marks are available **from 1982**;
- the bulk/API datasets likewise cover French marks from 1976, whether still valid or not.

Therefore a genuine French 1985 `CANELE` collective-mark filing is **within the stated historical coverage**. The remaining problem is not `too old for Data INPI`; it is one or more of:

1. exact mark wording differs from later retellings;
2. the historical deposant/holder was the pastry trade union or another predecessor, not necessarily the present Confrérie name;
3. the record is difficult to expose through ordinary web indexing/search UI;
4. the filing may have a historical legal categorization or owner-name normalization that requires advanced/bulk search.

This is now an **identifier/holder-resolution task**, not an archive-horizon guess.

## Historical holder / predecessor lead — do not promote yet

A currently active professional organization is independently identifiable as:

**`SYNDICAT DES PATRONS PATISSIERS DE BORDEAUX ET DE LA GIRONDE`**  
SIREN **781 846 068**; Bordeaux.

A separate legal-announcement trail shows this named organization represented in later corporate governance by **ANTOINE Daniel Jean Claude**, providing a real link between the professional organization name and the same Daniel Antoine associated with the canelé initiative.

Archives départementales de la Gironde also preserve a much older pâtissiers-glaciers professional-union history, including formation of a chambre syndicale in **1881**, so a professional-organizational predecessor line is institutionally plausible.

Current decision:

- `SYNDICAT DES PATRONS PATISSIERS ... / SIREN 781846068 = historical INPI mark holder in 1985` → **UNRESOLVED / OWNER-IDENTITY LEAD ONLY**;
- do not replace `Union départementale des pâtissiers de la Gironde` from the participant account with the later legal name unless a historical register or mark record connects them;
- search both names, Daniel Antoine, former addresses and organization variants.

This owner-line matters because searching only `CONFRERIE DU CANELE DE BORDEAUX` may systematically miss a 1985 filing made by the profession's union or predecessor body.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Nouvelle-Aquitaine's institutional history cautions that the famous convent-origin story is not supported by the archival/archaeological evidence it cites. Professional Gironde pastry testimony says that in 1985 the departmental pastry organization created the Confrérie, removed one `n` from `cannelé`, and filed the one-`n` name with INPI; regional and library sources independently report a collective-mark filing. INPI's own historical coverage includes French marks, valid or expired, from 1976 and BOPI mark publications from 1982, so the 1985 legal object falls within the official search horizon. Its exact number, holder and dates still need to be resolved.

Unsafe:

> Bordeaux nuns invented canelé in the eighteenth century using spare egg yolks from wine production.

Unsafe until exact legal object:

> The collective mark was filed on 24 March 1985 by the Syndicat des Patrons Pâtissiers under INPI number X and classes Y/Z.

No exact primary record currently supports those legal fields or holder identity.
