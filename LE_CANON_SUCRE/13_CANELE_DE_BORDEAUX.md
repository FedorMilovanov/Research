# 13 — Canelé de Bordeaux

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что действительно известно о происхождении canelé; есть ли доказательства монастырской истории, связи с винным осветлением/желтками и портом; когда фиксируются современная форма, написание и коллективная марка?

## Краткий статус

`CONVENT ORIGIN NOT ESTABLISHED / REGIONAL MYTH-CORRECTION STRONG / 1985 PROFESSIONAL INSTITUTIONALIZATION STRONGLY CORROBORATED / EXACT INPI MARK RECORD STILL TO ACQUIRE`

## Regional institutional source

Région Nouvelle-Aquitaine:
https://www.nouvelle-aquitaine.fr/le-territoire/produits-de-nouvelle-aquitaine/le-cannele-bordelais

This is currently the strongest myth-correction source. The regional page reports that archival/archaeological research conducted in 1995 did **not** find evidence supporting the modern canelé convent story and states that no trace of the modern cake was found before the twentieth century in that research.

The page also separates older `canaule/canauliers` vocabulary from the later modern pastry rather than assuming identity.

It reports:

- a 1937 written milestone associated with Jean Balde;
- the 1985 choice of `canelé` with one `n` by the Confrérie;
- a collective-mark deposit.

These last claims still need underlying exact source/INPI objects.

## Professional institutional source — 1985 Confrérie creation

Confédération / Pâtisserie Française:
https://www.patisseriefrancaise.fr/website/page/actualites_regionale?__ws=YToyOntzOjQ6InBhZ2UiO2k6OTA7czo0OiJhcmVhIjtzOjk6Im1haW5fbWVudSI7fQ%3D%3D

A professional French pastry-industry account gives a more specific institutional chain for **1985**:

- the Union départementale des pâtissiers de la Gironde decided to bring the regional specialty back into prominence;
- it created the Confrérie du Canelé;
- M. Marquet is identified as Grand Maître;
- the Confrérie established a logo/ring practice for member pâtissiers and a mission of defending/promoting the authentic product.

This is strong independent professional corroboration that the 1985 milestone was a real organized trade initiative, not merely a later tourism slogan.

Important boundary: the page does **not** replace the exact INPI legal record. The existence, legal type, filing number, filing date and specifications of any 1985 collective mark still require direct INPI acquisition.

## Public-library research service

Eurêkoi / Bibliothèque de Bordeaux:
https://www.eurekoi.org/je-suis-a-la-recherche-de-renseignements-fiables-fondes-sur-des-sources-authentiques-a-propos-de-lhistoire-du-canele-de-bordeaux/

This public-library research service triangulates the archival problem and provides bibliography/discovery paths. Its 2025 update likewise states that the Confrérie was created in 1985 and that a collective mark under the one-`n` spelling was deposited with INPI.

This independently strengthens the **existence of the reported legal event**, but still does not supply the exact INPI object/number.

## Participant / commercial caution

Baillardran:
https://www.baillardran.com/fr/content/7-l-histoire-du-canele

The house treats the convent story cautiously as myth/reality rather than certainty and confirms the 1985 orthographic/Confrérie turning point. This is useful participant context but not primary proof of eighteenth-century origin.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Current status by theory:

### Annonciades/convent invented modern canelé

`NOT ESTABLISHED / DO NOT PUBLISH AS FACT`.

The strongest regional institutional research explicitly says the attractive convent story lacks evidence.

### Wine fining created surplus yolks, therefore canelé

`PLAUSIBLE CONTEXTUAL STORY / UNRESOLVED CAUSATION`.

The existence of egg whites in wine-finishing practice does not by itself prove a direct recipe-origin chain. The causal step requires period evidence.

### Canauliers are direct makers of the modern canelé

`SEMANTIC/HISTORICAL RELATIONSHIP UNRESOLVED`.

Older lexical similarity does not prove recipe/form identity.

### 1985 Confrérie creation / professional institutionalization

`STRONG PROFESSIONAL + REGIONAL INSTITUTIONAL CORROBORATION`.

### 1985 single-n spelling / collective mark

`STRONG MULTI-INSTITUTIONAL REPORT / LOCATOR_HOLD` pending exact INPI object.

The legal claim must not be inflated beyond what has actually been acquired.

## Anatomy / technique

Modern technical characteristics to research from professional sources:

- fluted small mold;
- dark caramelized shell;
- custardy interior;
- copper mold tradition;
- beeswax/butter practices;
- rum/vanilla.

Do not project modern copper/beeswax practice back to an origin date without historical evidence.
