# 13 — Canelé de Bordeaux

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что действительно известно о происхождении canelé; есть ли доказательства монастырской истории, связи с винным осветлением/желтками и портом; когда фиксируются современная форма, написание и коллективная марка?

## Краткий статус

`CONVENT ORIGIN NOT ESTABLISHED / REGIONAL MYTH-CORRECTION STRONG / 1985 PROFESSIONAL INSTITUTIONALIZATION STRONGLY CORROBORATED / PROFESSIONAL PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY CONFIRMS INTENT TO FILE ONE-N CANELE AT INPI / EXACT HISTORICAL INPI MARK RECORD STILL TO ACQUIRE`

## Regional institutional source

Région Nouvelle-Aquitaine:
https://www.nouvelle-aquitaine.fr/le-territoire/produits-de-nouvelle-aquitaine/le-cannele-bordelais

This remains the strongest myth-correction source. The regional page reports that archival/archaeological research conducted in 1995 did **not** find evidence supporting the modern canelé convent story and states that no trace of the modern cake was found before the twentieth century in the research it cites.

The page separates older `canaule/canauliers` vocabulary from the later modern pastry rather than assuming identity.

It reports:

- a 1937 written milestone associated with Jean Balde;
- the 1985 choice of `canelé` with one `n` by the Confrérie;
- a collective-mark deposit.

The exact underlying 1937 page, 1995 research object and INPI record remain acquisition targets.

## Professional participant source — strongest current 1985 testimony

**Pâtisserie Française** / French professional pastry federation site, interview published 18 November 2020:
https://www.patisseriefrancaise.fr/website/page/actualites_regionale?__ws=YToyOntzOjQ6InBhZ2UiO2k6OTA7czo0OiJhcmVhIjtzOjk6Im1haW5fbWVudSI7fQ%3D%3D

This is stronger than a generic modern retelling because the professional source speaks from inside the Gironde pastry trade initiative. It states that:

- Daniel Antoine of Bordeaux launched the idea of promoting the departmental specialty;
- in **1985**, the Union départementale des pâtissiers de la Gironde decided to bring the recipe back into prominence by creating the Confrérie du Canelé;
- M. Marquet was named Grand Maître;
- members used a ring/logo and a cahier-des-charges style professional commitment;
- crucially, the interview uses first-person professional wording that **in 1985 they removed one `n` and filed the one-`n` name with INPI to distinguish it from the generic two-`n` spelling**.

Evidence decision:

- `1985 professional Confrérie initiative` = `STRONG A3 PARTICIPANT/PROFESSIONAL INSTITUTIONAL EVIDENCE`;
- `intent/action to file one-n CANELE at INPI` = `STRONG A3 PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY`;
- exact historical mark number, formal legal type, filing date, registration/publication date, classes and present legal status = **still `LOCATOR_HOLD` until the historical INPI/BOPI record itself is acquired**.

This source materially strengthens the legal-event narrative while preserving the distinction `participant testimony != legal register object`.

## Public-library research service

Eurêkoi / Bibliothèque de Bordeaux:
https://www.eurekoi.org/je-suis-a-la-recherche-de-renseignements-fiables-fondes-sur-des-sources-authentiques-a-propos-de-lhistoire-du-canele-de-bordeaux/

The public-library research service independently states that the Confrérie was created in 1985 and that a collective mark under the one-`n` spelling was deposited with INPI.

This independently strengthens the **existence of the reported legal event**, but still does not supply the exact legal object/number.

## Exact 24 March 1985 date — current status

Multiple independent later sources converge on **24 March 1985** as the founding date of the Confrérie, including Bordeaux-oriented/local historical pages and older reporting.

Current evidence decision:

- `24 March 1985 = Confrérie founding date` → `PROBABLE / MULTI-SOURCE SECONDARY CONVERGENCE`;
- `24 March 1985 = exact INPI filing date` → **NOT ESTABLISHED**;
- do not merge the association/founding date and the legal trademark filing date without the INPI/BOPI record.

This distinction is important because modern retellings frequently compress `foundation + spelling decision + filing` into one date.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

### Annonciades/convent invented modern canelé

`NOT ESTABLISHED / DO NOT PUBLISH AS FACT`.

The strongest regional institutional research explicitly says the attractive convent story lacks evidence.

### Wine fining created surplus yolks, therefore canelé

`PLAUSIBLE CONTEXTUAL STORY / UNRESOLVED CAUSATION`.

The existence of egg whites in wine-finishing practice does not by itself prove a direct recipe-origin chain. The causal step requires period evidence.

### Canauliers are direct makers of the modern canelé

`SEMANTIC/HISTORICAL RELATIONSHIP UNRESOLVED`.

Older lexical similarity does not prove recipe/form identity.

### 1985 Confrérie creation / professional institutionalization

`STRONG PROFESSIONAL + REGIONAL INSTITUTIONAL CORROBORATION`.

### 1985 single-n spelling / collective mark

`STRONG PARTICIPANT + MULTI-INSTITUTIONAL REPORT / LEGAL OBJECT LOCATOR_HOLD`.

The professional source now gives direct first-person support that the one-`n` name was filed with INPI, but the registry object itself remains necessary for exact legal metadata.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Nouvelle-Aquitaine's institutional history explicitly cautions that the famous convent-origin story is not supported by the archival/archaeological evidence it cites. Professional Gironde pastry testimony says that in 1985 the departmental pastry organization created the Confrérie, removed one `n` from `cannelé`, and filed the one-`n` name with INPI to distinguish the Bordeaux specialty; regional and library sources independently report the collective-mark step. The exact historical INPI record, including number and dates, is still being acquired.

Unsafe:

> Bordeaux nuns invented canelé in the eighteenth century using spare egg yolks from wine production.

Unsafe until exact legal object:

> The collective mark was filed on 24 March 1985 under INPI number X and classes Y/Z.

No exact primary record currently supports those legal fields.

## Anatomy / technique
