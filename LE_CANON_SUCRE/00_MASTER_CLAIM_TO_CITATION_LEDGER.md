## Reading rule

`evidenceClass` describes the source, not the truth of every historical claim printed on that source. For example, an official house page is normally `A3` for the fact that the house currently makes a statement; a claim by that page about an eighteenth-century invention still requires historical corroboration before it becomes quote-safe as an event claim.

Rights below concern reuse of the source object/visual, not the ability to cite a factual proposition in original editorial prose.
