# 02 — Paris-Brest

## Проверяемый вопрос

Как отделить дату велогонки от даты пирожного; кто и когда создал колесообразный Paris-Brest; почему в современных источниках встречаются `1891 / 1905 / 1909 / 1910 / 1911`?

## Краткий статус

`CONFIRMED RACE 1891 / DURAND FAMILY TRADITION ITSELF CONFLICTS 1909–1910 / LOW-GRADE LOCAL 1911 LEAD EXISTS / CONTEMPORARY CAKE RECORD REQUIRED / REJECT 1891 AS CAKE DATE`

## BnF / Gallica — race

https://gallica.bnf.fr/selections/fr/html/images/cyclisme

BnF identifies Paris–Brest–Paris as a **1891** cycling event initiated by Pierre Giffard. This closes the race date, not the pastry date.

## Pâtisserie Durand — current participant lineage: 1910

https://www.paris-brest.fr/

https://www.paris-brest.fr/notre-histoire/

Current family history states:

- Maison founded in 1907;
- Louis Durand created the wheel-form Paris-Brest in **1910**;
- the form references the bicycle wheel/race.

This is strong current participant tradition, but it is not contemporaneous 1910 evidence.

## Direct descendant interview — family line also preserves 1909

RTL interview with Stéphane Lévêque, Louis Durand's great-grandson:
https://www.rtl.fr/actu/debats-societe/stephane-descendant-du-createur-du-paris-brest-on-garde-le-meme-gateau-depuis-1909-7777480967

RTL's article dates creation to **1909**, and the direct descendant says the family has kept the same gâteau `depuis 1909`.

This is important because the 1909/1910 tension exists **inside later Durand-family testimony itself**:

- current Maison website = 1910;
- great-grandson interview preserved by RTL = 1909.

The interview is `B1` as a journalistic object containing direct participant-family testimony; it does not override the need for a contemporaneous record.

## Low-grade local chronology lead — 1911

A non-institutional local-history chronology of Maisons-Laffitte gives a **1911** entry saying that avenue de Longueil was known for pâtissier Bauget and that fellow pâtissier Durand created the Paris-Brest in 1911:

https://dt20050230.free.fr/maisons-laffitte.html

Evidence decision:

- source class = `C / DISCOVERY` only;
- `1911` is **not** promoted as a competing strong date;
- the lead is useful because it widens the period-primary acquisition window and highlights a local competitor/name (`Bauget`) that should be checked in directories/trade records;
- no claim about Bauget inventing Paris-Brest is accepted from this source.

A separate local-memory site preserves later recollections of Durand and 1910 but likewise remains non-primary:
https://www.memoire-de-maisons-laffitte.fr/historiettes/

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

- `1891` = race date, securely separated from cake creation.
- `1891` = cake date is rejected without cake evidence.
- `1905` = unreliable modern conflation at current state.
- `1909` = supported by La Poste and, importantly, by a direct great-grandson interview.
- `1910` = supported by current Durand family website, regional/local institutions and later journalism.
- `1911` = low-grade local-history discovery signal only; it widens search but does not establish an event.
- A possible reconciliation (`commission in 1909`, final creation/public launch in 1910) is plausible but **not proved** by a contemporaneous record and must not be presented as fact.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> The Paris–Brest–Paris race dates to 1891. Later Durand-family evidence itself is split on the pastry year: the current Maison website gives 1910, while a recorded interview with Louis Durand's great-grandson says the family has kept the same cake since 1909. A weak local-history chronology also preserves a 1911 date, which is useful only as a discovery signal. A contemporaneous 1909–1911 advertisement, menu, directory, trade notice or other record is required to close the chronology.

Unsafe:

> Paris-Brest was created in 1891 for the first race.

Unsafe:

> It was commissioned in 1909 and launched in 1910.

That is currently a reconciliation hypothesis, not a documented sequence.

## Хронология

- **1891** — first race, BnF.
- **1907** — current Durand family + later family profile: Maison/family establishment in Maisons-Laffitte.
- **1909** — La Poste recap + direct great-grandson RTL family testimony.
- **1910** — current Maison website + regional/local institutional tradition.
- **1911** — `C/DISCOVERY` local chronology only; search-expansion signal, not accepted event date.

## Права, архив и публикационные HOLD

Modern Durand/RTL/tourism photography remains rights-item-specific. Priority visual evidence is a real period Durand advertisement, menu, storefront photograph, trade notice or commercial directory around **1907–1912**.

`EVIDENCE_HOLD` remains on the exact cake year and on any 1909→1910 reconciliation story.
