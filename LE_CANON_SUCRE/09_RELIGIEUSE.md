# 09 — Religieuse

## Проверяемый вопрос

Каково самое раннее документированное значение `religieuse` в pâtisserie; можно ли доказать Frascati/1856; когда появляется поздняя стандартная конструкция из двух stacked choux?

## Краткий статус

`1873 PRIMARY FORM CONFIRMED / FRASCATI 1856 DISPUTED / MODERN TWO-CHOUX STANDARDIZATION UNRESOLVED`

## Historical primary evidence — Gouffé 1873

Chocolate form:
https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AGouff%C3%A9_-_Le_Livre_de_P%C3%A2tisserie%2C_1873.djvu/345

Coffee continuation/figure:
https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AGouff%C3%A9_-_Le_Livre_de_P%C3%A2tisserie%2C_1873.djvu/346

Gouffé directly describes `flan à la religieuse` using multiple choux/pains à la duchesse. The coffee construction uses six oval choux arranged in a crown and a round choux on top; the chocolate construction also uses several duchesse pieces.

This is critical evidence because it shows that `religieuse` in 1873 cannot be reduced automatically to the later familiar `large choux + small choux` silhouette.

## Modern institutional / participant claims

La Poste:
https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/pp/carnet-de-12-timbres-patisseries-francaises-lettre-verte/p/1124487

The modern institutional recap attributes creation in **1856** to Signor Frascati and explains the name through a nun's habit. No contemporaneous 1856 item has yet been acquired.

Stohrer:
https://stohrer.fr/notre-maison/

Current Maison also attributes a `religieuse à l’ancienne` to Nicolas Stohrer, creating a direct conflict with a simple Frascati-only origin narrative.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Wave 1 conclusions:

- a named `religieuse` family is directly documented in a major professional 1873 book;
- the 1873 architecture is varied/multi-piece;
- modern Frascati 1856 attribution is not yet primary-closed;
- current Stohrer participant tradition competes with Frascati attribution;
- exact moment the two-stacked-choux form becomes standard remains open.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> By 1873 Gouffé documents `religieuse` constructions made from multiple choux/duchesse pieces; the later two-choux silhouette should not be projected unchanged onto all nineteenth-century uses of the name.

Unsafe:

> The modern religieuse was invented at Café Frascati in 1856 and already had today's form.

## Хронология

- pre-1873 — Frascati/Stohrer origin claims unresolved;
- 1856 — modern La Poste Frascati attribution, `EVIDENCE_HOLD`;
- 1873 — Gouffé primary multi-choux religieuse construction;
- later 19th/20th c. — standard two-choux form emergence still to date.
