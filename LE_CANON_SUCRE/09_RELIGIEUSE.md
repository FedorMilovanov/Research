# 09 — Religieuse

## Проверяемый вопрос

Каково самое раннее документированное значение `religieuse` в pâtisserie; можно ли доказать Frascati/1856; когда появляется поздняя стандартная конструкция из двух stacked choux?

## Краткий статус

`1873 PRIMARY FORM CONFIRMED / FRASCATI 1856 HAS MULTIPLE MODERN SOURCES + DIRECT BAILLEUX-FRASCATI PROVENANCE BUT NO CONTEMPORARY RELIGIEUSE ITEM / TWO-CHOUX STANDARDIZATION UNRESOLVED`

## Historical primary evidence — Gouffé 1873

Chocolate form:
https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AGouff%C3%A9_-_Le_Livre_de_P%C3%A2tisserie%2C_1873.djvu/345

Coffee continuation/figure:
https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AGouff%C3%A9_-_Le_Livre_de_P%C3%A2tisserie%2C_1873.djvu/346

Gouffé directly describes `flan à la religieuse` using multiple choux / pains à la duchesse. The coffee construction uses six oval choux arranged in a crown plus a round choux; the chocolate construction is also multi-piece.

This is critical: `religieuse` in 1873 cannot be reduced automatically to the later familiar `large choux + small choux` silhouette.

## Frascati 1856 source map

### La Poste — modern institutional recap

https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/pp/carnet-de-12-timbres-patisseries-francaises-lettre-verte/p/1124487

Attributes creation in **1856** to Signor Frascati and explains the name through the colour/appearance of a nun's habit. This remains modern retelling, not contemporaneous 1856 proof.

### Christie's — first-edition Bailleux provenance and Frascati claim

https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-6415317

Christie's identifies the 1856 first edition of Louis Bailleux's *Le Pâtissier moderne* and states that Bailleux dedicated the work to his master **M. A. Cintract of Maison Frascati**, where Bailleux had trained. The same specialist auction description also calls Café Frascati the origin of the religieuse.

This is useful because the **Bailleux–Frascati professional connection is tied to the actual 1856 book/provenance**, but the sentence attributing the religieuse to Frascati is still a modern specialist description. No recipe/advertisement/page from Frascati in 1856 has yet been found.

### Puratos — modern form-history claim

https://www.puratos.fr/fr/ingredients-creations-patissieres/religieuse

Puratos says the original was square and cream-filled and only later evolved toward the rounded / small-on-large-choux form. This is useful as a research hypothesis because it agrees with the broader conclusion that the modern two-choux silhouette was not timeless, but it is not used as primary proof.

### Gault&Millau — modern professional synthesis

https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/la-religieuse

Gault&Millau also places a Frascati ancestor around 1856 and describes later form evolution. Again: strong professional context, not contemporaneous evidence.

## Participant conflict — Stohrer

https://stohrer.fr/notre-maison/

Current Maison attributes a `religieuse à l’ancienne` to Nicolas Stohrer, creating a direct competing participant tradition and another reason not to close Frascati/1856 merely by repetition.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Wave 2 conclusions:

- `religieuse` is directly documented in a major professional 1873 book;
- Gouffé's 1873 architecture is varied/multi-piece;
- multiple modern professional/institutional sources repeat Frascati 1856;
- Bailleux's 1856 first-edition provenance independently confirms a genuine professional link to Maison Frascati through his master M. A. Cintract;
- none of this yet produces a contemporaneous 1856 Religieuse recipe/ad/record;
- Stohrer preserves a competing old-style participant attribution;
- the first standard two-stacked-choux recipe/image remains open.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> By 1873 Gouffé directly documents multi-piece `religieuse` constructions. Modern institutional and professional sources repeatedly connect an earlier form with Café Frascati around 1856, and Bailleux's 1856 first edition independently confirms that its author trained at Maison Frascati; however, a contemporaneous 1856 Religieuse record has not yet been acquired.

Unsafe:

> Signor Frascati definitively invented the modern two-choux religieuse in 1856.

Also unsafe:

> The 1856 Frascati pastry already had today's two-stacked-choux form.

## Хронология

- pre-1856 — terminology/forms under investigation;
- **1856** — Frascati origin tradition repeated by modern institutional/professional sources; exact contemporaneous item still missing;
- **1856** — Bailleux first edition provides real Maison Frascati professional provenance through M. A. Cintract;
- **1873** — Gouffé exact primary multi-choux `religieuse`;
- later 19th/20th c. — two-choux standardization still to date.

## Myth vs Document

**DOCUMENT:** Gouffé 1873 exact pages.  
**DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL PROVENANCE:** Bailleux 1856 ↔ M. A. Cintract / Maison Frascati.  
**MODERN FRASCATI ORIGIN TRADITION:** La Poste / Christie's / Puratos / Gault&Millau.  
**PARTICIPANT CONFLICT:** Stohrer.  
**UNRESOLVED:** contemporaneous 1856 Religieuse object, earliest name and standard two-choux transition.
