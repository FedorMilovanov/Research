# 10 — Kouign-Amann

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать о Douarnenez, Yves-René Scordia и 1860; когда название/рецепт реально появляются в печати; что известно о поздней association / trademark protection?

## Краткий статус

`STRONG DOUARNENEZ TRADITION / 1860 NOT PRIMARY-CLOSED / 2001 INDEPENDENT REPORT EXPLICITLY NOTES NO INCONTESTABLE WRITTEN TRACE / BRETON INSTITUTIONAL TRANSMISSION CONFIRMED / c.1999 ASSOCIATION + INPI FILING INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED / EXACT 1999 INPI OBJECT OPEN`

## Local institutional source

Office de tourisme de Douarnenez:
https://douarnenez-tourisme.com/decouvrir/gastronomie/kouign-amann/

The official local tourism page preserves the Douarnenez narrative: Yves-René Scordia, **1860**, bread dough enriched/folded with butter and sugar, and the regional identity of the pastry.

This is strong evidence for current local tradition and cultural ownership. It is **not** a contemporaneous 1860 record.

## Breton-language institutional transmission

Bécédia / Bretagne Culture Diversité:
https://bcd.bzh/becedia/br/kouignou-ha-bleutou-breizh

The Breton-language heritage article attributes the now-named Kouign-Amann to Yves-René Scordia in Douarnenez around 1860 while also placing it inside a wider Breton cake/butter tradition.

Evidence use:

- useful independent regional/cultural transmission layer;
- valuable Breton-language terminology source;
- not a substitute for a nineteenth-century primary record.

## Independent historical-search counter-signal — Le Monde 2001

*Le Monde*, 25 July 2001:
https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/2001/07/25/le-kouign-amann-breton_4199992_1819218.html

This article is particularly important because it reports the historical work of Alain Le Berre, founder/president of the Douarnenez association defending the authentic pastry, **while explicitly stating that there was no incontestable written trace** for the Scordia-origin reconstruction then being advanced.

The article describes the origin as the result of Le Berre's historical research and places it in the **1860s**, rather than presenting a recovered contemporaneous 1860 document.

The same 2001 report also independently documents the modern protection layer. It says that **two years earlier** eighteen of Douarnenez's twenty-two pâtissiers had formed an association for the `véritable kouign-amann de Douarnenez`, and that the group **had filed the mark with INPI**. This is materially stronger than relying only on a current artisan's retrospective page for the existence of the association/protection action around 1999.

Evidence decision:

- `Scordia / Douarnenez / c.1860` remains a **strong local/professional tradition**;
- the fact that even the association's own historical research was reported in 2001 as lacking an incontestable written trace is a strong independent reason to keep the event under `EVIDENCE_HOLD`;
- association formation around **1999** and an INPI filing by that association are now **independently corroborated by near-contemporaneous national press**;
- *Le Monde* does **not** supply the exact filing date, application/registration number, classes, holder wording, publication or current legal status, so the legal-object `LOCATOR_HOLD` remains;
- this does **not** prove Scordia was not the creator; it proves the currently located documentary chain does not justify wording such as `archival records prove invention in exactly 1860`.

## Independent counter-signal — regional inventory

L'Observatoire des Produits du Terroir:
https://www.lopt.org/carte/cornouaille/specialites/kouign-amann-de-douarnenez

This source notes another documentary problem: a substantial Curnonsky/de Croze regional inventory does not mention the specialty. Absence is not proof of non-existence, but it is relevant negative coverage against treating the 1860 narrative as already fully documented.

## 1999 association / trademark layer — existence corroborated, exact legal object still open

A current Douarnenez artisan member of the association, Pascal Jaïn, preserves a detailed participant history:
https://www.gateaux-bretons-douarnenez.bzh/veritable-kouign-amann-douarnenez-jain-yves-rene-scordia/

The page states:

- **November 1999** — 17 Douarnenez bakers/pastry makers grouped to create an association;
- the group's purpose included protecting and promoting the Douarnenez identity;
- `Véritable Kouign-Amann de Douarnenez®` was reportedly filed at INPI on **15 December 1999**.

The exact participant counts/date wording does not perfectly match the independent 2001 article: *Le Monde* reports eighteen of twenty-two pâtissiers and only relative timing (`two years earlier`), while the current artisan source gives November 1999 / 17 members and the exact **15 December 1999** filing date. Preserve that distinction rather than harmonizing it silently.

Current decision:

- association/protection activity around **1999** plus the existence of an INPI filing = `INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED` through *Le Monde* 2001;
- exact **15 December 1999** filing date = `PARTICIPANT LEAD` until the register object is acquired;
- exact trademark owner, filing/application number, classes, publication, registration/current status = `LOCATOR_HOLD` until an INPI object is acquired;
- do not infer an IGP/AOP or other protected-origin status merely from the trademark narrative.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Keep four layers separate:

1. `LOCAL TRADITION`: Scordia / Douarnenez / c.1860;
2. `DOCUMENTARY CHAIN`: earliest printed mention/recipe — unresolved;
3. `INDEPENDENT AUDIT SIGNAL`: 2001 reporting explicitly says the Scordia reconstruction lacked an incontestable written trace;
4. `MODERN PROTECTION`: association around 1999 + INPI filing existence independently corroborated in 2001; exact register record and exact 15 Dec 1999 date still to acquire.

The correct response to incomplete early evidence is neither to promote the legend nor to reject the Douarnenez tradition outright.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Douarnenez's official and regional heritage sources preserve the attribution of Kouign-Amann to Yves-René Scordia around 1860. However, independent reporting on the Douarnenez association's own historical research noted the absence of an incontestable written trace, so the exact 1860 creation event remains unclosed in the current corpus.

Safe for the modern protection layer:

> By 2001, independent national reporting described a Douarnenez artisans' association formed around 1999 and stated that it had filed the `véritable kouign-amann de Douarnenez` mark with INPI; the exact register object and legal particulars still require direct acquisition.

Unsafe:

> Archival records prove Scordia invented Kouign-Amann in exactly 1860.

Unsafe until exact INPI acquisition:

> The trademark was legally registered on 15 December 1999 under a confirmed number/classes/status.

## Хронология

- **c.1860** — strong Douarnenez/Scordia tradition, not contemporaneous proof;
- earliest printed name/recipe — `UNRESOLVED`;
- **c.1999** — association formation and existence of an INPI filing independently corroborated by *Le Monde* in 2001;
- **November 1999 / 15 December 1999** — more precise association / INPI filing dates reported by a current artisan participant; exact legal object still open;
- **2001** — *Le Monde* documents both the association's protection activity and its historical-research effort, while explicitly noting the lack of an incontestable written trace for the origin reconstruction.

## Anatomy / technique

Current local/professional descriptions support the technique family:

`bread/yeasted dough + high butter + sugar + repeated folding/lamination + caramelizing bake`.

Do not project today's standardized ratios or folding instructions backward to 1860 without period evidence.

## Territory / language

Breton terminology:

- `kouign` — cake;
- `amann` — butter;
- Breton-language institutional material is now included through Bécédia.

Search historical corpora under:

- `kouign-amann`;
- `kouign amann`;
- `kouignamann`;
- OCR variants;
- `Douarnenez + gâteau + beurre + sucre`;
- Scordia / Crozon / bakery-address variants.

## Myth vs Document

**LOCAL TRADITION:** Scordia / Douarnenez / c.1860.  
**INDEPENDENT AUDIT:** 2001 reporting says no incontestable written trace had been found for the reconstruction.  
**NEGATIVE COVERAGE:** omission from later regional inventory is a counter-signal, not absence proof.  
**MODERN PROTECTION:** c.1999 association + INPI filing existence now independently corroborated; exact 15 Dec 1999 register details remain open.  
**UNRESOLVED:** earliest printed name/recipe and first directly inspectable source tying Scordia to the pastry.

## Права, архив и публикационные HOLD

Modern tourism, Bécédia, press and artisan photography remain item-rights-specific. Better authentic visual targets:

- nineteenth/early-twentieth-century Douarnenez bakery directory/ad;
- Scordia/Crozon civil or trade record;
- earliest printed `kouign-amann` recipe/name;
- exact 1999 INPI trademark record / bulletin;
- association founding document if publicly reusable.

`EVIDENCE_HOLD` remains on the exact 1860 invention event. `LOCATOR_HOLD` remains on the exact 1999 INPI object and exact legal particulars, but **not** on the bounded fact that an association protection/INPI filing action existed around 1999.

4. Identify the earliest printed recipe/name with recognizably laminated butter-sugar construction.
5. Keep `c.1860 local tradition` and `earliest documentary witness` as separate timeline nodes until a primary object closes the gap.
