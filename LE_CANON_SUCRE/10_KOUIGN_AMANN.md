# 10 — Kouign-Amann

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что можно доказать о Douarnenez, Yves-René Scordia и 1860; когда название/рецепт реально появляются в печати; что известно о поздней охране/association?

## Краткий статус

`STRONG LOCAL TRADITION / 1860 NOT PRIMARY-CLOSED / EARLY PRINT CHAIN OPEN`

## Local institutional source

Office de tourisme de Douarnenez:
https://douarnenez-tourisme.com/decouvrir/gastronomie/kouign-amann/

The official local tourism page preserves the Douarnenez narrative: Yves-René Scordia, **1860**, bread dough enriched/folded with butter and sugar, and the regional identity of the pastry.

This is strong evidence for current local tradition and cultural ownership. It is not a contemporaneous 1860 record.

## Independent counter-signal

L'Observatoire des Produits du Terroir:
https://www.lopt.org/carte/cornouaille/specialites/kouign-amann-de-douarnenez

This source notes a significant documentary problem: an extensive Curnonsky/de Croze regional inventory does not mention the specialty. Absence is not proof that the pastry did not exist, but it weakens any claim that the 1860 story is already fully documented.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Wave 1 keeps three layers separate:

1. `LOCAL TRADITION`: Scordia / Douarnenez / 1860;
2. `DOCUMENTARY CHAIN`: earliest printed mention/recipe — unresolved;
3. `MODERN PROTECTION`: association/mark history — exact registration still to acquire.

The correct response to missing early evidence is not to reject the local tradition outright, but to label it honestly.

Current local description supports the technique family:

`bread/yeasted dough + high butter + sugar + repeated folding/lamination + caramelizing bake`.

Future technical dossier should compare traditional ratios/methods only through open professional or participant sources; do not invent a single immutable recipe.

## Territory / language

Breton terminology:

- `kouign` — cake;
- `amann` — butter.

Search regional press and archives under both hyphenated and non-hyphenated forms, plus OCR variants.
