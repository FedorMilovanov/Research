## Wave 2 controlling findings

### Confirmed / materially strengthened documentary anchors

- Jules Gouffé, *Le Livre de Pâtisserie* (1873), directly documents recognizable Saint-Honoré, Religieuse, Éclair, baba and savarin forms with exact page locators already stored in the dossiers/ledger.
- **Éclair now has an earlier French documentary target than 1856.** Specialist historical-language research locates Charles Paul de Kock's 1848 *L’Atelier de demoiselles ou l’apothicaire de Pontoise*, printed p. 4 col. 1, to an explicit cream-filled `éclairs` pastry description. Hachette BnF independently confirms the original edition date **1848** and that the work belongs to the BnF-digitized preservation corpus. The underlying BnF/Gallica page itself still has to be directly acquired; therefore 1848 is the earliest **current locator**, not an absolute first-use or invention claim.
- Louis Bailleux's *Le Pâtissier moderne* (1856) is identified as a full historical professional object; specialist historical research tightly locates `Éclairs` to printed p. 74. The direct page scan still needs inspection before quote-safe promotion.
- The University of Michigan archive identifies the 1861 New York *Vanity Fair* volume as a public-domain U.S. periodical object; specialist research tightly locates an `éclairs` occurrence to 2 February 1861, p. 50 col. 1. Exact page inspection remains open.
- BnF/Gallica documents the Paris–Brest–Paris race in 1891. Current Durand family/regional sources date Louis Durand's cake to 1910, while La Poste gives 1909 and a direct RTL interview with Louis Durand's great-grandson also says the family has kept the same cake since 1909. The 1909/1910 conflict therefore exists even inside later family evidence and requires a contemporaneous record.
- Current Dalloyau material consistently attributes the canonical modern Opéra to the Maison in 1955. Wave 2 resolves the exact primary route for the older name lead to *Le Gaulois*, 18 March 1899, p. 3: `https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k5305801/f3.image.langFR`. Route resolved is not content verified; identity with Dalloyau 1955 is not assumed.
- Comité Colbert and Pierre Hermé Japan independently preserve a 1997 Ispahan anchor; earlier `Paradis` / rose genealogy remains separate.
- **2000 Feuilles naming context is materially strengthened.** Pierre Hermé participant material documents current composition/signature status, and a Gault&Millau article built around Hermé's direct recollections says that on the eve of 2000 he answered millennium-cake questions with a reworked mille-feuille, renamed `2000 Feuilles`. The millennium connection is therefore no longer merely an inference from the number. Exact first sale/catalogue date remains open. A first-person Hermé author-text additionally points to an earlier Ladurée `Millefeuille praliné` precursor, but the currently searchable copy is not on an authorized publisher surface, so that specific Ladurée sequence remains an acquisition lead until a legitimate edition/excerpt is obtained. Pierre Hermé's own site independently confirms the 2025 Plon book identity and authorship.
- Mont-Blanc now has a stronger primary chain. Artusi 1891 documents a close chestnut-strand + whipped-cream construction. Fannie Merritt Farmer's **1896 first-edition full object** is directly verified in Internet Archive (`bostoncookingsc00collgoog`, `ark:/13960/t0ns15r2w`), and printed p. **357** is directly located to a recipe headed `Mont Blanc`. This closes the bounded claim that a named Mont Blanc existed before Angelina's 1903 founding. OCR is not quote-safe by itself; page-image verification remains the final quotation/facsimile step.
- Escoffier 1903 independently gives French professional `Mont-Blanc aux marrons` continuity.
- Région Nouvelle-Aquitaine rejects the convent-origin story of the modern canelé as established history. The 1985 professional institutionalization is now independently strengthened by French pastry-industry material describing the Gironde pâtissiers' decision to create the Confrérie and identifying its early Grand Maître. Région and Bibliothèque de Bordeaux/Eurêkoi independently report the one-`n` spelling and collective-mark deposit. The **exact 1985 INPI mark object remains open** and must not be replaced by repeated summaries.
- For Tatin, Wave 2 resolves the exact primary route to *Le Journal*, 18 December 1899, p. 1: `https://www.retronews.fr/journal/le-journal/18-decembre-1899/129/238489/1`. Direct page content still needs inspection before the occurrence can be promoted to quote-safe primary evidence. The accident narrative remains `LEGEND / LOCAL TRADITION`.
- Additional Tatin primary routes are identified for the 1923 transmission layer: Gallica `Le Livret d'or ... Salon d'automne` and *Comœdia*, 29 December 1923, p. 4; exact relevant text still needs direct inspection.
- Tarte Tropézienne chronology remains split: current Maison creation tradition = 1955; independently documented Saint-Tropez filming of *Et Dieu… créa la femme* = 3 May–7 July 1956. Therefore `Bardot named it in 1955 during filming` remains rejected as current wording.

## Evidence boundary

`A3` participant/institutional pages prove what that participant/institution currently states; they do not automatically prove an earlier historical event.

`B1` can triangulate and discover exact locators but cannot be the sole quote-safe support for a disputed claim.

An exact archive URL is materially stronger than a vague citation, but **route resolved != content verified**. Gallica/RetroNews routes whose bytes/context were not directly inspected remain under `LOCATOR_HOLD`/content-verification hold.

An authored copyrighted text found on an unauthorized user-upload surface may identify a valuable acquisition target, but it is not promoted to quote-safe evidence merely because the wording is searchable. Obtain the authorized book/publisher/library object first.

OCR inside a verified historical full object can establish navigation and bounded content identity, but repository policy still requires page/context verification before a direct quotation is marked quote-safe.
