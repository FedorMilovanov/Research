# LE CANON SUCRÉ — master research plan

**Status:** `ACTIVE / WAVE 1`  
**Authority:** `00_CURRENT_AUTHORITY.md`

## Research question

For each candidate, establish separately:

1. earliest known name/use;
2. earliest surviving recipe or professional description;
3. creator/house attribution and its evidentiary basis;
4. evolution of architecture and technique;
5. historical geography and transmission;
6. later author/house reinterpretations;
7. myths, participant traditions and conflicting chronologies;
8. modern canonical recognition;
9. visual-document candidates and item-level rights;
10. exact wording that can or cannot be transferred to Product.

The method is adversarial: do not search only for confirmation. Every attractive origin story must receive a falsification pass.

### Independent secondary

- Le Monde;

Secondary sources are used to discover primary objects, resolve conflicts and describe reception. They do not substitute for primary proof of disputed origins.

## International archive pass

Wave 1 explicitly includes non-French discovery where it adds evidence rather than volume:

- United States: Library of Congress, NYPL Buttolph Menu Collection, Internet Archive / institutional scans;
- Japan: official Japanese maison archives, National Diet Library discovery targets, Japanese professional pâtisserie press;
- Italy: `Monte Bianco` / chestnut-dessert lineage;
- Germany/Austria: historical pastry and confectionery books, baba/kouglof/Mont-Blanc parallels;
- Poland: Alexandre Micka biographical trail and Polish pastry context;
- Breton-language/regional corpora for Kouign-Amann;
- Europeana/HathiTrust/WorldCat only as locators when full objects are not verified.

## Wave 1 qualified-source targets

Per dossier: aim for 8–15 qualified records over successive waves, preferably including:

- 1–3 historical full-object records;
- 1 institutional/archive source;
- 1 participant source where applicable;
- 2 independent secondary corroborators when a conflict exists.

No numerical quota can convert a weak source into `A` class.

## Full-object protocol

For a historical book/newspaper/menu:

- record title, author, edition, year, publisher/institution;
- stable item URL;
- exact page/scan leaf/column when available;
- distinguish OCR from verified image/page;
- preserve original language;
- add Russian translation only as editorial translation;
- record rights separately;
- do not promote a snippet, catalogue card or search-result fragment as full evidence.

## Visual protocol

Candidate types:

- historical cookbook pages;
- menus;
- newspaper advertisements;
- shop façades;
- packaging;
- creator/house portraits;
- pastry photographs;
- mark/trademark records;
- engravings/drawings.

Every visual row needs object identity, institution, date, creator if known, stable URL, rights statement, required credit and publication decision. `downloaded` is never equivalent to `PUBLICATION_ELIGIBLE`.
