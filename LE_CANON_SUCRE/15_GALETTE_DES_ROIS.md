# 15 — Galette des Rois

## Проверяемый вопрос

Как документировать французскую Epiphany/galette/fève традицию без ложной прямой генеалогии от Saturnalia; когда фиксируются современные формы и что именно представляет Élysée ritual?

## Краткий статус

`HISTORICAL GALETTE+FÈVE PRINT WITNESS AVAILABLE / MODERN ELYSEE RITUAL PRIMARY-CLOSED / ANCIENT CONTINUITY UNRESOLVED`

## Historical printed witness

*Le Pâtissier Royal Belge illustré*, chapter with Galette des Rois:
https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_P%C3%A2tissier_Royal_Belge_illustr%C3%A9/Chapitre_09

The scanned/transcribed chapter gives an explicit `Galette des Rois` preparation and places a **fève** in the pastry. Before placing this on the master chronology, the exact edition/year and scan-page locator should be pinned from the volume metadata rather than inferred from the chapter alone.

## Primary government sources — modern ritual

Élysée, 2019:
https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron/2019/01/11/reception-maitres-boulangers-epiphanie-galette

This presidential record states that the reception of master bakers for Epiphany is a tradition going back to **1975**.

Élysée, 2026:
https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron/2026/01/05/remise-de-la-galette-de-lepiphanie-1

This current primary government record explicitly identifies the republican Élysée galette as having **no fève**.

These are excellent sources for a modern ritual chapter. They do not establish ancient pastry genealogy.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

The dossier must keep separate:

1. ancient/medieval bean and king customs;
2. Christian Epiphany ritual;
3. French regional pastry forms;
4. northern puff-pastry/frangipane galette;
5. southern `brioche/gâteau des rois` traditions;
6. modern republican Élysée adaptation.

A resemblance between ancient lots/beans and later fève customs is not by itself a continuous documentary genealogy.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> Galette des Rois and the fève are documented together in historical pastry literature, while the Élysée directly documents a modern republican ritual: reception of bakers since 1975 and a galette without a fève.

Unsafe:

> Modern French galette des rois descends directly and continuously from Roman Saturnalia.

That continuity claim remains `EVIDENCE_HOLD` until a serious historical chain is assembled.
