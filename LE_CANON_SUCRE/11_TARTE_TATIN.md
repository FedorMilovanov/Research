# 11 — Tarte Tatin

## Проверяемый вопрос

Что документировано о сёстрах Tatin, гостинице и раннем названии; какие даты являются источниковыми; является ли знаменитая история случайного переворачивания фактом?

## Краткий статус

`LOCAL TRADITION STRONG / ACCIDENT STORY LEGEND / PRIMARY 1899–1926 ACQUISITION PRIORITY`

## Local institutional source

Mairie de Lamotte-Beuvron:
https://www.lamotte-beuvron.fr/ma-ville/lamotte-beuvron-ville-imperiale/ville-imperiale/berceau-de-la-tarte-tatin/

The municipality preserves the sisters/hotel/local-origin narrative and the later diffusion story. This is an important source for **local tradition**, but the municipality's retelling of the accident is not a contemporaneous witness to the event.

## Independent documentary synthesis

Académie du Goût:
https://www.academiedugout.fr/articles/la-mysterieuse-histoire-de-la-tarte-tatin_3268

This article is valuable mainly because it points to concrete historical nodes that can be acquired:

- a 1903 Lucien Jullemier account;
- a handwritten recipe associated with Marie Souchon;
- Paul Besnard, `La Tarte des Demoiselles Tatin`, published in 1921;
- Curnonsky's 1926 diffusion milestone.

A separate Wave 1 discovery lead points to an **1899** newspaper occurrence of `tarte de Mlle Tatin`; the primary issue/page is not yet in the corpus.

## Required tripartite model

### DOCUMENTED / document-targeted

- named Tatin dessert appears in early printed sources, with 1899/1903/1921/1926 targets;
- sisters/hotel context can be checked through local records;
- Besnard and Curnonsky are concrete publication targets.

### LOCAL TRADITION

- Lamotte-Beuvron origin identity;
- sisters as central figures;
- hotel story.

### LEGEND

- accidental inversion / forgotten pastry / rescue-by-flipping narrative, unless a contemporaneous account proves it.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Do not use absence of an accident record to claim the accident certainly never happened. The correct status is `LEGEND / UNVERIFIED`.

Conversely, repetition by tourism, cookbooks and popular articles does not convert the accident story into an A-class fact.

## Анализ и границы допустимой формулировки

Safe:

> The Tatin tradition is anchored in Lamotte-Beuvron and the Tatin sisters, while the famous accident story is a later legend unless a contemporaneous witness is found. Early printed milestones around 1899–1926 are being resolved individually.

Unsafe:

> Stéphanie Tatin accidentally dropped/forgot the tart, flipped it, and thereby invented tarte Tatin.
