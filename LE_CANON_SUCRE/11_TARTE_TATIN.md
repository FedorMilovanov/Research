## Jullemier — critical event-date vs publication-date correction

Académie du Goût:
https://www.academiedugout.fr/articles/la-mysterieuse-histoire-de-la-tarte-tatin_3268

The article says Lucien Jullemier writes in one of his *Contes de Sologne* **about a meal at Hôtel Tatin around 1903**, including a statement that Fanny was the creator of the famous apple tart.

This wording must not be transformed into `published in 1903`.

Bibliographic sources for Lucien Jullemier list *Contes de Sologne* as a **1915** publication, Paris, Imprimerie générale La Hure. An antiquarian auction record independently identifies an original Jullemier *Contes de Sologne* object, while regional biographical material also dates the book to 1915.

Current evidence decision:

- `c.1903` = date of the **described meal/event**, according to later historical synthesis;
- `1915` = current bibliographic date for **publication of Contes de Sologne**;
- exact 1915 book object/page containing the Tatin passage = still `LOCATOR_HOLD`;
- therefore the Jullemier text is **not a 1903 contemporaneous publication** even if its narrative describes a c.1903 experience.

This correction matters because a 1915 retrospective account has a different evidentiary relationship to the event than a publication printed in 1903.

Safe bounded wording:

> Later historical work attributes to Lucien Jullemier a recollection of a meal at Hôtel Tatin around 1903; the book *Contes de Sologne* in which the passage is said to appear is bibliographically dated to 1915, so the event date and publication date must be kept separate until the original page is acquired.

## Ответы, отрицания и альтернативные объяснения

Absence of an accident record does not prove the accident could not have happened. Correct status is `LEGEND / UNVERIFIED`.

Repetition by tourism and later cookbooks does not convert the accident story into an A-class event fact.

Identifying the 1899 archive route does not prove 18 December 1899 is the absolute first printed mention.

For 1921, identifying the exact serial and BnF holding is not the same as identifying the exact Besnard issue/page.

For Jullemier, **event chronology and publication chronology must not be merged**.
