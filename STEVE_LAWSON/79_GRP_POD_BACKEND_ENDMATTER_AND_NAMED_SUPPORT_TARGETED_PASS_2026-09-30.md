## 2. Global Register of Publishers — correct route verified, registrant still unresolved

Official GRP:
- https://grp.isbn-international.org/

The International ISBN Agency's own page states that the Global Register:

- contains publisher records supplied by national ISBN agencies in more than 200 countries;
- can be searched by **complete ISBN** or **ISBN prefix**;
- advises users who know the full ISBN but not the prefix boundary to remove digits from the right until a publisher record is returned.

Thus the correct live target is unquestionably:

- full ISBN `9798996167302`;
- then progressively shortened string toward the expected prefix `979-8-9961673`.

### Current access result
