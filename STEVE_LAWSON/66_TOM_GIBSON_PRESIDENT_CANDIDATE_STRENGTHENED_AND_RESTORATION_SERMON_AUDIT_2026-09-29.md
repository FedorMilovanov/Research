## 7. Why the FY2024 990 cannot close the title question

The FY2024 OnePassion return was filed on July 31, 2025 and still reports:

- Steve Lawson — President;
- Thomas Gibson — Secretary.

This can look superficially inconsistent with Archer's Sept. 25, 2024 statement that OnePassion already had a “current president.”

It is not necessarily inconsistent.

Form 990 Part VII reports officers/directors who served during the tax year. Lawson served as president for most of 2024 before resigning in September. A successor who held another listed office during part of the year may still appear under the role used for reporting.

Therefore:

> **The FY2024 filing cannot be used to falsify Archer's contemporaneous replacement-president statement.**

Nor can it establish Gibson's successor title.

A post-resignation corporate record, board notice, signature block, 2025/2026 title bio, or later Form 990 is still needed for direct closure.
