## 5. Stephens Valley has a formal membership process — attendance is not membership

Stephens Valley's current official `New Here?` page describes a structured process for newcomers.

Official source:
- https://stephens-valley-church-469943.churchcenter.com/pages/new-here

The page describes a three-step process. Its **Starting Point** course is a six-week class after which a participant may begin the membership process.

Thus:

> attending SVC ≠ completing Starting Point ≠ being received into membership.

This distinction is especially important because Lawson was publicly documented attending SVC from late 2024 onward and meeting with SVC pastors/elders.

### Guardrail

Even if Lawson attended Starting Point — which is not established here — that would not itself prove he became a member.

Do not infer formal reception from attendance, pastoral counseling, friendships, Communion participation, or regular presence in worship.
