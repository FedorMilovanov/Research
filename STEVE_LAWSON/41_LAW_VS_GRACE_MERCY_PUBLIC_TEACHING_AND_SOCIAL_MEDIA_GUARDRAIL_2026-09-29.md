## 6. What the 2026 diagnosis can safely mean

Assuming the Goodreads transcription accurately represents the physical pages, Lawson's phrase “more law than grace” is best read as a **retrospective diagnosis of disposition, practice and public instinct**, not as an admission that he lacked orthodox teaching on grace.

The distinction matters.

### Weak / misleading interpretation

> Lawson had never understood or taught grace before his scandal.

The pre-scandal archive contradicts that.

### Stronger interpretation

> Lawson already taught grace, mercy, love and peacemaking, yet later came to judge that his own practical and public posture had become more punitive, adversarial or retributive than the theology he was teaching required.

That is a sharper internal contrast because it does not depend on accusing him of theological ignorance.

## 7. The social-media engagement claim remains a self-report

The Goodreads reader further reports Lawson saying that provocative posts generated more likes/reposts than pastoral or encouraging material, creating an incentive toward sharper content.

This is potentially important because it provides a mechanism for the claimed imbalance:

> social-media reward structures may have reinforced a more confrontational public persona.

But the corpus does **not yet contain an independently reconstructed engagement dataset** for Lawson's historical X/Twitter account.

Targeted searches have found scattered historical Lawson posts and embedded quotations, but not enough surviving status-level metadata to establish statistically that:

- provocative posts consistently received more engagement;
- pastoral posts consistently received less engagement;
- the size of the difference was material;
- the trend changed over time.

### Publication rule

Safe:

> In the book, according to a reader transcription, Lawson says he noticed that provocative posts drew more engagement than pastoral encouragement.

Unsafe:

> Analysis of Lawson's social media proves that controversy generated more engagement.

The latter has not yet been independently demonstrated.

## 8. Strict teaching is not itself evidence of gracelessness

Lawson's strong teaching against false doctrine must not be retrospectively treated as proof of cruelty merely because he later criticized his own imbalance.

Biblical texts such as 2 John 10–11 genuinely require boundaries, and Lawson's exposition of them can be evaluated exegetically on its own terms.

The research question is not:

> Did Lawson ever speak strongly against error?

That is obvious and not inherently incriminating.

The better question is:

> Did his own broader public conduct, especially in discretionary rhetoric and social-media posture, become more punitive than the mercy, restoration, love and peacemaking he simultaneously taught?

At present Lawson's 2026 book provides the central self-accusation; the historical engagement record has not yet been quantitatively reconstructed.

## 9. Article-ready formulation

> Lawson's 2026 diagnosis should not be read as though he discovered mercy only after his fall. Months before the scandal became public, he was teaching Matthew 5:7 under the title *A Kingdom of Mercy* and Matthew 5:9 on peacemaking. In 2023, the same 2 John series moved from “Grace, Mercy, and Peace” and “Truth and Love” to the firm command to reject false teachers. His public theology therefore already contained both grace and boundaries. The sharper contradiction is practical: according to the book's current reader transcription, Lawson came to believe that his own public instincts had become more punitive than restorative and that social-media rewards favored provocation. If accurately transcribed, that is not a new doctrine of grace but a confession that the mercy he taught others was not governing his own public posture as it should have.

This preserves the force of the contradiction without falsifying Lawson's earlier theology.

## 10. What this does NOT establish

Do not state that:

- Lawson had no theology of grace before 2024;
- Lawson became gracious only after being exposed;
- every strong statement against false teachers was sinful or ungracious;
- doctrinal polemics caused the sexual misconduct;
- his social-media audience caused his fall;
- provocative posts objectively outperformed pastoral posts unless the archive is reconstructed;
- the exact 2026 wording is primary-verified before the physical pages are acquired.

The current evidence establishes a strong **teaching-versus-self-diagnosed-practice contrast**, not motive, causation or a complete social-media forensic result.
