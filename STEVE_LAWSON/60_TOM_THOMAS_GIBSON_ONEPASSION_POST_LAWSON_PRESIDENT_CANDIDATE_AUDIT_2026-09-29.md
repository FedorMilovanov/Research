## 4. OnePassion IRS record: Thomas Gibson is a longstanding officer

The OnePassion public IRS record repeatedly lists **Thomas Gibson** as an officer.

The FY2024 return includes:

- Steven Lawson — President;
- Grace Ann Bills — Vice President;
- Ty Miller — Treasurer;
- **Thomas Gibson — Secretary**;
- directors including Anne Lawson, Kent Stainback and Bill Dunlap.

Primary/current IRS-data carrier:
- https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/453788191

Historical public return indexes likewise show Thomas Gibson as Secretary across multiple earlier OnePassion filings, demonstrating long institutional continuity rather than a one-year name match.

### Critical timing guardrail

Form 990 Part VII lists persons who served during **any part of the tax year**. The FY2024 return therefore cannot tell us whether Gibson's title changed from Secretary to President after Lawson's Sept. 2024 resignation.

Thus:

> `FY2024: Thomas Gibson = Secretary` does not contradict a possible late-2024 succession to President, but it also does not prove it.
