# Steven J. Lawson — Jeffress 2026 post-fall identity corroboration of the four-condition temptation framework

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** `ATTRIBUTION materially strengthened / JEFFRESS NOW POST-FALL CORROBORATES SOURCE IDENTITY / ORIGINAL LAWSON OBJECT STILL NOT ACQUIRED`

## 1. Executive finding

The existing canonical file `40_JEFFRESS_PRE_FALL_LAWSON_TEMPTATION_VULNERABILITY_WITNESS_2026-09-29.md` established that Robert Jeffress publicly attributed to **Steve Lawson** a four-condition warning about temptation:

1. after great success;
2. when tired;
3. when alone;
4. while waiting on God.

That attribution was already valuable because Pathway to Victory's Sept. 9, 2021 devotional explicitly names **Steve Lawson** and says the material is excerpted from Jeffress teaching dating to **2010**.

A new 2026 Jeffress object materially strengthens the identity link after Lawson's scandal.

In a 2026 broadcast of **The Temptation Equation Revisited — Part 2**, Jeffress gives the same four-condition framework and then says the pastor-friend from whom he got it had been **“in the news in the last year”** after falling into **sexual immorality** and having to give up his ministry.

Source:
- https://www.truthnetwork.com/show/pathway-to-victory-dr-robert-jeffress/109292/

This new statement is important because it retrospectively corroborates that the earlier named `Steve Lawson` attribution was not an accidental or unrelated Lawson reference.

## 2. The 2026 broadcast

Truth Network preserves the transcript of Jeffress's 2026 Pathway to Victory broadcast.

Jeffress again teaches that Christians are especially vulnerable:

- after success;
- when tired;
- when alone;
- while waiting on God.

After presenting the four points, he says, in substance, that he got them from a pastor friend whom he will not name, but that this man:

- had been in the news during the prior year;
- had fallen into temptation himself;
- had to leave ministry because of sexual immorality;
- had known, written about and preached the very warnings he later violated.

The transcript's especially probative lines are:

> “I got these four times we were most vulnerable to sin from a friend of mine. Another pastor.”

and then:

> “I'm not going to say his name. But he was in the news in the last year ... for having fallen into temptation himself ... because of sexual immorality.”

Jeffress adds that the man had **written and preached** about these dangers and nevertheless fell.

Source:
- https://www.truthnetwork.com/show/pathway-to-victory-dr-robert-jeffress/109292/

Taken together, the most natural and highly supported identification is:

> **The unnamed fallen pastor in Jeffress's 2026 recollection is Steve Lawson, the same friend explicitly named as the source in Jeffress's 2021 devotional.**

This is stronger than the earlier state because Jeffress's post-fall description now fits Lawson's public 2024 removal and confirms the irony himself: the source of the warning later fell in sexual sin.
