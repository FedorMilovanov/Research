This is directly relevant today: `restored to ministry` is a headline-level interpretation, not a precise equivalent of `restored to office of presbyter`.

The phrase is **attributable to the reported elders' text**, and the disagreement is an evaluative reaction, not evidence of Anne's wishes or private circumstances.

**Motive alternatives:** A recommendation might have been addressed to limited church or organizational recipients for discretionary reference, rather than public broadcasting. Or leaders may have avoided controversy. Neither hypothesis is established. No promise that they intentionally deceived, suppressed exonerating evidence, or secretly installed Lawson is warranted.

**15-month reading:** Possible inclusive calendar counting (Sep 2024–Nov 2025) means one must not claim the letter reveals the church knew of Lawson's downfall before disclosure.
