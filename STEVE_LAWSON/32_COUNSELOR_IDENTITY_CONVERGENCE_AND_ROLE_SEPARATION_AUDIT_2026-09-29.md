## 1. Why this file exists

Several public sources describe Steven Lawson's post-September-2024 care using similar but non-identical language. Because the same Nashville church environment appears repeatedly, it is tempting to collapse all unnamed people into one group.

That would be methodologically unsound.

This file preserves the distinct role sets and records only bridges that are actually sourced.

### Evidence consequence

Bachmann is no longer merely a candidate by office or a reporter-facing spokesman.

> **JIM BACHMANN = CONFIRMED DIRECT MEMBER OF LAWSON'S RECURRING SVC PASTORAL-COUNSELING CIRCLE.**

The phrase “other Stephens Valley elders” also establishes a **plural unnamed SVC elder counseling circle**.

Thus:

> **Bachmann's counseling role is confirmed; Bachmann's identity as one of the “two professional counselors” is not.**

This distinction is now canonical.
