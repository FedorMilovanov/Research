## 1. Why this file changed

The original 27 Sep pass correctly documented a real November 2024 dispute but stopped too early. At that time the corpus had:

- *With All Wisdom* (WAW) saying Lawson was not pastor, elder, or formal member and reporting that Trinity was not managing a Matthew 18 process;
- G3 Ministries saying Steve and his wife were members, based on two sources inside Trinity;
- the archived Trinity `Leadership` page proving he was `Lead Preacher`, outside the elders list;
- no integrated later statement from a Trinity church-wide meeting.

A 29 Sep control pass located/reevaluated a later **Dec 11, 2024 Trinity church-wide fellowship/update meeting**, preserved through multiple carriers. That primary-event record materially changes the evidence status for formal membership and for what the elders themselves said about discipline.

The November dispute remains historically important. It is no longer the final state of evidence.

The idea that Lawson may not have been a member was still reported as what “some have said”; the hosts also described unsuccessful attempts to get clear answers from Trinity.

After speaking with a church staff member, WAW stated more strongly that Lawson was not pastor/elder/member. Their own primary audio contains the substance of this claim and their report that the staff member was reluctant/waffled on the membership question.

### Nov 14 written clarification

WAW specified that it meant **formal membership**: not merely attending or calling Trinity a home church, but completing a membership process and appearing on the rolls.

### Evidence limit

WAW relied on unnamed staff/member conversations and published no membership record. Their conclusion about membership was an inference from those conversations, not a church document.

This lane remains useful as evidence of the contemporary confusion and of weaknesses in Trinity's public communication. It is no longer the best evidence for Lawson's membership status.

## 6. Discipline — what the elders themselves claimed

The same Dec 11 meeting materially changes the old “no Trinity discipline” framing.

According to the carriers, the elders explicitly objected to the suggestion that Trinity had not disciplined Lawson. They described actions including:

- immediate removal from ministry duties;
- cessation of financial compensation;
- public exposure/announcement;
- treatment of him as permanently disqualified from ministry;
- expectation of repentance and attention to the wrong done to wife/family.

The elders' own position was therefore: **discipline had occurred**.

### What this does not settle

It does not automatically settle:

- whether their actions exhausted every Matthew 18 / 1 Timothy 5 requirement;
- whether a later formal excommunication/member-removal process occurred;
- which church/elders had continuing jurisdiction after Lawson moved;
- how Trinity's process interacted with OnePassion, TMS, counselors, or Stephens Valley;
- whether later cooperation/repentance differed from the elders' December snapshot.

Correct evidence status:

`TRINITY DISCIPLINE = ELDERS' OWN REPORTED POSITION ACQUIRED; ADEQUACY/CONTINUATION REMAINS ANALYTICAL/HISTORICAL`
