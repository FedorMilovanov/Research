## What this bulletin corpus proves

1. SVC had a substantial pastoral structure in place before Lawson's crisis.
2. King Counts was already active before the crisis and explicitly called pastor by Aug. 25, 2024.
3. Benny Collins joined the staff before the crisis and was explicitly called pastor by Aug. 18, 2024.
4. Terry Warren was a governing elder and Elder Council Clerk before the crisis.
5. By Jan.–Mar. 2025 Bachmann, Counts, and Collins were visibly active pastors while Lawson's recovery process was underway.
6. Counts was publicly leading marriage-enrichment work in March 2025.

## What it does not prove

The bulletins do not establish that:

- Counts counseled Lawson;
- Collins was one of Lawson's two pastors;
- Bachmann and Counts were the unnamed two pastors;
- Warren was assigned to Lawson's restoration;
- Warren sat on the accountability team;
- Warren was CCRMin's unnamed elder;
- any SVC officer reviewed or approved *Mercy in the Wilderness*.

Those require direct bridges.
