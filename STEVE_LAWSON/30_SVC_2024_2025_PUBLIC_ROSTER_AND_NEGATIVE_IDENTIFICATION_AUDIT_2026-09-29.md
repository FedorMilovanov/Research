# Steven J. Lawson — SVC 2024–2025 public roster / negative-identification audit

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** `PUBLIC ROSTER PARTIALLY RECONSTRUCTED / UNNAMED COUNSELORS AND PASTORS NOT IDENTIFIED / NO NAME SUBSTITUTION PERMITTED`

## 1. Purpose

This audit reconstructs the public Stephens Valley Church environment during Lawson's Nashville period while enforcing a strict rule:

> **A person being a pastor, elder, therapist, or staff member at the church is not evidence that the person counseled Steven Lawson or belonged to his accountability team.**

The objective is to prevent the research corpus from silently replacing Lawson's unnamed categories with nearby public names.

## 2. What the Lawson sources actually say

### January 23, 2025 — The Roys Report / Jim Bachmann

Jim Bachmann told The Roys Report that:

- Lawson had attended Stephens Valley Church for several months;
- Lawson was not then an SVC member;
- Lawson was not teaching, preaching, or serving in ministry there;
- Lawson had been seeing **two professional counselors** while in Nashville;
- Lawson also met regularly with **elders** for “friendship, support, advice, and prayer.”

Source:
- https://roysreport.com/steve-lawson-attends-nashville-church-pastor-believes-he-can-be-restored/

The source does **not** name either professional counselor.

It also keeps the professional counselors and the elders grammatically separate. They may or may not overlap; the article does not say.

### March 12, 2025 — Lawson's own statement

Lawson wrote that he had undergone extensive counseling for about five months and had submitted himself to:

1. weekly accountability to **two pastors**;
2. **the elders of a local congregation** who shepherded him;
3. an **accountability team** monitoring his progress and advising decisions.

Preserved statement:
- https://thechristianworldview.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Steven-Lawson-statement.pdf

Contemporaneous full-text carrier:
- https://churchleaders.com/news/507673-steven-lawson-speaks-publicly-for-the-first-time.html/2

No names are supplied for the two pastors or accountability team.
