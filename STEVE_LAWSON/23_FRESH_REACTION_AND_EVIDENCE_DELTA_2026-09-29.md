## 8. Strongest article-level inference after all Sep. 29 research

The responsible critical case is **not** that Lawson necessarily fabricated repentance, fled discipline, or refused his wife. Current evidence does not prove those claims and some evidence cuts against them.

The stronger case is an asymmetry of maturity and transparency:

1. There is credible evidence he entered structured counseling early and accepted severe ministerial consequences.
2. There is also credible evidence that, as late as March 2025, marital restoration remained a future hope.
3. The public record still does not map the final church-jurisdiction chain or current membership status.
4. No public evidence located in this pass identifies who within that accountability structure authorized/reviewed the Sep. 2026 book.
5. Yet the book now asks the wider church to receive Lawson's theological interpretation of his own fall, repentance, grace, and “wilderness.”

Thus the central question is not “Can a forgiven sinner ever speak?” but:

> **Has enough observable, accountable, relationally tested fruit matured for Lawson himself to become a public interpreter and teacher of his scandal — and who within the church that knows the facts made that judgment?**

That question preserves grace, takes repentance seriously, avoids heart-reading, and directly addresses the unusual features of the present case.
