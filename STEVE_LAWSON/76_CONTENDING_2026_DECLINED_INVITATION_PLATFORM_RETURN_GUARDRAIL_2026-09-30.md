**Snapshot:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** `NEW MATERIAL / CURRENT OFFICIAL ROSTER CHECKED / DECLINE REPORTED BY PROTESTIA SOURCES / NOT A DIRECT LAWSON STATEMENT / CORRECTS FALSE COMEBACK INFERENCE`

## 1. Executive finding

A targeted fresh-source pass located a materially useful 2026 event that was not represented in the canonical Lawson corpus.

On July 20, 2026 Steven Lawson briefly appeared on the public website for the **Contending for the Faith Expository Preaching & Teaching Conference** as a featured speaker/workshop teacher. Contemporary screenshots/reporting show him assigned sermon-preparation teaching sessions.

Within hours he was removed.

The crucial later update is not merely that the website changed. Protestia reported, citing sources close to the matter, that:

- conference organizers had invited Lawson;
- they advertised him **before receiving his acceptance**;
- the listing was made without his knowledge or consent;
- when Lawson learned of the listing, he communicated that he was **declining the invitation**;
- organizers then removed him.

## 5. Why this matters to the Lawson restoration question

Before this pass, a raw screenshot of Lawson on a 2026 conference website could be used to support a strong narrative:

> **Lawson was already attempting to return to preaching/teaching platforms before publishing the book.**

The July 20 correction materially weakens that inference.

The best evidence currently supports a narrower sequence:

1. an organizer invited him;
2. the conference prematurely advertised him;
3. according to sourced reporting, he had not consented;
4. according to the same reporting, he declined;
5. the current official conference site contains no Lawson appearance.

Thus the episode is **not reliable evidence that Lawson affirmatively sought or accepted a teaching return**.

If the sourced account is accurate, it is actually evidence of a concrete public-platform opportunity that he **did not take**.
