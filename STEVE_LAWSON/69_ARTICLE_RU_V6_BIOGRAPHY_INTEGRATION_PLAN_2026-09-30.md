## 4. Change C — add a short memoir-calibration bridge

### Best placement

After section 4 (`Главный вывод из шести причин`) and before the article enters the detailed 2024 fall chronology; alternatively immediately before the book/governance sections if flow testing shows better continuity.

### Suggested heading

`## Автобиография — источник, но не собственная проверка`

### Length

Approximately 5–8 short paragraphs, not a new major chapter.

### Core principle

*Mercy in the Wilderness* is primary evidence for:

- what Lawson now admits;
- what he remembers;
- how he interprets his own fall;
- what narrative he chooses to put into the public record.

It is not automatically independent corroboration for:

- disputed historical numbers;
- motives;
- relationship outcomes;
- other people's judgments;
- completion of reconciliation;
- adequacy of the restoration process.

### Concrete biography examples

Use at most two examples.

#### Example 1 — 1989 Billy Graham crusade

Later Lawson recollection includes roughly:

- `3,000 counselors`;
- a `50,000` crowd.

Contemporary Arkansas Baptist material located in the biography lane reports:

- `2,100+ certified counselors` before the crusade;
- average attendance `35,000+`.

Do **not** call these lies or simple contradictions.

Explain the possible denominator/time distinctions:

- trained/overseen vs certified by report date;
- peak single-night crowd vs average attendance;
- later rounding/memory.

The evidentiary lesson is enough:

> retrospective numbers require calibration against contemporaneous documents.

#### Example 2 — Dauphin Way 2003

Later compressed narrative: doctrinal/Calvinist conflict and being `put out`/`kicked out`.

Contemporary record: organized opposition, anonymous concerned-members letter, large petition, resignation of Lawson/full-time staff, then formation of Christ Fellowship around hundreds who followed.

Again, do not replace one slogan with its opposite.

Correct lesson:

> first-person summary can be substantially true in perspective while still omitting institutional complexity visible in contemporary records.

### Final bridge sentence

The subsection should end approximately with this logic:

> Therefore Lawson's 2026 book deserves serious attention precisely because it is his own testimony — but its most consequential claims should be evaluated by the same historical method used for the rest of his life: first-person testimony plus external corroboration wherever corroboration is possible.

This sets up the later accountability/book-gatekeeping discussion naturally.

## 9. V6 thesis after integration

The final article should be readable as one argument:

> **Steven Lawson spent decades building a public identity around precise biblical exposition and repeatedly taught many of the truths that could have exposed his own danger: private godliness before public usefulness, a sensitive conscience, vigilance against self-deception and temptation, mercy rather than mere severity, and responsibility toward his wife. The 2024 scandal therefore revealed not chiefly a deficit of theological information but a prolonged failure to live under known truth. His later confession and counseling process provide meaningful public evidence consistent with repentance, but the 2026 book is still principally his own interpretation of that process. Because earlier autobiographical retellings themselves require ordinary historical calibration, renewed public spiritual credibility should not rest on the subject's theological eloquence alone. It should be tested by durable observable fruit and meaningful external judgment from those actually charged with shepherding him. The public record proves that such shepherding existed; it does not yet make the finished-book gatekeeping chain transparent.**

That is the integrated V6 destination.
