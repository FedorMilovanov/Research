## Purpose

This directory consolidates the research on Steven J. Lawson's 2024 moral disqualification, the response of Trinity Bible Church of Dallas, OnePassion Ministries, The Master's Seminary / Grace Community Church figures, Lawson's March 2025 public confession, his earlier preaching on ministerial holiness/hypocrisy/conscience/self-deception, and the September 2026 publication of *Mercy in the Wilderness: How I Fell & What I Found*.

The central research question is narrower than a verdict on Lawson's conversion: **what can be established about the contradiction between his public teaching and his sustained hidden conduct, how did the ministries closest to him interpret that contradiction, and how much does his later public account actually explain?**

## Evidence semantics

This corpus follows the repository-wide policy in [`../data/repository-evidence-policy-v2.json`](../data/repository-evidence-policy-v2.json):

- `A1` — primary participant-created/legal/governmental record;
- `A2` — official transcript/report/primary institutional publication;
- `A3` — official event-specific institutional statement or decision;
- `B1` — high-quality secondary corroboration; never sole support for a disputed quote-safe claim;
- `C` — discovery/context/unverified; never quote-safe;
- `D` — excluded or unreliable.

A page being reachable is not the same thing as its content being verified. A surviving journalistic copy of a deleted social-media post is not silently upgraded into an official primary record. A public statement can be genuine without every claim inside it being independently verified.

## High-confidence core

The following is the stable core of the corpus as of 2026-09-27:

- On September 19, 2024, Trinity Bible Church of Dallas announced Lawson's indefinite removal from ministry activity after an "inappropriate relationship" with a woman was disclosed to the elders. The original institutional wording is preserved in multiple contemporaneous reports.
- OnePassion announced that Lawson had confessed an inappropriate relationship that disqualified him from ministry and that he resigned his duties. **Upgraded 2026-09-27:** an archived copy of the original statement page (capture 2024-09-20, `web.archive.org/web/20240920233142/https://onepassion.org/`) is preserved in custody as an `A3` institutional object (`_work/custody/onepassion_20240920233142.html`).
- The Master's Seminary communicated that Lawson had failed the elder qualification of being a "one-woman man," was no longer above reproach, and was permanently disqualified from ministry. The wording survives in contemporaneous reporting of the seminary communication.
- On October 20, 2024, John MacArthur addressed Lawson by name in an official Grace to You transcript. MacArthur explicitly said Lawson had occupied a position he had "no right to be in," said such behavior in leadership is fatal to a church, contrasted the hidden conduct with the "soundness of the theology," warned about a possible corrupting influence, and said he did not know how one could preach past one's conscience unless it were "completely scarred over." MacArthur also called Lawson a friend, said he did not love him less, and described his own heart as crushed for the sinner while grateful for the purification of the church.
- MacArthur did **not** in that discussion declare Lawson an unbeliever, apostate, or doctrinal false teacher. Any article that attributes such a verdict to MacArthur overstates the source.

Most of these derive from Phil Johnson statements later deleted from social media or quoted from correspondence, then preserved by journalists. The January 2025 Protestia report on a Trinity meeting additionally carries a later editor's update saying people with credible knowledge materially disputed parts of the elders' account and said Lawson was being shepherded and cooperating. The conflict is part of the evidence and must not be flattened.

## Interpretive boundary

The evidence supports a strong article about **moral hypocrisy, ministerial qualification, the inadequacy of doctrinal orthodoxy by itself, self-deception, false shepherds, the conscience, public repentance, consequences, and the difference between forgiveness and restoration to office**.

The evidence does **not** authorize us to pronounce infallibly on Lawson's regeneration, to diagnose a psychiatric condition, to identify the woman, or to convert reports and suspicions into facts.

The sharpest defensible formulation is therefore not "MacArthur proved Lawson was a false teacher." It is:

> MacArthur's own categories make the problem larger than merely asking whether Lawson's doctrine was orthodox. MacArthur explicitly insisted elsewhere that fidelity is measured in both teaching and life, and after Lawson's exposure he said sound theology had coexisted with conduct that disqualified him, could corrupt a church, and raised the frightening question of how a preacher could keep preaching past his conscience. Lawson's own earlier teaching likewise warned that church members and even shepherds can possess religious proximity and correct words without the spiritual reality they assume.
