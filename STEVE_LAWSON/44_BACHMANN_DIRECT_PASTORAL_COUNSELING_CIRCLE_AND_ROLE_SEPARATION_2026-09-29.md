## 1. Executive finding

A March 20, 2025 *Tennessean* report, preserved in a Yahoo/USA Today mirror and based on separate statements supplied by Steven Lawson and Stephens Valley Church senior pastor Jim Bachmann, materially upgrades Bachmann's role in Lawson's recovery.

Lawson's own attributed statement says he had **routinely met with Bachmann and other Stephens Valley elders to receive counseling and to discuss “a life strategy for moving forward.”**

Source:
- https://www.yahoo.com/news/prominent-texas-pastor-finds-refuge-161615366.html

This means Bachmann is no longer merely:

- the senior pastor of the church Lawson attended;
- a public commentator on Lawson's condition;
- a general member of the SVC support environment.

He is now **directly documented as part of Lawson's recurring pastoral-counseling circle**.

However, this does **not** identify Bachmann as one of the unnamed “two professional counselors” reported in January, nor does it prove that he is one of the “two pastors” Lawson named generically in his March 12 statement.

Those are distinct role questions and must remain separate.

The same report separately says Lawson:

> meets regularly with elders for “friendship, support, advice, and prayer.”

This separation is evidentially important.

The January wording distinguishes at least two descriptions:

1. **two professional counselors**;
2. **elders** providing friendship/support/advice/prayer.

The March Tennessean statement then says Bachmann and other SVC elders provided **counseling** and life-strategy discussion.

### What follows

The term “counseling” is broad enough to include pastoral counseling. It does not automatically mean licensed mental-health treatment.

Therefore:

> **Bachmann's direct counseling role is confirmed; Bachmann's identity as one of the two “professional counselors” is NOT confirmed.**

This distinction is especially necessary because King Counts is publicly known to be a licensed LMFT, while Bachmann's public role is pastoral. The current evidence does not name either man as one of the two professional counselors.
