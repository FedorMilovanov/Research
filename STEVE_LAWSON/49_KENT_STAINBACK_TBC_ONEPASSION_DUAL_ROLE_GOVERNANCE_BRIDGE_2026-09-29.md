### IRS timing guardrail

As established in `48_ONEPASSION_FY2024_FORM_990_GOVERNANCE_AND_RESIGNATION_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-29.md`, IRS rules define an officer/director/trustee who served **at any point during the tax year** as a current officer/director/trustee for Part VII.

Official IRS guidance:
- https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i990
- https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/exempt-organization-annual-reporting-requirements-reporting-compensation-in-part-vii-form-990-when-status-changes-during-year

Therefore the FY2024 return establishes Stainback's OnePassion director role **during 2024**, but not necessarily that he remained a director on Dec. 31 or in Jan. 2025.
