## 8. What this does establish about Lawson's book

If the high-confidence identity link is correct, the dedication means the book is personally rooted in Lawson's **Stephens Valley support environment**.

That is significant because it weakens any suggestion that Lawson produced the book while entirely detached from the Nashville church network.

The dedicatee is not merely an old conference acquaintance; he is very likely a longstanding leader in the congregation whose elders Lawson says shepherded him.

This finding also fits the separate CCRMin claim that people walking closely with Lawson “in the church” encouraged him to write.

### What it does NOT establish

It does **not** prove that:

- Terry Warren personally counseled Lawson;
- Terry Warren was one of the unnamed “two pastors”;
- Terry Warren was a “professional counselor” in Bachmann's sense;
- Terry Warren sat on the accountability team;
- Terry Warren was the unnamed elder who spoke to Justin Hoke/CCRMin;
- Beverly Warren counseled Anne Lawson;
- either Warren reviewed the manuscript;
- SVC corporately approved publication;
- the Warrens approved the timing, price, private imprint, or theology of the book.

Those remain separate acquisition questions.
