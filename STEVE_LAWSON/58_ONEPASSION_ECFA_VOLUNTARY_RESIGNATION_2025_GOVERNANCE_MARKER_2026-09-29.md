## 2. ECFA's own terminology matters

ECFA publishes a formal **Publication of Membership Status Policy** defining the labels used when member status changes.

Official policy:
- https://www.ecfa.org/PDF/Publication-of-Member-Status-Policy.pdf

The policy distinguishes several categories, including:

- `Voluntary resignation`;
- `Resignation while under review of compliance with Standard ...`;
- suspension while under review;
- termination for failure to provide renewal information;
- termination for failure to comply with a Standard;
- dissolution/merger categories.

ECFA defines **Voluntary resignation** as the case where an organization decided not to renew or resigned **at a time when ECFA had not initiated a review of a potential Standards violation**.

By contrast, if ECFA had already initiated a compliance review and the organization resigned, ECFA's published descriptor is:

> `Resignation while under review of compliance with Standard ...`

OnePassion is not listed under that descriptor. It is listed simply as:

> **Voluntary Resignation**.

### Evidentiary conclusion

The strongest safe inference is:

> **ECFA's published classification does not indicate that OnePassion resigned while under an ECFA compliance review.**

This is materially different from saying ECFA investigated and cleared OnePassion; the public classification does not establish that either.
