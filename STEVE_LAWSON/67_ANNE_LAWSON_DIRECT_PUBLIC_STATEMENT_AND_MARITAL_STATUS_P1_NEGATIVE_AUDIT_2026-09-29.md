## 7. Same-name court records are a serious false-positive hazard

Exact-name searches surface unrelated legal/court records involving people named:

- Anne Lawson;
- Stephen Lawson;
- Steve Lawson.

One surfaced example is an unrelated California case titled in substance:

> `Anne Lawson Vs Stephen Lawson`

from Orange County and an earlier period.

There is no reliable identity bridge connecting that record to **Steven J. Lawson the preacher and Anne Lawson** in the present corpus.

### Hard rule

Do **not** match divorce/court records to Steven J. Lawson merely because the names resemble:

- Anne Lawson;
- Stephen/Steven/Steve Lawson.

Required before using any legal record:

1. identity continuity;
2. age/location continuity;
3. spouse/family continuity;
4. preferably an independent church/family/public bridge.

Without that, same-name legal results are excluded.

This is both an accuracy and privacy requirement.
