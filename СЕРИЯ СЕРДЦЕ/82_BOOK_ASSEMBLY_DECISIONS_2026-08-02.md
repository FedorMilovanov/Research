## 3. `κατοπτριζόμενοι` decision

The lexical discussion remains a bounded excursus inside R8.

It is not:

- a separate chapter;
- a title-level thesis for the whole book;
- a reason to inflate source counts;
- permission to claim one English gloss is beyond dispute.

The note has one purpose: explain the translation range commonly represented by «взирая как в зеркало / созерцая / отражая» and show that the central claim of transformation from glory to glory does not depend on pretending the lexical debate is closed by one gloss.

Maximum size in reader manuscript: approximately one compact textual note or boxed excursus. The main chapter should continue to read naturally if the note is skipped.
