## 1. ЗАДАЧА (в): Section LVII — Ин 3:16 + 1 Ин 2:2 вербатим (стр. 117–123)

### 1.1 Метод Гилла (методологическое открытие)
Гилл НЕ разбирал «world»-стихи изолированно (как в *Exposition*). В *Cause of God and Truth* Part I он **собрал все «world»-места Иоанна** в одну SECTION и доказывал **лексически**: что עולם/κόσμος в писаниях Иоанна «is always used in a restricted and limited sense, for some only». Это более сильный метод, чем постиховой. Section LVII покрывает: Ин 1:29, 3:16, 4:42, 6:33, 6:51, 1 Ин 4:14, 1 Ин 2:2, 1 Ин 5:19.

### 1.2 Ин 3:16 — вербатим (внутри Section LVII, стр. 119)
> «When our Lord says, that *God so loved the world, that he gave his only-begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life* [John 3:16] — **by the world he cannot mean every son and daughter of Adam**; for this world is represented as the object of God's love, even of **his special love, which all men are not**: as such to and for whom God has given his only-begotten Son, which is not true of all mankind; who are brought to believe in Christ, in consequence of God's love… but all men have not faith; as such who shall **never perish**, though it is certain that some men will; and as such who shall have everlasting life, whereas some will go into everlasting punishment.»

**Ключевой нюанс (вербатим):**
> «the phrase *whosoever believeth*, does not design **a division of different persons, but a distinction of the same persons**; who, in their unconverted state, believe not, but, through the power of divine grace, are brought to believe in Christ for life and salvation.»

→ Это разделяет «верующий/неверующий» как **состояния одних и тех же избранных** (до/после благодати), а не как две группы людей — сильный аргумент против армининского прочтения.

### 1.3 1 Ин 2:2 — advocacy-аргумент (вербатим, стр. 120–121)
> «for whose sins Christ is a propitiation, their sins are atoned for and pardoned, and their persons justified from all sin, and so shall certainly be glorified; which is not true of the whole world… Besides, Christ is set forth to be a propitiation **through faith in his blood**. The benefit of his propitiatory sacrifice, is only received and enjoyed through faith… Add to this, that for whom Christ is a propitiation, **he is also an advocate**, ver. 1, but he is not an advocate for every individual in the world; yea, **there is a world he will not pray for** [John 17:9], and consequently is no propitiation for.»

### 1.4 Кульминация: талмудическая цитата (вербатим, стр. 122–123)
Гилл возводит «world = язычники» к иудейскому спору о том, получат ли язычники благо от Мессии:
> «It was a controversy agitated among the Jewish doctors, whether, when the Messiah came, **the Gentiles, the world, should have any benefit by him**; the majority was exceeding large on the negative… only some few, as old Simeon and others, knew that he should be a light to lighten the Gentiles.»

Затем — цитата из **Вавилонского Талмуда, Succa 55:2** (вербатим):
> «a saying of Rabbi Jochanan, ‹Wo, says he, **to the nations of the world, who are lost, and they know not that they are lost**; whilst the sanctuary stood, the altar atoned, or was a propitiation for them; **but now who shall be a propitiation for them?**›»

Ответ Гилла (вербатим):
> «Blessed be God, we know who is the propitiation for us, the nations of the world — one that was typified by the altar, and is greater than that, **even the Lord Jesus Christ**.»

→ «Золотая» находка для сайта: Гилл доказывает ограниченное искупление *из самого иудаизма* — Христос есть умилостивление для «народов мира», о котором скорбел Рабби Иоханан.

## 2. Вечное оправдание — верификация по первоисточнику (Body of Divinity Book II ch.5, ccel)

### 2.1 Что верифицировано вербатим (ccel doctrinal.iii.v.html)
Глава: **«Of Other Eternal And Immanent Acts in God, Particularly Adoption and Justification»** (Book II, ch.5). Первоисточник подтверждает и **обогащает** досье 08:

> «Justification… is **an act internal and eternal, taken up in the divine mind from eternity, and is an immanent, abiding one in it**.»

> «as God's will to elect, is the election of his people, **so his will to justify them, is the justification of them**; as it is an immanent act in God, it is an act of his grace towards them, is wholly without them, entirely resides in the divine mind, and lies in his estimating, accounting, and constituting them righteous, through the righteousness of his Son.»

**Scholastic-якорь (новое — метод Гилла):** Гилл цитирует **д-ра Эймса** (*Medulla Theologiae* l.1 c.28):
> «it is, as Dr. Ames expresses it, **›a sentence conceived in the divine mind, by the decree of justifying‹**.»

→ Это подтверждает досье 07 §3 (Гилл — схоласт в линии реформатской ортодоксии): он опирается на Эймса (как на Витзиуса). Для статьи — деталь метода.

**Различение active/passive justification (новое):**
> «Active justification is the act of God; it is God that justifies. Passive justification is the act of God, terminating on the conscience of a believer, commonly called a transient act, passing upon an external object.»

→ Это и есть прецизионное различие, защищающее Гилла от ярлыка «гипер»: *active* (вечная, в разуме Божьем) vs *passive* (во времени, в сознании верующего). Досье 08 формулировал это, но без первичной опоры.

### 2.2 ⚠️ Перефразирование в досье 08 (исправлено)
Досье 08 §5 цитата №1: *«not only before faith, but it is from eternity, being an immanent act in the divine mind»*. **Первоисточник читается иначе** (точнее): «it does not begin to take place in time, or at believing, but is antecedent to any act of faith… is an act internal and eternal, taken up in the divine mind from eternity». Досье 08 слегка переформулировал — теперь заменено на вербатим ccel (§4 EDIT-A).
