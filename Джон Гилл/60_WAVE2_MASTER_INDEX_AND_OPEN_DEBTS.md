## 2. Что теперь считается закрытым

### Высокая степень закрытия

1. Sandeman не является учеником/продолжателем Gill.
2. Gill признаёт широкую публикацию Евангелия и внешний call.
3. Это не равно Fullerian duty-faith и universal well-meant offer.
4. Eternal justification требует нескольких временных/онтологических уровней.
5. Body first verified edition: doctrinal 1769, practical 1770.
6. OT publication завершена в начале 1766, при title-page range 1763–65 для второй серии.
7. Eternal Sonship dissertation first printed posthumously 1773.
8. Its printed range is 2:534–564.
9. Elizabeth Gill primary printed source is located and genre-qualified.
10. Spurgeon’s relation is critical inheritance, not simple discipleship.
11. Spurgeon 1855 letter first known print is Baptist Times 1963, not original Autobiography.
12. Published six-volume Whitefield corpus does not document close Gill cooperation.

### Средняя степень закрытия

1. Gill–Whitefield direct personal contacts remain possible but unverified.
2. Carter Lane support for Kennington preaching requires church records.
3. Huntingdon breakfast report requires underlying diary/letter.
4. Gill’s exact relation to Fullerian responsibility requires concordance and sermon/application audit.
5. Patristic and rabbinic citation accuracy requires sample verification against modern editions.
