## 0. Что добыто

1. **Контекст рождения:** Гилл начал труд ~1733/34, потому что переиздавался *Discourse on the Five Points* **Daniel Whitby** — «accounted an unanswerable one»; Гилл решил ответить [Google Books preface].
2. **4-частная структура** (verbatim из предисловия): I (1735) — универсальные тексты; II (1736) — special/distinguishing grace; III (1737) — «arguments from reason»; IV (1738) — древние писатели до Августина.
3. **Вербатим Part III** — рациональные аргументы противников и ответы Гилла (искренность Евангелия, тайная/открытая воля, внешний/внутренний призыв). Это «мясо» для сбалансированного раздела о гипер-кальвинизме.
4. **Нюанс:** Гилл утверждал **внешний призыв** (external call) ко всем, но отвергал **универсальное предложение» (universal offer)** благодати — именно это место quote-mining'а.

## 1. Замысел и оппонент

- **Daniel Whitby**, *A Discourse concerning… election and reprobation… Extent of Christ's Redemption… Grace of God… Liberty of the Will… perseverance* (5 пунктов) — главный мишень [SBJT 25.1].
- Гилл видел в арминианстве/пелагианстве «the very life and soul of Popery»: «instead of lopping off the branches of Popery, the axe should be laid to the root of the tree, Arminianism and Pelagianism» [Google Books preface].
- Позже — стычка с **Джоном Уэсли** о претерпании святых (несколько книг с обеих сторон) [baptists.net/cause-of-god-and-truth-2].

## 2. Структура 4 частей (verbatim из предисловия Гилла)

- **Part I (1735):** «the several passages of Scripture made use of by Dr. Whitby and others in favour of the Universal Scheme… their arguments and objections are answered».
- **Part II (1736):** «the several passages of Scripture in favour of special and distinguishing grace… vindicated from the exceptions of the Arminian, and particularly from Dr. Whitby».
- **Part III (1737):** «a confutation of the arguments from reason used by the Arminians, and particularly by Dr. Whitby, against the above doctrines; and a vindication of such as proceed on rational accounts in favour of them»; + согласие Гоббса/стоиков с кальвинистами (со снятием клеветы); + защита возражений от предведения/промысла и «case of the Heathens».
- **Part IV (1738):** «the sense of the ancient writers of the Christian Church, before the times of Austin… that the Arminians have very little reason to triumph».

> Содержание Part IV (по Google Books): Ch 1 Of Predestination · Ch 2 Of Redemption · Ch 3 Of Original Sin · Ch 4 Of Efficacious Grace · Ch 5 Of Perseverance · Ch 6 Of The Heathens.

## 3. ВЕРБАТИМ: Part III — рациональные аргументы (золотые цитаты)

**(а) Искренность Евангелия / «offers»** — *Cause of God, Part 3, Chapter 1:4.6*:
> «And as for the non-elect, grace is neither offered to them, nor bestowed on them, and therefore there can be no falsehood or hypocrisy, dissimulation or guile… in the divine conduct towards them».
> «it should first be proved, that there are any offers of grace at all, made to any, whether elect, or non-elect. The gospel is not tendered to the elect, but is the power of God unto salvation to them. The grace of God is bestowed upon them, applied to them, and wrought in them, but not offered».
> «The ministers of the gospel are sent to preach the gospel to every creature (Mark 16:15)… not to offer, but to preach Christ… The ministers are kerukas, criers or heralds; their business is kerussein, to proclaim aloud, to publish facts, to declare things that are done, and not to offer them to be done on conditions; as when a peace is concluded and finished, the herald's business… is to proclaim the peace, and not to offer it».
> «the gospel is a declaration of salvation already wrought out by Christ, and not an offer of it on conditions to be performed by man».

**(б) Тайная vs открытая воля (reprobation/sincerity)** — *Cause of God, Of Reprobation*:
> «we do not believe, nor do the Scriptures teach us to believe, that God by his revealed will hath declared, that he would have all the individuals of mankind saved; for then all of them would be saved… Wherefore it is no contradiction to the revealed will of God… to believe, that by his secret will he has determined that some should perish».
> «the nature and will of God never contradict each other; and yet it is certain, that reprobation is according to the will of God; Whom he will, he hardeneth (Rom. 9:18, 22)».

**(в) Искренность призыва к покаянию** — *Of Reprobation*:
> «whenever God calls men to repent, he not only seems to be, but he really is serious, and in good earnest; but then the calls… in Ezekiel… respect not internal conversion, and evangelical repentance, but a national repentance, and an external reformation of manners… of which reprobates are capable».

## 4. Баланс: external call vs universal offer (ключ к спору)

- Гилл **утверждал внешний призыв** ко всем слушающим (команды к внешнему покаянию/вере), но **отвергал**, будто Бог «offers» спасение условно [sb.rfpa.org: Gill distinguished external and internal call, «The Cause of God and Truth, Section X»].
- **Эндрю Фуллер** защищал Гилла от обвинения в отрицании ответственности: Гилл «established the principle» of man's responsibility (Cause of God, Section XXX, point 3) [sb.rfpa.org].
- Вывод для статьи: Гилл — не «анти-призывник», а **противник условного универсального offer'а**; это и есть нюанс, который критики вырывали quote-mining'ом (см. досье 02/04/06).
