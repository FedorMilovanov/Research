## 1. ЗАДАЧА (б): 1 Тим 2:4 — Section XLV, вербатим из первоисточника (стр. 91–96)

**Заголовок SECTION (вербатим, archive.org chunk 45):**

> SECTION XLV.
> *Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.* — 1 Tim. ii. 4.
> «These words are often used to oppose God's decree of reprobation, and in favour of universal redemption; but with what success will be seen when it is observed…»

**Структура ответа Гилла (7 пунктов):**

**П.1 — природа «спасения», которое Бог хочет:**
> «the salvation which God here wills that all men should enjoy, **is not a mere possibility of salvation** for all, **nor putting all men into a salvable state, nor an offer of salvation to all**, nor a proposal of sufficient means of it to all in his word; but a real, certain, and actual salvation, which he has determined they shall have.»
*(Примечание: Гилл здесь опровергает именно арминианское толкование «offer» — сс. на Vorst, Curcellaeus.)*

**П.2 — воля Бога не условна и неизменна:**
> «this will of God, respecting the salvation of men, is one entire, invariable, unalterable, and unchangeable will; *He is in one mind; and who can turn him? and what his soul desireth, even that he doth.*»
*(Против арминианского различения antecedent/consequent will; ссылка Рим 9:16.)*

**П.4 — «all men» ≠ каждый индивид; есть отвержение:**
> «we are not to understand every individual of mankind… **for it is his will that some men should be damned, and that very justly, for their sins and transgressions; ungodly men, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation**» *(Иуд 4)*; «go, ye cursed, into everlasting fire» *(Мф 25:46)*; «if it was the will of God that every individual of mankind should be saved, then every one would be saved; **for who hath resisted his will?**» *(Рим 9:19)*.

**П.6 — «all» = «разные роды» (по Августину):**
> «It is better by *all men* to understand **some of all sorts, as Austin did long ago**… Christ has redeemed some of all nations… kings and peasants… rich and poor, bond and free… male and female… young and old… greater and less [sinners].»
*(СС. на Augustine, Enchirid. c.103; Kimchi.)*

**П.7 — предпочтительное толкование: «all» = язычники:**
> «I rather think that by *all men* are meant **the Gentiles**, who are sometimes called the world, the whole world, and every creature» *(Рим 11:12, 15; 1 Ин 2:2; Мк 16:15)*. Контекст (1 Тим 2:1) — против иудейского предрассудка «не молиться за язычников и языческих магистратов».

**ransom (1 Тим 2:6) — вербатим:**
> «this ransom is *ἀντίλυτρον ὑπὲρ πάντων*, **a vicarious ransom, substituted in the room and stead of all**, whereby a full price was paid for all, and a plenary satisfaction made for the sins of all, which cannot be true of every individual man, for then no man could be justly condemned.»

**Вывод SECTION XLV (вербатим, chunk 47):**
> «From the whole, since these words cannot be understood of every individual man, **they cannot be thought to militate against God's righteous decree of reprobation, nor to maintain and support universal redemption**.»

→ **Сверка с досье 02/19**: позиция Гилла в *Cause* Section XLV полностью совпадает с его экзегезой в *Exposition* (досье 19 §1.2) — но в *Cause* она развёрнута как полемический ответ Уитби пункт-за-пунктом, с постраничными сносками на Уитби.

## 2. ЗАДАЧА (б): 2 Пет 3:9 — Section LVI, вербатим из первоисточника (стр. 115–117)

**Заголовок SECTION (вербатим, archive.org chunk 56):**

> SECTION LVI.
> *The Lord is not slack concerning his promise… but is long-suffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.* — 2 Pet. iii. 9.

**Структура ответа Гилла (5 пунктов):**

**П.1 — Бог ДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНО хочет погибели некоторых:**
> «It is not true that God is not willing any one individual of the human race should perish, since **he has made and appointed the wicked for the day of evil**… ungodly men, who are **fore-ordained to this condemnation**, such as are **vessels of wrath fitted for destruction**; yea, there are some to whom God sends **strong delusions, that they may believe a lie, that they all might be damned**.»
*(СС.: Притч 16:4; Иуд 4; Рим 9:22; 2 Фес 2:11.)*

**П.2 — «any/all» ограничены местоимением «us» (κлюч текста):**
> «both the *any* and the *all* are to be limited and restrained by the *us*… **The key, therefore, to open this text lies in these words, εἰς ἡμᾶς, to us-ward, or for our sake**; for these are the persons God would not have any of them perish.»

**П.3 — кто такие «us»:**
> «they are distinguished from the scoffers… are called **beloved**… [which] is to be understood of their being beloved by God, with an everlasting and unchangeable love… God is unwilling that any of them should perish, but wills that all of them should have repentance unto life; and, therefore, **he waits to be gracious to them, and defers the second coming of Christ**.»

**П.5 — вывод (вербатим, chunk 57):**
> «these words do not furnish out any argument in favour of universal redemption, nor do they militate against absolute election and reprobation, or unfrustrable grace in conversion; but, **on the contrary, maintain and establish them**, since it appears to be the will of God, that not one of those he has chosen in Christ, given to him, and for whom he died, shall ever perish.»

→ **Сверка с досье 19 §1.3**: экзегезис *Exposition* и *Cause* Section LVI тождественны по существу («us-ward» = избранные; «any» = никого из их числа); *Cause* добавляет полемический каркас (опровержение арминианского «τις = distributive of all»).

## 3. ЗАДАЧА (а): переатрибуция «heralds»-цитат досье 12 (TODO-V1)

### 3.1 Мисатрибуция обнаружена
Досье 12 §3(а) приводит «heralds/kerukas»-цитату с атрибуцией: *«Cause of God, Part 3, Chapter 1:4.6»* (источник — calvinandcalvinism.com). Однако сама страница calvinandcalvinism.com [1] маркирует этот пассаж как:

> **«SERMON 95. AN ANSWER TO THE BIRMINGHAM DIALOGUE-WRITER»**

Т.е. цитата взята **не из** *Cause of God and Truth*, а из **Проповеди 95** («Ответ автору Бирмингемского диалога») — труда, входящего в посмертные ***Sermons and Tracts*** (3 тт. quarto, упомянуты Риппоном в *Brief Memoir*, 1838, как часть корпуса). Гилл в этой проповеди сам *ссылается вперёд* на свой *Cause of God and Truth* Part I: «as I have shown at large in my first part of The Cause of God and Truth; whither I refer the reader».

→ **Вывод:** это не «ошибка по существу» (Гилл действительно учил «proclaim, not offer»), но **цитата приписана не той книге**. «Heralds»-формулировка живёт в *Sermons* III (Sermon 95), а не в *Cause*. Досье 12 исправлено (см. §4, EDIT-A).

### 3.2 Подлинные первичные якоря доктрины «proclamation vs offer» (Level A, archive.org)
Доктрина «глашатаи провозглашают, а не предлагают» верифицирована **в двух местах самого** *Cause of God and Truth*:

**(I) Part I, Section XL — разбор 2 Кор 5:19** (archive.org chunk 40, стр. 80–81), вербатим:
> «that [the ministry of the word] is not an **offer** of reconciliation to the world; but a **proclamation or declaration of peace**, made by the blood of Jesus, of reconciliation by the death of the Son of God.»
> «nor did the apostles entreat all men to whom they preached, to be reconciled to God; the exhortation… is given not to all men, but to the believing Corinthians, for whom Christ was made sin.»

**(II) Part III, Chapter I «Of Reprobation»** (archive.org chunk 132–133, стр. 276–279) — здесь Гилл различает **will of command** (открытая воля: что должно делать) и **determining/purposing will** (тайная воля: что будет сделано):
> «they only express God's will of command, and show what is man's duty to do; and which, if done, would be grateful and well-pleasing to God, and approved of by him, but **not his will, determining what shall be done**.» *(стр. 278)*
> «the nature and will of God never contradict each other; and yet it is certain, that reprobation is according to the will of God; *Whom he will, he hardeneth*.» *(стр. 276)*

→ Это и есть подлинная база «external call ко всем / internal call к избранным» из досье 12 §4.
