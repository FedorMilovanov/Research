## 4. Current canonical synthesis

The project must preserve at least these distinct categories:

1. universal creational rebellion against known God;
2. covenant apostasy of Israel/Judah;
3. corporate/institutional apostasy;
4. personal moral fall with restoration;
5. temporary faith / rootless response;
6. external/shared covenant privilege without proven saving union;
7. doctrinal defection;
8. practical denial while profession remains;
9. progressive hardening after repeated rebuke;
10. judicial hardening;
11. final conscious repudiation after great light;
12. dangerous wandering that remains recoverable;
13. late-life falling after long fidelity;
14. late-life repentance after terrible apostasy;
15. divine preservation through appointed means.

Do not collapse them.
