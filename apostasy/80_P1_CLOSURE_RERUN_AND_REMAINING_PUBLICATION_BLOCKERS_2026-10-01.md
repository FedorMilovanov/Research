# 6. Stop rules

A remaining OT node moves from AMBER to GREEN when:

1. primary Hebrew/context checked;
2. whole-book/canonical controls mapped;
3. at least two serious conservative technical treatments found;
4. exact locator for the claim is recorded where available;
5. no final-state inference exceeds narrator’s statement;
6. practical/Puritan application is clearly separated from exegesis.

No arbitrary source-count quota.
