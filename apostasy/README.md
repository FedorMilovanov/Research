# 12. Current synthesis

> **Scripture presents apostasy, falling, wandering, temporary faith, privilege, doctrinal departure, practical denial, corporate infidelity, hardening and final rejection as related but non-identical realities. It also presents real restoration, real warnings and real divine preservation. The Reformed synthesis defended by this corpus does not neutralize warning texts: God preserves His people through living faith and appointed means, including Word, warning, mutual exhortation, repentance, discipline, prayer, Christ’s intercession and the Spirit’s work. The pastoral task is therefore neither false assurance nor premature despair, but exact biblical diagnosis, urgent warning, patient restoration where commanded, protection of the flock, and confidence in God’s preserving grace.**
