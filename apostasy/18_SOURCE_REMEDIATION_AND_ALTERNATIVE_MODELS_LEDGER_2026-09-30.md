## 1. Два разных понятия «готово»

Корпус уже достаточно силён, чтобы иметь рабочую богословскую модель. Это **не означает**, что он publication-ready.

### Doctrinal/research readiness

Вопрос считается исследовательски зрелым, когда:

- полный ближайший контекст прочитан;
- ключевые греческие/еврейские формы проверены;
- сильнейшие альтернативные чтения представлены своими источниками;
- вывод не зависит от одного спорного слова;
- текст согласован с более широким canonical corpus;
- обозначены места, где уверенность должна оставаться ограниченной.

### Publication readiness

Дополнительно требуется:

- источник соответствует глобальному evidence policy;
- прямые цитаты имеют exact locator + edition/version + context verification;
- B1 не является единственной опорой disputed quote-safe claim;
- text-critical claims не зависят от агрегатора;
- Product route имеет единственного claim owner;
- нет скрытого дублирования с соседней статьёй;
- explicit Product release witness.

До выполнения второго набора весь corpus остаётся `PUBLICATION_HOLD`.

## 4. Strongest-alternative rule

Каждая reader-facing статья должна включать **наиболее сильную форму** альтернативы, а не удобную слабую версию.

### Required representation discipline

1. **Reformed claims** — where possible use Reformed primary/confessional sources: 1689, Dort, Westminster, Calvin, Owen; do not let modern popular teachers substitute for the tradition.
2. **Lutheran claims** — use Book of Concord, not a Reformed description of Lutheranism.
3. **Arminian claims** — distinguish Arminius himself from later Wesleyan doctrine; never write “Arminius taught X” when X is primarily a later development.
4. **Wesleyan claims** — use Wesley or official Wesleyan/Methodist primary material for the actual assertion.
5. **Free Grace** — use direct GES/Hodges/Wilkin material as a representative school witness; classify it by evidence role, not as neutral academic adjudication.
6. **Roman Catholic spectrum** — use Trent/Vatican/official doctrinal texts for confessional claims; do not use these as verse-level exegesis unless the claim is specifically historical/doctrinal.
7. **Modern scholarship** — for disputed syntax/text criticism, prefer technical commentaries, monographs, journal articles, critical editions; a confessional document cannot settle a Greek syntactic dispute.

## 9. Do-not-publish formulations until remediation closes

Do **not** publish any of the following as settled:

- “Hebrews 6 is obviously about people who merely tasted and never really experienced anything.”
- “`μέτοχος` proves regeneration.”
- “Hebrews 10:29 grammatically proves Christ is the one sanctified.”
- “Hebrews 10:29 grammatically proves a regenerate apostate.”
- “2 Peter 2:1 proves universal atonement” or “cannot possibly refer to Christ’s redemptive purchase.”
- “The washed sow proves nothing inward happened” — the washing/escape language must be preserved.
- “`ἐν ἐμοί` in John 15 by itself proves saving union.”
- “Romans 11 is merely corporate, therefore the individual warning is rhetorical.”
- “Luke 8 says they never believed.”
- “Luke 8 says past momentary belief guarantees eternal life regardless of final unbelief” without explicitly identifying this as the Free Grace inference rather than the text’s undisputed conclusion.
- “Jude 5 unquestionably says Jesus” before apparatus closure.
- “Jude 21 and 24 repeat the same Greek verb.”
- “Judas did not repent because Matthew did not use `μετανοέω`.”
- categorical pronouncements that a living named person is irreversibly apostate.

## 11. Definition of corpus closure

The apostasy corpus may move from `ACTIVE RESEARCH` to a publication-candidate authority only when all are true:

1. all P0 rows above have a recorded verdict or explicitly bounded ambiguity;
2. strongest alternatives are source-complete;
3. published quotations are quote-safe under repository policy;
4. Greek/text-critical witnesses are version-locked;
5. article claim ownership is deterministic;
6. Product route plan matches current Product architecture;
7. no branch is the sole custodian of research needed for publication;
8. a final corpus authority names the exact Research snapshot;
9. Product creates an explicit release witness for the exact routes/content snapshot.

Until then, **green research progress is not publication approval**.
