### Hard cap rule

For a child deep dive, flagship should normally use:

- one paragraph to state the problem;

- one internal link.

If a subsection needs a Greek word table, it almost certainly belongs in the child page.

## 10. Shared terminology contract

All pages should use the same definitions unless a page explicitly discusses an alternative use:

- **temporary faith / временная вера** — a real but non-persevering response identified by texts such as Luke 8:13; do not equate mechanically with conscious hypocrisy.
- **grievous fall / тяжёлое падение** — severe sin/failure of a believer without proven final apostasy; Peter is canonical control.
- **apostasy / отступничество** — decisive departure from confessed truth/Christ; finality must not be pronounced prematurely about living persons.
- **common operations / общие действия Духа** — real non-regenerating operations/privileges in the classical Reformed explanatory model; mark as theological category, not a biblical technical term.
- **covenant privilege / заветная привилегия** — real participation in blessings/identity of covenant community without automatically asserting individual justification.
- **perseverance / стойкость** — continuing in faith through God-appointed means; not sinless performance.
- **preservation / сохранение** — God’s action in keeping his people; distinguish conceptually from the believer’s persevering activity without separating them.

Terminology drift between articles is a publication blocker.

## 11. Final architecture judgment

The series should not be a collection of independent essays written from scratch. It should behave as a **single distributed monograph**:

- hub = table of contents;
- flagship = thesis and whole-canon synthesis;
- child articles = footnotes expanded into rigorous exegetical chapters;
- research dossiers = evidence layer beneath those chapters;
- source registries/remediation ledger = provenance and quality-control layer.

That architecture preserves depth without forcing the reader through a 40–60k-word monolith and prevents multiple pages from competing to own the same doctrinal sentence.
