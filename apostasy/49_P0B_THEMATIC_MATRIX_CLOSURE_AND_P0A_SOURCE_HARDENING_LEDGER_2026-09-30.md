# 4. Anti-error rules now locked

1. A lexeme is evidence, not a complete doctrine.
2. A translated “backslider / return / deny / depart” cannot by itself establish former regeneration.
3. A severe warning is not automatically a narrated final-state verdict.
4. A restorative command is evidence against prematurely labelling the case terminal.
5. Corporate covenant judgment cannot be mechanically converted into individual ordo salutis.
6. God’s preservation and human exhortation are not competitors.
7. Historical theology may clarify reception and application but cannot overrule grammar/context.
8. A trusted institutional affiliation does not replace checking the named author’s argument.
9. TGC never functions as blanket confessional authority.
