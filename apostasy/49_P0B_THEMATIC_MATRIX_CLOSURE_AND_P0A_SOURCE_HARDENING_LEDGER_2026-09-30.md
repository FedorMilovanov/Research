## 3. Source hierarchy going forward

For every P0-A dossier:

### Tier 1 — text and language

- Hebrew/Greek text;
- morphology;
- textual issue if material;
- immediate literary context.

### Tier 2 — canonical controls

- direct quotation/allusion;
- same-author usage;
- explicit later biblical reuse;
- covenant/background texts.

### Tier 3 — modern technical commentary

Prefer major academic/exegetical commentary or an accessible commentary that names its bibliography and engages original languages.

### Tier 4 — historical theology

Use Calvin, Puritans, fathers, confessions and classic commentators to show reception history, not to substitute for exegesis.

## 4. Anti-error rules

1. A lexeme is evidence, not a complete doctrine.
2. A translated “backslider/return/deny” cannot by itself establish former regeneration.
3. A severe warning is not automatically a narrated final-state verdict.
4. A restorative command is evidence that the addressed case must not be prematurely labelled terminal.
5. Corporate covenant judgment cannot be mechanically converted into individual ordo salutis.
6. God’s preservation and human exhortation are not competitors; Scripture frequently joins them.
7. Final publication remains `HOLD` until P0-A source-hardening is complete.
