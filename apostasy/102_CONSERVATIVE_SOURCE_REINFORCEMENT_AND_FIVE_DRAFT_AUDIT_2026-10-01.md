# 2. Source-policy lock после пользовательского уточнения

## 2.1. Платформа не равна авторитету

Ни одна площадка не получает автоматического doctrinal trust только по бренду:

- TGC;
- GTY;
- Ligonier;
- TMS;
- Shepherds Conference;
- SBTS;
- Monergism;
- CCEL;
- издательская серия комментариев.

Каждый источник оценивается по:

1. автору;
2. конкретному claim;
3. экзегетическому аргументу;
4. владению языками/дисциплиной;
5. конфессиональным предпосылкам;
6. тому, не говорит ли он больше библейского текста.

### Следствие

> **Даже очень консервативный автор может быть сильным источником по доктрине и одновременно переоценивать конкретный narrative inference.**

Поэтому source filtering должен быть claim-by-claim.

---

## 2.2. TGC rule — ужесточён

TGC не используется как самостоятельная doctrinal authority.

Допустимые функции:

- discovery;
- author-specific accessible presentation;
- locating a named scholar’s argument;
- secondary bibliography trail.

Недопустимая логика:

> «TGC пишет X, следовательно X соответствует политике сайта».

Если TGC-страница принадлежит сильному консервативному экзегету (например, Thomas Schreiner), аргумент всё равно по возможности закрепляется:

- его собственной книгой/статьёй;
- институциональным publication host;
- техническим commentary;
- первичным текстом.

### Audit result

Ни один из пяти quotation-free drafts (`97–101`) не нуждается в TGC как evidence owner для своего reader-facing аргумента.

Это правильное состояние.

# 4. High-value new conservative controls to add to the evidence spine

## 4.1. Thomas Schreiner — prospective warnings as preserving means

### Source

Thomas R. Schreiner, **“Perseverance and Assurance”** (SBTS-hosted PDF):

https://cdn.sbts.edu/documents/tschreiner/2.1_article.pdf

### High-value contribution

Schreiner explicitly resists a reading that reduces Hebrews warnings to retrospective tests addressed only to “almost Christians.” His argument treats the warnings as:

- genuinely addressed to Christians;
- prospective;
- genuinely threatening damnation if apostasy occurs;
- means God uses so that the elect do not finally apostatize.

### Why it matters

This source is unusually useful because it allows the Reformed synthesis to retain **full rhetorical force**:

> the warning does not become stage scenery merely because God preserves His people.

### Project use

- Part I § warnings + preservation;
- Part V § exhortation as preserving means;
- Hebrews 3/6/10 synthesis;
- Jude keep/kept synthesis.

### Guardrail

Do not paraphrase Schreiner as saying the warning is “fake,” “merely hypothetical,” or addressed only to people who were never genuinely part of the Christian audience.

Status: `HIGH-VALUE / AUTHOR-PRIMARY ARTICLE / GREEN-PARAPHRASE`.

## 4.2. Schreiner/Mohler intramural Reformed difference

### Accessible institutional record

Southern Seminary summary of their Hebrews warning-passages discussion:

https://www.sbts.edu/news/written-to-christians-or-almost-christians-mohler-and-schreiner-debate-warning-passages-at-tgc21/

### Why it matters

Both men are conservative/Reformed-Baptist, yet they differ over whether the warning-address is best conceptualized as:

- Christians warned prospectively as means of perseverance (Schreiner), or
- a different audience-status formulation.

### Editorial significance

The flagship should not imply:

> “There is one obvious Calvinist exegesis of every Hebrews warning text.”

There is a common preservation doctrine with real intramural exegetical differences.

Status: `SECONDARY INSTITUTIONAL SUMMARY / USE FOR FAIRNESS, NOT TEXTUAL EXEGESIS`.

# 5. A critical example: even trusted conservative sources must not overrule the narrator

## R. C. Sproul — strong on preservation, too strong for our Demas claim

Ligonier’s publication of Sproul’s perseverance teaching is highly useful for:

- preservation grounding perseverance;
- visible-church profession vs possession;
- real seriousness of apostasy warnings;
- Christ’s intercession.

Example:

https://learn.ligonier.org/articles/more-conquerors

However, in discussing Demas, Sproul appears to assume his trajectory terminates in final destruction/apostasy.

### Project decision

We do **not** follow that specific inference.

2 Tim 4:10 states:

- Demas deserted Paul;
- motive: love of the present age;
- destination: Thessalonica.

It does **not** narrate:

- a later repentance;
- a later hardening;
- a final eternal-state verdict.

Therefore our wording remains:

> **Demas is a severe abandonment/world-love case whose final eternal state Scripture does not disclose.**

### Why this matters methodologically

This demonstrates that the source-policy is real rather than tribal:

> **we will correct overreach even when it comes from a theologian whose doctrinal framework we broadly share.**

Status: `SOURCE-CRITICISM CONTROL`.
