# 8. Three-level claim labeling is now mandatory

Every controversial conclusion must be tagged internally as one of:

### `DIRECT EXEGESIS`
What the grammar/context itself establishes.

### `CANONICAL INFERENCE`
What follows by explicit intertext / same-author / whole-canon control.

### `SYSTEMATIC SYNTHESIS`
What Reformed/Baptist theology responsibly concludes after integrating texts.

### Why

This blocks recurring errors such as:

- turning `πιστός` in 2 Tim 2:13 into the entire doctrine of perseverance;
- making `שוב` equal regeneration;
- making `ἁγιάζω` equal inward regeneration in every context;
- making “return” prove prior salvation;
- making historical judgment prove final damnation.

# 9. Stop rules against endless research drift

A node may move out of P0 when:

1. Greek/Hebrew text is verified;
2. immediate and same-book context is mapped;
3. strongest canonical control is identified;
4. at least two serious conservative technical treatments are located;
5. strongest materially different reading is represented fairly;
6. no reader-facing wording depends on an unverified quotation/page;
7. direct exegesis is visibly distinguished from systematic synthesis.

Once these are met, **do not reopen the node merely to accumulate more links**.
