# 2. 2 Timothy 2:11–13 — Mounce / Knight locator closure

## 2.1. William D. Mounce, *Pastoral Epistles*, WBC 46

### p. 501 — structure/source of the faithful saying

Independent scholarly citation verifies Mounce p. 501 as the place where he argues that 2 Tim 2:11b–13 need not be treated as a pre-Pauline fragment.

Locator support:

- Madison N. Pierce and Benjamin E. Reynolds, discussion of 2 Tim 2:11–13, cites: William D. Mounce, *Pastoral Epistles*, WBC 46 (Nashville: Thomas Nelson, 2000), **p. 501**.

Accessible source:
https://dokumen.pub/download/the-faithfulness-of-the-risen-christ-pistis-and-the-exalted-lord-in-the-pauline-letters-1481310909-9781481310901.html

**Status:** `GREEN LOCATOR` for hymn/source-form discussion.

### pp. 517–518 — denial vs temporary faithlessness

Multiple independent citations converge on Mounce **pp. 517–518** for his distinction between:

- line 3: serious/full apostasy/denial;
- line 4: a promise of assurance addressing temporary faithlessness short of apostasy.

One secondary dissertation quotes Mounce’s reasoning and explicitly locates it at **517–18**. Another SBTS dissertation cites **p. 517** for line 3 and reproduces Mounce’s distinction between full apostasy and temporary unfaithfulness.

Accessible verification:

https://tyndale.edu/wp-content/uploads/JODT-Vol14-No43-Dec10.pdf

https://repository.sbts.edu/bitstream/handle/10392/7314/HARRISON_sbts.pdp_0207A_10798.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1

### Important doctrinal handling

Mounce’s exact conclusion should be represented, not silently converted into the project’s final synthesis.

His locator is especially useful because he explicitly distinguishes:

- Peter-type temporary denial/weakness;
- apostasy in the fuller sense;
- Christ’s continuing faithfulness.

The project still adjudicates 2:13 canonically rather than making Mounce the doctrinal court of final appeal.

**Status:** `GREEN LOCATOR / INTERPRETATION TO BE WEIGHED`.

# 4. Official TMS Tyndale Center — 2 Timothy 2:19

This is now a high-value Tier-1 conservative technical control.

Official TMS Tyndale Center entry for 2 Tim 2:16–19 explicitly states:

- `ἀφίστημι` in v.19 communicates moving away / separating / withdrawing;
- “depart” fits the command;
- the wording parallels the Numbers 16/Korah context;
- faithful people were told to turn aside from the tents of the wicked rebels;
- Paul similarly commands those naming the Lord to depart from the wickedness of straying from truth.

Official source:
https://tyndale.tms.edu/?book=heJ3qWI%3D&chapter=heJ3qW4%3D&testament=

### Correction/precision

The TMS web note refers to the broader **Numbers 16** episode. For publication we should retain the more exact distinction already in dossier 53:

- first seal: clear Num 16:5 citation/allusion;
- second seal: likely continues Num 16 separation imagery (especially 16:26 in standard numbering), perhaps with other biblical resonance;
- do not label both clauses exact quotations of one verse.

### Why this matters

This is unusually valuable for the project because the same Greek family commonly associated with “departure/apostasy” is here positively commanded:

> **depart from wickedness**.

Therefore the object/direction of a movement verb is essential; lexeme alone cannot equal a full apostasy doctrine.

**Status:** `GREEN / OFFICIAL TMS CONTROL`.
