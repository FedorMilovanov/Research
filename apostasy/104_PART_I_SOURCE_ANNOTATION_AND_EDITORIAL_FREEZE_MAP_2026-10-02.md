## Claim freeze

Avoid:

> “The Bible has one technical word for apostasy and every occurrence means final loss of salvation.”

## Technical owners

- `29_1_PETER_2_25_RETURNED_TO_SHEPHERD_2026-09-30.md`;
- `30_GALATIANS_4_8_11_RETURN_AGAIN_TO_WEAK_ELEMENTS_2026-09-30.md`;

- `52_ACTS_7_HEART_RETURN_IDOLATRY_AND_JUDICIAL_TURN_DOSSIER_2026-09-30.md`.

## Freeze

Keep the line:

> **A lexeme is evidence inside a context, not a prepackaged doctrine of the person’s previous saving state.**

### Specific guardrails

- `ἐπιστρέφω` may describe first conversion, restoration or directional return.
- `πάλιν` may mark recurrence of a condition/structure, as Gal 4 shows, not necessarily return to the identical former object.
- OT `שוב` is directionally specified by complement/context; it cannot by itself prove prior regeneration.
- 1 Pet 2:25 must not be rewritten as saved → lost → re-saved.

# 4. §2 — taxonomy of forms of departure

The reader draft’s multi-category taxonomy is `GREEN`, provided each category remains descriptive rather than mechanically sequential.

## 4.1 Ordinary sin / deviation

### Owners

Gen 3; James 1; 1 John 1–2.

### Freeze

Not every sin should be labeled final apostasy. If every sin receives the terminal label, the category stops distinguishing anything.

## 4.2 Grievous fall of a believer

### Owners

- David: 2 Sam 11–12; Psalm 51;
- Peter: Luke 22; John 21.

### Freeze

Real believers can fall grievously; perseverance must not be defended by semantically shrinking their sins.

### Guardrail

Do not infer from restoration that consequences vanish or office restoration is automatic.

## 4.3 Recoverable wandering

### Owners

- Gal 6:1;
- James 5:19–20;
- 2 Tim 2:24–26;
- Jude 22–23.

### Technical owners

`67_...`, `91_...`, `53_...`, `57_...`.

### Freeze

If the apostolic instruction is to restore/rescue, the church must not pre-classify the living person as beyond repentance.

## 4.4 Temporary faith

### Primary owner

Luke 8:13.

### Technical owner

- `26_LUKE_8_13_TEMPORARY_FAITH_ROOT_AND_PERSEVERANCE_2026-09-30.md`;
- `69_LUKE_8_13_P1_SOURCE_HARDENING_AND_COMPETING_MODELS_SUPPLEMENT_2026-09-30.md`.

### Exact wording discipline

Must preserve both facts:

1. Jesus says they **believe for a time**; do not rewrite it as “they never believed in any sense.”
2. He also says they have **no root** and fall away under testing; do not infer saving perseverance from the bare verb `πιστεύω`.

### Model disclosure

- Reformed: real temporary/non-saving religious faith;
- Lutheran / conditional-security readings: genuine saving faith later lost;
- Free Grace models: distinguish salvation from discipleship/reward in different ways.

The reader article need not adjudicate every model exhaustively, but must disclose that prior-saving-status is the disputed inference.

## 4.5 Great privilege without proven saving union

### Primary owners

Judas; Heb 6:4–8.

### Technical owners

`02_...`, `60_HEBREWS_6_4_9_TECHNICAL_CLOSURE_AND_COMPETING_READINGS_2026-09-30.md`.

### Freeze

Heb 6 experiences are real and substantial. Do not trivialize “enlightened,” “tasted,” participation in Spirit/word/powers. Equally, do not make the vocabulary alone settle saving union against the contextual control in 6:9 (“better things … belonging to salvation”).

### Modern conservative controls

- Thomas Schreiner: warning to Christian hearers as prospective means of perseverance;
- John MacArthur: deep gospel privilege without true conversion;
- Owen/Reformed historical models: substantial common operations/privilege without final saving union.

Present these as models, not as if one Greek word ends the debate.

## 4.6 Doctrinal defection

### Owners

1 Tim 1:18–20; 4:1–2; 6:20–21; 2 Tim 2:16–19; 4:3–4.

### Freeze

The Pastorals treat doctrine as morally and spiritually consequential; false teaching can be both symptom and instrument of departure.

## 4.7 Practical denial while profession remains

### Owner

Titus 1:16.

### Technical owners

`54_...`, `55_TITUS_...`.

### Freeze

Profession can be contradicted by a settled pattern of works. But do not convert every inconsistency or individual Christian sin into proof of final apostasy.

## 4.8 Corporate/institutional apostasy

### Owners

Judges; Jeroboam; 2 Kings 17; prophetic corpus; Rev 2–3.

### Technical owners

`22_...`, `34_...`, `83_...`, `84_...`.

### Freeze

Corporate covenant judgment is real but does not automatically settle every member’s individual final state.

## 4.9 Progressive hardening

### Owners

Prov 29:1; Amos 4; Zech 7; Heb 3; Rev 9/16.

### Technical owners

`45_...`, `50_...`, `51_...`.

### Freeze

Repeated refusal can change a person/community’s receptivity to truth. Do not turn this into a mechanical numeric threshold (“five warnings then terminal”).

## 4.10 Terminal / near-terminal trajectories

### Owners

Judas; Heb 6/10; Synoptic blasphemy against the Spirit; 1 John 5:16–17; 2 Pet 2; Rev judgment scenes.

### Technical owner

`47_...`, `60–65`.

### Freeze

“Terminal” should be used only where the text supplies exceptionally strong finality or impossibility language. “Near-terminal” remains useful where warning severity is extreme but a narrator-level final verdict is not supplied.

## TMS/Shepherds canonical control

Abner Chou:

- Shepherds 2023 remnant/preservation;

Links already frozen in `102_...` and `103_...`.

## Reformed model disclosure

Conservative Reformed interpreters differ over the precise status/audience analysis of some warning passages. Part I must not pretend that preservation doctrine produces one uncontested grammatical reading of Hebrews 6/10.

## Freeze

> **Warnings are real means, not theatrical props; preservation promises are real promises, not permission to ignore the warnings.**

Avoid both:

- “the warnings cannot truly threaten because the elect persevere”;
- “the promises of preservation must be weakened because the warnings are severe.”

# 8. Assurance versus presumption

## Owners

- Deut 29:18–20;
- Jer 7;
- Mic 3:11;
- Matt 3:9; 7:21–23;
- Rom 2;
- 1 Cor 10:12;
- Rev 3:17.

## Puritan control

Watson, Owen, Brooks; source custody in `89_...`.

## Freeze

Assurance is not presumption. Presumption appropriates privilege/promise in order to neutralize God’s present warning. Biblical assurance rests in Christ and is compatible with self-examination, repentance and heed to warnings.

### Guardrail

Do not turn assurance into confidence in the quality of one’s introspection. Christ is the ground; evidences and perseverance are confirmatory/pastoral categories.

# 11. Editorial freeze list

The following theses may now be treated as frozen unless new primary-text evidence contradicts them:

1. Apostasy/fall/wandering language is multi-category, not one technical soteriological code.
2. Serious Christian falls are real and cannot be minimized to defend perseverance.
3. Recoverable wandering is a distinct biblical category.
4. Temporary faith is real; its saving status is interpretively disputed and must not be settled by `πιστεύω` alone.
5. Great privilege and participation language must not be trivialized; saving-union inference remains contextual/model-disclosed.
6. Corporate apostasy and individual final destiny are not interchangeable.
7. Repeated refusal can harden, but Scripture gives no universal numeric threshold.
8. Terminal language must be reserved for texts with corresponding finality.
9. Warnings and preservation promises belong together.
10. Assurance and presumption are opposites, not synonyms.
11. Final-state claims never exceed narrator strength.
12. No platform brand owns doctrine; Scripture owns the claim.
