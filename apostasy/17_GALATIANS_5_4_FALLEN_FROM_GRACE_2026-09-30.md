# Галатам 5:2–6 — «отпали от благодати»: Христос, закон и оправдание

**Дата:** 2026-09-30  
**Статус:** `WORKING / RESEARCH-ONLY / PUBLICATION_HOLD`  
**Parent spine:** `apostasy/00_MASTER_RESEARCH_MAP_2026-09-29.md`

## 1. Сначала убрать популярную ошибку

Гал. 5:4 **не** говорит о любом тяжёлом грехе, духовной холодности или временном падении.

Paul’s precise target:

`οἵτινες ἐν νόμῳ δικαιοῦσθε` — those who seek/attempt to be justified **in/by law**.

The contrast is not:

> grace vs a Christian who sinned badly.

It is:

> Christ/grace/faith vs law/circumcision as necessary ground or means of justification.

A future article must state this before using “fallen from grace” in perseverance debate.

---

## 2. Greek force is severe

Gal 5:4:

`κατηργήθητε ἀπὸ Χριστοῦ, οἵτινες ἐν νόμῳ δικαιοῦσθε, τῆς χάριτος ἐξεπέσατε.`

Greek access:
- https://biblehub.com/text/galatians/5-4.htm

### `κατηργήθητε ἀπὸ Χριστοῦ`

`καταργέω` can mean render ineffective/null, release/sever from relation depending context. With `ἀπὸ Χριστοῦ`, translations such as “severed/alienated from Christ” capture the relational force better than a weak “Christ is less useful to you.”

### `τῆς χάριτος ἐξεπέσατε`

`ἐκπίπτω` = fall out/from, lose one’s position in a sphere. The expression is intentionally alarming.

A.T. Robertson summarizes the contrast as leaving the sphere of grace in Christ and taking one’s stand in the sphere of law as hope of salvation:
- https://ccel.org/ccel/robertson_at/wp_gal.vi.html

### Research rule

Do **not** soften 5:4 to:

> “you merely stopped enjoying grace emotionally.”

Paul describes an incompatible soteriological stance.

---

## 3. 5:2–3 defines the threat

v.2:

> if you receive circumcision [in the agitators’ soteriological sense], Christ will benefit you nothing.

v.3:

> one who accepts circumcision this way becomes debtor to do whole law.

The issue cannot be physical circumcision in itself: Paul circumcised Timothy for mission prudence (Acts 16:3), and Gal 5:6 says circumcision/uncircumcision as such counts for nothing in Christ.

Therefore:

> circumcision functions as **chosen soteriological system/sign** — submitting to law as necessary for justification.

Historical commentaries correctly emphasize this context; see compiled discussion:
- https://biblehub.com/commentaries/galatians/5-2.htm

---

## 4. `δικαιοῦσθε` is conative/contextual, not proof that law actually justifies

Paul obviously denies that law actually justifies sinners (Gal 2:16; 3:10–14; 3:21–24).

Thus 5:4 cannot mean:

> “you are successfully justified by law.”

It means those **seeking/attempting/claiming justification** on that basis.

Robertson calls the present passive a conative use: “trying to be justified in law.”

This matters because a careless reading may treat 5:4 as a neutral description of two actually successful methods of justification, which is the opposite of Paul’s argument.

---

## 5. Immediate context after v.4: Paul still distinguishes “we” who wait by faith

v.5:

> “For **we**, through Spirit, by faith, eagerly await the hope of righteousness.”

v.6:

> in Christ Jesus circumcision/uncircumcision counts for nothing, but faith working through love.

Paul does not leave reader with an abstract warning only. He contrasts two eschatological orientations:

- seeking righteousness through law;
- awaiting final righteousness/hope through Spirit by faith.

This makes perseverance fundamentally **gospel perseverance**: continuing to rely on Christ rather than changing the ground of acceptance before God.

---

## 6. Gal 5:7–10: warning is severe, but Paul has not simply declared whole churches finally apostate

v.7:

> “You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?”

v.10:

> “I have confidence in you **in the Lord** that you will take no other view.”

This is critical for the apostasy dossier.

Paul can say, in the same paragraph:

- severed from Christ / fallen from grace as the logical state of law-justification;
- you were hindered;
- I am confident in the Lord about you.

Therefore the warning functions **prospectively and pastorally** to pull Galatians away from the destructive path, not merely as a coroner’s report after irreversible apostasy.

This fits “warnings as means” especially well.

---

## 7. Earlier letter context: they are in dangerous motion

### Gal 1:6

Paul marvels that they are `μετατίθεσθε` — deserting/turning from the One who called them in grace toward another gospel.

### Gal 3:1–5

They received Spirit by hearing with faith, not works of law; Paul asks whether having begun by Spirit they are now being perfected by flesh.

### Gal 4:9–11

Paul can say they know God / are known by God and yet fears their return to weak/beggarly elements and legal calendrical bondage.

### Gal 4:19

He labors again “until Christ be formed in you.”

### Implication

Galatians uses **strong relational/grace language for the addressees** while issuing equally strong warnings.

A serious Reformed reading cannot make all positive address mere empty politeness. A serious conditional-security reading cannot ignore Paul’s confident, corrective, not-finally-condemnatory pastoral posture.

---

## 8. Calvin: law mixed into justification renounces Christ/grace

Calvin reads v.4 as Paul declaring that seeking even part of righteousness in works of law deprives one of Christ’s benefit and abandons grace.

Primary source:
- https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom41/calcom41.iii.vii.i.html

The strength of Calvin’s point is not “Galatians were obviously never Christians”; it is **the exclusivity of Christ in justification**. Grace and law-righteousness cannot be mixed as co-grounds.

This is the correct doctrinal center even before perseverance-system conclusions.

---

## 9. Conditional-security reading

Strong argument:

1. Galatians are addressed as Christians who received Spirit by faith (3:2–5).
2. They began in Spirit.
3. Paul says those moving to law-justification are severed from Christ/fallen from grace.
4. Therefore a true Christian can leave saving grace through apostasy from gospel.

### Strength

This reading takes addressee language and 5:4 severity at face value.

### Pressure

It must distinguish:

- threatened/incipient trajectory;
- individuals already fully embracing agitator system;
- Paul’s confidence in 5:10;
- whether “fall from grace” here states reversal of completed justification or abandonment of grace-principle/sphere as means of justification.

The grammar alone does not answer every systematic step.

---

## 10. Reformed/perseverance reading

Strong form should be:

> Paul addresses visible churches pastorally according to profession and real Spirit-related covenant experience; the warning announces what adopting law-righteousness means — abandonment of Christ as saving ground. God uses that warning to preserve His elect in faith; those who finally embrace another gospel reveal a non-persevering faith/false profession rather than an irreversible justification becoming reversed.

### Strength

Integrates:

- Gal 5:10 confidence;
- warnings as means;
- Pauline justification logic;
- wider perseverance texts.

### Pressure

It must not make “received Spirit” (Gal 3) unreal or treat 5:4 as impossible hypothetical with no real addressee.

---

## 11. “Fallen from grace” is not the same question as Hebrews 6 irreversible apostasy

Galatians does not say in 5:4:

- impossible to restore;
- no repentance remains;
- person has committed the unique Heb 6/10 final repudiation.

Indeed the whole letter is Paul’s active attempt to **restore/correct** the churches.

Therefore:

> Gal 5:4 is a real soteriological warning, but should not be automatically classified as already-completed irreversible apostasy.

This is a crucial pastoral distinction.

---

## 12. “Christ profits you nothing” — not loss of rewards language

A weak security reading might say:

> Christ becomes ineffective only for sanctification/reward, while eternal salvation is untouched.

But v.4 explicitly concerns **justification**. The whole Galatians controversy concerns how sinners are right with God and inherit promise.

Therefore reward-only interpretation is contextually inadequate.

If one defends eternal security, it must do so by distinguishing warning/profession/perseverance — not by demoting the subject from salvation to rewards.

## 13. Competing-view matrix

| View | What happened / is threatened | Strength | Pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Reformed warnings-as-means | adopting law-righteousness is genuine abandonment of gospel/Christ; elect are preserved through this warning; final apostasy reveals false/non-saving profession | respects severity + Paul’s confidence + wider perseverance | must take Gal 3 Spirit language and real church address seriously |
| B. Conditional security | regenerate/justified Galatians can actually lose saving relation/grace by changing gospel ground | direct use of 3:2–5 + 5:4 | must decide whether 5:4 is completed loss or sharp prospective/logical warning; integrate 5:10 and wider Paul |
| C. Purely hypothetical | Paul states what would happen, though no true believer could ever do it | protects perseverance | risks making actual crisis/rhetoric unreal; 1:6, 3:1, 5:7 show real movement |
| D. Reward/fellowship only | relation/usefulness/fellowship with Christ impaired, salvation secure | maintains security | justification context makes this too weak |

A and B are the main serious contenders. C may function as nuance inside A but is weak as standalone model. D should not control exegesis.

## 14. What Gal 5:4 clearly proves

High confidence:

1. The danger is **soteriological**, not merely emotional or sanctification-level.
2. Seeking justification by law is incompatible with receiving Christ as sufficient ground of righteousness.
3. Paul can describe this move as severance from Christ and falling from grace.
4. The warning is addressed to actual churches in a real crisis.
5. Paul nevertheless expresses confidence in the Lord that the Galatians will not finally adopt the error (5:10).
6. Therefore warning and pastoral confidence coexist in one paragraph.

---

## 15. What Gal 5:4 does NOT prove by itself

It does not by itself prove:

- every serious post-conversion sin causes loss of salvation;
- every Galatian had already irreversibly apostatized;
- every individual addressed was certainly regenerate;
- “fallen from grace” is merely loss of joy/fellowship;
- completed forensic justification is explicitly said to have been legally reversed;
- circumcision as physical act is inherently damning regardless of motive.

---

## 16. Current synthesis

Galatians 5 is one of the strongest texts for this project because it shows exactly why perseverance cannot be reduced to generic morality.

The perseverance at stake is:

> **continuing in the gospel of Christ as the sole ground of justification.**

A person/visible church can be deeply Christian in history, Spirit-language and profession, yet face a real warning that adopting another ground of righteousness means abandoning Christ’s saving benefit.

The Reformed synthesis remains viable, but only if it treats the warning as **genuinely instrumental** in keeping believers from that path — not as a threat God knows has no pastoral function.
