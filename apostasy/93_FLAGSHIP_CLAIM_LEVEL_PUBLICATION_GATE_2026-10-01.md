## 1. Purpose

The research corpus is now too large for “publication ready / not ready” to be a useful binary.

This gate classifies **reader-facing claims**, not files.

### Labels

- `GREEN` — Scripture + claim-to-source custody sufficient for prose freeze.
- `GREEN-PARAPHRASE` — claim secure, but do not use a direct quotation until exact edition/page is frozen.
- `VARIANT-DISCLOSE` — claim publishable only with textual-variant disclosure or wording that survives the serious variants.
- `AMBER` — useful and probably correct, but source/locator custody still incomplete for flagship use.
- `HOLD` — do not publish in asserted form yet.

### Governing rule

> **A claim is allowed into flagship prose only at the strength supported by its weakest necessary evidence layer.**
