## 1. Кальвин, Комментарий на 1 Кор. 11 (пер. W. Pringle)

**URL:** studylight.org/commentaries/eng/cal/1-corinthians-11.html?print=yes
**Права:** PUBLIC DOMAIN. **Полный текст:** онлайн (URL).

### На 11:2 — «предания» = порядок, а не догматы

> «I do not deny, that there were certain traditions of the Apostles that were not committed to writing, but I do not admit that they were parts of doctrine, or related to things necessary for salvation. What then? They were connected with order and government. For we know that every Church has liberty to frame for itself a form of government that is suitable and profitable for it... Thus Paul, the first founder of the Corinthian Church, had also framed for its regulation pious and seemly enactments — that all things might be done decently and in order.»

### На 11:3 — κεφαλή; Гал. 3:28 не отменяет порядок

> «When he says that there is no difference between the man and the woman, he is treating of Christ's spiritual kingdom... Here, on the other hand, he reasons respecting outward propriety and decorum — which is a part of ecclesiastical polity.»

(Кальвин: «Христос глава мужчины и женщины без различия» — в духовном царстве; «внешнее устройство» — порядок творения.)

### На 11:4 — принцип и форма; το πρέπον

> «The covering... is an emblem of authority intermediate and interposed.»

> «Let us, however, bear in mind, that in this matter the error is merely in so far as decorum is violated, and the distinction of rank which God has established, is broken in upon. For we must not be so scrupulous as to look upon it as a criminal thing for a teacher to have a cap on his head, when addressing the people from the pulpit. Paul means nothing more than this — that it should appear that the man has authority, and that the woman is under subjection... In fine, the one rule to be observed here is το πρέπον — decorum. If that is secured, Paul requires nothing farther.»

(КЛЮЧЕВАЯ цитата для вопроса «форма vs принцип»: у Кальвина форма гибкая, принцип — порядок и благообразие.)

### На 11:5 — вопрос о 1 Тим. 2:12 / 14:34

> «It may seem, however, to be superfluous for Paul to forbid the woman to prophesy with her head uncovered, while elsewhere he wholly prohibits women from speaking in the Church (1 Timothy 2:12)... It may be replied, that the Apostle, by here condemning the one, does not commend the other.»

(Кальвин: 11:5 не разрешает женщинам пророчествовать — он откладывает вопрос до гл. 14.)

### На 11:7 — «образ» = порядок брака, не природа

> «The image, however, of which he is now speaking, relates to the order of marriage, and hence it belongs to the present life, and is not connected with conscience.»

### На 11:10 — ἐξουσία = метонимия «знака власти»; ангелы = добрые ангелы-свидетели

> «In the term power, there is an instance of metonymy, for he means a token by which she declares herself to be under the power of her husband; and it is a covering, whether it be a robe, or a veil, or any other kind of covering.»

> «Because of the angels. This passage is explained in various ways. As the Prophet Malachi 2:7 calls priests angels of God, some are of opinion that Paul speaks of them; but the ministers of the word have nowhere that term applied to them by itself — that is, without something being added; and the meaning would be too forced. I understand it, therefore, in its proper signification... We know that angels are in attendance, also, upon Christ as their head, and minister to him. When, therefore, women venture upon such liberties, as to usurp for themselves the token of authority, they make their baseness manifest to the angels. This, therefore, was said by way of amplifying, as if he had said, "If women uncover their heads, not only Christ, but all the angels too, will be witnesses of the outrage."»

(Кальвин отвергает «ангелы = священники» и принимает добрых ангелов-свидетелей богослужения.)

### На 11:14 — «природа» = подтверждённый обычай; цитата Эдвардса

> «He again sets forth nature as the mistress of decorum, and what was at that time in common use by universal consent and custom — even among the Greeks — he speaks of as being natural... he reckons as nature a custom that had come to be confirmed.»

(В примечании издателя — цитата Джонатана Эдвардса: «nature itself, nature in its proper sense, teaches that it is a shame for a man to appear with the established signs of the female sex». — Edwards on Original Sin, pt. 2, ch. 3, §3.)

### На 11:16 — «спорщик»

> «A contentious person is one whose humor inclines him to stir up disputes... Of this description are all who, without any necessity, abolish good and useful customs — raise disputes respecting matters that are not doubtful.»

## 2. Джон Гилл, Exposition of the Whole Bible, 1 Кор. 11

**URL:** studylight.org/commentaries/eng/geb/1-corinthians-11.html?print=yes
**Права:** PUBLIC DOMAIN.

### Введение к главе (извлечено полностью — см. вывод):

> «In this chapter the apostle blames both men and women for their indecent appearance in public worship... he observes, that as God is the head of Christ, and Christ the head of every man, so the man is the head of the woman... Another reason why the woman should be covered at the time of public worship is, because of the angels then present, 1 Corinthians 11:10... But if, after all the apostle had said on this subject, there should be any contentious persons disposed to wrangle about it, he observes, that they were not proper persons to be continued in the church.»

### На 11:10 — ангелы (по biblehub.com/commentaries/1_corinthians/11-10.htm):

> «The most and best interpreters understand here by angels, the good angels; for the apostle would hardly have spoken of devils under the notion of angels... and so it teaches us, that the good angels, who are ministering spirits for the good of God's elect, at all times have a special ministration, or at least are more particularly present, in the assemblies of people for religious worship, observing the persons, carriage, and demeanour; the sense of which ought to awe all persons attending those services, from any indecent and unworthy behaviour.»

(Гилл: добрые ангелы присутствуют в собраниях; отмечает иудейские параллели — ангелы собираются «слушать», как при открытии Закона; упоминает прочтение Тертуллиана о падших ангелах как менее вероятное.)

## 3. Чарльз Ходж, Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians (1857), на 11:10

**URL (цитата):** biblehub.com/commentaries/1_corinthians/11-10.htm
**Полный текст:** archive.org (1857, public domain) — TO ACQUIRE из песочницы.

> «Because of the angels — Some have explained this of good angels, who were supposed to be present in their assemblies (see Doddridge); others refer it to evil angels; and others to messengers or spies who, it has been supposed, were present in their public assemblies, and who would report greatly to the disadvantage of the Christian assemblies if the women were seen to be unveiled. I do not know what it means; and I regard it as one of the very few passages in the Bible whose meaning as yet is wholly inexplicable.»

(Честная позиция классика: Ходж признаёт неясность 11:10 — ценный образец калибровки уверенности для серии.)
