## Guardrail

No source classified `B1`, `C` or `D` may be the sole basis for a disputed quote-safe factual allegation. Images/screenshots require separate item-level rights review in `MEDIA_LEDGER.md`. Platform metadata proves platform state at a date, not underlying ownership, intent or legal completion. Accusation documents prove what was alleged, not the truth of every allegation. Search-index/crawl state is not equivalent to current live-site state.
