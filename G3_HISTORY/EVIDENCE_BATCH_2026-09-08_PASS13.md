## Why this matters to the G3+ transfer investigation

The evidence now gives a more precise post-G3 continuity problem than the generic question `who bought G3+?`:

1. Official historical G3 surfaces placed Living Heritage inside G3’s publishing ecosystem.
2. The FY2025 filing-derived narrative says Living Heritage was separated as an independent entity.
3. Current Living Heritage commercial surfaces still bundle G3+ access into recurring paid Premium benefits.
4. A third-party scholarship marketplace repeats the same G3+ bundle.
5. The checkout/member portal is Living Heritage-branded, but the current public checkout does not expose the G3+ supplier or merchant/legal entity.

This means the next proof target is not inference from family/leadership overlap. It is the **contractual fulfillment chain**.

## P0-adjacent acquisition questions

### Entity identity

- What exact legal person operates Living Heritage Homeschool?
- State of formation, entity number, formation date, registered agent, officers/managers/members if public.
- EIN / W-9 / approved ESA-provider payee identity if publicly disclosable.
- Is `7503 Elderberry Dr., Douglasville, GA 30135` a business address, mailing address, or residential/vendor contact only?

### Merchant / checkout

- Merchant-of-record shown on receipt/invoice/checkout legal disclosure.
- Payment processor account legal name.
- Seller named in refund/privacy/data-processing notices.
- Whether `livingheritagehomeschool.com` is operated by the same legal person as `livingheritage.info`.

### G3+ fulfillment

- Does Living Heritage provision a normal G3+ account, coupon, bulk license, family entitlement, SSO/link, or its own mirrored content?
- Who invoices whom for the $79.99-value membership?
- Was the entitlement grandfathered from the pre-separation G3 relationship, or created/renewed after the announced 2026 acquisition?
- What happens to Premium purchasers if G3+ is transferred to `Ministry X`?
- Which privacy/data-processing entity receives purchaser email/account data?

### Ownership boundary

Do not collapse any of the following:

`Living Heritage sells G3+ access`  
≠ `Living Heritage operates G3+`  
≠ `Living Heritage owns G3+ IP`  
≠ `Living Heritage is Ministry X`  
≠ `Living Heritage and G3 remained the same legal entity after separation`.

Each requires a different source object.
