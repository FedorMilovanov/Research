## 3. `strength under control` is not source-specific to Hughes

The dossier describes `gentleness is not weakness; it is strength under control` as Hughes's distinctive formulation.

That characterization is too strong.

Independent Christian exposition well before Buck uses the same standard definition of meekness/gentleness as `strength under control`, including Bible.org material published years earlier. The formulation is widely used in evangelical teaching and often illustrated by a tamed horse.

Therefore exact or near-exact use of this short definition does not by itself establish dependence on Hughes.

**Working grade:** `C` standing alone.

## 4. `pride and self-promoting arrogance sow disunity` is more source-specific

A pre-Buck 2016 republication explicitly presents Kent Hughes's discussion of Christian unity and includes the formulation that pride and self-promoting arrogance `sow disunity`.

That independently anchors the wording to Hughes before Buck's 2022 sermon.

If Buck audio confirms the dossier's near-verbatim wording without attribution, this is materially more probative than the generic `strength under control` line.

**Working grade:** `U → potential B`.

## 5. `make haste / do your utmost / urgent`

The Greek-root observation behind `make every effort` can naturally generate language about haste or diligence; none of those concepts is proprietary. But the dossier alleges a three-element sequence matching Hughes:

- make haste;
- do your utmost;
- urgent.

The sequence is more probative than any element alone.

**Working grade:** `U → C/B depending exact audio/source sequence`.

## 6. `no room for rivalries or hatreds or factions`

The exact Hughes formulation is independently reproduced in later Christian material with Hughes attribution. Its lexical cluster is more specific than a generic call to unity, though not necessarily unique enough to grade A from a single sentence.

**Working grade:** `U → potential B`.

## 7. `putty over the cracks`

The dossier identifies Hughes's colloquial phrase `putty over the cracks` in connection with false peace and claims Buck reproduces it.

Unlike `strength under control`, this is not an obvious stock definition of a biblical term and deserves higher source-specificity scrutiny.

**Working grade:** `U → potential B`, pending exact Hughes object, Buck audio and upstream-source search.

## 8. Methodological result

Item 04 demonstrates why the dossier's own `distinctive` labels must be independently tested.

At least one line it describes as distinctive (`strength under control`) is widespread evangelical vocabulary. Other lines (`sow disunity`, possibly `putty over the cracks`) are much more specifically anchored to Hughes.

Thus Item 04 cannot be honestly summarized as either:

- `all generic, therefore no issue`; or
- `every listed phrase uniquely belongs to Hughes`.
