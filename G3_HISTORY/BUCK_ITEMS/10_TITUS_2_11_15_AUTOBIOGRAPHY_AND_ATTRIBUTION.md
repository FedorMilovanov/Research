## 1. Why this item requires four separate questions

The accusation packet treats the marriage illustration as its most egregious example. A responsible audit must separate:

1. **textual wording** — did Buck use the published wording/formulation?
2. **source lineage** — was that formulation already Bryan Chapell's published autobiographical material before Buck?
3. **biographical truth** — were analogous circumstances also true of Tom and Jennifer Buck?
4. **attribution** — did Buck identify Chapell/Hughes or otherwise signal source use in the immediate context?

A finding on one question cannot silently answer the others.

## 4. Other wording rows also require grading

The dossier compares Buck with Hughes/Chapell on several non-biographical formulations, including:

- wonder/thanksgiving/determination after rescue from evil;
- godliness not being produced by human willpower;
- grace being available across age/class/gender boundaries;
- sensitivity to sin flowing from apprehension of Christ's love;
- holiness not ultimately produced by willpower, guilt or inspiring messages;
- a closing exhortation about love for Christ displacing worldly affections.

Some of these appear as sustained literary sequences and may be meaningful dependence candidates; others express ordinary Pauline/Reformed theology and require lower standalone weight.

No flat `all rows = A` classification is permitted.

## 5. Jennifer Buck counterevidence blocks the fabrication claim

A secondary report by Janet Mefferd reproduces Jennifer Buck's response that the accusation made it sound as though Tom had adopted someone else's life experience, whereas Jennifer says analogous words/circumstances described Tom and Jennifer roughly 35 years earlier.

Current locator:
- https://www.janetmefferd.com/p/how-a-qualified-pastor-can-ruin-you

Independent 2022 reporting also documents that Tom and Jennifer had a long marriage history and publicly discussed serious early-life/marital history years before the 2026 plagiarism controversy.

This does **not** erase a wording-attribution problem. It changes the proposition that can responsibly be made.

### Blocked formulation

> Buck fabricated a marriage experience that never happened to him and falsely presented Chapell's life as his own.

Current evidence does not establish that.

### Potentially supportable formulation after audio closure

> Buck used another author's previously published autobiographical wording/formulation to describe analogous circumstances in his own marriage, without adequate attribution.

That is a materially different claim.
