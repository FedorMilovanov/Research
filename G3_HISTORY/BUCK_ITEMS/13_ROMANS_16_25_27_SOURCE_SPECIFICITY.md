If Buck's original audio confirms the dossier's near-verbatim wording without attribution, this is a meaningful `B` dependence candidate and could carry higher weight depending on exact extent/context.

## 3. `become his own sons and daughters` is low-specificity standing alone

Adoption/sonship language is biblical and widespread Christian vocabulary. The exact wording `sons and daughters` appears broadly in devotional and theological writing independent of Hughes.

Therefore the second dossier row cannot responsibly be treated as a strong literary fingerprint on its own.

It may contribute marginally if it immediately follows the verified Hughes-derived sequence, but its standalone specificity is low.

**Working grade:** `C` standing alone.
