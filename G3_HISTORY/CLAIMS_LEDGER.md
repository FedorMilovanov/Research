## Rule for the future article

A narrative paragraph may combine several claims only if each sensitive factual component is independently supportable at its own evidence level. Rhetorical compression must never upgrade `INFERENCE`, `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`, `DISPUTED`, or `UNVERIFIED` into fact. Digital platform/search state must be dated and must never silently override stronger event-specific evidence. One underlying filing, letter, dossier or archived page must never be multiplied into multiple “independent” corroborations merely because it appears through several renderers or publications.
