# G3+ / Treefort — vendor-platform guardrail

**Status:** ACTIVE / PUBLICATION_HOLD  
**Question:** does Treefort evidence identify the unnamed ministry expected to acquire G3+?

## Observed platform relationship

Current G3+ app-store surfaces route privacy/terms links through Treefort Systems. Treefort's own public product site showcases `G3+` as a customer app and attributes a positive testimonial to G3 operations leadership.

Treefort describes its service model as a technology platform for media organizations: customers upload/manage media and branding, while Treefort provides the web/mobile app infrastructure, deployment, subscription and platform tooling.

The observable relationship is therefore consistent with:

`G3/G3+ = client/content/brand operator`  
`Treefort = app/platform technology vendor`

## Blocked inference

The following claim is not supported:

> Treefort appears in G3+ privacy/terms and platform infrastructure, therefore Treefort is the unnamed ministry acquiring G3+.

Vendor/platform control is not beneficial ownership, ministry identity, asset assignment, or transaction closing evidence.

The same guardrail applies to historical/current technical metadata that may continue across an asset transfer: a successor can retain a vendor, and a transferor can continue to appear in app-store/legal metadata during migration.

## Current Q006 consequence

Treefort evidence is useful primarily as an exclusion/interpretation control. It explains why Treefort domains can appear in G3+ platform/legal surfaces; prevents a technology vendor from being misidentified as the recipient ministry; does not identify the recipient; and does not prove or refute completion of the reported G3+ transfer.

`TREEFORT_VENDOR_RELATIONSHIP_SUPPORTED / TREEFORT_AS_SUCCESSOR_MINISTRY_UNVERIFIED_AND_UNSUPPORTED / NAMED_TRANSFEREE_HOLD`.

## Required proof remains unchanged

A named-successor conclusion still requires a recipient/transaction primary object such as a successor ministry announcement, a G3 communication naming recipient, a post-transfer legal provider/seller change, an assignment/purchase agreement, platform/domain/account assignment tied to a named legal entity, or another authoritative transaction record.

Until then, do not infer Treefort, Living Heritage, Right Response, or any other named organization as the acquiring ministry.
