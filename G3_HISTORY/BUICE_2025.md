# Josh Buice crisis — 2025 evidence dossier

**Status:** ACTIVE / PUBLICATION_HOLD

This file isolates the 2025 founder crisis from the 2026 Tom Buck controversy. Similarities between the two may be analyzed later, but evidence must not be merged.

## Primary institutional facts

According to the May 12, 2025 G3/Pray’s Mill statement:

- PMBC elders uncovered `irrefutable evidence` that Josh Buice had, for roughly three years, operated at least:
  - four anonymous social-media accounts;
  - two anonymous email addresses;
  - two Substack platforms;
- these were used to anonymously attack/slander Christian leaders, including some G3 speakers, several PMBC elders and others;
- Buice had been asked multiple times over roughly two years whether he had connections to anonymous accounts and denied knowledge;
- on May 4, 2025, after confrontation with evidence, he initially denied involvement and later admitted it;
- on May 8, after some G3 board members encouraged resignation, the board unanimously accepted his resignation as president;
- G3 cancelled the 2025 national conference and all 2025 workshops and promised refunds.

This remains the core factual baseline.

## Known targets

### Founders Ministries / Tom Ascol

Founders Ministries published its own statement saying a `large percentage` of Buice’s anonymous conduct was directed at Founders Ministries, Tom Ascol and Ascol’s family. Founders removed Buice’s content and characterized his actions as disqualifying for spiritual leadership.

This is a direct institutional statement from a named target organization and materially strengthens the record beyond G3’s general wording.

### Voddie Baucham

The Roys Report disclosed a December 16, 2024 email sent under the name `Tom Smith` from an email address that PMBC elders confirmed was one of Buice’s anonymous accounts.

The message attempted to interest TRR in two allegations:

1. questions about the use of roughly $1.4 million raised for Voddie Baucham’s 2021 medical crisis;
2. a claim that major portions of `Fault Lines` were ghostwritten using material associated with James Lindsay.

TRR says it did **not** pursue the anonymous tip when first received because of its anonymous nature. The email became news after PMBC confirmed the account belonged to Buice.

## Buice’s recantation

After the anonymous email was exposed, Buice publicly characterized his prior comments about Baucham/Founders as unsubstantiated, sinful, lacking evidence and harmful to Baucham’s reputation. Reports quote him saying the details originally shared with him lacked merit / proved unfounded.

This matters because the record is not merely `others accused Buice of slander`; Buice himself publicly recanted at least part of the allegations.

## Important unresolved chronology

Tom Buck publicly objected that Buice’s private apology meeting with Voddie Baucham apparently occurred **before** the TRR email itself became known, raising the question whether Buice had disclosed the anonymous media tip during that meeting.

Current evidence supports treating this as a real chronological concern, not as proof of insincere repentance. Exact dates and what was disclosed in the private meeting are not independently known.

## What is NOT established

- exact identity and full archive of all four social accounts;
- every target and every post attributable to Buice;
- whether any G3 staff/director knew of the accounts before May 2025;
- whether PMBC/G3 could reasonably have detected them earlier;
- whether the conduct was coordinated with anyone else;
- whether every statement made anonymously was factually false;
- whether the 2026 anonymous Buck campaign was directly inspired by Buice’s earlier behavior.

## Institutional response — credit and limits

### Actions G3 took

- board accepted founder’s resignation;
- conference/workshops cancelled;
- refunds promised;
- after initially saying legacy Buice content would remain, G3 reversed course and removed his material because of the public/egregious nature of the sin;
- post-crisis communications emphasized local church, anti-celebrity ministry and non-platform-centered resources.

These are real corrective actions and should be acknowledged.

### Questions that remain

- Did governance or reporting systems change?
- Were anonymous-reporting / whistleblower mechanisms added?
- Was board/church role separation strengthened?
- Were written ethics/conflict policies changed?
- Was there an independent review of how a founder could maintain the behavior for several years?

The available public reform language is much stronger on **vision and content strategy** than on verifiable internal control reforms.

## Analytical significance

The 2025 crisis establishes a verified pattern of prolonged anonymous and deceptive public conduct by G3’s founder. The later 2026 crisis establishes a separate admitted anonymous external campaign by a different group of leaders.

It is legitimate to ask whether the recurrence reflects institutional culture. It is **not** legitimate, on recurrence alone, to claim a common conspiracy, direct continuity, or shared knowledge.
