The screenshot itself visibly presents a G3-branded subscriber message signed `The G3 Ministries Team` and headed `G3 Plus is transitioning.`

This file treats the screenshot as **one underlying recipient artifact**. The host article, search-index copies, screenshots of the host page and quotations of the same email must not be multiplied into independent corroborating sources.

## What the email directly represents

The email states, in short, that:

- G3 Ministries is winding down;
- G3 Plus **is being acquired by another ministry**;
- existing subscriber access, library and price were represented as continuing without interruption at that point;
- new teaching was represented as continuing;
- the acquiring ministry's name was not yet being disclosed;
- a later follow-up from G3 or the new ministry was promised when the transition became final, including practical account/billing details.

Two short controlling phrases from the visible artifact are sufficient for chronology:

- `G3 Plus is being acquired by another ministry`;
- `When the transition is final`.

Together they establish an **announced acquisition-in-progress / transition-in-progress state**, not a proved completed closing as of the email.

## What this materially upgrades

The evidence no longer needs to be described merely as vague secondary reporting that G3+ *might* be transferred. A subscriber-facing artifact directly represents that G3 Plus was in the process of being acquired by another ministry.

## What remains unresolved

The email does **not** identify or establish:

- the name/legal identity of the acquiring ministry;
- whether the transaction ultimately closed;
- the closing date;
- purchase price, donation value or other consideration;
- whether the structure was sale, donation, assignment, license, operations handoff or another arrangement;
- board approval, recusals or conflicts;
- liabilities assumed;
- legal treatment of subscriber personal/payment data;
- domains, trademarks, source code, app-store accounts or content-library ownership;
- G3 Press as an asset included in this same transaction.

The host article separately reports that some Living Heritage users were told that the same unnamed person/ministry was taking over G3+ and G3 Press. That is a **different evidence layer** and must not be silently imported into the email itself.
