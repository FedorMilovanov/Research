# PMBC primary response object — 2026-08-16 pre-publication announcement

**Status:** ACTIVE / PUBLICATION_HOLD  
**Scope:** event-specific institutional-response evidence; **not** proof of later resignation mechanics

## Primary/participant object

On 2026-08-16, immediately after Pray's Mill Baptist Church's Sunday-evening service and before Protestia's Aug. 17 article was published, pastor **Matt Sikes** addressed the congregation about the expected report.

A downloadable text transcript is preserved at:

- `https://protestia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PMBC-8-16-26-transcript.txt`

The hosting article also exposes the transcript as a distinct download object. The transcript identifies the date and speaker and records Sikes speaking in the first person/plural on behalf of the church leadership.

## What Sikes himself said — bounded factual propositions

The transcript supports the following participant-statement facts:

1. leadership had learned over the weekend that a report was coming and wanted members to know before publication;
2. Sikes identified Jonathan Frazier and Jarrod Wilson as former members whom leadership believed were spreading rumors about the church;
3. he said Protestia had requested comment and asked questions, but PMBC leadership had decided not to respond;
4. he explicitly acknowledged that leadership had **not seen the forthcoming article** and did not know all it would say;
5. he said leadership knew at least one situation relevant to the coming report and that the elders and deacons had been told what leadership knew;
6. he invited members to ask elders/deacons questions;
7. he connected the situation to the church's current 1 Peter preaching and described the church as experiencing attack from Satan.

The exact downloadable transcript should control wording over secondary paraphrase. Any quotation used for publication still requires quote-level rights/context review.

## What this closes

It is now supportable to state, with attribution to Sikes/PMBC leadership, that before the article appeared the congregation was told that leadership had declined comment, had not seen the article, knew at least one underlying situation, and framed the episode in the context of the church's 1 Peter teaching and spiritual attack.

This is useful institutional-response evidence because it captures PMBC leadership's own pre-publication framing rather than a later recollection.

## What this does **not** close

This object does **not** establish:

- the truth or falsity of Frazier/Wilson's underlying allegations;
- whether Protestia's questions were in fact `leading`;
- whether PMBC's spiritual framing was manipulative, prudent, mistaken or justified;

- whether the later resignations were voluntary in the ordinary sense, compelled, or formally tendered after a removal demand;

Those remain separate evidentiary questions.
