### Evidentiary consequence

The strongest supported state is no longer merely `a transfer was vaguely reported`.

It is:

`RECIPIENT_ARTIFACT_VERIFIED / ACQUISITION_IN_PROGRESS_REPRESENTED / TRANSFEREE_UNNAMED / COMPLETED_CLOSING_UNVERIFIED`.

The email itself does **not** establish G3 Press as part of the transaction. Reports that the same unnamed person/ministry was taking G3 Press come from Living Heritage/subscriber reporting around the email and remain a weaker evidence layer unless a recipient/transaction object names Press directly.

Canonical control:

`PRIMARY_SOURCE_G3PLUS_SUBSCRIBER_EMAIL_2026-08-27.md`.

## 2. What remains unresolved about the G3+ transaction

No acquired primary public object currently supplies:

- transferee legal/name identity;
- proof that closing actually completed;
- closing date;
- consideration or valuation;
- sale vs donation vs assignment vs license/operations handoff structure;
- board approval, recusals or conflict handling;
- assumed liabilities;
- subscriber-data/payment-data transfer terms;
- exact disposition of domains, trademarks, code, app-store accounts or content rights.

Therefore neither `G3+ definitely closed to [named ministry]` nor `the transaction never closed` is supportable at the current cutoff.

## 4. Treefort: technology/vendor role is not successor ownership

Current G3+ privacy/terms/app surfaces route through Treefort infrastructure, and Treefort publicly presents branded media-app/platform services.

That is consistent with a technology/vendor relationship. It does not identify the beneficial owner or ministry transferee.

`Treefort = acquiring ministry` is therefore blocked absent a recipient/transaction object.

## 5. Current platform seller/developer identity remains G3

### Apple

Current G3+/G3 Plus App Store surfaces continue to identify `G3 Ministries For The Church, Inc.` as developer/provider/seller.

### Google Play

Current G3+ surfaces continue to expose G3-linked support/developer metadata including `admin@g3min.org` and the Douglasville address.

Different Google surfaces have exposed conflicting update dates (Jul. 23 vs Sep. 4, 2026). Preserve the date conflict rather than choosing the convenient value.

### Boundary

App-store metadata is dated **platform-account state**. It can lag or survive a private legal transfer/operations handoff.

Therefore:

- `App stores still say G3, so no transfer occurred` — **REFUTED inference**;
- `App-store G3 identity proves G3 still beneficially owns the app` — **UNSUPPORTED**.

## 6. Post-wind-down live services

As observed Sep. 7–8:

- `plus.g3min.org` remained reachable;
- `shop.g3min.org` remained a commerce-style G3 Press storefront with cart/catalog surfaces.

These prove dated web/platform continuity only. They do not establish beneficial ownership, fulfillment, merchant beneficiary, inventory title or reversal of the announced wind-down.

## 7. G3 Press remains more fragmented than G3+

No acquired G3+ subscriber email directly states that G3 Press is included in the same acquisition.

Separate recipient/secondary reporting says some Living Heritage users were told the same unnamed person/ministry was taking over G3+ and G3 Press. That is a real lead but is weaker than the directly visible G3+ email.

Title-level evidence shows why `G3 Press transferred as one monolithic asset` is unsafe:

- Dave Jenkins' *The Word Matters* has an observable post-G3 publishing/sales presence under his current Servants of Grace / Theology for Life ecosystem, materially corroborating reported title-level rights return;
- Darrell Harrison's request/inquiry about rights return is reported, but completed reversion is not established;
- current G3 Press storefront visibility cannot resolve contract rights or residual inventory.

For each title/content class distinguish:

- copyright/author rights;
- publisher license;
- e-book/audiobook rights;
- physical inventory;
- G3+ streaming/content license;
- storefront/marketing rights;
- fulfillment/distribution.

## 8. Corporate and tax-record boundaries

Georgia Secretary of State still listed `G3 Ministries for the Church, Inc.` as **Active/Compliance** on Sep. 8, 2026, with Scott Aniol as registered/designated agent.

That can coexist with operational wind-down, liquidation, servicing and private asset transitions. It does not identify a transferee.

Raw FY2024/FY2025 Schedule L is acquired and does **not** identify a G3+/G3 Press/Living Heritage transfer. Do not use the unrelated interested-person salary disclosure as circumstantial successor evidence.

## 10. What can still close Q006

Repeated observation of unchanged app-store/storefront metadata is now low value. The remaining high-value gates require genuinely new evidence:

1. follow-up subscriber email naming the new ministry after final transition;
2. successor ministry's own primary announcement;
3. G3 board/corporate notice naming recipient and approved disposition;
4. purchase/donation/assignment/license agreement or closing statement;
5. post-transfer Terms/Privacy/merchant identity naming another legal provider;
6. authoritative domain/account-transition evidence tied to a named entity;
7. title-level publishing assignment/reversion instruments;
8. later Georgia filing if corporate status/disposition changes.

Until such an object appears, the research route is not `forgotten`; it is a **new-primary-evidence hold**.
