# G3 Ministries — evidence timeline

**Status:** ACTIVE / PUBLICATION_HOLD  
Dates below are event dates where established; filing/publication/archive dates are not silently substituted for event dates.

## 2011–2012 — conception

- **2011-08-10 (publisher’s date):** in G3’s [RSS feed](https://web.archive.org/web/20110927042330id_/http://www.g3conference.com:80/?feed=rss2), the second item announces a conference in metro Atlanta for January 2013. Signed by Buice, posted under G3 Team. Capture on 27 September 2011 establishes public circulation by that date, not the time of private conception. `G3-S003`.
- **2011-09-23 (publisher’s date):** [Steve Lawson announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110928165117id_/http://www.g3conference.com/?p=99) sets 24–26 January 2013, describes two proposed addresses and a Friday panel, and says registration is not yet open.
- **By 2012-03-18:** the [2013 theme page](https://web.archive.org/web/20120318150142id_/http://www.g3conference.com/?page_id=2) explains *The Gospel: Message and Mission* and the three pillars; publication day of that page not displayed.
- **2012:** conference promotion develops around speakers including Paul Washer. An advertised limit of around 1,000 is a separate promotional lead, not a figure in `G3-S003` or an attendance count; original registration object needed.
- **2012-era media:** G3’s site already carries explicit cultural/political commentary touching presidential voting, abortion, marriage and Christian political conviction. Later surviving G3 pieces from 2015–2016 continue direct discussion of race/social justice and presidential politics. This is an important control: **the early conference program was strongly theological, but the broader G3 media surface was not apolitical before 2018**.

## 2013–2017 — early conference era

- **2013-01-24 to 2013-01-26 (circulated schedule):** [programme relayed on 25 January](https://missionalevangelists.blogspot.com/2013/01/g3-conference.html); the first meeting itself is attested by G3 and Challies. Theme: `The Gospel: Message and Mission`. Early speaker ecosystem includes major conservative/Reformed evangelical figures. `G3-S004`.
- **2013-01-24 evening / 25 January:** [G3 recording index](https://web.archive.org/web/20140201124351id_/http://www.g3conference.com:80/?page_id=639), sessions 1–3 and Q&A: Miller → Lawson → Challies; Buice named as panel moderator. [Contemporary notes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130722013610id_/http://www.bereanwife.net/2013/01/qa-panel-discussion/), item 7, recall Lawson on Galatians 1 the previous evening. Index captured February 2014; the capture is not the event.
- **2014-01-23 to 2014-01-25:** conference at Pray’s Mill emphasizes the church from a biblical perspective: preaching, discipleship, discipline, polity and mission. Official G3 material rejects dependence on the `latest church growth techniques`; a contemporaneous Founders Journal advertisement names the venue, dates and theme. Like the Southern Equip notice, it records a programme, not each actual appearance. `G3-S004`.
- **2015:** `The Bible is the Word of God`; strong Scripture/inerrancy/sufficiency emphasis. `G3-S004`.
- **2015-03-22 (as reported by G3):** the [Project Africa appeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160130071448id_/http://www.g3conference.com/?page_id=1960) names Kabwata as sending church for Lackson Mudenda in Sesheke, and Pray’s Mill as check payee. Eight-year funding target, not an audited transfer.
- **2015–2016 media:** G3 hosts explicit race/social-justice and presidential-politics commentary alongside its theological conference corpus. These articles are evidence of the broader media ecosystem, not evidence that the national conference theme itself had become political.
- **2016-01-21 to 2016-01-23:** [ticket dates](https://web.archive.org/web/20151125102857id_/http://www.g3conference.com/?espresso_events=g3-conference-2016) for the main Trinity meeting on the Pray’s Mill campus. The [host’s account, published 25 January](https://web.archive.org/web/20160129072313id_/http://www.deliveredbygrace.com:80/reflections-on-the-2016-g3/), distinguishes the three main days from Wednesday and Sunday sessions.
- **2017-01-19 to 2017-01-21:** `Reformation 500` scheduled at the Georgia International Convention Center; [on-site broadcaster’s account](https://ironsharpensironradio.com/2017/01/31/january-30-2017-show-with-interviews-from-the-2017-g3-conference-part-1-todd-friel-rich-pierce-and-doug-mcmasters/) confirms the exhibition hall was used; strong local-church and ordinary-pastor emphasis. Voddie Baucham and Paul Washer warnings against conference-celebrity displacement of the local church are important later context. Secondary exact-locator work still needed before quoting.

## 2018–2020 — social-justice boundary formation + institutionalization

- **2018-01-17 / 18–20:** [FirstLove’s report published 30 January](https://www.missionsfirstlove.org/united-states-blog/g3-conference-in-atlanta-georgia) distinguishes the Wednesday Spanish-language gathering from the Thursday–Saturday main event at the same center.
- **2018-06-19:** official Statement history says **14 men** meet at Herb’s House in Dallas. Josh Buice organizes the meeting; Michael O’Fallon and Phil Johnson help facilitate it; the group decides to formulate a theological statement concerning developments under the `Social Justice` banner. `G3-S005` / PASS4 candidate.
- **2018-08-31:** a contemporaneous *Iron Sharpens Iron* program identifies Buice as Pray’s Mill pastor and founder/director of the G3 Conference while interviewing him on `What Is Social Justice?`. Participant chronology only; not by itself an institutional policy document.
- **2018-09-04:** Statement on Social Justice and the Gospel is published. It becomes increasingly important within the G3 coalition. `G3-S005`.
- **2019-01:** a G3-associated `Social Justice & the Gospel` preconference makes the controversy an explicit conference-program layer. Surviving session records include Voddie Baucham, James White, Josh Buice, Tom Ascol and a Statement Framers Panel with Buice, White, Baucham, Tom Buck, Phil Johnson and Ascol, moderated by O’Fallon. Main-conference coalition nevertheless remains broader than a simple all-signers bloc.
- **2019-06-06:** `G3 Ministries for the Church, Inc.` incorporated in Georgia, control no. 19085916. `G3-S001`.
- **2019-08:** IRS tax-exempt recognition year/month recorded by IRS-derived data. `G3-S002`.
- **2019–2020:** early corporate officer records are founder-centric; Josh Buice appears as CEO/CFO in state registration material. Exact annual registration ledger to be completed.
- **2019:** national conference centers `The Mission of God`. `G3-S004`.
- **2020-01:** national conference on worship. G3 reported 5,600 attendees in its [FY2020 return, III.4a](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/842403597/202110919349301551/full), submitted 2021-04-01; not a registration audit. `G3-S004`, `G3-S158`.

- **2020-05-07:** [Buice](https://web.archive.org/web/20200508224710id_/http://www.deliveredbygrace.com/g3-expository-workshops/) distinguishes the 2019 pulpit initiative from the regional schedule unveiled at the January 2020 conference. [May registration](https://web.archive.org/web/20200522092702id_/https://g3conference.com/events/2020-g3-workshops/) offers a June online workshop and two later church venues. Not a delivery report.

## 2021–2022 — ecosystem buildout + regional/local-church layer

- **2021-07-12 (publication):** [Ahlheim](https://web.archive.org/web/20210725130211id_/https://strivingforeternity.org/leaving-the-sbc/) reported Buice and Walker introducing a forthcoming network in a Zoom call. Its date is not specified; this is earlier than the visible application.
- **By 2021-08-02:** [network application](https://web.archive.org/web/20210802180311id_/https://g3min.org/g3-church-network/) already public: non-exclusive affiliation, confessional review and minimum $500 annual commitment; not necessarily the first application or admission date. `G3-S021`.
- **2021 conference:** `Christ`; FY2021 Form 990 reports attendance increased to **6,500** and that G3 hired an Executive Director of Operations and Executive Vice President while expanding online resources. `G3-S002`.
- **pre-2021 conference partnership boundary:** Tim Challies later recounts first-person that Buice regarded him as insufficiently aligned and that renewed invitation would require Dallas Statement alignment and ceasing promotion/quotation of specified Christians. This is direct participant evidence that the social-justice dispute had moved from public argument to practical platform boundary.
- **2021-09-01:** Scott Aniol announces move into G3 as Executive Vice President / Editor-in-Chief and describes planned expansion into books, curricula, journal and online resources. `G3-S007`.
- **2022-01-01:** Pray’s Mill Baptist Church leaves the Southern Baptist Convention. Reasons publicly associated with complementarianism, accountability, social-justice/CRT concerns, pragmatism and perceived theological drift. `G3-S025`.
- **2022-02-09 (publication):** [The Garden Church](https://gardenchurchnb.com/the-g3-church-network/) says it decided to join earlier that year, while retaining its own governance.
- **2022-02-08–09 (planned), 10–12 (held):** [Aniol’s October 2021 invitation](https://web.archive.org/web/20211001132935id_/https://g3min.org/introducing-g3-biblical-worship-workshops/) is for a worship workshop on the first two dates. [Matt Sikes’s retrospective list](https://web.archive.org/web/20220218110652id_/https://g3min.org/what-we-sang-at-the-2022-g3-regional-conference-in-douglasville-ga/) describes congregational singing at the later regional conference in the same church. His estimate of around 700 is for the conference, not the workshop.
- **2022-05-25 (G3 index date):** the [announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20220602040128id_/https://g3min.org/gloria-deo-journal-of-theology-now-available/) offers the first *Gloria Deo* issue online and in print. `G3-S022`.
- **FY2022:** G3 reaches strongest financial year in current record: revenue **$1,735,978**, expenses **$1,010,186**, surplus **$725,792**, net assets **$1,318,931**. The amended raw return also reports one **$590,000 commercial-real-estate contribution**, valuation method `BROKER ESTIMATION`, with land $354,000 + buildings $236,000. `G3-S002`.

## 2023–2024 — sovereignty conference, right-flank dispute and financial reversal

- **2023-04-26 / 2023-05-03:** Scott Aniol uses G3’s platform to argue against Christian Nationalism as the New Testament prescription and proposes `Christian Faithfulness` instead. Virgil Walker’s Apr. 27 G3 article uses a more qualified definition, showing internal nuance rather than one monolithic script.
- **By 2023-05-08:** [Aniol’s online Wilson review](https://web.archive.org/web/20230508124256id_/https://g3min.org/a-review-of-mere-christendom-by-doug-wilson/) was already in circulation; [Wilson’s reply](https://web.archive.org/web/20230601041844id_/https://dougwils.com/the-church/s16-theology/public-theology-comes-out-your-fingertips-also.html) is preserved by 1 June. A version also appears in GDJT 2 (2023), pp. 127–143; the exchange does not date the release of the journal.
- **2023-06-02–03:** [Pray’s Mill workshop listing](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/g3ministriesworkshiops/834823); [Lesley’s participant report](https://web.archive.org/web/20230625194207id_/https://michellelesley.com/2023/06/15/6-reasons-you-should-attend-the-next-g3-womens-expository-teaching-workshop/) describes Buice and Buck teaching and women leading small groups. Her [6 June 2024 update](https://web.archive.org/web/20240606100014id_/https://michellelesley.com/2024/06/06/6-reasons-you-should-attend-the-next-g3-womens-expository-teaching-workshop-2/) reports returning as an apprentice. - **2023 preconference:** official G3 programming explicitly debates Christian Nationalism, theonomy and postmillennialism; political theology is now an explicit program subject.
- **2023 national conference:** official G3 material anchors the main theme as **`The Sovereignty of God`**, with a contemporaneous official article describing **13 main sessions and 15 breakout sessions**. This prevents political-theology programming from being mistaken for the whole national-conference identity.
- **2023 attendance:** the raw FY2023 filing reports a national conference with **8,300 attendees**. [Prata on 21 September](https://the-end-time.org/2023/09/21/g3-conference-2023-day-1/) says 8,200; [on 22 September](https://the-end-time.org/2023/09/22/g3-conference-2023-day-2/) she says more than 8,200. - **FY2023 financial reversal:** revenue **$1,705,689** remains near FY2022, while expenses rise to **$2,071,986**, producing a **$366,297 deficit**. Raw Part IX reconciles the **+$1,061,800** expense increase exactly: conferences/meetings **+$817,283**, advertising **+$246,511**, wages only **+$3,664**, payroll taxes **+$41,728**. - **FY2023 property disposition:** the raw return reports an `Other` asset sale with **$550,000 gross proceeds**, **$590,000 basis** and **$40,000 loss**; the prior $590,000 land/building accounting object disappears while remaining equipment persists. A new **$416,227 notes/loans receivable** appears. The sale is primary-closed at filing/accounting-object level; buyer, parcel and receivable/note linkage remain open. `G3-S002`.
- **2024-05-09/11 and 2024-10-03/05:** Reformation regional registration and Cessationist publisher listing, respectively; see [conference history](CONFERENCE_HISTORY.md#after-the-national-meeting-continuity-and-limits-20242026) for the separate participant testimony.
- **FY2024:** revenue **$1,212,253**; expenses **$1,401,429**; deficit **$189,176**; net assets **$854,674**. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2024 Schedule L:** raw filing discloses `KARIS L BUICE`, relationship `Daughter of Board Member`, transaction `SALARY`, amount **$30,409**, revenue sharing false. The filing reports `EngagedInExcessBenefitTransInd=false`. This is a disclosure fact, not proof of self-dealing or wrongdoing. `G3-S002`.

## Historical interpretation — HOLD

A plausible but not publication-authorized interpretation is that G3 moved through several overlapping phases: church-centered conference → increasingly explicit ideological boundary disputes → multi-product parachurch ecosystem while retaining regional/local-church programming → financial stress → two successive anonymous-conduct crises and operational wind-down. The 2025 and 2026 crises may reveal governance/culture vulnerabilities, but causal claims must remain proportional to the reconstructed board evolution, role overlap and decision evidence.
