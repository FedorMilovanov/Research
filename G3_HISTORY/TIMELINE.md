# G3 Ministries — evidence timeline

**Status:** ACTIVE / PUBLICATION_HOLD  
Dates below are event dates where established; filing/publication/archive dates are not silently substituted for event dates.

## 2011–2012 — conception

- **2011-08-10 (working date):** Josh Buice publicly announces/plans what becomes the G3 Conference for January 2013. Early framing centers pastors, missionaries, evangelists, church members, sound doctrine and the gospel. `G3-S003`.
- **2012:** conference promotion develops around speakers including Paul Washer. Attendance capacity was advertised around 1,000. `G3-S003` / early G3 pages.
- **2012-era media:** G3’s site already carries explicit cultural/political commentary touching presidential voting, abortion, marriage and Christian political conviction. Later surviving G3 pieces from 2015–2016 continue direct discussion of race/social justice and presidential politics. This is an important control: **the early conference program was strongly theological, but the broader G3 media surface was not apolitical before 2018**.

## 2013–2017 — early conference era

- **2013:** first G3 Conference. Theme: `The Gospel: Message and Mission`. Early speaker ecosystem includes major conservative/Reformed evangelical figures. `G3-S004`.
- **2014-01-23 to 2014-01-25:** conference at Pray’s Mill emphasizes the church from a biblical perspective: preaching, discipleship, discipline, polity and mission. Official G3 material rejects dependence on the `latest church growth techniques`; a contemporaneous Founders Journal advertisement independently confirms Pray’s Mill, dates and ecclesiological theme. `G3-S004`.
- **2015:** `The Bible is the Word of God`; strong Scripture/inerrancy/sufficiency emphasis. `G3-S004`.
- **2015–2016 media:** G3 hosts explicit race/social-justice and presidential-politics commentary alongside its theological conference corpus. These articles are evidence of the broader media ecosystem, not evidence that the national conference theme itself had become political.
- **2016:** Trinity conference. `G3-S004`.
- **2017:** `Reformation 500`; strong local-church and ordinary-pastor emphasis. Voddie Baucham and Paul Washer warnings against conference-celebrity displacement of the local church are important later context. Secondary exact-locator work still needed before quoting.

## 2018–2020 — social-justice boundary formation + institutionalization

- **2018-06-19:** official Statement history says **14 men** meet at Herb’s House in Dallas. Josh Buice organizes the meeting; Michael O’Fallon and Phil Johnson help facilitate it; the group decides to formulate a theological statement concerning developments under the `Social Justice` banner. `G3-S005` / PASS4 candidate.
- **2018-08-31:** a contemporaneous *Iron Sharpens Iron* program identifies Buice as Pray’s Mill pastor and founder/director of the G3 Conference while interviewing him on `What Is Social Justice?`. Participant chronology only; not by itself an institutional policy document.
- **2018-09-04:** Statement on Social Justice and the Gospel is published. It becomes increasingly important within the G3 coalition. `G3-S005`.
- **2019-01:** a G3-associated `Social Justice & the Gospel` preconference makes the controversy an explicit conference-program layer. Surviving session records include Voddie Baucham, James White, Josh Buice, Tom Ascol and a Statement Framers Panel with Buice, White, Baucham, Tom Buck, Phil Johnson and Ascol, moderated by O’Fallon. Main-conference coalition nevertheless remains broader than a simple all-signers bloc.
- **2019-06-06:** `G3 Ministries for the Church, Inc.` incorporated in Georgia, control no. 19085916. `G3-S001`.
- **2019-08:** IRS tax-exempt recognition year/month recorded by IRS-derived data. `G3-S002`.
- **2019–2020:** early corporate officer records are founder-centric; Josh Buice appears as CEO/CFO in state registration material. Exact annual registration ledger to be completed.
- **2019:** national conference centers `The Mission of God`. `G3-S004`.
- **2020:** national conference centers `Worship`; contemporary participant evidence places the event around 5,000 attendees while later G3-linked history gives about 5,600. Publication wording remains a range pending an official final count. `G3-S004`.

## 2021–2022 — ecosystem buildout + regional/local-church layer

- **2021:** G3 Church Network is launched/developed as a voluntary network rather than denomination, with strong confessional/ecclesiological requirements. `G3-S021`.
- **2021 conference:** `Christ`; FY2021 Form 990 reports attendance increased to **6,500** and that G3 hired an Executive Director of Operations and Executive Vice President while expanding online resources. `G3-S002`.
- **pre-2021 conference partnership boundary:** Tim Challies later recounts first-person that Buice regarded him as insufficiently aligned and that renewed invitation would require Dallas Statement alignment and ceasing promotion/quotation of specified Christians. This is direct participant evidence that the social-justice dispute had moved from public argument to practical platform boundary.
- **2021-09-01:** Scott Aniol announces move into G3 as Executive Vice President / Editor-in-Chief and describes planned expansion into books, curricula, journal and online resources. `G3-S007`.
- **2022-01-01:** Pray’s Mill Baptist Church leaves the Southern Baptist Convention. Reasons publicly associated with complementarianism, accountability, social-justice/CRT concerns, pragmatism and perceived theological drift. `G3-S025`.
- **2022-02:** G3 holds its **first Regional Conference** at Pray’s Mill. An official Matt Sikes article dated Feb. 18 says roughly **700 people** were packed into the church auditorium. This proves that regional/local-church-scale programming continued alongside the much larger national conference model.
- **2022:** first volume/launch period of `Gloria Deo Journal of Theology`. `G3-S022`.
- **FY2022:** G3 reaches strongest financial year in current record: revenue **$1,735,978**, expenses **$1,010,186**, surplus **$725,792**, net assets **$1,318,931**. The amended raw return also reports one **$590,000 commercial-real-estate contribution**, valuation method `BROKER ESTIMATION`, with land $354,000 + buildings $236,000. `G3-S002`.

## 2023–2024 — sovereignty conference, right-flank dispute and financial reversal

- **2023-04-26 / 2023-05-03:** Scott Aniol uses G3’s platform to argue against Christian Nationalism as the New Testament prescription and proposes `Christian Faithfulness` instead. Virgil Walker’s Apr. 27 G3 article uses a more qualified definition, showing internal nuance rather than one monolithic script.
- **2023 preconference:** official G3 programming explicitly debates Christian Nationalism, theonomy and postmillennialism; political theology is now an explicit program subject.
- **2023 national conference:** official G3 material anchors the main theme as **`The Sovereignty of God`**, with a contemporaneous official article describing **13 main sessions and 15 breakout sessions**. This prevents political-theology programming from being mistaken for the whole national-conference identity.
- **2023 attendance:** the raw FY2023 filing reports a national conference with **8,300 attendees**. This is stronger than earlier attendee/exhibitor approximations and should control the filing-based article wording. `G3-S002`.
- **2023-06:** Michael O’Fallon resigns from the G3 board during the wider conservative-right conflict over Christian nationalism. His own reproduced statement describes the departure as his resignation; exact internal causation remains unresolved.
- **FY2023 financial reversal:** revenue **$1,705,689** remains near FY2022, while expenses rise to **$2,071,986**, producing a **$366,297 deficit**. Raw Part IX reconciles the **+$1,061,800** expense increase exactly: conferences/meetings **+$817,283**, advertising **+$246,511**, wages only **+$3,664**, payroll taxes **+$41,728**. Both FY2022 and FY2023 classify all functional expenses as program services and report zero management/general and fundraising. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2023 property disposition:** the raw return reports an `Other` asset sale with **$550,000 gross proceeds**, **$590,000 basis** and **$40,000 loss**; the prior $590,000 land/building accounting object disappears while remaining equipment persists. A new **$416,227 notes/loans receivable** appears. The sale is primary-closed at filing/accounting-object level; buyer, parcel and receivable/note linkage remain open. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2024:** revenue **$1,212,253**; expenses **$1,401,429**; deficit **$189,176**; net assets **$854,674**. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2024 Schedule L:** raw filing discloses `KARIS L BUICE`, relationship `Daughter of Board Member`, transaction `SALARY`, amount **$30,409**, revenue sharing false. The filing reports `EngagedInExcessBenefitTransInd=false`. This is a disclosure fact, not proof of self-dealing or wrongdoing. `G3-S002`.
- **2024:** filing describes substantial ongoing activity: workshops, conferences, publishing and Church Network activity. Financial weakening therefore should not be described as institutional inactivity.

## 2025 — founder crisis and attempted reset

- **2025-05-04:** according to G3’s official statement, Pray’s Mill elders confront Josh Buice with evidence relating to anonymous online activity. `G3-S008`.
- **2025-05-08:** G3 board accepts Buice resignation. `G3-S008`.
- **2025-05-12:** G3 publishes statement: for roughly three years Buice operated at least four anonymous social accounts, two anonymous email accounts and two Substack platforms; he had denied connections before later confessing when confronted. The 2025 national conference and workshops are cancelled. `G3-S008`.
- **2025-05-12 board point:** contemporary reporting still identifies Tom Buck as a G3 board participant. Current B1 evidence places his later departure after this point but before Scott Aniol’s presidency began July 9. Exact resignation date/instrument and motive remain open.
- **2025-05 to 2025-07-20 archive boundary:** both identified official historical board-roster paths, `/about/who-we-are/` and `/vision/leadership/`, have successful bounded Wayback acquisitions returning valid empty CDX results for the relevant transition window. This exhausts those two known archive routes but does **not** establish any resignation/start date.
- **2025-06-16:** G3 announces a `Renewed Vision`, emphasizing local church and rejecting celebrity culture. `G3-S009`.
- **2025-06-30:** G3 announces Virgil Walker’s transition out of ministry leadership to local-church service. `G3-S023`.
- **2025-07-09 / announcement mid-July:** Scott Aniol becomes G3 president. Jonathan Frazier is publicly identified as board chairman in the announcement. `G3-S010`.
- **2025-07:** Pastor-Theologian initiative announced as part of a stated local-pastor / anti-celebrity reset. `G3-S024`.
- **2025-12-22:** official G3 Buck Braswell author material identifies him as a G3 board member. `G3-S045`.
- **FY2025:** revenue collapses to **$399,645** while expenses remain **$950,642**; deficit **$550,997**; net assets **$304,459**. Raw Schedule L again reports `KARIS L BUICE` / `Daughter of Board Member` / `SALARY`, now **$31,880**, with excess-benefit indicator false. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2025 program-history narrative:** raw `IRS990/Desc` states that G3 separated **Living Heritage Homeschool as an independent entity from G3 Ministries during 2025**. This is `VERIFIED_PRIMARY` for the separation; it does not identify Living Heritage as any later 2026 G3+/Press successor. `G3-S002`.
- **FY2025 receivable:** the continuing notes/loans receivable ends the filing at **$153,000**, after carrying from FY2023 $416,227 through FY2024 $251,197. Exact debtor and transaction linkage remain open. `G3-S002`.

## 2026 — Buck dossier and second leadership crisis

- **2026-02-06:** official G3 content again describes Buck Braswell as a member of the G3 Ministries Board of Directors. This extends the individual late-board primary anchor into 2026 but does not establish the full board roster. PASS3 `G3-S058` candidate.
- **2026-03-27:** according to first-person testimony by Phil Johnson, Tom Buck sends him the accusation packet; FBC Lindale elders contact Johnson the same day. `G3-S012`.
- **Spring 2026:** FBC Lindale investigates and consults externally. Available evidence supports real attribution problems in some Buck sermons, while not establishing that he systematically preached others’ complete sermons. `G3-S012`, FBC statement materials.
- **2026:** a dossier reviews 17 sermons and alleges numerous instances of plagiarism using a broad methodology. All 17 now have dedicated item-level forensic work in Research, but **item coverage is not proof count**; original-audio/source/attribution gates remain open per item. `G3-S014`.
- **2026-07-21:** an acquired official G3 `Who We Are` Wayback snapshot explicitly lists the Board of Directors as **Buck Braswell, Matt Broome, Jon Norton, Matt Sikes, Dylan Joyner and Ron Mooney**. This roster is `VERIFIED_PRIMARY` for July 21 only; exact continuity through the late-August crisis remains open. `G3-S096`.
- **2026 summer:** anonymous packets are circulated beyond FBC Lindale to outside associates/partners of Buck. Direct recipient testimony supports existence of mailed packages; Texas postmark and false return address are specifically supported by first-person testimony. `G3-S012`, `G3-S026`.
- **2026-08-17:** public reporting exposes the anonymous campaign and alleged involvement of G3/Pray’s Mill figures. Secondary reporting is a discovery/corroboration layer, not sole proof for disputed details.
- **late Aug 2026:** Scott Aniol, Jon Norton, Matt Sikes and Dylan Joyner acknowledge wrongdoing in anonymous external circulation/interference and announce their pastoral resignations at Pray’s Mill. Their reproduced joint wording says they voluntarily resigned; separate secondary reporting says three were required to resign and Joyner resigned voluntarily. **Exact resignation mechanics remain DISPUTED pending a primary PMBC/deacon/congregational record.** `G3-S015`, `G3-S042`, `G3-S043`.

- **2026-08-27 onward:** reporting and subscriber screenshots indicate G3+ and possibly G3 Press are being transferred/acquired by **another ministry**, but no acquired first-party object names the transferee or transaction terms. Current Living Heritage G3+ bundling is a continuity lead, not ownership evidence. `G3-S019` / Q006.
- **late Aug 2026:** 2027 conference registration backend records cancellation and refunds. `G3-S011`.

## Historical interpretation — HOLD

A plausible but not publication-authorized interpretation is that G3 moved through several overlapping phases: church-centered conference → increasingly explicit ideological boundary disputes → multi-product parachurch ecosystem while retaining regional/local-church programming → financial stress → two successive anonymous-conduct crises and operational wind-down. The 2025 and 2026 crises may reveal governance/culture vulnerabilities, but causal claims must remain proportional to the reconstructed board evolution, role overlap and decision evidence.
