## 3. Direct Fee-1987 positions now closed

### Material practice

Fee explicitly canvasses three major possibilities:

1. an external head covering;
2. short hair as the covering problem;
3. loose/unbound hair.

His direct conclusion is calibrated: a **modified traditional external-covering reading** (a loose wrap/shawl rather than a full face veil) appears to create fewer difficulties, while the loose-hair reading remains viable. He rejects a simple short-hair equation as poorly fitting vv.5–6.

```text
FEE_1987_MATERIAL_EXTERNAL_COVERING = PREFERRED_MODIFIED_TRADITIONAL_OPTION
FEE_1987_LOOSE_HAIR = VIABLE_ALTERNATIVE
FEE_1987_SIMPLE_SHORT_HAIR = REJECTED_AS_WEAK
FEE_1987_FULL_FACE_VEIL_MODEL = NOT_THE_OBJECT
```

### `ἐξουσία` in v10

Fee directly rejects the passive interpretation in which the woman 'has' another person's authority over her. He argues that neither `ἐξουσία` nor the construction 'have authority over' naturally supplies that external-subjection sense.

He also finds a simple metonymic equation `ἐξουσία = veil` inadequately motivated.

His own direction is that Paul appears to affirm the woman's own authority/freedom with respect to her head, while Fee explicitly leaves the exact contextual referent unresolved.

```text
FEE_1987_EXOUSIA_PASSIVE_EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY = REJECTED_DIRECT_BODY
FEE_1987_EXOUSIA_AS_SIMPLE_METONYM_FOR_VEIL = REJECTED_AS_INADEQUATELY_MOTIVATED
FEE_1987_EXOUSIA_WOMAN_OWN_AUTHORITY_DIRECTION = DIRECT_AUTHOR_BODY
FEE_1987_EXOUSIA_EXACT_REFERENT = UNRESOLVED_BY_FEE
```

### Angels

Fee directly rejects the lustful/fallen-angels / Genesis 6 threat reading in this edition. Beyond that rejection, he admits substantial uncertainty regarding the exact function of the angels.

```text
FEE_1987_WATCHERS_LUST_THREAT = REJECTED_DIRECT_BODY
FEE_1987_EXACT_ANGELIC_FUNCTION = OPEN_UNCERTAIN
```

### `κεφαλή`, creation and interdependence

Fee does not treat v3 as a simple statement establishing a hierarchy of female subordination. He uses the literal/metaphorical head relation to frame the problem and reads vv.11–12 as qualifying vv.8–9 so the creation argument is not turned into simple male authority over woman.

```text
FEE_1987_SIMPLE_SUBORDINATION_HEADSHIP_FRAME = REJECTED
FEE_1987_VV11_12_QUALIFY_VV8_9 = DIRECT_AUTHOR_BODY
```

### Historical Corinth trigger

Fee rejects the simplistic reconstruction that Christian women were merely rebelling against husbands and discarding a veil as a symbol of subordination. His preferred reconstruction links the problem to Corinthian over-realized eschatology / pneumatic self-understanding and an attempted erosion of sex distinction. He remains explicit that the ad-hoc historical trigger cannot be recovered with certainty.

```text
FEE_1987_HUSBAND_INSUBORDINATION_TRIGGER = REJECTED_AS_SIMPLISTIC
FEE_1987_OVERREALIZED_ESCHATOLOGY_TRIGGER = PREFERRED_RECONSTRUCTION
FEE_1987_EXACT_CORINTH_TRIGGER = ACKNOWLEDGED_UNCERTAIN
```

Fee also directly treats women as genuinely praying and prophesying in the gathered assembly.

```text
FEE_1987_WOMEN_ACTUALLY_PRAY_AND_PROPHESY_IN_ASSEMBLY = DIRECT_AUTHOR_BODY
```
