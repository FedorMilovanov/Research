# Third-pass source-access discipline — 1 Corinthians 11

Date: 2026-08-10

This note records the access rule behind the third audit:

- web-accessible material is researched by the agent rather than requested from the user;
- third-party reading routes are research locators, not permission to redistribute copyrighted books;
- a table of contents, abstract, snippet or review is not mislabeled as a fully read chapter;
- closed-book acquisition is reserved for full section + notes where page-level context adds material evidentiary value;
- current/revised editions control an author's latest commentary position unless an edition-delta study is explicitly being done.

Key bibliographic deltas established in this pass:

- Garland: BECNT 2nd edition (2025) is the current Garland target; 2003 is historical comparison only.
- Fee: NICNT Revised (2014) controls current Fee-commentary attribution; 1987 is historical comparison only.
- Fitzmyer: the 11:2–16 section locator is pp.404–424; do not ask the user merely for locator/bibliographic access.
- Thiselton: public TOC locators establish pp.800–847 and internal subheadings, but not complete technical argument/footnotes.
- Ciampa/Rosner: public TOC locators establish pp.503–540 and major verse-group starts, but not the full section/notes.
